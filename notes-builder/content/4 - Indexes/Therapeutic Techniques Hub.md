# Therapeutic Techniques Hub
**Details**
[[The Methods of Treating Mental Disorders]]: The basis of the types of treatments of therapy.
[[Other focuses (rather than symptoms)]]

[[Method of EMDR]]: A method that people are still unsure of how it functions but is effective for PTSD.
[[Cognitive Therapy]]: The basis of treatment for many mental illnesses and mental disorders.
[[Free Association]]: One of the founding methods of psychotherapy, relatively ineffective nowadays due to its requirement of time and work.
[[Somatic Treatment]]: Effective for PTSD patients as well as it helps them feel safe and content in their own bodies.
[[Communal Rhythms and Synchrony]]: Effective for PTSD as the patient is able to reintegrate themselves back into the society that they think they differ from. 
[[Arising Methods of Therapy]]: A lot of methods that are arising
[[Schema Therapy for Shame]] : This is a form of therapy for BPD that was developed by Dr Young who was Aaron T Beck’s apprentice. It basically combines CBT with lighter forms of psychoanalysis perhaps ?
[[DBT for Bipolar Disorder]]: A dialectical form of CBT developed by Linehan, especially effective for BPD, Bipolar,...
[[Nature-Based Interventions]]: Improving other factors rather than just symptoms with other treatments catered for mindfulness.
[[The Comprehensive Guide to DBT]]: Skills training in a session, basically how it should go
