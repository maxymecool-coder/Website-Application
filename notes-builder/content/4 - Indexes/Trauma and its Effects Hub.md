# Trauma and its Effects Hub
**Details**
[[The consequences of trauma]]

[[Body-brain connection]]: explains why a person might have chronic, unexplained physical pain, digestive issues, or fly into a panic without "thinking" about the trauma. Their _body_ is remembering the threat long after the event is over.

[[Attachment Theory]]: explains how our first relationships have effect on our future trauma and vulnerability.
[[Disorganized Attachment]]: the type of attachment in children that is lesser known, though it is the most commonly attributed with PTSD.
[[Unhealthy Attachment]]: the usual types that most parents encounter and can usually be changed when they grow up through sufficient teachings.
[[Polyvagal Theory]]: explains how the brain reacts in reaction to danger and in turn trauma.

[[Amygdala in PTSD]]: explains how the Amygdala functions in people who have PTSD
[[Medial prefrontal cortex]]: relates heavily to the Amygdala.
