# The importance of Preparation
Before doing anything, you need a clear idea that well-defined tasks will beat willpower, its not about training to have the willpower to do things but rather not having to use it. And that is when you have to effectively organize/take notes.

The purpose of a Zettelkasten is to create a web of those resources, allowing you to just pick out info and write a paper that you want to.
