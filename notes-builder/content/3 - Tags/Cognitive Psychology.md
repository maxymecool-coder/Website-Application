# Cognitive Psychology
**Details**
It is the basic science of understanding how the mind works by conducting empirical research into how people perceive, remember, and think. This has a huge hand that leads into learning, as the theories of human development can be used to shape the tools of education-such as testing regimes and resources for special groups. Neuroscience, also use tools to view the human brain and understand their mechanism, but its not far enough yet that it helps us improve learning.
