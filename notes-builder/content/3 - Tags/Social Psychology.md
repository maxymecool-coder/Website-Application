# Social Psychology
**Details**
Social psychology is the scientific study of how people's thoughts, feelings, and behaviors are influenced by the actual, imagined, or implied presence of others. It explores how individuals interact within social contexts, examining the impact of social situations on human behavior.

This field is the study of people's thoughts and behaviors in social situations. It is a known fact through [[sociology]] that people react differently under emotions, stresses, certain institutions and so on. But not only do we believe in what we think but what we BELIEVE that others think and believe that the situation is, as we operate through a subjective lens of the world rather than an objective one, just like anyone else.
