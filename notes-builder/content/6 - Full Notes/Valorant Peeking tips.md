# More Valorant Tips
**Details**
First, we will want to talk about trying to move in a perpendicular T when peeking the enemy, this ensures you will have the best possible chance of killing them due to your movement.

Secondly, try to avoid peeking angles where your opponent would have a clear advantage. For example, we have Haven's A heaven, which the enemy only needs to aim at you from that singular spot whilst you have to clear long, short, default, etc... Even when you need to do so, try to use util to assist yourself as well as pre-aim common angles to combat them. (Or simply jump onto ammo and catch everyone off-guard).

Thirdly, advantage, like positional advantage, try to A-D strafe when you're holding for example to make yourself a harder target to hit. Or using an off-angle that still ensures you have an "out" after getting a kill, like playing with Chamber's TP, Jett's Dash,...
