# Theories and Hypotheses
**Details**
These two things are separate concepts. A theory is a set of propositions meant to explain a group of observations. Hypotheses on the other hand are expectations of what would happen if a theory is correct. For example, if I had a theory that people with OCD had a little person on their shoulder talking about some "wash your hands" stuff, the hypothesis would be that they are compulsively washing their hands. Now see that there are two part to the hypothesis, a little person telling them to wash their hands and them washing their hands. The latter is correct, but the former may not be, and that must be proven to test if both are correct or only one is.

To determine the plausibility of a theory, one must consider its fallibility, its repeatability and its testability. Quickly determining these terms, we understand that it can be proven (testability), it can/cannot be disproven (fallibility), and it can be repeated under the circumstances in the test trials (repeatability).
