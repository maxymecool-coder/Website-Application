# Qualia and Epiphenomenalism
**Details**
"Qualia" is the technical term used to describe the qualitative, subjective characters of experience—the redness of a rose, the pang of a headache, or the taste of wine. If Jackson's argument holds, these qualia are real but non-physical. This leads to the position of Epiphenomenalism. If the physical sciences already provide a complete account of the _causes_ of behavior (e.g., neurons firing cause muscles to move), then there is no causal work left for qualia to do.

And so when we "think" that we are making a decision consciously, it is at the end just our mind having us feel the visceral experience, not that we are actually having a choice necessarily. Simpler, take the advantage of Pong, we push the ball around, and it makes it as if we feel that the pixels are hitting the ball and making it bounce. But rather, there is a chip that is calculating the trajectory and physics of the ball and simply letting the pixels visualize it all.
