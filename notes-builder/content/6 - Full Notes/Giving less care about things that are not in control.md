# Giving less care about things that are not in control
**Details**
- In life, in the world, there are so many things that we cannot control, the weather, the traffic, how others feel, how they might insult us. But why should we care about them ? We should just let it not take space in our mind where we think, and instead focus on what we can change, ourselves. We cannot prevent traffic from happening, but instead of swearing and getting all annoyed or emotionally unstable about it, we can just...accept it and do other productive things while stuck in traffic, thinking of our schedule, hobbies, all of that stuff. Things happen and most of the time it doesn't go the way that we want it to, but the thing that we can do is how we act towards it, don't let it control us, be stoic, be calm, think rationally and deal with it.
- Care less about what people think of you and just focus to work on yourself and those who truly cares.

**Supporting Content**
- Ultimately, basing our happiness on achieving specific outcomes sets us up for disappointment.
