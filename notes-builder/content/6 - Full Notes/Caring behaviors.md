# Caring behaviors
**Details**
- This method insists on partners completing 3-5 things they have agreed on doing for each other and increasing love for both sides as they feel loved and cared for. This helps partners erase the thought that their partner telepathically knows what they want, and when they do not fulfil it they feel attacked and want to repel back by not fulfilling it as well. It also teaches partners to be more unconditional for each other, as they are not asking for anything in return, just showing love and care to their partner. Lastly, it shows partners that the other is not LIKE you, you may give them what YOU would like but they would not appreciate it as its not what THEY would like.
  
  So be mindful.
