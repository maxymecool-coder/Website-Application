# Shutdown Ritual
**Details**
- Begin with a rhythm, repeat every time that deep work is involved. This helps your brain to release all work-related thoughts and relax to prepare for the next day
- It is suggested to check your task list and mark unfinished tasks make up a plan to finish it (short).
- Move thoughts or new tasks into task list so you don't forget it later on.
