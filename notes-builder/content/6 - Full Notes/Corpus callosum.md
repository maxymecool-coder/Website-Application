# Corpus callosum
**Details**
This is the part of the brain that helps the two hemispheres communicate with each other. This structure allows the two halves to function collaboratively. In some cases of sever epilepsy, this neurological bridge (and some other ones) may be cut so the seizures will not get to the other hemisphere. This surgery may relive suffering but the two hemispheres will now act as separate brains. And so stuff that happens in one or the other brain that requires their functions may not work, like visual, language, and so on. This though, does not mean that the patient will completely lose their skills and abilities.
