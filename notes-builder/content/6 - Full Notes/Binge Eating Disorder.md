# Binge Eating Disorder
**Details**
This is very similar to [[Bulimia Nervosa]], but basically without all the trying to get it out, even though they still feel shame. Also, these people, different from the two other disorders, are usually obese. These can inhibit the same way that the previous two did, but moreover, it may even lead to social ineptness due to social standards bleeding into and people judging obesity and so on. Stuff like type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular problems, breathing problemms also happen.
