# Tropane Alkaloids
**Details**
The "power plants" of the _Solanaceae_ family are characterized by **Tropane Alkaloids**. An alkaloid is a chemical produced by a plant containing a basic nitrogen atom. The two primary tropane alkaloids discussed are Atropine (a mixture of stereoisomers) and Scopolamine, identical to Atropine but possess one additional oxygen atom. These molecules are found in plants like _Atropa belladonna_, _Datura_, and _Brugmansia_. They are potent poisons found in all parts of the plant (seeds, leaves, roots).

Tropane Alkaloids are antagonists at the **Muscarinic Acetylcholine Receptor**. The Acetylcholine is an important neurotransmitter functioning the parasympathetic nervous system. When it is blocked, the fight or flight system goes haywire as there is nothing to keep it in check.
