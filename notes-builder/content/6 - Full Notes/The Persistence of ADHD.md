# The Persistence of ADHD
**Details**
How well does ADHD persists throughout one's life ? Well professor Susan Campbell answered that in her landmark study of testing children through 8 hours worth of test then repeating it when 6th grade came.

The result was that 50% of the original children remained with ADHD, but there were actually 2 main reasons playing into this. Firstly, biological cause, the severity of the initial symptoms. Children with "difficult temperaments" (high intensity, low effortful control) were more likely to remain ADHD. Secondly, though, was their environment, how their parents reacted with invalidation, sarcasm, unseriousness, resulting in them significantly more likely to have ADHD. This illustrates a "coercive cycle" or "reciprocal chain." A child with a difficult temperament frustrates the parent; the parent responds with harsh control; this harshness increases the child's stress and impulsivity, leading to worse behavior, which elicits more harshness.

The opposite of this environment was a "good holding environment" which allowed children to be energetic whilst protecting them from harms and also not escalating the situation due to the parents' emotions. This allowed the children to develop self-regulation.
