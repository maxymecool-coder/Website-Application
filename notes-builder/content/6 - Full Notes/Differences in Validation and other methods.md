# Differences in Validation and other methods
**Details**
In BPD, validation usually is the first response that people who knows how to deal with it steer to. This is because the person with BPD deal with problems relating to invalidation lots.

To specify this, we can start with talking about how BPD enacts invalidation around a person’s life. Inherently, people with BPD are unstable, they are emotionally sensitive, and so they react to actions accordingly, meaning very emotionally. When someone tells them to cheer up or talk them and all they would feel disrespected or treated like a child or that the person doesn’t understand them rather than actually be happy (lol). And when you instead not say anything it instead becomes you not caring.

That leads us to understand that we should validate above all else as it is perhaps the most if not only effective way to communicate to someone with BPD that you care.
