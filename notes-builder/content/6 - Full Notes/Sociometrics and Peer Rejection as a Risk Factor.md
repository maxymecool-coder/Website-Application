# Sociometrics and Peer Rejection as a Risk Factor
**Details**
Sociometrics defines the likeness of a particular student in a class or group by asking their classmates to nominate how they feel about the other students. This results in five classifications: Popular, Average, Neglected (low visibility), Controversial (liked by some, hated by others), and **Rejected**.

Of all factors, peer rejection is arguably the single strongest predictor of negative long-term outcomes, including juvenile delinquency, school dropout, and adult mental health needs as children are incredibly sensitive to social/behavioral deficits as well as pay insane amounts of attention to their surroundings. But, rejection also denies the child's opportunities to learn social skills, compounding their deficits.

While having a high-quality friend can be a protective factor, this largely depends on their ability as a friend, like if they are empathetic and so on.
