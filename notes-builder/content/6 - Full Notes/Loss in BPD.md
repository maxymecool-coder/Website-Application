# Loss in BPD
**Details**
People with BPD experience lots of loss in their lives, and so that accumulated loss can cause them to act like loss has happened even if it hasn’t yet. This can further show in two ways, one is that they become hypersensitized to it, even at the first sign of loss they can already react as if it had already happened as said before, or secondly, they can be indifferent to it. This second situation is something extremely similar to those who have PTSD, they become desensitized to any and all emotions, positive or negative to protect their own mental state from being overwhelmed. (Inhibited grieving. This can also be automated, like a reflex rather than a mindful decision).

Moreover, people can also alternate between these two states, feeling extreme grief to not feeling any. This can also manifest in them displaying emotions that are different from what they are actually experiencing in order to avoid facing what will eat them up and what they think would actually destroy them.
