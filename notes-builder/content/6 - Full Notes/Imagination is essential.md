# Imagination is essential
**Details**
Imagination is important in everyone lives, it gives us food for thought, let us think about all kinds of stuff while we're going on with our daily lives, making life more interesting and giving us the ability to envision futures. Basically it enhances our pleasure, alleviates pain and so much more. Though traumatized individuals when experiencing their daily lives can't escape the grasps of their pasts and think about a better future, constantly being pulled back to the time they felt such intense emotions and involvement, this makes them lose hope for such future.

**Supporting Content**
For example, a person walking down the street for most of us is just someone walking, but for a rape victim, may see someone who is about to molest them and go into panic mode. The same goes for children, one who was abused by their parents can view adults as monsters that will hurt them.

Or in children who had been abused, they cannot imagine their way out of a bad situation, no matter how inherently benign and happy it is. Because in their mind the universe is an antagonistic and cruel place that would crush the sight of hope the moment it is present. And they will take this view to the extremes, every image, every sounds, of every moment presents a threat to them.
