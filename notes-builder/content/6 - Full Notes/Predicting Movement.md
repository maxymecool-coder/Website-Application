# Movement Reading
**Details**
The player will know the bot's movement patterns, and will react accordingly to the "cue" that it produces when doing so.

And to do so, one will need to learn "tension management". We must view this as an ability that can be used in a short amount of time before its effectiveness drops, one can allocate this tension into their forearm, wrist or arm to increase control in that area. And different scenarios will require different amounts of tension.

When flicking, it is important not to do it too fast nor explosive, and instead focus on trying to read the movement, not react to it fast.
