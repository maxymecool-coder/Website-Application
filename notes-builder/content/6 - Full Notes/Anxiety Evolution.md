# Anxiety Evolution
**Details**
Back in the early days of humans, our genes made sure that we had the capability to be able to notice/distinguish danger even if it’s a false positive as we were vulnerable. This causes our biology to be primed for fear. However, in the modern world, this evolution is strictly unnecessary, but nonetheless the adaptation exists. And so when it is triggered by non-lethal threats (e.g., elevators, social evaluation), it causes impairment. And so hyper anxiety may not be a broken biological system but rather a defective or maladaptive one.

Diagnosing anxiety requires a strict developmental lens due to how fears change as cognitive maturity increases. Toddlers fear separation, darkness, animals since their survival. Children fear injuries, the paranormal, death. Teenagers fear social and existentialism. A clinician must determine if the fear is suitable to diagnose the disorder.
