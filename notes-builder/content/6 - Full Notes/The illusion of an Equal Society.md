# The illusion of an Equal Society
**Details**
The popular idea is that society would be better if we all started equally, as this would make any results we get and furthermore our own, not affected by heredity. This view, though idealistic and optimistic, cannot exist. Heredity does not just flow through statuses or money, but also talent, genetics. We cannot easily or efficiently identify those who are above in that matter for us to take care of them and give them less advantages so to say as it would be unfair, unjust to them then, perhaps a paradoxical principle of a supposedly just world.
