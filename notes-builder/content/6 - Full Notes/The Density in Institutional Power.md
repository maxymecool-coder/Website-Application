# The Density in Institutional Power
**Details**
In the lower power institutions, these places have less connections from the governing power to the people below. For example, birth certificates, there is no proof someone exists, there is not an easy way to mail something to them if one wanted to. Instead, in places like Africa, it is transferred from person to person, village leaders, tribe leaders and then eventually ending up in the hands of the person. But one can clearly see the flaw here, the connection is weak and thin, and so the collective power is in turn also small.

In developed institutions like growing and developed countries, they have all the information they need for anything, they get constant support from the people who they in turn manage back. This means that these institutions have a large collective power and are very powerful. In other words, they can "get a lot done."
