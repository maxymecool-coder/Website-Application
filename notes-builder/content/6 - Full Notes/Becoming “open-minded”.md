# Becoming “open-minded”
**Details**
As said before, developing topics and questions from what we have is like taking them out of context of a pretty rich network which comes with material we can use. But this also comes with another advantage: We become more open to new ideas. 

This is due to the fact that we become less attached to something since we are familiar with it. Because of that we know its limits, its possibilities, and so we can be playful with it, play and modify it to spot new and different ideas without repeating old ones believing they are new. 

If open-mindedness is all that is needed, the best artists and scientists were hobbyists. Jeremy Dean, who has written extensively on routines and rituals and suggests seeing old ways of thinking as thinking routines, puts it well when he writes that we cannot break with a certain way of thinking if we are not even aware that it is a certain way of thinking (Dean, 2013).
