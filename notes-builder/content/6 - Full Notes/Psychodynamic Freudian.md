# Psychodynamic Freudian
**Details**
Sigmund Freud was an Austrian physician, he developed the first psychodynamic theory. This kind of theory relates personality to the interplay of forces within the individual, including, especially the unconscious ones. Freud's influence has been immense, extending into many other fields, but in recent times, as more of his theories have been debunked and so on, his influence in psychology has truly gotten weaker.

Freud developed the theory from hypotheses that we had pent up desires that existed due to our childhood experiences and similar circumstances. And so when we release it, we would feel better, may even cure our mental illness in certain cases, this was termed **catharsis**. However, later scholars who reexamined the medical records of Freud's patients saw no actual benefit from the treatment.
