# The treatment of BPD
**Details**
Mainly, the treatment of BPD includes DBT, which consists of skills training. They are ranked in order of importance: stopping behaviors that are very likely to destroy therapy; skill acquisition, strengthening, and generalization; and reducing therapy interfering behaviors; also to decrease quality-of-life interfering behaviors.

This process contains basically everything thay DBT offers as a treatment. Working on skills are usually much more boring than working on the therapeutic process/collaboration,… but it is more than necessary to do so. Moreover, the way that they get treated also is important, as they need to be collaborative. If none of these criteria are met, the two need to discuss with each other.
