# Balanced responses to better outcomes
**Details**
Building a so called “protocol” to respond to people in their crisis is very important so as not to get drawn into their spiral and be able to help them.

Firstly, pause, take a breath, notice your physical sensations, label them as the emotion you are experiencing. For example, “Uh oh, I’m having a panic attack, I just saw my abuser, my feeling here is RAGE (or ANGER).” Then, pay attention to your body posture, try to relax your muscles, unclench your hands, smile a bit (not because you’re happy but because you’re trying to calm down). Validate and cheerlead yourself. This can be something like “my emotions are understandable given what’s going on.”, “I love this person, so of course I would feel mad.”

In addition to that, you can also try to find triggers that makes you yourself more emotionally vulnerable to their actions:

1. Recall a few times when the interactions went poorly.
2. Did it start off emotionally, or was there a build, or an explosion ?
3. Were there other factors that may have affected you that particular day ? Sickness, tired, work ?
4. Do a little analysis of the event
A. When did it start ?
B. When did it get to the point of no return and problems would start happening ?
C. What happened after the explosion ? Did you change ? Did they change ?…

And so when you feel something like anger, you DISENGAGE. Give them space, tell them a word that you need to go because its too much and that you love them. Then completely disengage, do not stay connected as anger will stay up or even increase. After that, practice kindness, compassion. Do something kind for them... as *anger cannot be maintained in the face of compassion.*

Sadness on the other hand makes you wanna give up, because they're hopeless. Try to do stuff, just activate your support system to do things unrelated to the BPD person, don't let yourself stay still and upset.
