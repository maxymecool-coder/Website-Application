# The use of treatment manuals
**Details**
These manuals are detailed books on conducting psychological treatment (e.g., Wiley Treatment Planners). They provide specific procedures for the therapist to use at each stage of the treatment, which are mostly for those who are starting out. With manuals, someone reading a study about psychotherapy can get a gist of what is happening in these sessions. It is actually incredibly advised to use it in psychotherapy research and training in clinical psychology (e.g., Nathan & Gorman, 2002) and (Crits-Christoph, Chambless, Frank, et al., 1995). Although controversies and problems have arise, these planners have adapted and gotten better by the times.
