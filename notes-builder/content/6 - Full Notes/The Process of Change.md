# The Process of Change
**Details**
The change in between idealisms of a period usually takes centuries. For example, taking the move from Feudalism to Capitalism. In the times where feudalism was dominant, there were jobs such as artisans, blacksmiths, and other occupations that grew because of the conditions that Feudalism created for them. Though it did help them, people eventually wanted to be wealthier, more powerful, and by that they needed the services of these occupations, but they could not get it easily. Feudalism also were keeping them from growing more, people could not just hired labor from these people due to the laws and boundaries where serfs went with the land. Marx saw these concepts as "fetters" which meant chains that were preventing society from progressing. It came to a point where revolutions were bound to happen, where people were going to band together as a thesis and clash with the antithesis to make society go forward.
