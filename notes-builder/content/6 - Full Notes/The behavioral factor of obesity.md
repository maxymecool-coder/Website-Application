# The behavioral factor of obesity
**Details**
Now we come to the usual reason, people eating too much. So why then people eat too much ? There are hypotheses that may explain this, starting with the externality hypothesis.

This hypothesis suggested that people who are obese usually have an appetite towards food and an insensitivity towards their internal hunger state. This means that they would like to eat more and they wouldn't recognize from their internal body signals that they do not need to eat. More recent studies have cast some doubt on this. The evidence for people greater sensitivity to external cues have been inconsistent and so on and so forth.

Another hypothesis is the setpoint hypothesis, this hypothesis instead theorizes that there is a point of weight predetermined for each of us. So when obese people go on diets and break it, they will return to that weight as soon as possible.
