# Circumscribed Accuracy
**Details**
Bill Swann (1984) posed quite a question: if FAD is so pervasive, why hasn’t social life collapsed ? Well, he shows that people visit same situations and such repeatedly. A waiter behaves subserviently every visit; even if you wrongly attribute this to his personality rather than his job, your prediction of his future behavior in that restaurant remains correct. You're wrong about why but right about what he'll do next time. The error only surfaces when you encounter him outside the restaurant and ask him to fetch you a Diet Coke from 7-Eleven. Second, outside the lab, people choose their situations—a librarian probably does prefer quiet environments, so the dispositional inference that happens to be wrong for the wrong reasons may accidentally be right. Third, prolonged situational exposure may genuinely reshape personality, making initially wrong dispositional attributions retroactively accurate. The implication is unsettling: we navigate social life successfully not because our reasoning is sound, but because ecological regularities compensate for systematic inferential errors.
