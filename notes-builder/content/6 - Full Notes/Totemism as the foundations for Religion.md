# Totemism as the foundations for Religion
**Details**
The totemic system, in its entirety, represents the most primitive religion currently observable. Its religious nature is undeniable, as it is founded upon the fundamental division of the world into the sacred and the profane. Its primitiveness is established by its inseparability from the clan-based social organization, which is the simplest form of society known. The clan's very unity often derives not from blood or territory, but solely from sharing a name, an emblem, and a cult—that is, from its totemism. A religion so intrinsically fused with a social structure of such elemental simplicity can rightly be considered the most elementary form of religious life. Therefore, to discover the origins of these totemic beliefs is to likely uncover the very causes that gave rise to the religious sentiment in humanity.
