# Specialized Techniques of Therapy
**Details**
Scheduling activities with the patient: As the patient see themselves as ineffective, we must enable him to be active for him to observe himself in a different, more positive light. Any activity that is scheduled can help the patient structure their day. As these patients often resist attempts to get them to be "busy," it is essential to use a variety of incentives, such as the notion that doing this will make him feel better.

Graded Task Assignment: This is a method that is used to give the patient a series of successes, and is sometimes called "success therapy." The therapist starts with a simple assignment that they know the patient will succeed in. Then they will together, design more activities that gets more complex and longer.

Mastery and Pleasure Therapy: The essence of this kind of therapy is to help the patient keep tracks of their "wins." It is used to bypass the inability of the patient to be satisfied in situations that they are successful.

Cognitive Reappraisal: Has been discussed before, it is finding the faulty cognitions and also the faulty basic premises and assumptions to change them.

Alternative Therapy: This method consists of two different approaches: (1) Considering other views for experiences when the depressed patient shows a systematic negative bias in his interpretations. By thinking of other explanations, he will be able to substitute it with more accurate ones. (2) Considering alternative ways of dealing with such situations. This is to help the patient find ways to deal with his problem that he considers insoluble.

Cognitive Rehearsal: This technique is used to expose the patient's problems that are keeping them from carrying out their activities. They will imagine themselves doing the activity and find the "obstacles" that are interrupting them, then those obstacles will be the main topic of discussion.

Homework Assignments: This is of course an essential part as the patient has to integrate therapy into his life as well. They are expected to carry out certain activities that will counteract their symptoms. In addition, they will keep a log of negative thoughts and then figure out the logical response to it.
