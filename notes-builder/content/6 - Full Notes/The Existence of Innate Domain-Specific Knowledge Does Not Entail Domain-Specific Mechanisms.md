# Fodor’s innate knowledge or learning
**Details**
In this aspect, he argued that domain specificity and encapsulation of a cognitive mechanism on the one hand, and its innateness are different properties, completely independent. He instead argues that the knowledge that we have inside instead tells us what to do during our lifetimes.

His second argument is that the evolutionary poverty of the stimulus argument is that natural selection is not necessarily more sensitive than learning, and in some cases it is the other way around.
