# Self-punishments and self-rewards
**Details**
When a person perceives a deficiency in his behavior or performance, he may criticize himself harshly, he may make assumptions that he is bad, he is stupid, unworthy. This will make him feel guilty, feel sad, and so eventually leads to depression (the types where these ideas are paramount). 

Self-reward is the opposite, it fuels someone praises for themselves, but it also can turn someone to be self-punitive. They think themselves to be jerks, to be pieces of crap.
