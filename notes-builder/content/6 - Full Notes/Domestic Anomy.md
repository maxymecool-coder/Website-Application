# Marriage Anomy
**Details**
Furthermore, not just residing in the economic plane, the anomy branches over to marital situations as well. Exemplify by something like widowhood, which forces the spouse into a situation where they have not been used with for a long time, weakening their resilience to suicide. More importantly though, Durkheim also argues through a view of the data, showing that there is a strong correlation between the legal institution of divorces and the suicidal rates across Europe's countries and provinces. This parallelism suggests that marriage functions as a regulative force. The social and legal norms surrounding marriage provide a discipline that contains passions and desires. When this regulatory structure is weakened or dissolved through divorce, it creates a state of domestic anomie, which is a regular and specific factor in suicide.
