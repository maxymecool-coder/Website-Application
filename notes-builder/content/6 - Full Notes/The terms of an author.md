# The terms of an author
**Details**
This is the first rule of reading, it is the basic element of communicable knowledge .

A term isn’t a word, but rather a specific meaning of a word or sentence that the author has specifically crafted, as a word can have many meanings, especially an important one. When an author intends a word one meaning and the reader interprets another, although an exchange of word has passed in between them, they have not come to terms, no communication has been made. 

And so it is necessary for both parties to use the same words with the same meanings, to come to terms. A term can be defined as a word that is used unambiguously. These things you cannot find in dictionaries, though the bricks to build them are there. These only occur in the process of communicating with others, either through books and such. And so we can think of it as “a skilled use of words for the sake of communicating knowledge.” (Mortimer J Adler). 

Whilst a good writer will try to reach us through the language barrier to help us understand, we must also try to reach him, be willing to be taught by him.
