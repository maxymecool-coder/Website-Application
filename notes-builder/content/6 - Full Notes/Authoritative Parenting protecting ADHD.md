# Authoritative Parenting protecting ADHD
**Details**
Even though there's nothing that can cure the neurological effects of ADHD, authoritative parenting can be a protective factor against the social effects of it. In a study predicting social competence among boys with ADHD, the single strongest predictor of positive peer nominations was not the child's specific behavior, but the mother's score on "Authoritative Parenting" (high warmth, high structure/limits).

Because of how tiring ADHD is for the caretaker, the mothers most of the time give up and let the kids have their way with their lives and day. Those who are able to keep their warmth and such though help their children with their social behaviors.
