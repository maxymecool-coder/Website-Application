# Egocentrism
**Details**
Children are not exactly selfish in the sense of their actions and so on., Instead, they saw the world as centered around themselves and therefore cannot easily understand other people's perspective. For example, if a child demands an illogical and unfair apology from one person, in their eyes, they were right, they did the right thing, and that you were wrong, not understanding that what the other person did at that moment might not be directed at them and so on. Psychologists say that a **theory of mind** is what they lack, and what they eventually will develop.
