# How to find Key Sentences
**Details**
Having understood the nature of propositions and sentences. Now the reader has to find which sentences contains those propositions. There is usually a telltale sign for this, the sentences that are important _for you_ are the ones that you need to put effort in to understand, because at first sight they are not intelligible. And so you must read these slowly and give yourself time to interpret these sentences.

A book will usually contain much more than a series of arguments or the bare statement of one, since the author may want to explain how did he come here, why did he come here, and so on. He may even indulge into the works of others, comment on them. But the heart of his communication lies in the statements that he is making, the arguments, the affirmations, the denials and the reasons for these things.

A tip for doing this is using the words and terms. As said before, similarly to words and terms, we can use them to try to locate these key sentences, and wonder about the meaning of the sentence all you want, because eventually it will lead you to wisdom.

**Supporting Content**
Some author may help you with this of course, as said before, they may use syntax, underline those sentences, and so on. Or give a cue, like a repeating sentence or question whenever they are about to make a proposition.
