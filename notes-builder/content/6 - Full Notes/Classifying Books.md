# Classifying Books
**Details**
The main type of books that the reader should be finding is expository books which usually has knowledge as it primary information. Any book that consists opinions and such and proof for those things are expository work. You identify these by using the techniques in inspectional reading. But moreover the reader also have to distinct between theoretical and practical books.

Of course reading entertainment books are also fine, as you can understand the underlying ideas of the writer, but usually this is much harder and requires many times of reading before grasping every idea that the book represents.
