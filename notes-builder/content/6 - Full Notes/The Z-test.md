# The Z-test
**Details**
This test asks whether a sample mean plausibly belongs to a population. To do so, take the gap between the sample and population means and standardize it: z = (x̄ − μ) / σ_x̄. The trap is the denominator. You're placing a mean within the spread of means, not a score within the spread of scores, so you divide by the standard deviation of the distribution of sample means, σ_x̄ = σ / √n. Keep those two distributions distinct: scores scatter widely (σ = 20, individuals at 30, 70, etc.), but sample means cluster tighter around 50 because averaging smooths out variation. Here σ_x̄ = 20/√16 = 5, so z = (61 − 50)/5 = 2.2.
