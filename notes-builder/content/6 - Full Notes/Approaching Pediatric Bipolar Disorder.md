# Approaching Pediatric Bipolar Disorder
**Details**
One attempted resolution to the debate has been coined by researcher Ellen Leibenluft, proposing that the disorder should be split into phenotypes according to how close it matches the adult version. The "Narrow" phenotype would be those who fully match the adult criteria, which is extremely rare. The second is the "Intermediate" phenotype which describes children who experience these symptoms temporarily (1-2 days) or characterized by irritability. The "Broad" phenotype or Severe Mood Dysregulation would describe children with chronic, non-episodic explosive behavior and irritability. Longitudinal data suggests that the former two would grow into adult Bipolar disorder and the latter would turn more to depressive or anxiety disorders.
