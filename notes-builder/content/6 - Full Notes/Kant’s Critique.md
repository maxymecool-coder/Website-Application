# Kant’s Critique
**Details**
Against Hume, Kant argued that the very stance that Hume is taking also directly contradicts itself. 

Hume puts billiard balls on a table. Ball one moves, hits ball two, ball two moves. Causation isn't visible — granted. But notice what Hume does take for granted: that the events happen in space, that one happens before the other in time. Yet there are no sense organs for space. There are no sense organs for time. These aren't given in the stimulus any more than causation is. Leibniz already argued there's no such thing as absolute space (against Newton) — empty space is nothing, and nothing causes nothing. And Kant works in this space, clearly these mechanisms work, and so, what must already be the case for them to work ? The concepts that work don’t depend on our senses, so it cannot be that.
