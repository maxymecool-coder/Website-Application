# Functions
The Old Brain unconsciously absorbs information such as pictures, audios and then from those pieces of information it forms categories to define how it should act towards someone. This ranges from nurturing to being scared/avoiding. 

Your brain usually tries to find partners that resembles their parents, not because they want to be nurtured the same as the way their parents did, but to finish unresolved traumas that their parents instilled into them when they were a child. Then they want to turn their partners to fill into those spots, recreate those scenarios and then finish what their parents started, change their partners and solve those negative aspects that they were impacted by, presumable creating the =="perfect lover"==.

The Old Brain thinks that if you try to whine and get annoyed enough then someone will come to your rescue and solve everything for you.
