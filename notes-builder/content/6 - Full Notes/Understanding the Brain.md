# Understanding the Brain
**Details**
Even if we perceive our own brain as one singular ginormous control center for everything, that is just our imaginative understanding of it. If we peek deeper and try to understand it scientifically, we may actually find that the brain is split into multiple sectors and multiple parts and that no one singular part has control over everything. And so it is more like a federation (combination of multiple parts working/fighting against each other) rather than a CEO.

Our brain is contralateral, meaning one side actually operates the other. But again, they are still connected through the corpus callosum, allowing both to get information from each other and work off of that information (even offloading efforts of one work to the other). When this connection is severed (like in epilepsy) the difference shows clearly.
