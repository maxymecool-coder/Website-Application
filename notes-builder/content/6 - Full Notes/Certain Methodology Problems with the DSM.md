# Certain Methodology Problems with the DSM
**Details**
DSM-5 was tested in academic places on patients who already had confirmed diagnoses to see if it was accurate, not real world applications, even though its purpose is to be used out there. This has raised certain concerns about the reliability of it due to the nature of the test itself and the results that people have gained throughout the test. It has been reported that the accuracy itself dropped by about 30% of some sorts through the trials.
