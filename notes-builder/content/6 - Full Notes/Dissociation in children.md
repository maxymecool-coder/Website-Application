# Dissociation in children
**Details**
This phenomenon manifested in feeling lost, overwhelmed, abandoned, and disconnected from the world and in seeing oneself as unloved, empty, helpless. She found a resemblance between maternal disengagement and misattunement during the first two years of life and dissociative symptoms in early adulthood. Which she concluded that children who are not seen by their parents grow up into adults that also don't have these abilities.

If the caregivers ignores the child's needs, they are most likely to learn to anticipate rejection and withdrawal. They cope by blocking out the hostility or neglect as if it doesn't matter, but PTSD says "The Body Keeps the Score" and makes you stay in a state of alert anyways. But maybe the most devastating symptom of this is not feeling real inside. When someone has this, it makes it impossible for them to protect themselves from danger, as they feel that nothing is real, nothing matters. 

Although things such as poverty, single parenthood can increase the probability of this happening, it only does so because it decreases the quality of early caregiving, which is the main reason that all of this happens.
