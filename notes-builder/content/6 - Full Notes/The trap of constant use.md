# The trap of constant use
**Details**
According to [[The Epidemiology of Smoking]], most adolescent smokers smoke occasionally, about 35% smoke everyday, they try to maintain a causal relationship with the drug. But as the data suggests, these teenagers, as they age with time, so does their relationship with the drug, they start using it more and such, turning into dependent users. By the time they reach age 26, 80% are daily smokers.

People have this belief that they can merely "chip" and "snip" at the substance without being completely addicted and drawn into it, even though in the first instance of record of the drug, like _Pharmako/Poeia_ people have warned: "It will get you if you play with it."
