# The Shadow of Ourselves
**Details**
Throughout our lives, we as humans attempt to categorize most things into good and evil, one or the other, or we use other measures to indicate how it is more one or the other or that it is completely on the fence. Naturally, then we try to eliminate the bad aspects that we or other possess, even though we cannot and will not ever be able to avoid it.

It is inherent for us as humans to attempt to do so, even as rather than supporting us to become a better human, this rather represses us, our emotions, our "self" which concurrently leads to more suffering, not release. We are fighting parts of ourselves that we cannot win against.

The alternative is not to just accept the bad parts of ourselves, the cruelty, the bias, but rather to accept that there is inherently some potential value in said concepts. Even as they are seen in a bad light by society as going too far one side or the other of the spectrum will result in equal disaster. We must understand that these values, if we understand them completely and understand how to use them for good, can contribute to that.
