# Active Engagement
**Details**
This is basically just a fancy word for focus, attending to the information that one is trying to learn, which goes with alertness, as those two go hand in hand with each other. The particular alertness that one can put on something will make the brain understand that this piece of information is different and should be handled differently, so to reinforce the connections of the neurons, or weaken, etc.

And so one should try to engage their attention into the topic that they want to learn, rather than believing something is so interesting that it will wrench that attention out of them and make them attend. The straining that one feels when they are focusing on knowledge is something good, as it is their brain changing to adapt to the new important information
