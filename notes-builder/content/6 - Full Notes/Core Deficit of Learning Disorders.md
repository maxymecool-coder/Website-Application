# Core Deficit of Communication Disorders
**Details**
The core deficit of the majority of reading disorders is auditory and linguistic. It is the failure of phonological awareness, unable to realize that words are made up of different components and the rules governing them and linking them together. While visual reversals are common for younger kids in kindergarten and first grade, persistent reading failure stems from an inability to "crack the code" of sound-symbol correspondence. Research of the brain shows that this is a rather difficult task for those with these disorders.

Identifying these issues early is crucial for intervention, yet the behavioral predictors are often misunderstood. It is not that aggressiveness (traits of ADHD) predict these disorders, though it may be quite understandable due to the symptoms of it, the best predictor is actually inattention. Interestingly, the aforementioned traits tend to emerge after the academic failure has started, due to the sheer frustration of failure and loss of self-esteem from peers.
