# Imagination in IFS
**Details**
When performing Internal Family Structure, sometimes one will run into a roadblock, even when all of the parts have given way to the Self and the exile, the Self although can empathized, feels like it can't help directly.

For example, a female patient who clearly feels remorse for the exile, still has fear over the situation and rather would try to talk the exile out of it, or preventing it from happening in the first place. This is good, of course, but it is eventually not the goal of IFS. When one cannot imagine themselves getting their own past self out of the situations that made them that way and can only instead think that they can prevent it from happening, they inherently still haven't moved on from that event.

As stated, therapy isn't going back to the past and preventing the event that caused the patient to be like they are from happening, it is dealing with the consequences and the effects that such event had on the patient. And when the patient is still afraid of confronting such circumstances, they haven't been treated completely.
