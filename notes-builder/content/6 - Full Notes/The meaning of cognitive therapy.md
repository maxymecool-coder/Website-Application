# The meaning of cognitive therapy
**Details**
Since the problems considering mental illnesses revolve around certain aberrations in thinking, the challenge to psychotherapy is to offer the patients techniques that they can use to overcome this problem. A point that can lead us is the fact that patients responds relatively effectively and realistically to situations that doesn't concern his neurosis. This means that beyond his vulnerabilities, he can function fine. Moreover, before these mental illnesses happen, the patient frequently shows adequate development of his conceptual tools for dealing with them.

Psychological skills (integrating, labeling, and interpreting experience) can be applied to correct these aberrations. As both of the problem and the solution/remedy to it are related to the patient's thinking, we call this form of help cognitive therapy. Though this does not mean we disregard the emotional aspect in it, rather we get to his emotions through his cognitions (the source of the problem).
