# MDMA (ecstasy) in PTSD
**Details**
It is a controlled substance, classified in 1985 after being used as a recreational drug for some time. Like Prozac and other psychotropic agents, it is unknown how it truly works but it is known to increase concentrations in oxytocin, vasopressin, cortisol, and prolactin.

It increases people’s awareness of themselves as well, which is critical in treating PTSD, and those who take this drug usually report overall a better state of mind, more energy and so on. Moreover, Mithoefer and his colleagues also found that it decreases fear, defensiveness, and numbing, as well as help access the inner world. This helps patients stay in their window of tolerance for them to revisit their traumatic memories without becoming overwhelmed. 

Nonetheless, psychedelic substances are powerful agents with high-risk side effects. It is highly warned to use them carefully to avoid worsening the conditions of patients.
