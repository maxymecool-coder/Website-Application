# Awareness in Relationships
**Details**
In relationships, people need to be aware of their own happiness and comfort in it, and the balance between these emotions with the negative emotions that comes from problems in between the two people. Understanding which emotions are genuinely justifiable and understandable and which aren't are quite difficult, a simple approach to this would be to understand that all of your emotions matter and so you should communicate and talk to your partner about it.

Though this has a disadvantage of you possibly misunderstanding how a relationship is genuine not working out and when its just a problem that you have not resolved with the other person. The way to notice this is to try to work out the problem with the other person and if even when finished nothing changes and the tension and problems are all still there, that is now a fundamental problem of the relationship.

**Supporting Content**
Care about your own feelings, but care about your partner's too. A balance needs to be stroke between the two so that you can work on your own well-being as well as the state of the relationship.
