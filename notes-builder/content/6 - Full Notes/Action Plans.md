# Action Plans
**Details**
There are no formula on these, you'd have to work on it depending on the client's goals, aspirations, and your conceptualization of them. Now the action plan is basically homework for the client to do a couple things like teaching them, collecting data, changing their behaviors, thoughts, the whole way.

There are a few items considering to be written and noted on this:
- Reading therapy notes: after discussing an issue/problem, the client will summarize or report on what they think is most important to do.
- Monitor the automatic thoughts that change their mood.
- Evaluating and responding to these thoughts
- Doing behavioral experiments, like testing negative predictions and then letting the client conduct the experiments and seeing the result to then let the clients change their behaviors depending on the outcome.
- Identify the steps and problems in achieving these goals then training and solving these problems.
- Engaging in activities to lift affect their mood and to work towards their goals.
- Credits lists is to well, for the people to give themselves credit and self-motivate, train them to understand and see objectively their efforts.
- You can also greatly reinforce important concepts of CBT by asking the patient to read books for consumers or booklets and then for them to take note in here.
- And also the action plan helps shorten the review time of the session, allowing for more therapy time.
