# Self-Invalidation
**Details**
A lot of the times people use self-invalidation as a coping method for when their situation gets too overwhelming, even though it is a particularly weak method to deal with their stress. This is mostly because no one actually tells them how to cope and deal with their emotions effectively.

Most of the time, in childhoods, people are shunned and yelled at by their parents to just handle it, and that its not that tough or that its not that difficult, and so they learn helplessness, nobody cares, listens, or they get gaslit into thinking genuinely that its not that much. They can also be overly perfectionistic, chasing the absolute peak of something without realizing how impossible it might be.
