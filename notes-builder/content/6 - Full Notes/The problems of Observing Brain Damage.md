# The problems of Observing Brain Damage
**Details**
That said, there are key drawbacks to this method still. Practically, no one is gonna have the same amount of brain damage to another, and the damage is very spread out, meaning that it can go to other parts of the brain as well, this compromises the test of similar cases. Another disadvantage is that we do not have a baseline, there are few parameters that we can run through to have a gist of an idea how the brain was functioning before it was damaged. In fact, we cannot even know if it is functioning less than it was before. The next disadvantage is related to neuroplasticity, the brain is always changing in response to information, and brain damage is information. In these cases, the brain will transfer the missing functions to other parts of it, greatly distorting the result that we can gain as it may already "healed" itself in a sense. Lastly, a function of the brain is affected by many relationships, not just one, and so even with all this, we can only be aware of one of the areas that might have been responsible for that function.
