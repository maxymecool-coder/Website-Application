# Active Inhibition in Learning
**Details**
Active inhibition is the ability to forget or suppress a piece of information so that it does not interfere with another that is being absorbed or learnt. Prioritizing focus on the newer information to fully process and understand it.

This is especially important in Zettelkasten because we would want to utilize this effectively. Most of the times students usually try their best not to forget and retain all the information of their lessons, but this is actually counter-productive as it goes against inhibition. What we can do though is externally extract it, for example to a piece of paper, a notebook,... but then to actually remember it and be able to retrieve it we would need to connect it to as many meaningful contexts as possible, which is what actively happens in the slip-box. And so it turns into an interconnected network that naturally has cues for each other to remind the creator of such ideas and concepts.
