# Criticism = Negative
**Details**
- Before criticizing someone (Especially your partner), think about it again to see if its hurting them, fair to them or not. Firstly see if it incites some negative emotions in you, what thoughts you have, deeper feelings, or... if it is simply unmet needs from yourself, as you are mad that you are not good at this one thing and project it onto your partner just because he is not good at something similar to it ! Or if you were forced to give up something when you were small so now you're seeing your partner do it and you get mad at them.
