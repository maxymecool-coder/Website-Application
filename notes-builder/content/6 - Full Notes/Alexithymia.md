# Alexithymia
**Details**
This is the definition of not being able to describe how one feels. It happens when one cannot describe what their physical sensations are telling them, they look furious but deny anger, appear terrified but say they’re fine. Not being able to discern what is going inside their body because they’re out of touch with their needs, and have trouble taking care of themselves. We all have this problem to an extent but these people have it worst. People with this tend to register emotions as physical problems rather than as signals that something deserves their attention. Instead of feeling angry or sad, they experience muscle pain, bowel irregularities, or other symptoms for which no cause can be found. About three quarters of patients with anorexia nervosa, and more than half of all patients with bulimia, are bewildered by their emotional feelings and have great difficulty describing them. Because traumatized people have trouble sensing their own bodily feelings, they react to stress differently, spacing out or excessive anger, but they can’t tell what is upsetting them.

**Supporting Content**
People who experience this may attempt to self-harm in order to "physicalize" the pain and show others that they are indeed, in pain. Creating a tangible wound provides a concrete locus for the suffering, offering a temporary, albeit maladaptive, solution to the problem of emotional inarticulacy.
