# Institutions
**Details**
In sociology, institutions are defined by this: "a pattern of expected action of individuals or groups enforced by social sanctions," with sanctions meaning punishments and rewards. These patterns are defined by certain principles, they are usually replicated throughout society and they usually provide some purposes or meanings.

These ideas of an institutions have its hard nature, meanings and rules about it that does not change, even though secondary rules that do not necessarily make it loses its meaning can change and exist.

The process of an institution being birthed goes like this. Let's say that a group of students create a club, certain rules about who can join, about the benefits of being in the club, or just being friends, how many times they meet per year, who pays, so on and so forth. When the years go by, the event is looked forward to, the members of the institution expects and have ideas on what to do more to improve the experience. And even when the members start leaving and going on their own ways, the institution, the idea of it will still remain, even though it might have some rules changed, others might continue pursuing it.
