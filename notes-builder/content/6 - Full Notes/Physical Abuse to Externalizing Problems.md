# Physical Abuse to Externalizing Problems
**Details**
Physical abuse usually leads to externalizing problems alike ODD, traits like aggression. This is because if a child's been abuse, they would generalize it to the world outside, they would figure that the world would be the same, abusive and harsh, this is also called a Hostile Attribution Bias. For example, a neutral event, such as bumping into another person in the cafeteria, would be a frustrating thing for a normal person, for an abused child, they might think it was intentional and get into a scuffle because of it. This contributes to the "cycle of violence," where abused children are at higher risk of becoming abusive parents. However, this transmission is largely **environmental** rather than genetic; twin studies indicate that the trauma of abuse itself, rather than shared genes, is the primary driver of these negative outcomes.
