# Psychological stress to physical abnormalities
**Details**
As experimental evidence has shown that individuals overreacts under stress in a particular physiological system, it is crucial to set the terms to understand what "stress" truly is. In most interpretations, stress means external conditions that presumably produce internal strains that lead to physical disorders. These internal strains are manifested by states of excitation experienced subjectively as anger, anxiety, or euphoria.

Emotional arousal is accompanied by increased activity of the autonomic nervous system. One or more physiological systems or organs may be affected by this. The system that is activated is not necessarily predictable based on what emotion is being felt, but there appears to be a characteristic reaction for a given individual: Depending on which system that is overly reactive, the somatic manifestation may be a lesion or disturbance of the gastrointestinal tract, skin, bronchioles or cardiovascular system.

This formulation may appear plausible, even though it jumps over many links in the chain. We can identify the variables that we have jumped by examining the nature of stress and the interaction of the cognitive and emotional systems in response to it. Various of stress-inducing situations have been implicated in the precipitation of psychophysiological disorders.
