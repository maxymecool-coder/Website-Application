# Environmental Social Contagion
**Details**
Environments shape behavior, but an environment is not just physical space and physical cues; it is also the people within it. You can radically alter your habits by surrounding yourself with people who already possess the mindsets, characteristics, or methodologies you want to acquire. Your brain is wired for social mimicry and naturally absorbs the characteristics of the "tribe" you belong to. By embedding yourself in a culture where your desired behavior is the normal behavior, you will naturally adopt their traits and methodologies. This results in significant personal improvements that happen almost entirely unconsciously, requiring far less willpower than trying to change in isolation.
