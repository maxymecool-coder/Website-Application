# Static Aiming
**Details**
It is clicking on stationary targets that is all one hit. The most used method is the "bardoz method", which involves a wide flick into a micro correction and then a nearly instantaneous confirmation before clicking on the target. Fluidity is how fast one can do the micro correction and confirmation before moving to another target, which is necessary to reach the highest level of static clicking. 

Something to understand is roadmapping, which is guiding to other targets whilst still in the process of doing one. Another is "Flick consciousness", knowing where one's crosshair is after shooting a target. Cluster farming is to smoothen, slow one's aim when approaching a cluster of targets.
