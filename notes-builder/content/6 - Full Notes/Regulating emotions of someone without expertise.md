# Regulating emotions
**Details**
In regulating emotions of someone with BPD without expertise, one can reorient attention - meaning do something not related to what’s upsetting. Then down regulating the physiological arousal by doing something opposite to the emotions being felt (basically acting like relaxing muscles, smiling, so on…tricking the body into thinking it’s positive). Another is to stop doing whatever it is our emotion and mood tells us to do as it is irrational and can screw up lots of stuffs. Finally, having a life with goals in it independent with the emotions.

When someone refuses your help however, you can then try your best not to invalidate the person and wait until the storm goes by. If they can’t seem to calm down, then you can diverge to assessment, which is basically listening to the person saying all about their internal experiences and emotions/thoughts.
