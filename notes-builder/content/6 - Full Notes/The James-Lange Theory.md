# The James-Lange Theory
**Details**
James Lange found out that we did not feel emotion due to the situation, but rather our body's own response to it. He found out that our body acts faster than we feel emotions, the brain sends a non-conscious physiological response to our body for it to do an action, then our limbic system catches up and starts inciting the emotions that would be correlated with the situation.

At first, this theory was discredited due to the thought of such a theory being so inefficient feels wrong. An experiment that proved this was the Stanley Schachter's classic experiment showed that subjects injected with epinephrine (adrenaline) did not feel a specific emotion, but rather amplified whatever emotion was being socially modeled for them, be it anger or joy. The epinephrine created a state of physiological arousal, and the brain latched onto the most available social cue to label that arousal.
