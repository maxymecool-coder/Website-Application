# Alzheimer’s Disease
**Details**
When people get older, they usually have a higher chance to get Alzheimer's Disease, which kills off braincells constantly, deteriorating their cognitive functions and thinking until they lose all functions and wither away. This means that any cognitive function, be it memory, vision, comprehension, it all reduces, manifesting in disorientation at times. Losing these functions may lead the person into full-blown depression as the illness worsens.

An observation from the advancement of PET scans have shown us about how plaques and neurofibrillary tangles existing more in the brain than those of the same age. We do not really have any idea of the relationship between the disease and these things so they are not necessarily useful information currently.
