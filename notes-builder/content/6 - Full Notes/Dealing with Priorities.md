# Dealing with Priorities
**Details**
When doing therapy, people also have to take into consideration to care about issues or goals, depending on the context. For example, if they are able to resolve their issues with their activities, move to discussing goals, or if they do not want to work on, then you should respect their decision, only working up to a certain point if you do want to push it. (Remember to state so in the agenda).

Sometimes people are also unable to realize what is the situation that is causing their issues, which is when you also need to help them, by perhaps letting them imagine situations where one problem is gone to narrow it down to the worst factor contributing to their stress.

When dealing this, sometimes you would have to interrupt the client, which may result in them either accepting that you’re right or getting mad/negative. When they are negative, apologize for misunderstanding how important what they were talking about was, and then set it into the agenda.
