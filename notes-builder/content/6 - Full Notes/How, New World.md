# How ? New World
**Details**
Normally, sciences would more often than not make the evolution of past societies into current one seem more seem-less and rational than it really was. Frankly, the industrial revolution happening was an unprecedented part of history compared to what was happening at the time and the dynasties involved in said eras.

The spread of modernity unfolded in distinct temporal and geographic waves, which in turn continually shifted the prevailing explanations for its cause. Initially, around the mid-eighteenth century, England was the sole nation to escape agrarian constraints, leading contemporary observers to view this unprecedented industrial shift as a uniquely English miracle. By the nineteenth century, as these structures spread to proximate nations like Belgium, Germany, and France, the narrative expanded to describe a broader Western European phenomenon, often attributed by thinkers like Max Weber to cultural factors such as the Protestant work ethic. The late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries saw North America and Japan successfully adopt this blueprint, proving that Asiatic societies could also industrialize and expanding the concept into a Euro-American-Japanese miracle. This progression continued through the mid-twentieth century with the rapid rise of the "Four Little Tigers" in Asia, culminating in the late twentieth century when massive economies like China, India, and Brazil began their own modernizing transformations, effectively globalizing what was once an isolated regional anomaly.
