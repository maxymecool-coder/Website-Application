# The differences of severity in PTSD
**Details**
Although some has said that due to having experienced an unfinished trauma in earlier of their lives, people tend to find those who are similar to the ones that has caused that trauma (eg. caretakers), to finish the job and resolve their conflicts. But this is only true for some cases.

Other cases when trauma is so severe, patients, instead of trying to find ways to resolve the trauma, gets so heavily affected by it that they go into life-or-death responses when something reminds them of the trauma.

This brings up an important point, and why these relationships fail. The thing is, neglected children, or those who had "Disorganized Attachment" with their caretaker, usually failed to recognize the emotional needs of others. Moreover, they even see the caretaker as both a source of terror and love, same as their partners later in life if they deal with trauma this way, except now they can do something. So they both hate and love their partners at the same time, doing irrational things, hurting them and so on.
