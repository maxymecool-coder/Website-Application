# Medications in Mental Disorders
**Details**
For some mental disorders, the frontline treatment is not psychotherapy, but rather just medications that can help people with their symptoms, some popular disorders that are treated with medications are bipolar and schizophrenia. This method of treatment is necessary due to the symptoms of these type of disorders being too much to be changed by normal psychotherapy due to them being neurobiological disorders. That is not to say psychotherapy does not work, it can be used, just not as effective as medications or that it does not address the symptoms that medications does.

For example, since the problem with bipolar is constant mood swings, mood stabilizers like Lithium are used to help the patient. Or for schizophrenia, the main negative symptom is psychosis, which can be treated with anti-psychotics (1st and 2nd generation). Of course these medications all cause weight gain, and when they get overweight/obese they might get illnesses, which will cause them to reject medications which can cause relapses.
