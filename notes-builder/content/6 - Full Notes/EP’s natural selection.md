# EP’s natural selection
**Details**
The evolutionary psychology views that the developments of the brain’s functions derive from the problems that have been prevalent over multiple generations in the environment specific to the species that are often not observable during a singular lifetime. Whilst domain-general mechanisms are limited to knowing what should be done from perceptual (innate) information. Since natural selection runs the actual test of what it needs through so many subjects, it can run the experiments over millions of people, and then choose statistically what is the best choice.
