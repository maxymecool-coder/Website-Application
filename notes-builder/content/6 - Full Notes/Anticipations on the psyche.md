# Anticipations on the psyche
**Details**
These things influence people’s experience and can fuel their expectations or dull them with unpleasant expectations. They may come in a visual form, an anxious person seeing visions of disaster when facing a new situation, or a depressed person dreaming about failing at everything they do. 

In social situations, people keep track of each other’s reactions, and base on that, they wonder what they will say, will think of them, their thoughts is to immediately regard any reaction as if it will become enduring, or lasting.

The rules and the syllogisms based on it are of interest to the therapist as it helps them explain the illogical behavior and the abnormal responses. When these rules are applied excessively or arbitrarily, they are likely to produce psychological or interpersonal problems.
