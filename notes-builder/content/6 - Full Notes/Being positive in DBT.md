# Being positive in DBT
**Details**
Once having committed treating an individual, a person needs to prepare to rid themselves of any reluctance, reserve, or antagonism, or on unwanted commands from others, will markedly improve the chances of developing a strong relationship. It is also important to talk with patients about any forceful therapy or external factors and work it out to them about the views on therapy.

**Supporting Content**
It is impossible to get too much commitment! A skills trainer should not assume that others have gotten the patient to be committed.
