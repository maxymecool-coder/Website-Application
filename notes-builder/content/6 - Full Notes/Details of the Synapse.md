# Details of the Synapse
**Details**
There are two neurons that are in this process, called the presynaptic neuron and the postsynaptic neuron, they are the consequently the neuron that sends the message and the one that receives it. To begin, it starts in the tiny knoblike axon terminals of the presynaptic neuron. In these swellings are numerous tiny saps, or synaptic vesicles that are filled with neurotransmitters. When the presynaptic neuron fires, these vesicles burst and eject their chemicals through the terminal's membrane into the synaptic gap in between the two cells. These molecules then diffuse across this gap and impinge upon the membrane of the other.

One the chemical is across, the transmitters activate specialized molecular receptors in the postsynaptic membrane. When these receptors are active, it opens or closes certain ion channels in the membrane. Which basically makes the voltage difference across the membrane decrease, rendering it less stable. As more and more transmitters molecules do this, the more the difference in voltage is, and when it reaches the excitation threshold, the action potential is triggered and the impulse speeds down the post-synaptic cell's axon.
