# Medications for Bipolar Disorder
**Details**
The symptoms that makes this disorder require medication is the psychotic symptoms or episodes, this is when antipsychotics are required for the person to be stabilized immediately. For long term mood stabilization, mood stabilizers may be required, such as Lithium or anticonvulsants like Depakote are standard to prevent recurrence. Psychotherapy also plays a major role in helping a person potentially manage their conditions, recognize early cues, stabilizing daily rhythms as well. Untreated bipolar disorder has a 50% rate of suicidal attempt and a 20% success rate from that.
