# Information Consolidation Theory
**Details**
This theory derived from experiments on people who were sleeping, where it was expected by the Respiration-Restoration Theory that the brain would shut down and that there would be little to no activity, the opposite was true, there were a lot of activity in the brain. This proves that the wirings in the brain when we sleep is not random, but perhaps it is the brain forging new connections, forging new synapses. For example, Geometry Dash, the reason why people return to it after optimal sleep and get better is related to the very theory above.

Applying this to lower mammal such as parrots, we can see that this theory is true to do their ability to solve mazes getting better and better with sleep. Now that does not mean this theory is full proof. These parrots' brain have also shown to show activity in random spots as well.

Further support for this theory may come from [[The Stages of Sleep]], where it is argued that dreams that we have during REM sleep may actually be our brain consolidating memories that it regards as important.
