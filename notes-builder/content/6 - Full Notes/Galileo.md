# Galileo
**Details**
Continuing with the theme of the epistemic explosion, Galileo happened, which caused the whole astronomy world to go haywire as he disproved everything that they ever believed in. Before him, people would believe that the Earth was the center of the universe, and that other planets moved around us in perfect circles. This would require complex mathematical equations to prove so.

With Galileo’s observations in 1609, he proved that Venus did not, was not just a crescent, disproving Aristole’s observations, same as how he saw the moon had mountains and such.
