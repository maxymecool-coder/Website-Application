# The Lost of the Self
**Details**
Patients of trauma, when they shut down their emotions, their own thoughts it also makes them unaware of what has truly happened to them. So overwhelmed by the event their brain would make them forget, so that they can continue functioning. But the body remembers, the body keeps track of the trauma and reacts to it still. Her immune system, her muscles had all kept the score yet her conscious mind lacked a story that could communicate the experience. 

This is even more present in incest children, they are sexually abused by their parents, but who do they run to for comfort when the source of comfort is the very thing that is causing that terror ? When they try to find it from their parents they are met with an adult conspiracy of silence and disbelief. And so they eventually cope with by staying silent. Furthermore, children are programmed to be fundamentally loyal to their caretakers, even if abused by them. Terror increases the need for comfort, even when the source of comfort is the source of terror. And the feeling of rebelling, the feeling of getting out, it violates the very loyalty one has to their family-deep inside one still felt like they need it to protect them against their fears. And the price of this is loneliness, despair and the eventual rage of helplessness. Rage has nowhere to go except the self, in form of depression, and other actions. A patient has one said "It is like hating your home, your kitchen and pots and pans, your bed, your chairs, your table, your rugs." Nothing will ever feel safe-least of all your own body.

When asked about this later in life, most of these children would say that they are merely exaggerating it, or that it is supposedly their fault. Whenever a situation of this sorts happen, they shut down, their heads “goes into the clouds”. And they look down onto their mortal body, they were not looking down at themselves, but some other little girl.
