# Reading Speed
**Details**
- Reading speed is usually more necessary in cases where it mainly requires the reader to change reading speed accordingly to details that does not matter to the details that does and needs comprehension which is going to need a slower, more thorough read. Basically just focus on comprehending the material being read rather than focusing on your speed.
