# How to abstract inside the box
**Details**
To get insights and insights exceptional texts, one must play with ideas after making these smart notes. 

To tinker with these ideas we have to remove them from the context by abstracting and re-specifying them. Abstracting is the middle step in making heterogeneous ideas compatible, not the conclusion that we are aiming for. Abstraction is necessary because it gives us concepts that can be applied in different situations to garner more insights. 

But of course, a system is needed to put this into practice, and the concrete standardization of notes in one format…basically the nature of the slip-box allows us to do so.
