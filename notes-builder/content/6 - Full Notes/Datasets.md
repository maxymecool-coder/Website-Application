# Datasets
**Details**
In RVC, or AI voice making, it is basically an audio file containing the voice of the model that will be replicated. It is recommended to go for a 40+ minutes of dataset without silence. Variety is also important in the dataset since RVC doesnt have the ability to generate diverse range of audios. To get the best quality, ensure that there isnt much background noise, reverb, overlapping voice, music, distortions or small silences. WAV or FLAC should also be used as it is the highest quality of audio. Moreover, sounds like loud “S” or “SH” or popping like “P” shouldnt be included. If these criteria is not met, the model can have artifacts, which means the voice can sound robotic or glitchy after the inference or model training process. With specifically my current case, Spek or Audacity ? should be downloaded and checked to determine the sample rate as well as to truncate silence and maybe normalize audio
