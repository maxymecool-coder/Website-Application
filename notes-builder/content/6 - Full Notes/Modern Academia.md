# The meaning of Academia
**Details**
Academic pursuits, the gathering of hard intelligence—these are generally seen as noble. They are said to enrich the soul and the mind. This is true, but not infinitely. It is possible to trespass so far down the spiral of knowledge that we actually make ourselves less intelligent. 

In the current day of the Internet, this idea has become more prevalent, we sit around days upon days watching videos, learning about philosophy, believing ourselves to come to conclusions on life, but in fact, we are merely coming to conclusions on conclusions. The type of knowledge that is untested by any real-life situation. We are only reading and thinking about the interpretation, the thoughts of something rather than the thing itself. 

We become supposed scholars of shadows, the mere images of the objects that reflects those images. We are trapped debating, building upon that shadow, unbeknownst to the vast and beautiful knowledge of the object itself.

Academia, in all its beauty, is inherently finite. We may regurgitate and republish work forever, but it is still finite. It can only provide us a facsimiles of our own ability, it can only copy the work of reality at its best. Emerson tells us that we have always the resource of life. Scholars lose their hours as they work, and the rest of us gain ours as we live.
