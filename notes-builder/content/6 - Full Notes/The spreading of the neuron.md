# The spreading of the neuron
**Details**
The action potential may have explained the "firing" of the neuron, but it does not explain the spreading to neighboring regions. The reason for this is that the temporarily positive voltage inside the axon induces an opening of ion channels at adjacent regions, which first changes the voltages at these regions and make more channels open up. The upshot is that the impulse moves down the entire length of the axon and into the rest of the neuron as well. This is known as propagation of the action potential.
