# The truth of Creativity
**Details**
Creativity isn’t something linear, it’s usually unpredictable. And so we can’t follow a plan to come up with creative ideas, but we can make our environment allow us to be creative. 

The blockage of independent thought is our own habits of thinking up many ideas and instead deep thinking. And of course the slip-box helps us do this. Our brains love familiarity, it is a known fact, and so it tries to make the info we read fit into the known or eradicated it from our perception. And most of the time we don’t even notice when it does that. We need to fix this then.
