# Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) in Brain Research
**Details**
- **Methodology**: Applies magnetic pulses through the skull to temporarily inactivate or stimulate neurons. Pulses vary in frequency/intensity for inhibitory/excitatory effects. Targets scalp areas to disrupt functions and study processes like language, perception, or action. Observes behavioral changes to infer area involvement and connections. 
- **Advantages**: Selectively activates/inactivates brain areas. Identifies locations/functions without reorganization (unlike brain damage). Studies various processes non-surgically. 
- **Disadvantages**: Invasive with short-term side effects (e.g., headaches, balance issues, hallucinations, apathy, compulsions). Imprecise/approximative results. Limited to specific applications.
