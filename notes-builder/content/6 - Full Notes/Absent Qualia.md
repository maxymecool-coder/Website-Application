# Absent Qualia
**Details**
Functionalism intends to answer how our brain functions and behaves, but how does that visceral and soulful feeling converts to a mere chemical function in our brain ? The mental state is the function in functionalism, there is an absence of any "mind" involved perhaps, in the soul-like sense.

But this leads to a dumbfounding correlation, we feel like it ISN'T a mere chemical connection because it is so difficult to imagine two things of such radically different bases be connected to each other or being the same thing. It is as if saying the number 9 is a rock, who would ever believe that ? Nonetheless, critics argue that the approach above ignores the intrinsic qualitative experience (absent qualia).
