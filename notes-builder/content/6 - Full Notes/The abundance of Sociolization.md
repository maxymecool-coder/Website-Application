# The abundance of Sociolization
**Details**
In society, when we act, the environment and other people usually responds to us correspondingly to show us that they are registering our existence and they respect us to some extent so that they are acknowledging us. This is called [[Mirroring]], and it is a biological trait of our kind. For example, when you sit down in some crowded place that you have little space to do so, the other person may move themselves a bit out of your space, or if they do not have any space, they would wiggle to show you that they are trying.
