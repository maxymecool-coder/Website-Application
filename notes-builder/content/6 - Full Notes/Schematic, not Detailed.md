# Schematic, not Detailed
**Details**
Even if we have a schematic understanding of minds alien from our own, like understanding that a person might feel urges, hunger, thirst, we cannot feel how that feeling actually is. It is all just our own subjective interpretations. Campbell illustrates this with bat sonar. A bat must scream loudly to get an echo, but to avoid deafening itself, it must switch off its hearing implies during the scream, then immediately amp it up to catch the echo, all while flying at high speed. We can describe this mechanism computationally. We can map the brain cells involved. But we cannot simulate the **phenomenology**—the actual _feeling_—of that sensory stream. No amount of physical detail fills in the "pixel-by-pixel" subjective image of the bat's world.
