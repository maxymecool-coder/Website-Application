# Transcendent and the Transcendental
**Details**
Between these things, there are actually quite notable distinctions between them. The transcendent are realms that are beyond our human sensory experience which traditional rationalism attempts to explore. Kant argues that such pursuits cannot yield genuine knowledge, as there is no experiential basis. In contrast, the transcendental is a priori that allows itself to make some experience possible, and so we research it in the sense of cognitive models and formal conditions for us to perceive and understand those things.
