# The Fallacy of Reductionism
**Details**
Putting forth the question of "Why does Reductionism not work in psychology/neuroscience ?", we will try to understand this from an example.

For example, if we have a neighbor who is kind of mysterious to us, they act in certain ways that we just don't really understand why, Reductionism would posit that we could scan everything about them from their movements to their expressions to understand what they would do next, what would happen next. But that is where it ends, we may be able to predict or guess but we will never understand "why ?", why is the definitive question of Reductionism, you reduce a concept down so that you could understand why it happens, but that doesn't work in psychology/neuroscience. Rather, we get a correlation, not causation.
