# The Problem Solving Process
**Details**
When doing math problems, it is quite simple, we have to read the statement carefully, think of ways to solve and look for ideas and then finally finalize the proof. But there are nuances to this, for example, every idea can contribute somewhat to the overall process, even if the idea doesn't lead to the solution. Additionally, there is a step that most people overlook, which is looking back. Students more often than not will move on to another problem or figure out where they were wrong, but they don't look back at the problem and think about the different applications or situations where their ideas would have worked. This can be especially beneficial as it prepares them for future situation and also furthers their problem solving abilities.
