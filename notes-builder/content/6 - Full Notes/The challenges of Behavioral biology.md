# The challenges of Behavioral biology
**Details**
When studying human behavior through a biological lens, we face three distinct challenges in how we relate to the rest of the animal kingdom:

- In some cases, we are biologically the same as other animals. At times, our biological functions inherently have no difference to other animals. This makes quite a lot of sense due to how our genome differentiate little from baboons for example, so certain biological processes that we have certainly exists in other animals.
    
- We can also use those processes differently. Say most of the animals in the animal kingdom have a tendency to feel anxiety when their lives are in danger, actively. We, as humans, do not even need that to be anxious, stuff like thinking about our own mortality, riding roller coasters, novel thinking,... already does that for us.
    
- Lastly though, we are still very much unique in many ways, such as the fact that we have sex constantly (without making offspring) and talk about that fact. We also enjoy the process and try to make it as pleasurable as possible.
