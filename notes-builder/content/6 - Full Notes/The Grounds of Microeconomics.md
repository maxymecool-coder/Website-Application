# The Grounds of Microeconomics
**Details**
So, what is microeconomics ? As the name suggests, the science focuses on how individuals and firms try to make themselves as well off as possible under scarcity (meaning the current state already). People do not simply just choose how things are but rather that these choices happen inside constraints and conditions. Scarcity means that resources, time, attention, money, land, labor, and productive capacity are limited, so an action always displaces some other possible action. This is called opportunity cost: the cost of doing something in turn of the next best alternative that must be given up.

And so microeconomics can be described as constrained optimization. Economic agents face trade-offs, compare alternatives, and try to choose the best available option given the rules. The “dismal” side of economics is that it constantly points out that nothing is truly free, because any use of scarce resources rules out another use. The “fun” side, at least from an MIT-style perspective, is that this makes economic behavior analyzable with the same spirit as engineering: given constraints, what is the best solution? The economic way of thinking does not require assuming that choices are morally good, emotionally wise, or socially fair. It begins by asking what options exist, what constraints bind, what is sacrificed, and what decision follows from those sacrifices.
