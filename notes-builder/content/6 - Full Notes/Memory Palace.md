# Memory Palace
**Details**
A memory palace is basically a mnemonic device used to hold and organize large volumes of material. It is based on the method of loci which people mentally imagine themselves to have basically a location where they tie prominent features of the place with certain memories. There are so many features of the place that you can practically assign a countless number of visual cues to retrieve later. Depending on certain orders, the cues can be sequenced in a route throughout the house.

Humans remember pictures more easily than words. And so it is easier to tie concepts with these pictures since it would be easier to retrieve them from memory. There are many examples of this concept being utilized to help students learn. Psychology teachers from many universities lead their students on tours of cafes and memorize the place so that they can store the multitude of knowledge they will learn in the following months and prepare themselves for the tests.
