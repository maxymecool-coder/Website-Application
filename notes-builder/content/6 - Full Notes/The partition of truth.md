# The partition of truth
**Details**
Still in [[Propositional Logic]], we can categorize sentences not by their specific content, but by their behavior across the possibilities of the sentence meaning. The classification partitions these sentences into three disjoint groups, being: valid sentences: which are true in every possible scenario; unsatisfiable sentences: false in every scenarios; and finally contingent sentences: true in some but false in others. These are the most useful for communication, as they can be used to convey specific information.

Another term that is similar to valid is equivalence, which entails how a term/symbol can be perfectly replaced by another if they are completely equal to each other in every way. Logical entailment on the other hand, is a directional relationship.
