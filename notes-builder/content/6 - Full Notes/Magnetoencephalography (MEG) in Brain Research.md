# Magnetoencephalography (MEG) in Brain Research
**Details**
- **Methodology**: Records magnetic fields generated by brain activity (e.g., from ionic currents and synaptic transmission in neurons). Uses non-invasive scans to detect communication between brain areas by tracing waves of activity from origin to processing regions. Involves expensive machinery similar to PET. 
- **Advantages**: Excellent temporal resolution for tracking rapid brain activity. Non-invasive. Detects inter-area communication effectively. 
- **Disadvantages**: Poor spatial resolution. Very expensive equipment.
