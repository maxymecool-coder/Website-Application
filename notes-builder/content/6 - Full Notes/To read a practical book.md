# To read a practical book
**Details**
The difference between this and a theoretical book is that the ends, these books solve pragmatic problems, he sees in between the lines, seeing the rules that the principles haven’t talked about. 

That is how practical books should be read, or else it will fail to be practical. If the intelligibility of rules is to be found in principles, it is no less true that the significance of principles will be found in the rules they lead to. 

Finally, ends, usually in a theoretical book, you’d depend on if the propositions are right or not to define if you agree or disagree with it. But in practical books, the ends are instead if it works, and if following it leads you to solve your problems.
