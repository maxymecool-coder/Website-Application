# The Problem of Self-Continuity
**Details**
How could you ever know that you are still the same person as you were yesterday or tomorrow ? Seemingly every piece of evidence we reach for, be it memories, photographs, witnesses, are in principle fakeable or fallible. Memory is quite reconstructive as it is already inherently glitchy and unstable currently for us. Joseph Butler pushed this further in the 1700s, arguing the self "lives and dies, begins and ends continually" — today's self is merely a similar successor to yesterday's, not the same entity. This is a concept that is imbued in our own lives: we apologize for our past selves, we sleep late without caring much about tomorrow's tiredness, treating as if its a different person's problem. We would need temporal (timely) difference and distance so that we can admit fault. John Locke further argued that our identity would then extend as far as consciousness can reach backward, and while amnesics prove this isn't technically true, we _feel_ it is. We only never question this due to the fact that it would be counterproductive form an evolutionary perspective.
