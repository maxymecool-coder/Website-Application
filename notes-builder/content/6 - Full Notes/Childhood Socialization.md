# Childhood Socialization
**Details**
From a very young age, the brain of the child is already absorbing information and understanding the norms and what society is presenting what or who they need to be. There are some models that can explain how they learn this and how they know what to be. The first group is the "passive learner" models, which includes conditioning, indoctrination, imitation. The second group is the "active seeker" models, including the Cognitive-Developmental Theory, Generalized Other, and Anticipatory Socialization.
