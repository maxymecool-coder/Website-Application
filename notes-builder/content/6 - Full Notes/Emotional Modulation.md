# Emotional Modulation
**Details**
Now usually there are two sides to this, a good one and a bad one, as I would split them. Of course there's the modulation so that a person can function and not get consumed by their emotions to not get destroyed inside and so on. This is a valuable skill for people to learn as it allows people to deal with situations more effectively, though it does require a person to also recognize to not modulate too much, turning it instead to inhibition. Inhibition, which is the other form, is particularly bad because it instead causes people to bottle up their feelings and build up negativity inside them. Not only that, it may even lead to dysfunction, a person unable to take care of certain stressful situations as they are prone to extracting themselves from those situations; they also won't build any skills so that they can be able to take on those situations.

**Supporting Content**
Another point is the emotional catharsis one would get from releasing their emotions, it has a tangible effect on their minds, moods, and so on.
