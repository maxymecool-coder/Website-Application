# Development of Asylums
**Details**
The original definition for asylums were safe places. These buildings would be located in the countryside far from the places where it was dirty and just overall worse. A lot of people were getting better living in those asylums, although some did not, they were still in safe places.

Furthermore, these asylums usually housed people for a long time, or for their whole lives, rather than just resting for a period of time and then getting back to work. The result of this was that asylums started getting overcrowded as more and more people came, which meant the countryside ran out of space to build asylums, and they had to do so in the cities, where conditions weren't exactly fulfilling for the recovery of mental illnesses.

The fact that asylums were in cities also made patients there become entertainment for others, they were being used and abused. These places turned into bedlams, which were horrible places for the mentally ill to rest in. Luckily, there was Philippe Pinel who wanted to treat these people better, and started hospitals which were a new version of asylums... which still turned overcrowded as well.
