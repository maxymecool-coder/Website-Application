# The importance of feedback
**Details**
Studies have shown that having feedback after a test strengthens retention more than just testing alone, and even further, delaying this feedback briefly also produces better long-term learning than immediately doing so. This corresponds with the research that has been done in learning motor tasks, like making layups or driving a golf ball toward a distant green. In these sports, trial and error with a delayed feedback may be awkward but it is more effective.

Feedback also gives you a more accurate assumption of your current level of knowledge. It helps you recognize what information you are missing and what you are strong at and so on. Differently, student who just reread often are overconfident, which leads to them getting too cocky and performing worse on their tests and so on.

Research on any component of this method of learning has been done and has proven to be quite proven that it is scientifically more efficient to learn in these ways.
