# Advantages of observing brain damage
**Details**
The main advantages to this method is the precision, as we have mentioned before, clinicians and researchers can almost be 100% sure of the fact that this damage is somehow directly or indirectly related to the inability of a certain part in the brain, as the brain parts are interconnected to each other extremely closely. Another main advantage is that it is non-invasive, the researcher only needs to view the correlation of the brain to the damage to the patient's behavior.
