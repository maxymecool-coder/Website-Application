# Radical Empiricism
**Details**
Modern science operates under a materialist **mythos** (worldview) that often dismisses subjective mental states as secondary to brain physiology. William James, the father of American psychology, countered this with **Radical Empiricism**: the rigorous study of _all_ phenomena, including internal, introspective states.

James argued that "rational consciousness" is only one type of consciousness, separated from other potential forms by the "filmiest of screens." He posited that a complete scientific account of the universe cannot ignore these altered states (often induced by chemical stimuli/nitrous oxide), as they represent distinct types of mentality with their own adaptations.
