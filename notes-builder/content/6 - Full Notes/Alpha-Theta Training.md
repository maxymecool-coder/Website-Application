# Alpha-Theta Training
**Details**
This is a particularly special procedure in neurofeedback as it can induce the sorts of hypnagogic states (Hypnotic trance like in EMDR). When the theta waves (low frequency) predominate the brain, the mind focuses on the internal world, a world of imagery. Alpha brain waves then act as a bridge between the external and internal world.

As discussed in EMDR, the challenge in PTSD is to help the patient imagine their way out of situations, so that the present is not a continuous reliving of the past. The trance states can help loosen the conditioned connections between stimuli and responses, such as loud cracks acting as gunfire, and so on. A new association can be introduced so that those very same loud cracks can be of fireworks, of people applauding.

In these states that are created from this training, the traumatic events can be safely reexperience and change in their minds.
