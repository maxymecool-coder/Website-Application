# The applicability of the New World's Theory
**Details**
In the making of his theory, Carl Rogers did not just consider it for psychologists or anyone specializing in the field of psychology, even though they were the ones who requested him to do so. He also did this for "the intelligent layman" as the phrase goes. The way that he created it is to help people enrich themselves, their souls and thinking and personalities.

Another reason is of course for the ones who wants to know his thoughts and methods, especially those that were specializing in psychology, or use it in their daily lives. But moreover, he is also doing this for himself, as he wants to speak out about his own view in contrast with the popular opinions, meaning the Freudians, viewing patients as objects so much, and rejecting any further research that can be done in the field.

More than just scientific findings and statistics, he wants to affect the very theory behind it all and add more to it. He wants his findings to change the very nature and the way people interact with each other in relationships, and for his findings to provoke people to be more mindful about it, to spend more resources to improve it. In the way he publishes his work, he hopes that people who are interested will find it, not it finding the audience that comprise of the bulk of the community.
