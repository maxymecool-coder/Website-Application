# Wakefield's Synthesis
**Details**
This model, created by philosopher and social worker, Jerome Wakefield, is named "harmful dysfunction" and it is quite a fantastic model in identifying mental illnesses and reducing stigmas of those who have them.

First, we will start with one of the two criterion, harm. This component is rooted in cultural and social distinctions, it requires so that the individual must be causing harm to others or to themselves to be sufficient for this model. This makes it so that the social deviation will not determine the diagnosis due to it not exactly being harmful to society yet. For example, a six toe genetic mutation may deviate from the societal norm but it does not create any pain for society.

Second, the other part of the equation is dysfunction. This component is to be purely scientific, it determines that the symptoms one is experiencing must be STATISTICALLY different from what the norm experience. This though, is particularly difficult and is the only glaring drawback of this model. Even in the current day, we cannot prove a dysfunction in the complex mental processes of the human being. Perhaps in the future if we can invent machines to completely see and understand, keep track of the brain, we can notice the abnormalities and come to a conclusion. Unfortunately, for now, this model remains a highly theoretical model prevented by technological advancements.
