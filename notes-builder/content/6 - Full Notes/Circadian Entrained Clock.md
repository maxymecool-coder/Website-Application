# Circadian Clock
**Details**
In combination with everything that has been mentioned, it will create something called a "circadian-entrained rhythm." This means that the body will be more used to predicting the schedule and what is going to happen at the start of the day and of course later in the day. This may be incredibly useful for people who are having to shift their schedule for anything that is happening in their lives.
