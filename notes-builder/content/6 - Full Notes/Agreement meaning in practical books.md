# Agreement meaning in practical books
**Details**
First, we must discuss about the questions that must be answered in books. The things that change are the priority of what the book is about becomes the primary directive. You must know what problems he was trying to solve, and what he wanted to do. This is because knowing this also lets you know what he wants you to do, and that is obviously important. 

Second question doesn’t change much either. But in Rule 8, whereas it required you to state the problems he did not solve, this will become discovering and understanding the means of what he is saying and why it leads you to such ends. 

The third question, verifying the truth is changed more drastically. In the case of a practical book, this becomes comparing whether the author’s objectives align with what he proposes to reach them, alongside with your conception of what is to be seek, and the best method to do so. The last is changed the most. Agreement with a book, practical one, is implying you’ve done what it said and it worked. There exist situations where your ends do not correspond with the author, and so even if what the author is saying is true, you do not accept or agree with it. 

Lastly is the groups of people that does these things. Even if you agree, you may not think that you are the one that should do it, doesn’t mean the author is false, means you are just not in the position that you should be applying said rules.
