# Encouraging for clients
**Details**
The [[Action Plans]] are clearly useless unless the client actually does the work, so we need to figure out how to get them to adhere to this, and there are actually quite a couple different things we can. Some guidelines to remember are to tailor it to the person, provide rationale, be collaborative, make it easy (because no one can jump that far) as you can also change it latter when the problem is too hard, provide instructions, set up a reminder system and even doing the action plan in the session and imagining it to figure out the feeling/problem they think would happen when they do do it.

To achieve these goals, try to ask them questions to elicit their response and understand their reasoning and try to involve their own opinion in this as well. To go into more details of the guidelines that are difficult alike setting up a reminder system. For this, try to make them remember where they keep it and how they will remember to look at it like having a reminder, alarm, sticky notes, bracelet to wear,...

For the last remaining two, it is to try to gauge them to see if they will be able to do it in session and also understand the feeling of doing it, if its good or bad, if they succeed or encounter a problem, and that would encourage them to do it outside of session later, understandably so.
