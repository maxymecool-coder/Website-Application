# Peer Rejection of ADHD
**Details**
Using the same sociometrics at a summer camp, children were asked after 3 hour of participating in it, from 9 AM until lunch, and their responses of who was the most aggressive mostly pointed towards the kid with ADHD. By that first afternoon, boys with ADHD were 5.5 times more likely to be rejected by their peers.

What's notable about this is that the reputation is nearly impossible to get rid of and start anew. This then implicates that when an effective treatment has been found for those with ADHD, it's best not to stop the treatment when entering a new environment or simply continuing said environment as it can cause peer rejection.
