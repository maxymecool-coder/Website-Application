# Reflection as  a learning principle
**Details**
Reflection in any fields, in any subjects can lead us to stronger learning as it involves retrieving knowledge and earlier training from memory, connecting to new experiences, and rehearsing what to do differently the next time. As we can see, after a test for example, when you deliberately go through the questions that you got wrong, maybe even the ones you got right but took a long time, you would be able to see why you got stuck on it and make up new ideas, strategies for you to tackle it differently next time.

This is actually an extremely important part of deliberate practice, as you are continuously building on your own knowledge and becoming more meticulous and careful each time you do this.
