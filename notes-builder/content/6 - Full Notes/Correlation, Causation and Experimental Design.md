# Correlation, Causation and Experimental Design
**Details**
The problem with doing psychological experiments to push the field usually comes down to two reasons: the ethics behind the experiments (like doing it on children and kidnapping people and what not) _correlation as a statistical technique_ is independent of whether your _design_ is experimental or correlational. You can perfectly well run a correlation analysis on data from a fully experimental design. "Correlational" names a kind of _study_; "correlation" names a kind of _math_; the two do not have to travel together. To solve the first problem we use observational and correlational studies by the way.
