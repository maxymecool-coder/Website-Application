# Vision as information
**Details**
Almost 25% of our brain is dedicated to vision, to process all the stimuli in the environment through our eyes. Because of this, psychologists are interested in finding out more information about this, and so it is clearer than other senses.

Firstly, we have to identify what we are viewing, which is electromagnetic energy that comes from light sources. This light may come in different forms, which can come in two categories: amplitude and wavelength, corresponding with the intensity (or brightness) of the light and the color (or hue) we perceive.
