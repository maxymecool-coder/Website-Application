# Multi-tiered System Support
**Details**
The MTSS is a system that implements EBPs effectively, and a data-driven system that has been proved to work incredibly well. Its philosophy is to group all of the extra classes that a student might have to take if they fall behind their peers into the school's system, improving the connection between these classes to the main ones by allowing the school to have control over them. Moreover, there are also scientific evidence to prove that they help students socially and mentally as well.

Within this system, there are three tiers which will be summarized accordingly. The first tier is the overall education offered to all of the students regardless of their level of learning. The education is different from others by that it uses EBPs alongside it, which has proven effectiveness due to 75-80% of students of this tier passes their tests. Moving onto the second tier is where specialized classes are necessary, students are grouped together into special classes that utilize other EBPs to supplement the main education, about 10-20% of students overall needs this. After evaluations, tier 2 students are either returned to tier 1, continue having another set, or move into tier 3, which is individualized education. At this tier, it is no longer a teacher managing the class but rather a interventionist, they apply even more specialized EBPs, with more time and resources, and also an overview of the process.

The other framework for this system is SWPPBIS (**S**chool-**W**ide **P**ositive **B**ehavioral **I**nterventions and **S**upports), which handles the social and behavioral skills of students. Applying with the same principles that the former does, but with different EBIs, notably, the SEL. The SEL aims to improve the student's emotional intelligence originating from the EQ tests (The one that we researched in summer), basically implementing methods to overall improve student's skills. Each tier goes on the same, with the higher tiers requiring students to get even counseling or mental health providers. It has even been further proven that these methods can be performed by those in the school system, not necessarily outsiders. It has been proven to improve the overall positivity of the environment, mental health, reduction in distress,... but also the academic performance of students.
