# The Private Society
**Details**
As raised in [[The Unworkability of Utility]], we have basically stated that social unity cannot be grounded in shared values about the good life, so the question becomes: is democratic society merely a collection of individuals pursuing private ends with no deeper common bond ? Society merely because a use or means to private purposes rather. The question is whether this is the best we can hope for, or whether there is a happier possibility that pluralistic democratic society might achieve.

Though nobody would deny what has been said above, it must also be known that humans cannot fulfill their potentiality in a lifetime, which of course corresponds to their personal goals and attritions. What does this mean for society ? It means that social cooperation is not just mere means to personal goals but quite necessary in the grand scheme of things. When people with similar potentialities cooperate, the group can realize capacities that no individual could fully develop alone. When people with complementary potentialities cooperate, they make possible activities that none could carry out individually.
