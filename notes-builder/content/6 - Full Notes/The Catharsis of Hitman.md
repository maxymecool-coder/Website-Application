# The Catharsis of Hitman
**Details**
In the story of Hitman, Agent 47's targets are powerful people, but they are not just powerful in terms of wealth, but also in influences, connections. And of course they are selfish enough to try to get even more powerful, making them more and more selfish, narcissistic, until they represent some of the worst part of humanity. They believe that they are safe, of everything, from everything, except no one is untouchable... because there exists Agent 47. But at the end of the day, this does not truly change anything, there are enough people to continue filling in the space and simply continuing everything, as that kind of power does not just suddenly disappear.

It can be reflected in the gameplay how powerful people just see you for your outfit, they do not see the person, they only see the occupation, the work, because they are too powerful for you to be worth their mind.
