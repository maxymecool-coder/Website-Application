# DBT for Bipolar Disorder
**Details**
Though originally deriving from CBT, the treatments for Bipolar disorder usually requires a longer-term commitment, going from long months or even years of treatments. The symptoms for bpd may suddenly pop-up long after the treatment plan and will recur with either certain events or on their own.

With this difference from other mental disorders come with the change in approach from the therapist as well, they not only have to focus on helping a person overcome from the symptoms and recover from its effects but also prepare the patient for future episodes. This long-term form of treatment may vary as the condition does, requiring perhaps weekly during the first phase when skills are taught and symptoms are reduced. As they move onto the next phases, it may turn to biweekly or even less frequent than that. It is even advised for the patient and therapist to keep in contact.
