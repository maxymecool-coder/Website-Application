# Writing Task 2 Tips
**Details**
As a culmination of writing about 3 dozens essays, I believe that there are some personal problems and tips to address. Firstly, let's talk about the introduction and conclusion. I usually start and end an essay much simpler than whatever I write in the body, which may break the overall structure of the piece of academic writing because of said discrepancy, so definitely something to remember.

Furthermore, I do not think that figuring out the ideas for an essay is particularly difficult depending on most topics to be honest. I believe that what one should actually focus on is the approach that one can take in light of the topic. Regarding the writing task 2, there are many approaches that one can realistically take in this task, depending on how wide or narrow they want their response to be. I think it is best to focus on figuring out which approach would allow me to write the best possible essay rather than anything else.

Concerns regarding grammar and vocabulary are also extremely real. At certain times there are visible "tuxedo-flip-flops" which basically means words that are advanced but then go with another that is quite poor. A total revision of the essay's flow, structure, vocabulary, and grammar is recommended after the initial draft, or you can just do so after each paragraph.

**Supporting Content**
Additional concerns: the precision of the usage of a word, try not to use words in a way that would impose a harsher meaning or undermine the meaning of a word.
