# Purpose of DBT
**Details**
As DBT is catered to cases of BPD or Bipolar, which are difficult cases though not unsolvable, they need to have their own sets of goals as well, not just alleviating the symptoms of the patient. First is to instill skills into the patient to manage their condition, then it is to improve and maintain their motivation to change and engage with the treatment, as well as ensuring that the generalization can happen in their lives, enhancing the therapist's motivation to deliver effective treatment. And finally, to assist the patient in rebuilding their environment so that it caters more to their growth.

To accomplish these functions effectively, there are modes of treatments to do so: individual therapy/case management, group/individual skills training, between-session skills coaching, and a therapist consultation team. Each modes has different targets (situations) and so different strategies for reaching those targets. And with that comes even smaller sections where the skills are divided into.
