# Self-criticisms in depression
**Details**
The genesis of the harshness of these criticisms usually comes down to the depressed patient failing in something. When he fails, the attribute that he once counted on to help him resolve and deal with life, will be regarded by him as getting worse, as degrading. And as that attribute appears to fade away, he believes that he cannot get satisfaction out of life and all that he can expect is pain and suffering.

The patient would self-blame in self in ways that he would scream and blame others. Which means he will insult, find the bad things in himself and then encompass his total image of the offender. Finally he would try to punish them and sever the relationship to reject them fully.

When a depressed patient makes a negative evaluation of himself, he generally does not feel angry with himself; in his frame of reference he is simply making an objective judgment. Similarly, he reacts with sadness when he believes that somebody else is devaluating him.
