# Anticipatory Socialization
**Details**
Basing off of the [[Generalized Other]], people who are aspiring to be in a group can develop mental models of how a person from that group would act like and then follow that example to eventually become a member. This is quite abundant in society, as it is involved in almost every situations in the daily life. For example, somebody wants to join Stanford, they would have to research who a Stanford student is, their principles, their characteristics and try to become someone similar to that so that they can join Stanford.
