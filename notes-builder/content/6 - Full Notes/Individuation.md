# Individuation
**Details**
To truly live happily, we have to individualize ourselves. Understand that in our lives we are built up by values that parents instill in us, that our own culture instills into us. For the people who follows these values to a tee, they will eventually run into a wall, be it a college graduation or a middle life crisis. They don't understand, there's nothing left for them to do, and they do not know what to do.

To avoid coming to these conclusions, a person can start by finding values in themselves, by trying to split themselves apart from whatever values that had been instilled by them by their parents, by their cultures, so that they can live by whatever they believed and loved in. And in no way will they find this in self-help books, another distraction, another offer for a "way out".

As the modern society grows, self-help books increasingly become more popular because of how people are constantly finding ways to escape the vicious cycles and fulfill the tasks that they need to fulfill. But reading these books does not help one escape because people follow others' methods instead of devising their own by understanding the meanings and principles in which those methods function.

We have to understand that we have to balance our values in our lives, its an act of constantly balancing to achieve happiness or whatever that is that we want or need. It is fine that we cannot do this immediately, as there are so many factors that can affect it, so offer ourselves the same kindness that we can for others with their own flaws.
