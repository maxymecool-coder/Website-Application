# Food timing for waking
**Details**
Eating may activate your metabolism and so eating in the morning may also help you become more awake and aware. But more than that, in addition to food timing, what you eat is also necessary. Eating a lot may also make you tired as your body has to diverge a lot of resources into processing all that food.
