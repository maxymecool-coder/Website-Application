# Approaches to Healing Trauma
**Details**
Broadly speaking, there are 3 main approaches to trauma recovery.

The first is a top-down approach. Through communicating with others, allow one-self what is happening to them and processing the memories of the trauma that happened.

The second is using medications that shuts down certain useless alarm reactions, which are alarms that originally wasn't there and trauma caused it them to become defective, subsequently causing normal actions to trigger defensive actions. Or using other technologies to alter how the brain organizes information.

The last is bottom-up, which is allowing the body to re-experience the trauma that has happened in a safe space. This results in it contradicting what the brain thinks about such actions, helping the patient's brain to recalibrate itself.
