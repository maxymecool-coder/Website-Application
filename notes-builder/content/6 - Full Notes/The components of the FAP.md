# The components of the FAP
**Details**
Getting into how the FAP is triggered and how it is developed, we encounter the stimulus, which is what incites the FAP in the first place. Ethologists developed a toolkit to identify these triggers. For example, a red dot over the mother bird's beak where the child would peck to signal to her that they want food is the **releasing stimulus**, then they would replace it with a **super stimulus** by showing a paper and a big red dot on it, sending the bird into a pecking frenzy. This experimental spirit has reached its zenith with modern neuroethologists who build robotic bees that can perform the "waggle dance" so convincingly they can send an entire hive flying off in the wrong direction. It is a science built on clever, respectful, and sometimes cruel, manipulation.

Another way to show another element that was the **adaptive value** of the FAP itself. Showing this with cruelty, Niko Tinbergen did, by flipping broken eggshells over and ruining the camouflage of chicks, predators swoop in and took them away.
