# Finding important words and using them to come to terms
**Details**
This rule has two parts. First is to locate those words that make a difference. The second part is to determine the meaning of these words, with precision. 

This is the first rule for the second stage of analytical reading, the aim is now to interpret its contents and message. 

And so the step is to deal with the language as such, and then to go beyond the language to the thought that lies behind it. And so the only thing to do is to make the best use of language, and when you want to convey or receive knowledge. The main point is that one word can be the vehicle for many terms, as well as one term can be expressed by many words.
