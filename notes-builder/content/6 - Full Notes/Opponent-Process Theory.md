# Opponent-Process Theory
**Details**
The eyes are not the only thing that is used in vision, as we've talked about the [[Visual Cortex]]. Now this specifically links to the theory above. This theory states that we view images in paired opposites, red versus green, yellows versus blue and so on. When we view at something for an extensive amount of time focusing on it and then look at something of a plain color, we will experience one color after the removal of others, which is negative afterimages.

The explanation for this is not clear yet, but psychologists say that it depends on cell in the nervous system that increase their activity in the presence of a certain color, and decrease the color opposite from that.
