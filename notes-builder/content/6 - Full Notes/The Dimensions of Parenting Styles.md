# The Dimensions of Parenting Styles
**Details**
In measuring the parents' abilities and such, we can be able to divide it into two categories, responsive/warmth and control/demandingness. Simultaneously, they are related to how warm and caring a parent is and also how much expectations they have, how much rule-setting do they do and so on. From these two factors we can derive into 4 different types of parenting styles:

- Authoritative Parenting: This style usually yields the best social outcomes and academics in society in Western context.
- Authoritarian Parenting: This has lower warmth and so they are associated with acquiescence and rebellion.
- Permissive: This has lower control, yields more creativity but less impulse control.
- Neglectful: Low on everything, can result in situations that are worse than children who are abused.

**Supporting Content**
The way these are estimated are from results from surveys, parents can often report falsely or outright lie to the survey, statistics have proven that when children who are mature enough do these reports, the correlation between the parents and children are quite low.

As same for any other factor, these are highly dependent on culture, for Black people it might actually be beneficial to be authoritarian because they have to endure a racist and hostile environment later on in their lives. Though this may have ranged from decades ago, the intergeneration trauma persists nonetheless.
