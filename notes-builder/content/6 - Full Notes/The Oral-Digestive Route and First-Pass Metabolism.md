# The Oral-Digestive Route and First-Pass Metabolism
**Details**
How do chemicals, drugs, substances actually have an effect on us ? Now, drugs do not actually get injected into our brain the moment we ingest them, contrary to popular belief (sarcasm). It rather travels through our blood, and yes, that would mean it travels all throughout the body and has an effect even though it is miniscule. Since blood travels through our brain multiple times again and again, the chemicals EVENTUALLY will be able to effect us through that transmission process, but not before going through the liver.

The liver is what everything our body consumes passes through before entering the blood and all. The reason for this is to eliminate any weird or harmful stuff from what we consume so that our body won't shut down or go wrong. And so it biotransforms and often deactivates a portion of the toxins (drugs) before they reach the brain. Because of this process, the time to onset for orally ingested drugs is roughly **20 minutes**.
