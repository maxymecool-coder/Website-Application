# Moffitt’s Taxonomy
**Details**
Terrie Moffitt’s longitudinal research in New Zealand revolutionized the understanding of antisocial behavior by identifying two distinct developmental pathways.

The first group (which accounts for approximately 5% of boys) shows aggression from age 3. They suffer from cumulative risk factor as many negative factors in their lives like low verbal IQ, difficult temperaments, neurological deficits, and coercive parenting result in them accounting for the majority of delinquency cases or crimes in adulthood.

A much larger group (approx. 40% of teens), these are well-behaved children who engage in delinquency during puberty. The reason they do this is because of the feeling of needing to be treated and to have adult privileges which they clearly aren't suitable for just yet. They break rules to prove autonomy but eventually settle down once they get jobs and a family and such.
