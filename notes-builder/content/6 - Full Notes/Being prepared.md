# Being prepared
**Details**
- Stoics are usually somewhat emotionally prepared for anything that comes their way, so when it does happen, although the shock is huge, they can still take the blow and continue to focus on the task at hand. You shouldn't try to look for the best things in life, but rather prepare for the worst things, as its more likely that those things or something similar will happen.
