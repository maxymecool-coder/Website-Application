# Reinforcers
**Details**
This principle, formulated by Edward Thorndike, explains how the environment shapes behavior. It states that actions followed by a positive outcome (**reinforcement**) are strengthened and more likely to be repeated. Conversely, actions followed by a negative outcome (**punishment**) are weakened and less likely to recur. This mechanism of learning through consequences suggests our motivations are molded by the rewards and punishments present in our environment. This can explain to us about academics, students that vie for achievements and not focus on themselves, the other aspects of themselves.
