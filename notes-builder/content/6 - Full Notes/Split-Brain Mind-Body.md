# Confabulation
**Details**
When split-brain patients were presented with a snow scene and chicken claw from left to right simultaneously, the left hand pointed to the shovel and the right pointed to the chicken head. The explanation for this is that the right side responded to the snow science whilst the left responded to the chicken claw. Where the language center resided (being the left side) was asked why did they pointed to the shovel (as they did not see the snow), that system activates and makes up a story/evidence on why they did that.

This gives us insights into how our brain functions, muscle memory, mental shortcuts, and then our conscious mind spins a story to claim credit for said instincts.

Continuing on, split-brain patients have evidently been able to operate two tasks at the same time (exemplified by how they are able to draw two shapes at the same time or pick two separate things and so on and so forth). This goes against the Imaginative Understanding view, as how could we ever understand how they feel when they do this when we physically lack the capabilities to do so ?
