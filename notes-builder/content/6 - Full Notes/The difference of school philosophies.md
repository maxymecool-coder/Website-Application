# The difference of school philosophies
**Details**
In western countries, the freedom of students are much more prevalent than eastern countries. And though we have no complete evidence to suggest which is better than which, we can still observe how they both work in different ways. In eastern countries, even though the dominant philosophy of education leans on the stricter side, there are few countries who has established this, like China or Singapore. But more than that, these countries also just generally has had better figures related to their educative system (I would presume). The level of strictness is variable but it seeks like they always have a certain minimum that only increases from there.

On the other hand, there’s the western countries, on the complete opposite side of the spectrum. Mental health is a big part of their education system as it is a valued side of psychology that has been extensively studied to assist students. Both of these methods work, in different ways certainly, as the nature of people that these two philosophies produce are also widely different.

Students in these environments (Eastern) often are discontent and compare themselves to the Western educational systems (and also these systems are catering to those). Sociologists argue that these students fail to see the fact that the goals that their teachers are aiming to teach them are different from how the Western systems are.
