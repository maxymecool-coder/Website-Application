# The Dopamine Hypothesis
**Details**
Thanks to observations from medications testing in schizophrenia whilst trying to prove that dopamine is largely correlated with it, psychologists found out that drugs that increased dopamine can induce psychosis and drugs that blocked the receptors reduce these psychotic symptoms.

Further research showed that two parts of the brain was functioning in this exchange, the Mesolimbic Pathway and the Mesocorticol Pathway. The former receiving too much dopamine leading to the positive symptoms that is typically experienced in schizophrenia due to the sheer amount of dopamine making its way to the reward systems. In the latter however, there is hypoactivity, which means that there is too little of this dopamine in it, which projects to the PFC, this links to the negative system. The PFC due to not having any information to work with, does not do its executive functions.
