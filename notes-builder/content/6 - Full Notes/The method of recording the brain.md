# The method of recording the brain
**Details**
This is a new imaging technique, is non-invasive and easy to use. By combining this imaging with smart experimental designs and good data analysis, we can determine the function of brain regions and their interactions. The main new imaging methods are:

1. the Positron Emission Tomography, the fMRI,
2. the functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging,
3. the magnetoencephalography, the electroencephalography,
4. the Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation.

PET, first method, records blood flow and brain metabolism. The second, fMRI, records blood flow and oxygen concentration. The third, records magnetic field produced by the brain. EEG records electrical activity of groups of neurons through electrodes attached to the skull. Finally the TMS, stimulates or inhibits some part of the brain.
