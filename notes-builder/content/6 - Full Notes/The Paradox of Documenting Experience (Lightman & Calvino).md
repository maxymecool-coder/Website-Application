# The Paradox of Documenting Experience (Lightman & Calvino)
**Details**
To keep this frank and short, basically, even if we document or that we do not document, it is the same case that we are responding in ways that might not matter to quite a terrifying condition, the condition of forgetting. And the same can be said for any events we want to make memorable, they are optimized for active recall rather than to capture the specific authentic experience.
