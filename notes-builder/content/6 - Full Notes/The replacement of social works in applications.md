# The replacement of social works
**Details**
Although voluntary work for the community can help immensely for a student's profile, it is sometimes a problem for others as they do not have time for it as they have jobs and so on.

To deal with this problem, one must look into the work that they are already doing and see the impact that it may have on their own community and neighborhood. Moreover they can use the skills, specific skills that they have gained from their job to contribute.
