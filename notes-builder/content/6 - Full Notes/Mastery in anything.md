# Mastery in anything
**Details**
Mastery in any field require a gradual accretion of knowledge, conceptual understanding, judgement, and skill. The way of getting theses skills are from deliberately learning and thinking about your own learning processes. Memorizing facts and information cannot do this, its like stocking a construction site with the supplies to put up a house, without having understood the knowledge and concepts of those material to apply systems and principles to make a good house. This shows us that to master something, we have to have both knowledge and conceptually understand it all.
