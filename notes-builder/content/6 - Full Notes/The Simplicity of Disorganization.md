# Disorganization
**Details**
A good organization system of notes will help us know what to do and when to do it to the best of our abilities. This is why planning is inefficient as you would waste so much time preparing the schedule and then tweaking when little annoyances come up and ruin your workflow/focus.

Everything from reading info to writing it is closely connected, that is why you need to make them as easy to follow as possible so you will seamlessly go through each other and allow you to enter flow. It still needs to be flexible enough to do what needs to be done. A complete system that you can trust in will allow you to focus on the task at hand and get everything out of your brain.

Trust in the process, more often that not, notes with similar properties will appear and follow up and create a large chain/group of notes of a system.
