# The Reverse Inference Problem
**Details**
Anybody’s traits and mental states are not visible to anyone else at all. So the logical inference of anyone’s traits are derived from their emotional states and moments, something that is shockingly inaccurate due to how situations affect our activities. We only ever see their behavior, and so we run the logic back from that single instance, and then deduce that they had hostile intent, was in an angry state and is overall an angry person. This phenomenon is quite seductive because it makes us feel as if we are able to predict and understand someone on a fundamental level and so we can somewhat manipulate and control the situations we find ourselves in with them and so on.
