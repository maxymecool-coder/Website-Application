# Intimate Relationships
**Details**
Now this is a piece of work. To be honest, there really isn't a certain or sure definition scientifically for the term, but of course we can work it to a certain point. Perhaps the idea of intimate relationships resides in sexual desires. There is a somewhat nebulous idea about how people think about sex and how it represents perhaps exclusivity or intimacy, and so the appearance of said variable would precipitate the relationship to be in that sense.

Moreover, this is only one dimension in relationships, there might be more or other variables that we have not accounted for due to the nature of the axis we are measuring them on.
