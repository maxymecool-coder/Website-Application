# Determinism and Free Will
**Details**
The famous Laplace demon example states how something, a being can completely predict what happens in the future without fail, as we are made of physical matter, we too can be predicted. Determinism asks if that even allows moral responsibility if everything in the universe is already set in stone.

There are two positions regarding this question, obviously so, in completely opposite ends of the spectrum. The libertarians argue that free will exist so determinism must be false while the hard determinists argue the opposite. But there also exists a third view of the compatibilists, which constitutes that even though we have rules that govern over us, we are still free beings that can decide for ourselves in ways that matter morally.

**Supporting Content**
It may even be the case that determinism is false due to quantum mechanics still being unproven.
