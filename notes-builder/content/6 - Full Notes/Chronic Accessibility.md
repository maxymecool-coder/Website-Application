# Chronic Accessibility
**Details**
What is Chronic Accessibility ? So basically, this concept entails the schemas of ourselves, based off of how influenced we are by our environment, culture, and so on. Its as if you're chronically primed, like being called your name so many times, you would instinctually respond whenever someone calls it.

But this phenomenon also extends externally to other works like Romeo and Juliet, if you were to hear one, you would immediately jump to the other, or your brain would, unconsciously, it would bypass the cognitive barrier. So John Bargh (surprise surprise) did another hallmark experiment that proved this. By getting people who were chronically accessing the word "talkative" and what else, he would then prime them with a different word. Then after certain amounts of time, he would then test which word would come out if they were told a neutral story that could be interpreted either way ? Short time (15s) resulted in the primes winning, long time (180s) resulted in the chronic winning.

From that, we can conclude that even though primes have a significant effect on us shortly after we interact with them, who we are and what we are internally eventually drives it all back into our system after a certain amount of time.

**Supporting Content**
Even more interestingly, he then researched the effects the same things would have on behaviors, priming on behaviors. People would act accordingly to the words that they were primed with time ago in situations after. For example, in his RCTs, he let people see two words, rude or kind and then they were to then placed into a situation where they could act either way. If they were primed with rude, they would be ruder, if they were primed with kind, they would be kinder.
