# Why do we do anything
**Details**
The fundamental question, "Why do we do anything?" can be initially answered by exploring two major theories. The first proposes that our actions are determined by our environment, making us "slaves to the environment" (Behaviorism). The second suggests our actions are driven by our brain's innate wiring, making us "slaves to our brains" (Neurobiology of Reward). There are further points to elaborate the complexities in the human society and so on as well.
