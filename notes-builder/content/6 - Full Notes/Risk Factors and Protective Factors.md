# Risk Factors and Protective Factors
**Details**
Risk factors are basically the aspects that may negatively contribute to an individual's mental health. It can make them more at risk to be depressed or experience any kind of mental disorders. An example of this may be seen in bullying, if a kid gets bullied, there is a higher probability that that kid will experience negative emotions and perhaps even a mental disorder.

On the other hand exists the protection factor, this factor is the opposite of risk factors, which means they can inspire positivity or reduce the effectiveness of the risk factor. To exemplify this, someone that takes care of the child like a mentor can give him comfort, help him to deal with his bully and generally allow him to foster and become a better person.
