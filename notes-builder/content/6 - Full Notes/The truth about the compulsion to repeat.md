# The compulsion to repeat
**Details**
The compulsion to repeat, this is basically a theory that the reenactments of traumatic events might have been an unconscious attempt to get control over the past situation that one went through. Possibly allowing to master and resolve it, ending the suffering and pain. This is all related to the old brain, which is the part that remains storing the trauma leftover from earlier life.

There is no actually evidence for this, in fact, most references points to it actually leading to further pain and self-hatred. Or prove to the patient that they can't escape from their past.

# The stimuli
The body can learn to adjusts to any level of stimuli, meaning that even in adverse conditions, eg skydiving, going on slides, going to the theme park,... it can eventually overcome those fears and turn into pleasure. Same goes for trauma, when one is so used to trauma, they can see it as pleasure instead of fear, making them experience withdrawal symptoms when that is not available. And in the long run, patients maybe preoccupied with the withdrawal rather than the activity itself.
