# Plasticity of the Receptors
**Details**
The receptors of proteins are not just static, they do not just exist and accept neurotransmitters, in fact, they can adapt themselves to function correspondingly to the context of the body system. Dysregulation of this system is detrimental as it can be a contributing factor in mental illnesses. For example, in depression, serotonin has less time to be in the system to transfer information, making it so that the auto-regulating receptors reduces, creating a downwards spiral. Another example would be in onset diabetes, where the patient’s fat cells become “insulin resistant” because of insulin receptors reducing.
