# The difference of wings
**Details**
When assessing the wings of an F22 and a Cessna, we can clearly see a huge difference in the shapes of their wings. Because the F-22 Raptor is dictated by the behavior of air at transonic and supersonic speeds, it would need to have its wings swept back, to ensure that only a portion the air accelerating over the curved upper surface normal to the chord line, tricking the wing to believing its flying slower than it actually is, which delays the compressibility effects and shockwave formation. But what about the straight wing of the Cessna ? Well you’re cooked, the air that passes over the chord line may accelerate to supersonic speeds before the aircraft does, creating shockwaves that act as barrier to lift and immense drag.
