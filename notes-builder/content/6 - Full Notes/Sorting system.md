# Sorting system
**Details**
With ideas from Getting Things Done, we can further sort our ideas and notes into more categories. By starting it all in an inbox (or fleeting notes) and then organizing them into what we want to do with it, like a maybe folder, the permanent folder, and others. This helps us in multiple ways, like how ideas we aren’t sure about can still be accessed and assessed.
