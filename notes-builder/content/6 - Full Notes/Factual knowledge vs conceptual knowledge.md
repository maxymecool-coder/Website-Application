# Factual knowledge vs conceptual knowledge
**Details**
Recall and recognition require something known as “factual knowledge,” which is considered to be a lower level of learning that “conceptual knowledge.” This knowledge on the other hand requires an understanding of the connections between the elements of a bigger topic/structure that enables them to function together. An example could be-seen in identifying birds, rather than differentiating between bird from bird, we identify their complex prototypes which can help us grasp the kinds of contextual and functional differences that go beyond the acquisition of simple forms of knowledge and go further in comprehension.
