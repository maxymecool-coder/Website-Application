# Additional Necessities of Automatic Thoughts
**Details**
Other than merely recognizing/identifying the automatic thoughts, it may also be beneficial for the therapist to assess characteristics of the thought like how certain the patient views it and how they feel because of it. After appealing with the automatic thought, it may also be required to evaluate how these characteristics have changed in accordance with the treatment.

This allows the therapist to recognize what might be going wrong in the process of treating the automatic thought and find out alternatives to it.

In dealing with automatic thoughts, offering/challenging these thoughts may not be as effective as Socratic questioning, which is basically allowing the patient to answer for themselves and merely only offering them the question (alike asking them what evidence may support/deny the thought, etc.)

In addition to cognitive rehabilitation, it may be more effective for adolescents to have behavioral experiments that disconfirm their original thoughts.
