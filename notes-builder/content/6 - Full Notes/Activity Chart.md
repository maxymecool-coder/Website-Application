# Activity Chart
**Details**
Even further, people can also get to scoring the activities from how much they don’t enjoy it or that they enjoy it fully. This may help them view their aspirations and goals to then commit themselves to it, as well as allow them to better schedule the activities into their days, or view their negative interpretations in a different light, where it is affecting them. People who are depressed cannot just go from doing nothing to doing something hard, they may need good scheduling to interspace rest and inactivity between working.

Getting the ratings after a meal may help the person to see where they are being cognitively affected by their thoughts (as emotions are governed by it). This subsequently allows us to better schedule self-care activities accordingly to help the patient. Ask yourself which parts of their day is missing and isn’t as balanced or much as it should be. Remind yourself that not every client will want to do this.

Additionally, you can even use the chart to predict how they would feel about their activities and then depending on the outcome, prove them wrong (by themselves) or tackle the cognitive distortion.
