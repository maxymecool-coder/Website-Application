# Advantages of PET
**Details**
The advantages of this method include its greater sensitivity, especially when compared to CT or MRI scans, the ability to detect different levels of brain activity featured in different colors, the easy interpretation of the results, and a good spatial resolution. Finally, this method allows for the observation of the same brain activity both during a task and during a rest state.
