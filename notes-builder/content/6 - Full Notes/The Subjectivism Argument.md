# The Subjectivism Argument
**Details**
The argument against Kant is basically that his philosophy is subjectivism, as he is saying that we impose the concepts of space and time onto experience. His viewpoint practically supposes that everything is both objective and yet subjective at the same time perhaps ?

I think this is a misunderstanding of his argument. The task is to explain how we can obtain objective knowledge from a framework we impose onto experience. Everyone interprets it the same way and works up to interpret it the same way, so the subjectivity does not mean the subjectivity of the result. Just as Copernicus showed that accounting for the observer's position produces better astronomy, not worse, Kant argues that accounting for the mind's contribution to experience produces objective science, not private fantasy.
