# Touch or Cutaneous senses
**Details**
What we think of as touch is actually a combination of several partly independent senses: pressure on the skin, warmth, cold, pain, itch, vibration, movement across it, and stretch of the skin. Each of these sensations depend on certain receptors. A pinprick feels different from a light touch, and different from a burn as the receptors for each situation are different. These senses altogether are known as the cutaneous senses. Although they are most prominent on the skin, they are also in our internal organs (skin is the biggest organ !). Therefore these senses are aka somatosensory system.
