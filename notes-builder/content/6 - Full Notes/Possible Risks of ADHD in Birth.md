# Possible Risks of ADHD in Birth
**Details**
A significant non-genetic risk factor is Low Birth Weight (LBW).

- **LBW:** < 5.5 lbs (approx. 7-8% of births).
    
- **Very LBW:** < 3.5 lbs.
    
- **Extremely LBW:** < 2.2 lbs (1 kg).

Being born with a low birth rate may predict ADHD because the artery supplying the frontal lobes is prone to hemorrhaging in premature infants, damaging self-regulation centers. The same goes for alcohol consumption and smoking as well. (You'd be lucky to be alive if these were your parents traits anyways)
