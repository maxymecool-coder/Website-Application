# Active-Passivity
**Details**
Referring to basically people problem-solving by putting their problems on other people’s head. Those with BPD usually suffer from this, the main reason being they do not believe in themselves to have the ability to problem solve or the confidence to apply said skills.

You might think its completely and utterly nonsensical to say someone doesn’t have problem-solving abilities, after all, even children have the basic capabilities to solve problems, though said problems are not exactly psychological, social. To answer the question, people with BPD have always been having to deal with BPD for the most of their lives, not allowing them to focus or pay attention to solving problems effectively, which of course leads to them not having adequate levels of said skill.

But as said, that is only an answer to one of the reasons, the other being lack of confidence. This stems from them being discouraged from a young age when failing to solve problems or similarly, which inherently makes them fear having to put their hands on a problem later in life. And so to account for that, they divert it all to you, their loved one who they know will help.

There is some additional reason behind them going to you though, if said person with BPD usually just tried to yell louder or demand more as a child to have their needs fulfilled (forgot the attachment here), they will apply the same method to get you to do something.
