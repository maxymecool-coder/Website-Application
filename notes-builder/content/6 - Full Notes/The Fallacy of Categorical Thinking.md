# The Fallacy of Categorical Thinking
**Details**
In our lives, to simplify thoughts and processes, we usually divide them into categories so that we can easier deal with them. This is a natural impulse, and like most natural impulses, it has quite some detrimental effects:

- It makes us underestimate the difference between two facts that fall into the same category. For example, let's say, the alphabet are all just letters right ? And if you switch a letter to another, most of the time it would not have a big effect. Right... so now if I tell you to fuckle up, what do you think I really mean ? Buckle up.

- On the opposite side of the spectrum, it makes us overestimate as well. For example, the number 80 and 82.5, normally that's not a very big difference right ? Only about 2.5 numbers, and yet, when we take it into context like rounding up to a test score. Suddenly the 80 stays an 80 and the 82.5 gets rounded to an 83 or 85 (depending on the teacher).

- Lastly is the ignorance of us thinking about boundaries. Let's say memorization of numbers as an example, if I told you a set of numbers resembling a phone number, you would catch it right ? Now I switch the tone and speed at which I say the number, and you get quite disorientated. Why ? Because you were expecting me to categorically say each pairs or group of numbers, not randomly spitting the numbers out.
