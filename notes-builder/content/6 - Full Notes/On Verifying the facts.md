# On Verifying the facts
**Details**
Emotions inherently create biases in our perception of a situation, that is obvious, and so people with BPD usually perceive situations very emotionally and differently from others, leading them to blame themselves rather, which only worsens their conditions.

For when this happens, the best course of action is to help them check in with the facts, not saying that "oh well it happens to the best of us" but quite literally helping them see the objective reality. This can be started by telling them what exactly happened without themselves involved in it, then moving onto talking about their emotions when it is happening, validate said emotion and then disconfirm their interpretation/generalization about themselves and how they are as a person.
