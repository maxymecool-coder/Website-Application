# Emotions helping memory
**Details**
As emotions are strongly related to the amygdala, and the amygdala is highly related to the hippocampus as well. The amygdala responding to emotions alerting the neurons that certain information is important and makes the neurons and hippocampus reinforce them better, stronger.

For example, when you hear someone's name in a context that is highly emotional, for example you've taken a liking to them or something of the sorts, it will prove to be easier to memorize that.
