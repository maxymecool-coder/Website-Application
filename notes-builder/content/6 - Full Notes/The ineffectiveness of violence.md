# The ineffectiveness of violence
**Details**
For the larger part of the existence of the US and Mexico, they have been in a back and forth battle of drugs. Since the US is the biggest user of substances and coincidentally, Mexico is not only right next to it but also has some of the best conditions all over the world to grow these plants, and then illegally smuggle it all through the US borders. Since the US border is so wide (3000 miles+) it is more than difficult to regulate and deal with all of this.

Throughout the years, the US has been trying to dealing with this problems in numerous ways. Most recently, president Trump has been calling for the extension and reinforcement of these borders. In the 2010s, the US has had operations in Mexico to eliminate the cartels. This only resulted in more violence and more civilian deaths due to the cartels splitting to take control of the suddenly void of power.

The situation with Mexico is extremely delicate and difficult to deal with, unlike how Colombia was at the peaks of its drug empire with Pablo Escobar due to factors like how close it is to the US, the extensiveness of the borders and the fact that many cartels rule over the industry, not just one empire like Colombia. In fact, in the same timeline as the US tried rehabilitating Mexico, the same events happened in Colombia, including resuscitation of the government, implementation of laws and so on, yet only Colombia came out better.
