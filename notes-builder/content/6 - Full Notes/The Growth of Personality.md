# The Growth of Personality
**Details**
Personality is not entirely fixed; it shows patterns of both stability and change over a person's life. Research into trait psychology reveals how personality develops from childhood and is influenced by age, culture, and generation. As mentioned in [[The Big Five Model of Personality]], people who express early temperament of certain personalities as a child may retain such traits and have their future changed by such traits. With more time, these traits also start to become more stable, meaning one may express such traits with more detail and not be affected by situations, showing their traits through it.
