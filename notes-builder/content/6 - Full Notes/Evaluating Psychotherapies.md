# Evaluating Psychotherapies
**Details**
Before starting to evaluate everything, we will distinguish between a system of therapy and a simple cluster of techniques. A system would provide a format for understanding the disorders and give a blueprint of the reasons and the methods of doing it. It would include (a) a comprehensive theory or model of psychopathology and (b) a detailed description of and guide to therapeutic techniques related to the model.
