# State Capacity and Policy Success
**Details**
During the Great Depression, the U.S. government launched two parallel programs to stabilize prices: the Agricultural Adjustment Administration (AAA) and the National Industrial Recovery Administration (NIRA). The AAA was a remarkable success, while the NIRA was a complete failure and collapsed.

In the AAA, they already had connections with the farmers and political institutions that have knowledge and so on about the lands. On the other hand, the NIRA was the opposite, they had no idea of any of these topics that the AAA knew and more than that, the industrial workers were completely against any of this control that was imposed on them.
