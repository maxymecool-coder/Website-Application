# The questions of the slip-box
**Details**
As we work on the slip-box, we inevitably generate questions in between connections that are actually good questions. They have the balance between being narrow and focus, as well as having enough prior information to be able to answer. 

This works so well with the slip-box because of its nature. The slip-box already creates questions as we work through as discussed before, but even better the vast majority of questions are usually not viable and not worth answering. The slip-box addresses this problem by making the user question it every step of the way, eventually crossing out a lot of them. 

This helps us to not have to brainstorm of a topic to write but instead write on the questions that we already have in our slip-box that are already good.
