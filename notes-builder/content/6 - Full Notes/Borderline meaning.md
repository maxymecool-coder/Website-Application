# Borderline meaning
**Details**
In the early 1900s, there didn’t exist a specific or effective treatment for BPD, which at the time was quite prevalent, as they were the majority of mental disorders that clinicians encountered. At the time, the symptoms were in between neuroses and psychoses, meaning in touch with reality or out of touch with reality, and with that treatment was fairly ineffective, ranging from 0 - 0.2 Hedge’s g, maybe even backfiring.

Clinicians then came up with the term “borderline” because it was in between, unable to be sorted between the two types of disorders. Which nowadays has turned into a stigma that makes people avoid BPD patients in general due to how it is associated to a difficulty in dealing with the patient. This difficulty stems from how the problems manifest, in a way that doesn’t allow the therapist to disconnect themselves to the patient, affecting their mental capacity lots.

Nowadays though, BPD treatment is shown to be quite effective, with a g range from 0.5 - 0.7, which allows most people to be able to function normally in life.
