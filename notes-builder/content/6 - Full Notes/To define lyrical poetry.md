# To define lyrical poetry
**Details**
The simplest definition which is somewhat limited is that it is what poets write. But there are other definitions, poetry may be the author expressing himself, his personality in the form of written words, but also action, musical sounds, or even just feelings. But we will confine it to one definition for the sake of reading it efficiently, and that is “whatever may be the origin of the poetic impulse, for us, consists of words, and that is of words that are arranged in a certain way”.

Of course there are other definitions that forces poetry to always have meaning, or apply a specialized language, but that is just mixing modern notions with antique ones. So we stand in the middle, not too broad or narrow either.
