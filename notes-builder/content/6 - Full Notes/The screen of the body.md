# The screen of the body
**Details**
We use our brains for many things, discovering facts is one of those things, but also hiding facts… the screen hides most effectively… the body, which is the ins of it, like a veil thrown over the skin to secure its modesty, it hides the inner states of the body, those that constitute the flow of life. Although it helps us to attend to problems in the outside world, it also prevents us from feeling our “self”. 

I don’t know for sure what the screen is supposed to be but I do know that the brain structures that register background feelings are located near the housekeeping functions, this is because to manage the organism, we need data on the current state of the body.
