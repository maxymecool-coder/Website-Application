# Types of Risk Factors
**Details**
Types of Risk Factors:

- **Spurious:** A variable that predicts an outcome but is not the true causal agent. The relationship is better explained by another, underlying factor (e.g., aggression predicts school failure, but the underlying ADHD is the stronger predictor).
    
- **Fixed:** A risk factor that cannot be changed (e.g., sex, genes).
    
- **Malleable:** A risk factor that can be changed through intervention (e.g., parenting styles, income levels, peer groups). These are the primary targets for treatment and prevention.
    
- **Causal:** A malleable risk factor that, when changed, directly causes a change in the outcome. Identifying these is the ultimate goal, but it is difficult without true experiments, which are often unethical in this field. It is unethical because kids cannot be put under adverse conditions so that we can study which factors are causal and such.
    

A related concept is **vulnerability**, which can be thought of as an early-appearing, deeply-rooted risk factor like genetics or temperament.
