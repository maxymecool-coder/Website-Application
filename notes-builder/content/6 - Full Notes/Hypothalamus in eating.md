# Hypothalamus in eating
**Details**
The liver may regulate food intake. Many researchers believe that the brain itself contains cells that are also sensitive to the glucose levels in the blood, with these cells concentrated, the brain structure becomes responsible for so much of homeostasis: the hypothalamus. Evidence for such receptors come from experiments where it is injected with a chemical to make it unresponsive to glucose, result was ravenous eating.
