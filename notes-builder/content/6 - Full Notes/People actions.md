# People actions
**Details**
- In an argument, people usually act out of their emotions, they feel attacked, disappointed and so they rage, they say whatever they can to insult the other person, or you in this case. The thing to do in this situation is just to be calm, and think about it. Why are they so mad ? What happened to them ? How should I respond to this ?
- Humans are never evil, its just their situations makes them act irrationally and out of their emotions, understand this, to think and respond logically, to allow the argument to fizzle out, to end peacefully and beneficially. Humans are selfish, they are emotional, everything that is bad but in the end they are still our family, our friends, our kind and we must learn to appreciate them no matter what happens. But we can just not act like them.

**Supporting Content**
- Also you can just not assume too much about one person who is similar to someone you hate, think first, then say.
