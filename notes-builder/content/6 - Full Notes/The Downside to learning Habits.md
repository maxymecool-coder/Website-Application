# The Downside to learning Habits
**Details**
As said before, people get it wrong when trying to push themselves and study to improve their habits, they also get the purpose of doing so wrong. They get too comfortable and subsequently basically forget to deliberately practice to improve, they get too comfortable with their current skills that they forgot to go further to achieve mastery or to change the identity of themselves.
