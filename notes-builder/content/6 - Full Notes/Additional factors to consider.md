# Additional factors to consider
**Details**
Amongst military populations, traumatic brain injury and PTSD often co-occur. The rates of comorbidity suggests that over 20% of veterans suffer from both. 

Another set of factors include severe dissociative symptoms. Though this may heavily interfere with therapy, most people don’t anymore restrict patients exuding these symptoms from PE. In fact, they found that effectiveness doesn’t get affected much at all (depersonalization, emotional numbing,…). The maintenance required though is of course more as it does interfere with recurring PTSD symptoms. Personality disorders are illnesses that are at high rate of comorbidity with PTSD as well. And though on the psychopathology scale these patients have higher score in both pre and post-treatment, the degree of improvement is the same. PE of course also help other cases including Complex PTSD, guilt, shame, anger, and so on.
