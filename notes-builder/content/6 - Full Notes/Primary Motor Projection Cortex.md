# Primary Motor Projection Area
**Details**
This was amongst the first discoveries in the study of cortical function. It is the departure point for signals that are sent to the lower parts of the brain (hindbrain and the spinal cord) which will result into muscle movement. It is called projection (taken from cartography) because the sensory and motor primary projection areas seem to form maps in which regions of the cortex correspond roughly to the part of the body they control. It is located in the frontal lobe.
