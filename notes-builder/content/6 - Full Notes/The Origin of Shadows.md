# The Origin of Shadows
**Details**
As we are born into life, there is almost an inherent concept about how we are a mixed bag of already determined "traits". And so to be the best possible version of ourselves, we try to pick out and determine which is bad and which is good. One cannot simply eliminate this concept out of their lives because it is what creates culture.

For example, in the Eastern society, people gravitate towards traits such as interdependence, integration into society, social skills perhaps, and so they discard abilities that foster a person's inner world, understanding and all. We cannot eliminate this act from society as it takes away the meaning of the cultures and values of said society. Does this mean that the shadow is something that we will always encounter ? Not necessarily. When we are sorting these concepts out, we are largely uneducated about what they really are, we forget that they are cultural interpretations, cultural structures rather than metaphysical truth that we have to take as gospel. And in doing so, we inherently manifest and breed shadows.
