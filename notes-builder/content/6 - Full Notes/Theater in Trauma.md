# Communal rhythms and Theater
**Details**
The idea of people responding to your actions and such accordingly is inherently present in theater, or communal rhythm, which as talked before is heavily necessary in healing from trauma. This is because in a play all the characters and environment has to interact with each other, and in doing so, it helps the patient feel like they’re not out of place anymore. 

Continuing, being in a play that makes you be someone else, be a person who is powerful, who is different from the usual image of yourself that is so pathetic helps you to change your mindset, feelings and such on a more personal level than other methods. Perhaps its because people actually treat you like that and so you feel like that, although I am not sure in this matter. 

Last point I want to mention is how it forces actors to express their emotions more. In trauma patients, it is very usual that they experience Alexythmia, but the role of playing a character evokes strong emotions in them, and so they will start having to feel again. This might be scary at first, they can react violently, and run away, but encouraging them to keep on feeling will help them recover. 

But another point I want to bring up is community. When these people are not hushed away and instead accepted by the community in spite of their actions, they will eventually stay (considering the community is suitable for those individuals).
