# Environment Regulating Genes
**Details**
The transcription factors are what regulates the gene's function depending on the outside factors. These factors can be internal of the body or externally from the environment. When one of these transcription factors are affected, they can channel a whole network of genes to act, not just one. For example, when we feel fear, the transcription factors might do something to the DNAs so that they create proteins that receive the stress hormones of the body.
