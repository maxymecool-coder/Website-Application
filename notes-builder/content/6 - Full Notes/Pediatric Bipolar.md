# Pediatric Bipolar
**Details**
Diagnosing depression in children carries a hidden risk: the child may actually have latent Bipolar Disorder. Since the first episode of Bipolar is often a severe depression rather than mania, it can easily be mistaken for Unipolar Depression. The danger with using SSRIs in this situation is it flipping a switch and making the child experience mania instead, which may be extremely dangerous as they can become reckless and suicide. Clinicians look for predictors such as a family history of Bipolar, psychotic depression, or "atypical" symptoms like hypersomnia and diurnal variation. This has led to clinicians being careful with the admission of SSRIs, they would rather carefully screen everything to avoid the condition.
