# FNAF Mechanics
**Details**
The basis of mechanics for this "sub-genre" of games (it has so many fangames oof), are quite simple. The premise is always about how the main character is in an office (or something of the sorts) and trying to fend off killer animatronics from many entry points (or right there in the office) with their systems, like cameras, or flashlights, or masks even.

Throughout his games, Scott Cawthon built upon these mechanics and styles, so that the gameplay can stay fresh every single game. The same can be said for the fangame community of FNAF, as so many people being passionate about creating adjacent game styles just with different lore, art style or even gameplay. One of the MOST different games in this community is Nikson's The Joy of Creation. Instead of being a 2D ah game, it is instead a 3D ah game with incredible mechanics.

Obviously we have to start with how the game is in 3D, but not the open world kind, the setting is still in offices, is still in places where the player have to defend themselves. But the thing is, the mechanics in these places are still new and fresh, possible due to the engine change.
