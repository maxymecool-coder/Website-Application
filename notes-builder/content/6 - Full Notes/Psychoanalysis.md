# Psychoanalysis
**Details**
**Psychoanalysis**, developed by Sigmund Freud, is a distinct method for explaining and dealing with personality. Its central principle is that human personality and behavior are shaped by the dynamic interplay between conscious and unconscious forces.

The goal of a psychoanalyst is to explore these hidden forces to understand and address issues of personality. Practitioners who adhere to Freud's methods and theories are known as psychoanalysts, and while their views have evolved over the decades, they remain loyal to the foundational concepts established by Freud. The method itself is often referred to as the "talking cure," emphasizing dialogue as the primary tool for uncovering the unconscious influences on an individual's life.
