# Schema Therapy for Shame
**Details**
The therapy for BPD always has to start at the root, feeling the wounded child inside one self. Quite similar to [[IFS]], a person has to try to interact with the so called "exiled" part of themselves so that they can comfort and remember so that their mind can integrate the memories back. Talking about their feelings and such when these memories come up also does help immensely.

Next is to understand the defense/coping mechanisms that one might be instating because of their past (i.e.y by avoiding or overcompensating). This is where one must ask themselves about them being hypercritical or defensive about criticism, so on and so forth. These symptoms should also be kept track of so that they can be solved in the later stages. Most importantly, the lack of genuine feelings, which needs to be brought to the surface more readily.

For this example, we will take a man who has this life-trap. He is incredibly and completely devoted to gaining success and status so that he can impress people and all. But at the end of the day, these things does not help him feel good, it only is him running away from the fact that he needs to get rid of his narcissism and such to settle down.

There are many more detailed steps about this type of therapy, going through the program step-by-step.
