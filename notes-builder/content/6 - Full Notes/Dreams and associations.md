# Dreams and associations
**Details**
Alike a trance, in dreams, it is much easier to express the unconscious thoughts, and in doing so it would disrupt said dreams. But this is a compromise between propelling it into consciousness and blocking it from consciousness. It may appear in a disguised form, it will be distorted that finally results in the dream experienced. Condensation, displacement, symbolism are all used to transform this seemingly unacceptable dream into something that is, although it is extremely disconnected and hard to be traced back to its origin. 

The method to interpret these dreams follows the concepts above. Each element of the dream are isolated and associated to. The structure of neurotic symptoms and motivated errors in general are all identical to that of the dream.
