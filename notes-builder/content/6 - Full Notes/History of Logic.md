# History of Logic
**Details**
Logic has been seen in the earliest instances of civilization, as the Egyptian people of the past has used it to triangulate and re-establish property lines after the Nile floods. The Greeks, particularly Euclid and Aristotle, had used it to systemize it into a rigorous framework of axioms and theorems. For centuries, this geometric model—building complex truths from "self-evident" axioms—was viewed as the ultimate form of scientific theory.

But with the coming of the modern times, these claims of the "self-evident" was abolished due to how it could be wrong as people moved to epistemology, testing and experimenting to see if it is truly correct or not. And so logic was left in the dust and confines of linguistics until Boolean revived the field by reinstating it in mathematics instead. This shift transformed logic from a philosophical tool into a mathematical science, mirroring the revolutions of Copernicus and Newton in physics.
