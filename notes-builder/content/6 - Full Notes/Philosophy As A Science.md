# Philosophy As A Science
**Details**
Philosophy isn't necessarily the first subject, even as all subjects have derived from it. Philosophy is still governed by the laws of our universe which means the studies of said variables would at the end, rule above it. But nonetheless, it is where all the other sciences are born. As J.L. Austin metaphorically described, philosophy is the "initial central sun," a tumultuous body that throws off cooled, regulated planets that become independent sciences. When a subject is unrecognized, people do not have the vocabulary and such for it, the subject will likely reside in philosophy, but when it does, they will separate into a distinct science, as happened with mathematics, psychology, physics, and linguistics. Logic is a unique case in this sense, as it is seeing its migration out of philosophy, though only part of it, deductive reasoning, while inductive logic remains distinct.
