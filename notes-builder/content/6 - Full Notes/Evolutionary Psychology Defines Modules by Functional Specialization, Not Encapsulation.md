# Modularity for Evolutionary Psychology
**Details**
This term, though currently heavily debated, was once a key point of EP. The meaning of it was the tendency of biological systems to evolve specifically for certain functions, and “module” would mean an evolved cognitive specialization that resulted from that process, regardless of the level that it exists in a heavily policed system or operates on other information in the architecture (or the brain).
