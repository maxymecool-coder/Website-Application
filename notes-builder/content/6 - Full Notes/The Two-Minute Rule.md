# The Two-Minute Rule
**Details**
The Two-Minute Rule is a powerful technique for establishing new behaviors. It involves aggressively dumbing down a desired habit until it takes less than two minutes to perform, thereby removing almost all friction and restricting the commitment required. But its power goes beyond just making things easy. When I talk about this as an "investment," I mean that performing this two-minute action acts as a prior anchor. By showing up and doing the easiest possible version of the habit, we are putting investments into our identity. This forces our future selves to have an easier time committing to the larger, full-scale version of the habit later. It establishes the routine before optimizing it.
