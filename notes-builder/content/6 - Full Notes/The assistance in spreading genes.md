# The assistance in spreading genes
**Details**
More than just trying to spread our own genes, species also try to spread the genes of their relatives, because those genes of such relatives have levels of relatedness to them. This concept is inherently called **kin selection**. The concept also explains how animals can recognize their own relatives and cooperating with them to spread their genes. As shown in the vervet monkey study, when an infant gives an alarm call, other monkeys don't just look at the sound—they look at the infant's mother, understanding the kin connection and anticipating her response.

An example of some way a person may try to help his relatives spread genes may be glazing the crap out of her, telling others about how funny she is, how hot she is and all, and further.
