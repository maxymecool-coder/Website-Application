# Reliability and Validity
**Details**
This should probably be simple if one were to pay attention to the nuances of words in the english language. Reliability is repeatability: if you take the measurement many times, do you get the same answer? A reaction time or an eye-blink response tends to be highly reliable. Validity is aboutness: does the measurement actually capture the thing you intend to measure?

These two characteristics are independent of each other so the experimenter must be quite attentive of how they are. Suppose you want to measure stress. Measuring stress hormones (corticosteroids) in the blood is highly _valid_. This is because we are drawing the chemical directly from the source. Yet, it is highly unreliable due to how different people may respond to stress and how that may cause variations. The same goes for vice versa.
