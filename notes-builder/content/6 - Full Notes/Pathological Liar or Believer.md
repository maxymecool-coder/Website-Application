# Pathological Liar or Believer
**Details**
Throughout the series, Whiterose has always been portrayed as a convincing person, someone with the ability to make anyone believe or trust in her plans, like how Angela did. The thing is, it is quite unsure about if she really is good at deceiving people or that she genuinely believes in the machine or future that she is brewing.

As we go deeper and deeper into the series, I believe that this question remains... mostly unanswered, to the very end it seem as if she genuinely believes that she can bring her lover back by building her machine and going to an alternate timeline. But towards the very end of said scene, she herself is completely taken aback by Elliot's speech about humanity and almost seems to be questioning herself about if a part of her doesn't care or believe that the machine will work, only that it will kill millions of lives... But nonetheless, it is unsure as she then proceeds to say how she has been trying to move the project elsewhere so that it wouldn't affect the urban population.

And so psychologically, I believe that although on the outside she does believe that she wants to create a machine that can bring her lover and perfect life back, she also resents people and does it not for the good of humanity but rather for her own good, perhaps for a life ideal to her but no one else's in particular. At the end of the day, humanity did take her lover and life away from her which then got her onto this path of the Deus group and so on. She may as well just be a traumatized child who got her life taken away from her and is fighting against humanity as she positions it to be her enemy, whilst saying that she is its messiah, or savior.

Even further than that, the path that she took required tons of manipulation, tons of killings, delusional, and so on, if she admitted that it was for nothing, it might break her from the inside out mentally.
