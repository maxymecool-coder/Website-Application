# The Difference between Scientific and Psychological Explanations
**Details**
Simplifying these concepts altogether, scientific explanations are the mathematical and statistical algorithms that may help us determine a person's tendencies to do something or the probability that they will do it. What this doesn't explain though is their motive, the reason behind their decision, like how perhaps it is a past experience or memory that make them act that certain way.

Without the Imaginative Understanding, or empathy, the understanding is completely stripped of any humanity or warmth, which is something that is always needed.

For example, even if we act like a bat, trying to sleep in lockers (the dark) and eat like them, we can maybe perhaps know every physical fact there is to know about a bat's neurophysiology. However, no amount of physical data will allow a human to truly possess an _imaginative understanding_ of the bat’s inner life. We cannot know "what it is like" to be a bat. If physical facts are exhaustive, yet they leave out the subjective experience, then physicalism seems incomplete. This underscores the isolation of the mind: physics describes the hardware, but subjective experience remains inaccessible to outside observation.

**Supporting Content**
And so the conclusion is the consciousness is not something that we can understand scientifically but rather only imaginatively.
