# Non-Suicidal Self-Injury
**Details**
To be able to separate between suicide and actions close to suicide but not intended to do so, people have classified the latter as NNSI ([[Suicidal behaviors]]). Even though NNSI and suicides are behaviorally different, NNSI is nonetheless still also a strong predictor for future suicides.

The "theory" for why people do NNSI is that the view of blood and feeling of pain incites a physiological state that allows them to forget the painful mental state that they are in temporarily. Though the shame quickly comes back which makes them hide their wounds and then repeating the self harm behavior.

**Supporting Content**
Additionally, this can even lower the threshold for others to try and do this due to peer modeling and normalization.
