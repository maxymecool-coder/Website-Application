# The truth about Selection
**Details**
Championed by V.C. Wynne-Edwards, his theory on group selection about how group selection acts on the whole group, choosing those who are altruistic, who would sacrifice themselves for the group was fundamentally flawed. As this would mean that genes would favor altruism as a trait, as a behavior in groups, until all the members were inherently altruistic and therefore would sacrifice themselves, all of them, leading to extinction.

A more plausible theory is individual selection, where species try to multiply as many copies of themselves as possible, they try to distribute their genes amongst the population as much as possible. This selection operates through two primary mechanisms:

-  **Natural Selection:** Favoring traits that enhance survival to the point of reproduction (e.g., being fast enough to escape a predator).
    
- **Sexual Selection:** Favoring traits that enhance reproductive success, even if they hinder survival (e.g., a peacock's tail is metabolically costly and makes it easier for predators to spot, but females prefer it).
