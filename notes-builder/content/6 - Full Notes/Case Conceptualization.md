# Case Conceptualization
**Details**
When doing therapy, to be able to work out the most effective treatment for the patient, you have to understand who they are, what are their behaviors, rules, and such. Therapies like CBT and DBT did work about which modules and techniques would be most effective for certain patients, but they were never as detailed as conceptualizing a case. As talked about before [[CBT Principles]], we need to update these conceptualizations along the therapy process and as new data and info, and we should also try to confirm the data with the client as well as consider if new data is new data at all or just something we already recognized.
