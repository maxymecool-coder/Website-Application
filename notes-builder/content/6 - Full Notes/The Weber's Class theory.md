# The Weber's Class theory
**Details**
Class is a group of people who have common life chances in the market. Which means they have certain chances of getting certain jobs in a market due to their backgrounds, connections. This class has many forms and a person can be in many "markets." For example, a landlord who abuses his workers, perhaps one farmer who is accusing him and standing up to him may not do much, but a whole group may be formed by all of those farmers to stand up against him and that would make a change, an effort. This is highly likely due to them being in the same "Class" together.

Crucially, Weber also argues that classes are mainly of potential, not actual ones. One can be in a class without realizing the shared interests and the group that it is. For example, UC Berkeley students all share the same interest of keeping the value of their degrees, and so this would create a basis for them to have an Alumni Association, assisting the school to retain its status and by proxy, retain their own statuses.
