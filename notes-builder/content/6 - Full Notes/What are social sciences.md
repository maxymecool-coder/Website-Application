# What are social sciences
**Details**
Its multiple entities, perhaps subjects. Social science divisions usually include departments of anthropology, economics, politics, and sociology. Why do they not ordinarily include as well schools of law, education, business, social service, and public administration, all of which draw on the concepts and methods of the social sciences for their development? The reason commonly given for the separation of these schools from the social science divisions is that the main purpose of such schools is to train for professional work outside of the university, while the previously mentioned departments are more exclusively dedicated to the pursuit of systematic knowledge of human society, an activity that usually goes on within the university.

There is currently a trend in universities toward the establishment of centers and institutes for interdisciplinary studies. Such centers are producing studies and reports that incorporate findings of a dozen or more. Sophistication is required to discern the various strands of these efforts, let alone judge the validity.

What about psychology ? Well it should be regarded as a social science oh the grounds of inseparability of the individual from his social environment. It is also becoming one of most popular subjects among students.

And so so many other fields. So we can define social sciences as subjects that are core like anthropology, economics, politics, and sociology. Though there are other subjects that can fall into this definition as well.
