# Screening for information
**Details**
A necessity in any and all interventions, this helps the therapist to maximize efficiency and minimize patient burden (like personal issues of what we talked about before). Some extreme factors that fully interfere of the program is: risk of self harm and hospitalization, stable housing, commitment, unmanaged bipolar,… and medical issues. Continuing into the therapeutic process, assessments are also needed to ensure a fit between the patient and program as well as to monitor their progress using the PCL5 or something of the sorts.
