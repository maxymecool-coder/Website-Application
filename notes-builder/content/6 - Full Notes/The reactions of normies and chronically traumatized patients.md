# The reactions of norms and chronic traumatized patients
**Details**
The striking difference is the activation of the prefrontal cortex (PFC), it normally helps us to assess the person coming towards us, and our mirror neutrons help to pick up intentions. However, PTSD patients did not do this, they did not imagine, did not mirror their stranger, but what they did was activate areas known as the “Periaqueductal Gray” which generates self-protective behaviors. No social engagement, they just go straight into survival mode.
