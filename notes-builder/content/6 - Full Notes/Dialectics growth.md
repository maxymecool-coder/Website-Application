# Dialectics growth
**Details**
Similar to [[Karl Marx's Dialectical Theory]], Linehan argues the same that in people there is always a thesis and an anti-thesis, a yin and yang, and the intertwining of these two factors can synthesize growth.

Rather than the view that we shut down the contradictions and our flaws, we may be able to foster ourselves by finding the wisdom in contradictions as even those have their own contradictions. Trying to fight or hide our own negative sides may bring more tension and stress rather than bring us catharsis. Upon the synthesis of these factors can we evolve and build upon a solution that is constantly changing.
