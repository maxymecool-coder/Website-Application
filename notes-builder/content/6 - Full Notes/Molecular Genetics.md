# Molecular Genetics
**Details**
Molecular genetics moves beyond estimating heritability to investigate the mechanisms by which genes exert their influence. Its central goal is to identify the specific DNA sequences or genes that are associated with a given trait. The completion of the Human Genome Project provided the fundamental tools for this work. For complex traits, the focus is on finding **Quantitative Trait Loci (QTLs)**, which are multiple genes that each contribute a small, probabilistic effect. This allows science to move from abstract estimates of genetic influence to concrete biological pathways.
