# Therapy as a Whole
**Details**
While there are hundreds of "brands" of therapy, the field is dominated by a few evidence-based modalities. And despite various approaches, the "matching hypothesis" stating how specific therapies can match certain individuals have failed, research show that good therapies are generally good across different disorders. Instead though, the quality of treatment has gotten to the therapeutic alliance being the singular biggest predictor of therapy succeeding or not.
