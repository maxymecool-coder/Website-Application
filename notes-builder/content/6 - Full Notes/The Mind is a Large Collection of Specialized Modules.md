# Evolved mind model
**Details**
Evolutionary psychologists perceive the mind as a system that processes the input from the environment into either data structures or behavioral outputs. 

The second feature of these modules is that they have been chosen by natural selection overtime, like adaptations. They justify this by asserting that these modules are richly structured and complex, meaning that only natural selection could have generated this.

The third and last is that because natural selection is a hill-climbing process which picks out the best traits possible most of the time, and that choices are an immense amount over the vast expanse of time, it causes an accumulation of better functional designs (or just more complex). Though this does not mean perfection of any kind.
