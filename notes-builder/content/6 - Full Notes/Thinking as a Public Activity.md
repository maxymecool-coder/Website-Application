# Thinking as a Public Activity
**Details**
People tend to believe that "thinking" is moving around things in a hidden mental space, in our head basically. However, when we consider writing a problem's solution out on a blackboard, people can all see our thinking process and how we encounter errors as the correction is public.

When we believe that we are doing the same activity in our head, it is still the same thinking, just silent. And so that clearly demonstrates that our private thinking is a derivative of the public thinking rather than the other way around.
