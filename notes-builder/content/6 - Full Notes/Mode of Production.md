# Mode of Production
**Details**
He thought that the force that drove history and humanity forward was the concept of "mode of production" which has two components, forces of production and social relations of production. This concept is how society produces and reproduces things materialistically and economically for it to survive. The forces of production are basically the workers, the labor, the technology that is all involved in a field. The social relations on the other hand are the concepts that keep the forces of production going, the religion that tells people to milk the cow, the law that gives people ownership/permission to farm on their lands,...

The things that are involved in the mode of production and make it work are the classes, which of course can be defined differently from [[The Weber's Class theory]]. In his theory, Marx defined classes to basically be workers and owners, those who owned the land and those who worked on that land.
