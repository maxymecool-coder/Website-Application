# Difference in Genetics
**Details**
A human has 46 chromosomes (23 pairs) and each is made up of hundreds or thousands of genes that contain DNA. Different forms of the same gene are called alleles. These alleles are found at the generally same location. A genetics polymorphism refers to a difference in DNA sequence on a gene that has occurred in a population of a chromosome.

The DNA in genes is transcribed to RNA. In some cases, it turns into amino acids, which then form proteins, and such proteins make cells. These particular types in gene expression are called promoters. These promoters are recognized by proteins that are called transcription factors.

An area of molecular genetics that has been studied extensively the last decade has been single nucleotide polymorphisms or SNPs (pronounced snips). These things are differences between people in a single nucleotide in the DNA sequence in a strand of DNA. Another are of interest between the differences in people in gene structure are copy number variations (CNVs). A CNV can be present in a gene or multiple genes. These are abnormal copies of one or more section of the DNA in genes. Sometimes these CNVs can come in form of extra copies, or less copies than there actually should be.

SNPs contribute to about 90% of the differences of genes in people whilst CNVs make up the other 10%, but do not be fooled, the 10% is very substantial as people with schizophrenia, autism, and ADHD have been found to have CNVs.
