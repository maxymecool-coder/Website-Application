# The effects of trauma
**Details**
Trauma other than its commonly known effects like panic attacks and so on also fundamentally reorganizes how the brain manage perceptions. It affects every aspect of thinking, such as what, how and even the capacity to think. A sexual assaulted teenager may not be able for think for herself whenever engaging in intimate actions, can't stop thinking about the trauma, and in turn preventing further connections. Their own bodies may even respond and be hypervigilant even when in a safe space.

For real change to happen, the body and mind must learn that there is no more danger present and that reality is the only thing that remains and it must live in it to experience happiness.

**Supporting Content**
In other paradigms like the biological, the psychological and the cultural (and others), there are other effects such as shame.
