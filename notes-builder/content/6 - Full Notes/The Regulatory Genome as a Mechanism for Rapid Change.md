# The Regulatory Genome as a Mechanism for Rapid Change
**Details**
The mechanism for punctuated equilibrium also lies in the vast, non-coding regions of DNA (95% of the genome). This is not "junk DNA"; it is the **regulatory genome**, an instruction manual that determines _when_ and _where_ genes are turned on and off. It contains **promoters** and **repressors**—switches that control gene expression. A single mutation in a regulatory region can have a massive impact, not by changing one protein slightly, but by altering the context, timing, or network of entire sets of genes, providing another plausible mechanism for rapid evolutionary jumps.
