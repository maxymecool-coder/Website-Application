# Motoneuron
**Details**
The easiest neuron to describe is this. It provides an efferent way from the CNS through the spinal cord or a cranial nerve, winds up in a muscle fiber and makes that fiber contract. The axon of the motoneuron transmits the nerve impulse initiated at its dendrites to the muscle fiber at its juncture which produces a chemical that makes it contract. Some of these axons may be long, extending from the head to the base of the spinal cord, others from the spinal cord to the limbs.

There are other kinds of efferent control, like the endocrine and digestive glands of the smooth muscle fibers surrounding the digestive tract and blood vessels.
