# Pheromones - Triggers of Sexual Behaviors
**Details**
Existing in both males and females are triggers for sexual behaviors. This goes all the way from natural biochemistry to tactile feels (such as touching erogenous zones and so on). There is quite a difference between how sensitive males and females are to these chemicals, but neither can fully create the reaction without the other (testosterone and estrogen). These chemical signals can convey a vast amount of information, including species, gender, health, reproductive status, emotional state, and even genetic relatedness.

A landmark research was done by studying males who were castrated and females who were ovariectomized. Both types of subjects did not produce pheromones or reacted to it, they could not recognized the other pheromones dispersed in the space. Then they were both injected with their corresponding pheromones and all functions were regained.
