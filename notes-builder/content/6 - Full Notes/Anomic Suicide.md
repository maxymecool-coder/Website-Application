# Anomic Suicide
**Details**
This type of suicide depends on the social regulation, when it drops enormously for example in events like economic depressions and rapid industrialization (rapid worsening and rapid improvements), society loses the sureness of its norms and values for some time. This leaves the individuals without a clear way to live, a clear path to follow in society, suddenly losing their anchors and with that comes the increase in suicide, together with frustrations and disillusionment.

These people who is suddenly losing the normalcy of their lives, the situation that they have been living so happily for some time suddenly flipping on its head, making them directionless, having nowhere to go. This concept causes angriness, towards whatever that has caused them to lose that state, and if it themselves, they will suicide, if it is someone else, they will do something like commit homicide then suicide. The dominating note is one of irritated disgust with a life that has deceived them. This state can range from acute over-excitation to a weary melancholy, but unlike the languorous sadness of the egoist, it is always tinged with frustration and impatience.
