# Hypomanic state
**Details**
Basically the opposite of the depressive. These patients perceive a significant gain in their life experiences. They indiscriminately attribute positive values to their experiences, unrealistically expects favorable results and exaggerate their own abilities. These positive evaluations lead to euphoria. Moreover, the continued bombardment of inflated self-evaluations and overly optimistic expectations energizes and propels him into continuous activity.
