# Offsetting Forgetting
**Details**
People say a lot in their lives that they like to read to learn new things, or that they are a visual learner, an auditory learner, someone who learns better in groups, and so on. But when scientifically drained down, these things stop mattering, and only remembering the information that they are given matters. And science has found out that the most effective way to learn is to access the memory components of the brain, to offset forgetting.
