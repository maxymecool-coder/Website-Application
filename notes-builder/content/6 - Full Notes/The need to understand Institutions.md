# The need to understand Institutions
**Details**
Institutions are concepts that even leaders are unsure about, so for the general population, they would come to be baffled by this even. And so they cling onto whatever they are already used to to try and explain these concepts, like their family, or a sport perhaps. For example, Americans use baseball as a proxy for them to project institutions onto. Looking into it, baseball has everything, teams facing each other, certain rules, morals that one has to follow. When a player gets involved in some kind of scandal, like a betting scandal, we usually feel disappointed, angry at that person as we shame and shun them away, just as we would do to a high-ranking officer of an organization. We know that the ideals of the place and know how to separate the individuals from it.

This does not mean we should necessarily view politics or the economy in this way, because in baseball whilst cheating will always be a shameful act, in the current state of society, doing dirty activities behind companies are most of the time, seen as the norm. Which shows us another difference between baseball and institutions, baseball have timeless rules, institutions constantly change.
