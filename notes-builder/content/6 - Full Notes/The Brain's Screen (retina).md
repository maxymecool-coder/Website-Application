# The Brain's Screen (retina)
**Details**
The retina is the light-sensitive layer of neural tissue at the very back of the eyeball, acting as the biological screen upon which focused light falls. It is here that the most magical step in vision occurs: **transduction**. This is the critical process of converting the physical energy of light into the electrochemical language of neurons, a signal the brain can finally process. This conversion is performed by millions of specialized photoreceptor cells, which are divided into two distinct types: rods and cones.
