# Multi-Systemic Therapy (MST)
**Details**
Before this existed, there was no effective way anyone could treat people who had severe ADHD, CD,... And so psychologists saw a need for a method to deal with said problems and developed well... this. Individual talk therapy or group therapy have been relatively ineffective, sometimes outright backfiring as the teenagers copy each other's bad behaviors. Pharmacology also lacks a "magic bullet"; while stimulants help with comorbid ADHD, they may simply allow a "pure bully" to be more calculated, and antipsychotics or mood stabilizers often come with sedating side effects.

As evidence has shown, MST is the most effective treatment with a reduction to long-term delinquency rates of 30-60%. It is quite rigorous as dealing with these disorders require the person's environment to be monitored and intervened 24/7 for 6 to 12 months, in which the same time the family has to be caring and empathetic and enduring as well.
