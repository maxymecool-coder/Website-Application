# Space and Time, Intuitions
**Details**
Space and time are not physical realities we discover through sensory experience, nor are they mere concepts derived from rational deduction. Instead, they are pure intuitions, meaning they are the foundational, a priori frameworks that human consciousness imposes upon the world. Kant uses the concept of incongruent counterparts, such as chiral objects like right and left hands, to prove this. A mirrored hand maintains all internal rational logic, yet cannot fit into the opposite glove, proving that spatial orientation relies on an inherent aesthetic intuition rather than pure deduction. Space acts as the necessary precondition for organizing our external reality, while time provides the framework for ordering our internal mental states. Otherwise, other explanations for this would be us crafting everything from prior experiences, which neurologically might mean that we will not live long enough for this way of processing.
