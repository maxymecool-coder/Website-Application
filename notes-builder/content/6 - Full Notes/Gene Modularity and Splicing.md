# Gene Modularity and Splicing
**Details**
Genes are not a constant form of information but rather a modular consisting of two parts:

- **Exons:** Coding sequences that contain instructions for a protein.
    
- **Introns:** Non-coding sequences that interrupt the exons.

There is a phenomenon called splicing where enzymes take out the introns and instead stick the exons together, creating a final, functional blueprint. This change would allow exons to be spliced and combined together to create numerous different proteins structural wise, allowing for a ginormous change rather than just gradual steps.
