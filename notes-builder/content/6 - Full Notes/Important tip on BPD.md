# Important tip on BPD
**Details**
Distracting one self may not be the permanent solution to a problem but it is a way one can gather their mind and such to then deal and go through it. BPD individuals suffer from this as they usually seek to end it all as the pain is too much rather than go through the process and survive, they themselves think they won’t survive through the process.
