# On reading imaginative literature
**Details**
Fiction, as far as difficulty, is way more difficult than non-fiction, as well as more confusing. Although saying that it is difficult, many people claim to possess the ability to read fiction effectively. This is because people deceive themselves from their true ability, they usually cannot state what they truly enjoyed about the book, only knowing that it was “a good book”. This doesn’t mean they are a good reader and a bad critic, usually they cannot criticize a book because they haven’t read it deeply enough. Moreover, it doesn’t help that imaginative literature tends to please more than teach, and it is much easier to be pleased than taught, but harder to know why one is pleased. As written: “Beauty is harder to analyze than truth”.
