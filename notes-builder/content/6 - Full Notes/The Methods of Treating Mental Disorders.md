# The Methods of Treating Mental Disorders
**Details**
There are mainly two methods of treating people with mental illness, which are biomedical and psychological methods. The biomedical methods consists of medications, electrical convulsive therapy, and so on. After the application of these methods, perhaps the symptoms of the mental illness will come down. This method has many problems though, ranging from the effectiveness of the treatment to the additional side effects of it, like increased resistance to the medications. Many psychologists have argued that it only temporarily solves the problems only, because it is not solving the causes under the problems, the person's abilities and their "self."

Now to counteract these drawbacks, it is proposed to include psychotherapy to alleviate them, as it deals with the underlying problems and teaches the patient to build skills of their own to manage themselves. This approach to treatment consists of many methods, a few are: psychoanalysis, cognitive therapy, behavioral therapy, somatic experiencing, and so on.
