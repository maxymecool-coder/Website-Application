# The Formalization of Child Maltreatment
**Details**
For much of history, how parents treat their children has largely been a private matter rather than a public one. More often than not, children would be abuse and such and be without a way to escape or get out as it is considered a family matter. The shift only happened recently in 1962, when radiologists, rather than psychologists, noticed that kids coming into the ER had broken bones that could not be explained by mere falls [[Developmental Psychopathology]]. And with that came the publishment of the "battered child syndrome", acknowledging the potential for child abuse and its effects.

Since the work's inception, laws have been implemented to protect these children from being abused, as that can clearly turn to [[Disorganized Attachment]]. Not only that, jobs including teachers, doctors, therapists are lawfully obligated to report SUSPICIONS of child abuse to authorities for them to take into account, even if they are not sure. This would of course, allow false positives to happen (parents are not the reason) in turn for false negatives (failing to save an abused child). Psychologists feared that this may push away the families who needs the therapy the most, but surveys and statistics shows that this would benefit the children much more as there are less cases of them dying from abuse, which is above any and alliances. Some may also not act because of the personal relationships and such that they have developed with the family, nonetheless, this prevents further spiraling and development of the generational trauma, neurological, mental disorders and of course, biological injuries (like being threw against a FUCKING WALL).
