# Freud and hysterics
**Details**
When Freud was a researcher, he demonstrated that patients who suffered from disabilities but evidenced no obvious impairment - were regarded as malingerers, are actually suffering because of their mind’s ideas of their inabilities.

Freud and Breuer wrote separate theoretical chapters, suggesting hypotheses on why some experiences (particularly of intense feelings) are usually dissociated from the rest of the mind. Breuer said that it was because it took place during altered states of consciousness. Freud on the other hand argued that it is because the content of such memories and feelings was disturbing, unacceptable and conflict with the rest of the other memories (groundwork for PTSD).
