# Validating and Empathizing
**Details**
- This step emphasizes on not having to actually agree with your partner but just understanding why they would think that way, recognize that they are not you but a person of their own and that you are not losing yourself by validating their POV.  Empathizing is basically just reinstating your understanding and saying the things that they are trying to get you to say at that moment.
