# Perspectives and Moods
**Details**
The cognitive framing we use when approaching a task significantly impacts our ability to perform it. There are always multiple, often opposite, perspectives from which to view any given problem or obligation. Merely taking a moment to consciously change that perspective can result in a dramatically better mood and provide more encouragement to do the work. For example, changing the internal narrative from "I have to wake up early to work out" to "I get to wake up early and build my physical strength" shifts the task from a burden to an opportunity. This simple cognitive reframe can be enough to overcome initial resistance.
