# Immersion
**Details**
Thinking back to when someone is a baby or child, they are surrounded by language, by actions, and so on. That was the stepping stone and an overall effective method for us all that can be applied to many aspects of life. We can immerse in so many aspects or all of it. It is just that the degree of immersion we have to do is important. May it be learning another language or doing a job, immersion can all help us in mastering these skills. This aspect can be mixed up in all kinds of different methods.
