# Game Theory and the Prisoner's Dilemma
**Details**
The dynamics of reciprocal altruism can be mathematically modeled using **game theory**, particularly the **Prisoner's Dilemma**. In this scenario, two individuals can either "Cooperate" or "Cheat" (Defect). The payoffs are structured such that:

1. **Temptation (You Cheat, They Cooperate):** The highest payoff.
    
2. **Reward (You Both Cooperate):** The second-highest payoff.
    
3. **Punishment (You Both Cheat):** A low payoff (a loss).
    
4. **Sucker (You Cooperate, They Cheat):** The worst possible payoff.
    

While cheating on a cooperator gives the best single-round outcome, if both players do this, they end up with a poor result. This model formalizes the central conflict of reciprocal altruism and allows for the testing of different behavioral strategies to find the optimal solution.
