# Transgressions
**Details**
This is where anger can stem from. The main character who experiences or subjected to an unpleasant experience by one or more adversaries. He is the object of deliberate physical attack, criticism, coercion, thwarting, rejection, deprivation, or opposition. These situations are noxious as they encroach on his personal domain. Even if not stemming from malice, it will most likely be perceived as such by the protagonist.

Another is the preservation of commands and restrictions which the individual also perceives as encroaching on his rights. For example, a restriction by a person of authority may make someone angry though he had no prior desire to engage in the forbidden activity. His "rights" may include not only autonomy, but also expectations or respect, courtesy, consideration and loyalty from other people. Social or professional status may prompt the expectation of special privileges and may cause offense if one is not accorded, or anger if a person of lower status tries to claim benefits in which they are not entitled to.
