# Difference in Philosophies of Theism and Atheism
**Details**
As discussed thoroughly through many philosophies, it is quite obvious how theists and atheists hold different beliefs and assumptions about the world and life itself. And so it is no surprise that survey data would prove that they also engage in different paradigms of philosophy as well. Theists are significantly more likely to endorse substance dualism and libertarian free will, while atheists skew heavily toward physicalism and compatibilism. This makes sense as those who believe in God would more likely believe in "brain stuff" and dualism which is dominated by Descartes and so on whilst the atheists would necessitate explaining human agency within the bounds of physical laws, leading to compatibilism
