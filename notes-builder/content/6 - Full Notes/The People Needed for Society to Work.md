# The People Needed for Society to Work
**Details**
This is another point for socialization, from a view of functionalism no less. The functionalist view states that society is like a well-oiled machine, or a complex organism, it needs its parts to work effectively for it to survive and thrive. And so one may question how does it get people to become the individuals that would make these parts work ? The answer is socialization. 

From the moment we are born, society already has cultivated inherent values that would allow a person to succeed, it rewards those characteristics and encourages them through the systems that one has to their names, their family, the school, their relationships. We would turn from doing and achieving such characteristics for the sake of our systems into for the sake of ourselves, as a way for us to achieve what we want, what we desire.
