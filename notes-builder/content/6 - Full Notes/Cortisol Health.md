# Morning Tool
**Details**
When we wake up in the morning, be it 10 AM or 6.30 AM to 8.30 AM, there is a substance, aka Cortisol that helps us heat up and is one of the factors of why we wake up. Now, cortisol (as mentioned a lot in my writing) is generally considered a stress hormone, meaning it makes us feel stressed and puts a strain on our mental and biology and so on. This is still true, the thing is, it can also be helpful as it improves the immune system, as well as your concentration; only at the right time though, and that time is, of course, the morning. You would want this peak arrives early in the day, not any other time, not noon, afternoon, or night.
