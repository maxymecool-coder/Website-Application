# Exaggeration of external demands
**Details**
Many patients are overwhelmed by the every problems that they have with living. Before depression, they may have been able to fulfill certain responsibilities that have now become impossible burdens. Some patients may feel so crushed by these burdens that they may turn to suicide. When this problem is talked about with the therapist however, we can see that it is greatly exaggerated. Through rational exploration, like the methods that we have talked about above, the patient may regain his perspective and then set on about defining what has to be done and how to go about it. The therapist would of course gently guide the patient through this process.

Since the implementation of the plan may be blocked by self-defeating thoughts, cognitive rehearsal is a method that should be used to elicit such thoughts and dealt with before applying the plan.
