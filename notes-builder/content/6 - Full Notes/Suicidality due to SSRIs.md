# Suicidality in Youth
**Details**
It feels like quite a contradiction as SSRIs are medication that is tailored to depression or mental disorders in general. Even though its effects are notable, being 15% higher than placebos, it also has a risk factor to it: increasing the suicidal ideation by about 4-5% compared to placebo. According to the "activation hypothesis", medication restores energy for the patient who takes it, even when they are still experiencing hopelessness and major depressive symptoms before it improves mood. And so when the depressed patient has access to the medication, they are finally able to go commit suicide or something along those lines. This was a huge problem with Prozac, an antidepressant that was widely used though the main side effect (this) was hid away by the company due to concerns that it would affect sales. TADS study suggests that the combination of medication and CBT is the gold standard, offering the fastest symptom relief while protecting against suicidality through therapy.

**Supporting Content**
But again, completely removing the medication doesn't help. This has happened once in the history of America, and the result was an immense 75% increase in the suicide rates of teenage girls, perhaps this is because their depression goes untreated from the [[The Triple Bind Hypothesis]].
