# The indirectness of genes
**Details**
Additionally to [[Molecular Genetics]], genes do not necessarily induce behaviors, but rather the factors that lead to those behaviors and how others react to us. For example, "heritability of extroversion" which of course is involved in personality studies, but sometimes, the extroversion is caused by a more beautiful face, physical features that make others respond more nicely and positively to those people. This is a far more subtle and powerful model: genes don't determine our destiny, but they may influence the path we walk and how others react to us along the way.
