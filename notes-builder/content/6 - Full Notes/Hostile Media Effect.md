# Hostile Media Effect
**Details**
This is extremely relevant to the subjective construct that people make. Back in the Palestine Israeli conflict in the 2000s, people who were on either side were ask to judge if a news outlet was being unbiased or not. People who weren't on either side judged the news to be neutral but those who were on either side thought the opposite, they would think the news was against them and their groups. Furthermore, they would even say that the news are actively what people against them would say/be like.

These individuals were clearly exhibiting subjective constructivism in how they believed that they were the most neutral individuals possible and that it was just a coincidence that the side they chose were right. You understand where this is going right ? And so when they were to interpret those who were exhibiting more pro Israeli or pro Palestine, they would say those individuals were the bias ones, not themselves.

Perhaps this phenomenon is due to how being "neutral" makes you and your arguments perhaps more convincing.
