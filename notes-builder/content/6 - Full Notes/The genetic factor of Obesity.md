# The Factors of Obesity
**Details**
Obesity in the modern day is extremely common, and it has also been thoroughly researched. Due to this, we can separate the factors of obesity into categories like bodily, genetic, and behavioral.

Firstly, most of us take for granted that our body weight is a simple function of caloric intake and energy expenditure. More than that, constitutional factors may predispose one person to get fat even if he exercises more and eat less than his neighbor. This can just be how a specific body of a person functions rather than anything related to the stuff mentioned.

Next is genetics, this is the reason for the person's body making them fatter than the other despite a lot of diets and so on. Evidence from identical twins (the normal method) have shown this. In addition though, one specific kind of severe obesity seems to come from a defect in a gene that regulates the manufacture of leptin - the chemical that announces that it is no longer needed to eat.
