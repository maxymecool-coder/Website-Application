# Reconstructing the Inner Map
**Details**
Trauma patients usually instinctively blame themselves for everything bad that happens to the people around them. They know that it isn't rational but they would do it anyways. So when telling them to become more reasonable, to talk them into doing so, it only makes them feel lonelier and isolated, it confirms that no one would be able to understand how it feels to be them. And so the solution is to help them reconstruct their inner map.

To solve this we need to find a way to make them accept that the trauma was not their fault and not caused by their own defects, and no one could ever have deserved what happened to them.
