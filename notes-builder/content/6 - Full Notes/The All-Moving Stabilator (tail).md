# The All-Moving Stabilator (tail)
**Details**
Due to moving from subsonic to supersonic flight, planes needed a fundamental change in tail design as they were fixed and would make a phenomenon known as the “Mach tuck” to happen, where the aircraft would nosedive uncontrollably. The solution found was to pivot the entire horizontal tail surface.
