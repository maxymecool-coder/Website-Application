# Psychoses
**Details**
This subject is incredibly complicated, though it might be useful for us to compare it with that of neuroses. The ideational themes of psychotic depression are analogous to those of neurotic depression. Paranoia or paranoid schizophrenia shows a similar content to that of paranoid states. The manic to the hypomanic. The content of psychoses though is even more bizarre, grotesque, and extreme than that of the neuroses. For example, a neurotic depressive may view himself as being socially inadequate, whilst the psychotic depressive may believe he emits disgusting odors that alienates others.

This shows us the pronounced level of cognitive impairment that psychoses have over neuroses. the perseverative ideation is more intense and less subject to modification through corrective experience (therapy).
