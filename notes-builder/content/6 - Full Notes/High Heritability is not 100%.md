# High Heritability is not 100%
**Details**
A common view is that high heritability means that it would be fixed, unchangeable which is completely incorrect as proven by PKU or Phenylketonuria. PKU is a metabolic disorder that is **100% heritable**. It is a simple Mendelian condition caused by inheriting two recessive genes, one from each carrier parent. An individual with this "double recessive" genotype cannot produce the enzyme needed to metabolize an amino acid called phenylalanine.

In cases without intervention, the toxic chemicals build up in the brain and impairs the cognitive functions, effectively reducing the patient's IQ. But in cases where it is taken into account and dealt with from a young age by intervening with medications, and a phenylalanine-restricted diet, which helps the child's brain develop normally, their average IQ returning to above/around 100.

Even if high heritability is present in a trait/condition, that does not mean that it is completely unavoidable. It only describes the variation of a population within its given environments, when introduced with interventions, it can be prevented.
