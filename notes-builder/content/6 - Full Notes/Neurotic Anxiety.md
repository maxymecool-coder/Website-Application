# Neurotic Anxiety
**Details**
This is a theory developed by Freud that neurotic anxiety is caused by the perception of an internal danger being aroused and possibly coming to the light. It is the fear of what would happen if the forces of repression fail to prevent this event from happening.

**Supporting Content**
It is heavily still in debate, and there has been paradoxes to it such as: "If "anxiety" is a signal that repressed instinctual forces have begun to erupt...why should the alarm burn down the house ?" which is referring to why the supposed cure of anxiety destroying the well-being of the person experiencing it more than the anxiety itself.
