# Dissociations with Memories
**Details**
These disorders have had us questioning about how memory works under stress. The psychodynamic theory suggests that trauma is repressed by these dissociations. Cognitive psychologists question this due to evidence proving that stress actually enhances memory of trauma rather than impairs it. Explanations have come out in the form of memories being locked up and distorted to protect the person from thinking of such a painful event. Of course this regaining of the memory can prove to be maladaptive due to some actually never existing but was brought out nonetheless, causing incorrect evidence.
