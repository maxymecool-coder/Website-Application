# Semantics and Symbols
**Details**
According to Searle, the fundamental misconception that the computer model of mind is the confusion between Syntaxes and Semantics. They think that just because the computer have and is able to move syntaxes around in its internal system that means that it understands them. The understanding of these symbols is semantics.

Just as a calculator can multiply "12 x 3" by flipping binary switches without knowing what the number "12" is, a computer can process the word "Apple" without knowing what an apple is. Functionalism tries to build a mind entirely out of syntax (rules and shapes), but Searle argues that "Syntax is not sufficient for Semantics." No amount of symbol shuffling will ever add up to the conscious experience of meaning. Therefore, the scientific study of the mind based on the computer analogy is fundamentally mistaken because it ignores the semantic content that defines human thought.
