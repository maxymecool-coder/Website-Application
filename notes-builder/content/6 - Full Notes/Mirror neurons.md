# Mirror neurons
**Details**
Mirror neurons fuel our ability to "feel into" someone else. It is the base for many functions of the human mind that are fairly unexplainable such as empathy, imitation, synchrony and even the development of language. We pick all these up not only from another person's movement but their emotional state and intentions as well. This means that people who are mirroring each other have a tendency to mirror how they walk, sit, the rhythm of speaking, and so on. But also, they will mirror each other's negativity, which can have a bad effect on both. Trauma invariably involves not being seen, or mirrored and treatment needs to reactivate the capacity to mirror, be mirrored but also protecting one's self from others negativity.
