# Spatial Resolution
**Details**
This is the "where" of brain research. Basically, since the brain contains billions of neurons, we need to try to identify which part/clump of the neurons are doing a specific job or function. Since neurons are so small, these parts can also be very small (only a few millimeters). Different methods can yield high or low spatial resolution, meaning it can zoom into a few millimeters or a few centimeters, the latter only helping us recognize the general location like if it is happening at the front or back of the brain, not a specific part.
