# Goldilocks Zone
**Details**
When trying to improve one’s skills at a particular field, we usually either do tasks too simple and learn nothing from said tasks due to the repetition in mistakes, as we do not learn anything new and only practice what is already known. On the other hand, practicing problems way over our skill level may have the same effect though just in a different light: we would suffer from not learning anything due to the radical difficulty rather. So the optimal zone would be somewhere at the border of the difficulty line, just hard enough so that we have to genuinely stop and think about the problem so that it would give us insight into our abilities but not hard enough for us to not learn anything from it.
