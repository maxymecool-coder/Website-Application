# The Checkpoint in Horror
**Details**
Ever since its start in the 1990s, Scream has become a landmark in the horror genre, with every film being released showing the state of the genre at the period of time that it has been released. Not only that but it has also been one of the longest-running horror series ever, which is quite rare for the genre.

The original first 4 films were all directed by Wes Craven and written by Kevin Williamson, their work cultivating in some of the best slashers from the 1990s through 2010s. Wes Craven in particular is an icon of the series, he's revolutionized the genre so many times through his movies, including The Last House on The Left, Nightmare on Elm Street, and Scream. He always understood and loved the genre, and stays an icon even after his passing in 2015.

Moving onto the 2020s, there finally came somebody, well actually multiple people, who were chosen to continue the legacy of Scream - Radio Silence - a horror film making group consisting of 3 friends who made Ready or Not. With the aesthetic that Ready or Not had, it was simple to see how Radio Silence would fit into directing Scream, even though it was big shoes to fill for such an icon in the genre. They took it by heart and directed the series as fans first, filmmakers second, and the result was fitting for a Scream movie. The series has been renewed and more sequels are coming on the way, Kevin Williamson is back as an executive producer as a spiritual guardian for the series.
