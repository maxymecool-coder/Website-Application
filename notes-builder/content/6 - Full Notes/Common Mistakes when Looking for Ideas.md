# Common Mistakes when Looking for Ideas
**Details**
One is made by those who want to keep track of every single interesting idea and quote that they come by. Of course the clear advantage of this is that they most likely will never miss an idea, but the disadvantage is that these notes will never build up to "critical mass". Because truly good ideas will be diluted by all the other ones, which may be only relevant for specific projects or aren't even that good at a second glance.

Secondly is the opposite, collecting notes related to certain projects. At first thought, it makes absolute sense, you're only looking for the things that you are more than confident will help you to do the task at hand... but when that task is done you will lose all the lines of thought in those notes.

Lastly, of course, is treating all notes like fleeting ones. This is evident by the large amounts of rough notes that one has, as it continuously builds and builds, resulting in the wish of starting from scratch.

What these approaches all have in common is that the values of notetaking decreases with the number of notes. It should be the opposite, the more one learns and collects, the more beneficial these notes will become, the more ideas can mingle and birth to new ones, with less efforts.
