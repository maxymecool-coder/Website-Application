# On disagreements and agreements
**Details**
When having read a piece of work analytically and thoroughly, the readers can be expected to be on the same level of knowledge and understanding with the author. This allows them to be able to meet each other and in the end reach significant disagreement or agreement with each other.

There is one particular case where the readers do not understand is of the faults of the author for making such a book (though this usually does not happen). If this happens then the reader must verify it, find the sources of trouble, the disorderly structure or the illogicality of said book. Until he can do that then he has no further obligation to participate in the book.

There is one point that the reader should be warned of, and that is that understanding isn’t agreeing. Even when a reader fully understands a book, he can criticize if it is true or not depending on the context of daily lives, of their examples, evidences that are given.
