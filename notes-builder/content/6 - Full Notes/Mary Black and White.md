# Mary Black and White
**Details**
Mary is a genius, a genius who lives in a black and white room and is forced to see and understand everything through a black and white monitor. Even though that is the case, she is able to understand and conceive everything through said monitor, every piece of knowledge about the physiology of vision, of color. She has the "physical story" of the world.

If physicalism were true, if Mary were to leave said room and be able to see color for the first time, she wouldn't be able to know anything new, because she has already learnt everything there is to learn. But it is seemingly undeniable that she will learn something new: how it experiences to see redness. And so, that proves that physicalism is false.
