# Explanation for the sheer depth of Stigma
**Details**
With how stigma of mental illness travels from cultural to public and so on, people have come up with possible theories for this. Stemming from a combination of lack of knowledge, prejudicial attitudes and the discrimination against PWLEs, this is the main force that is against them. The social cognitive model attests to this, it endorses how these stigmas are breed from negative stereotypes, leading to prejudice if people keep endorsing them. Specifically in public and self-stigma, as public may view PWLEs as dangerous as they do not know what that actually means, which evokes fear, while PWLEs might view themselves as being incompetent and evokes low self-esteem.

To explain the learning of these stereotypical ideas against PWLEs, we must take a view into the modified labelling theory, which emphasizes how public stigma connects to self-stigmatization, with the explanation that they do this from learning from public stigma from a young age. Finally, the fact that these values exist is because there is identification and separation between the differences between people, and so stereotyping stemming from dominant cultural beliefs.

The reasons that they attach onto these people are because of how people value certain characteristics from their ideology, as it emphasis on good moral character, hard work, self-control, maintaining social order and harmony, which are the opposite of the behavioral symptoms of mental disorders. People believe that their symptoms are just their bad behaviors, them being bad people, not that its an illness.
