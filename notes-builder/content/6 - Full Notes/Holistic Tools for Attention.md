# Holistic Tools for Attention
**Details**
Additionally to sleep (which is one of the defining necessities for memory), meditation and affirmations can also be of much use. Research indicates that a mere 10-12 minute guided meditation period through an 8-week period can yield significant impacts in cognitive performance and stress reduction. The reason this is so effective is meditation forces the brain to think about the moment instead of rumination of the past or fearful thinking of the future.

Another is affirmations, which is basically basically cognitive reframing as we train ourselves to think a certain way about ourselves, more positively. It can be combined with exercising to combine the benefits of the two.
