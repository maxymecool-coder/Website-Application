# Domination Norms among the poor
**Details**
Contrary to the belief that out-of-wedlock births among the poor result from a rejection of mainstream values, the research in _Promises I Can Keep_ by Edin and Kefalas shows the opposite. The behavior of poor young women is driven by their strong adherence to dominant American norms of responsibility, purpose, and marriage. These classes tend to have higher chances of having children and being unmarried because of their particular views on these ideals.

They believe that having a children is the symbol of women, they think that it will make them responsible, show that they have it and furthermore, not considering how much work and knowledge they actually need to take care of one. They are not married because they hold it in such a high regard that they will refuse to do so under completely optimal situations, even if they do not have anyone to help them, especially the father of their children.
