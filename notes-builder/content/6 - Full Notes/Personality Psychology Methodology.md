# Personality Psychology Methodology
**Details**
There are two ways for us to approach it, called the nomothetic and the idiographic approaches. **Nomothetic** meaning "legislator," and the approach seeks general laws about various aspects of personality based on studies of groups of people.

Idiographic on the other hand is of the individual, it intensively studies individuals. Now this method is largely overlooked due to the little value that it actually gives us in terms of the field.

In trying to find out the nomothetic traits of the human being, psychologists have gone far and wide to collect data and narrow the words of the English dictionary down to just a few that describe people.
