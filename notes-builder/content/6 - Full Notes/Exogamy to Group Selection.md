# Exogamy to Group Selection
**Details**
The social structure created by **female exogamy** provides a direct pathway for **group selection** to occur. When females leave their birth troop, the resident males are all highly related (brothers, cousins). This kin structure fosters intense cooperation and transforms the entire group of males into a cohesive, coordinated unit.

This "band of brothers" now has a **collective trait**: organized, lethal inter-group aggression (warfare). Evolution can now act on this group-level trait. When one such chimp group encounters another, the conflict is not between individuals, but between two organized armies. The group with the more effective collective strategy (better coordination, more aggression) will win, eradicating the other group and taking their territory and resources.

This is a modern, viable model of group selection: selection is not acting "for the good of the species," but on the relative fitness of one group versus another, based on an emergent, collective behavior that is itself built upon a foundation of kin selection.
