# Heuristics as Rules of Thumb
**Details**
William James Sidis has talked about how the world is a "messy, buzzing place." And so because of that, and because we also want to make sense of things, connect, and make sense of everything, we develop heuristics to be able to make mental shortcuts and function finely in our lives. This can be exemplified by the rule of thumb: in medieval times, kingdoms measured inches by the distance from the king's knuckle to his thumb-tip. Within one kingdom, this worked flawlessly, but when other kings came, it would fail because other kings would yield different inches.
