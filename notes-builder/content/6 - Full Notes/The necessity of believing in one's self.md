# The necessity of believing in one's self
**Details**
As talked about before, people can block themselves from improving upon a certain skill because they are convinced that it is fixed or that they are inherently bad at it. This, combining with the fact that we create narratives also prevents us from playing and exploring our ability at something.

Each of us has a large basket of resources depending on the situation that we find ourselves in like our social standings, our financial situation, but at the end of the day it shapes how we learn and how we overcome our shortcomings. Though that is the case, on any list of what matters most for learning, language fluency and reading ability will always be somewhere near the top.
