# The problems of Identical Twins study
**Details**
Researchers came to studying identical twins (100%) and fraternal twins (50%) to figure out the differences between the twins. If the fraternal twins were not identical in this one thing and the identical twins were, then that difference can be attributed to the extra 50% of gene similarity they had. It may sound logical at first, but what researchers did not account for was the fact that identical twins get treated like a singular unit, they are treated hyper-similarly, not equally to the fraternal. And since the environment the fraternal twins are treated in are different than the identical, we cannot attribute the difference to the genes.

People tried to turn to adoption studies, seeing the effects that the biological parents had even when the children are taken care by adoptive ones. There were flaws in this one too, due to the prenatal environment (the womb) being crucial to the development of the child as well as the fact that adoption centers try to look for adoptive parents with similarities to the biological ones. And so in these studies, the environment is also contaminated.
