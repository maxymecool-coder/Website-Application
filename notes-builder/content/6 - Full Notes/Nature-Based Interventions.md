# Nature-Based Interventions
**Details**
This is a type of intervention that focuses on the mental wellbeing rather than the symptom reduction, allowing the person access to psychoeducation and understanding the method, which actually encourages people to work on their mental wellness.

Recently, a trial pilot examining the "Roots and Shoots" NBI saw how truly effective this method was. Including all kinds of mindfulness activities such as psychoeducation, habit wheel, coping tool, honeycomb challenge, mood and nature connection tracking, expressive writing, nature videos, meditations and breathing exercises), users were found to all find them easy to do and quite effective as they saw improvement in their wellbeing.

Most of the results were medium-sized (in terms of Hedge's g), larger ones were seen in relative increases in mindfulness, stress reduction and increased nature connection (g = 0.7; -0.67; 0.61). Sleep disturbance change was small (g= -0.17). The average scores on PHQ8 and GAD7 decreased so much that they moved from clinical caseness to non-caseness after 4 weeks. Not only that but the completion rate was surprisingly high, with 88.2% of participants with more than 50% of the 4 week period.

**Supporting Content**
These effects were similar to those found in previous full trial research like self-help CBT.
Also this could be integrated in therapy as another step for it or additional to the traditional psychotherapies.
