# Utility in Economics
**Details**
Utility is the mathematical presentation of preferences. If indifference curves are the graphical map, the utility function is the algebraic form of said map. But this form only has meaning in the ordinal sense, because it is merely measuring the preferences of people between things, not exactly about the psychological tendencies or moral implications behind it so one should not define it as such and use it as such (meaning a tool of morality and psychology).
