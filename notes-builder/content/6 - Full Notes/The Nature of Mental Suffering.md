# The Nature of Mental Suffering
**Details**
In our lives, even if we achieve society’s standards of “the best life” it does not mean that we will be fully happy or not be mentally stressed or ill. In fact, it is because society that often these signs are hidden and held back. The reasons can vary anywhere from Eastern’s stigmas to Western’s ignorance due to them having medication.

The current Western society still views mental health like physical health as if it can be fixed just by having rest days or medication, rather than therapy treatment. This shows in how the statistics are with millions of alcoholics, tens of thousands of suicides (and even more failed attempts). Just because someone does not have present symptoms of mental illnesses does not mean they do not have it. The nature of these illnesses are incredibly hidden and practically unable to be seen normally.
