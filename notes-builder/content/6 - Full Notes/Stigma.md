# Stigma
**Details**
Stigmas refers to the destructive beliefs and attitudes held by a society against some groups considered different to the norm is some way, which are characterized by the four characteristics below. The assumptions that people infer from people who are stigmatized are usually incorrect and very hurtful to such individuals.

Stigmas derive from labels, which are behaviors unusual from the norm of society. It starts with the label being applied onto somebody with irregular behaviors, which are then described as undesirable attributes that makes the person different, then they are distanced from other groups and shunned away resulting in a stigma. In fact, mental illness is one of the most stigmatized concepts in the world.

Of course these things may be reduced and get rid of but it requires communal acknowledgement and such large concepts. Despite what people think, being educated in mental illness and talking more about stigmas more does not mean that it will reduce. Things that are required may be discriminatory laws, employment, decriminalization , and so on.
