# Elaboration
**Details**
This is the best-researched and most successful learning method. It is very similar to what the Zettelkasten promotes - writing smart notes and combining them with others, which is the opposite of reviewing. So in short, this method is just thinking about the meaning of what is being read, and how it could inform different questions and topics and how it could be combined with other knowledge.

The reason why the slip-box is so efficient in this is because it helps unload the brain of certain tasks, like connecting, detailing and it is a long-term memory resource that keeps information objectively unaltered. And so the brain can focus on getting the gist, deepening its understanding and seeing the bigger picture, then it can be more creative.

But there is a caveat, it is not the best choice for learning isolated facts and information as it promotes connection.
