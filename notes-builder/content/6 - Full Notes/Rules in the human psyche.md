# Rules in the human psyche
**Details**
The way in which people act in many situations are similar in certain important aspects. This may become is predictable we put a tag on them essentially defining what or who they are. 

These observations suggest that someone has a set of general rules to guide how they act in such situations, and not only externally but also internally. It also provides him the standards by which he judges his actions, his worth and attractiveness. It is involved in certainly almost all aspects of his life like: understanding the situations, understanding people, instructing, leading, and so on. It is also crucial to decipher which someone is being offensive to us or we are to them. 

Their origins derive from childhood most likely, as they participate in social situations, they observe and adapt it through seeing other people and their behavior in those situations.
