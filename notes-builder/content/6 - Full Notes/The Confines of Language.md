# The Confines of Language
**Details**
Language cannot allow someone to explain the feeling of not being oneself. It is primarily used to share “things out there”, not to communicate inner feelings. 

And to solve this, we can engage in the self-observing system, which speaks through sensations, tone of voice, body tensions. Being able to perceive these sensations is the very foundation of emotional awareness. So when someone expresses their trauma, therapists would ask them what happened inside when their patients told them about it, where it is registered in their body. Only when they listen to the heartbreak and pain their body and mind are exerting can things begin to change.
