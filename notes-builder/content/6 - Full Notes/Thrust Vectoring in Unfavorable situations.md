# Thrust Vectoring in Unfavorable situations
**Details**
Fighter jets may need to enter spaces where they may not have the capability to maneuver in like high altitudes where the air density is insufficient for wings and rudders to “grip” the air. Similarly, the same thing happens during high angle-of-attack (high alpha) maneuvers, the fuselage may block airflow to the tail, or the speed may be too low to generate lift. In these scenarios, thrust vectoring from the 2 engines, each carrying 30000 pounds of thrust, and so the aircraft can pitch and yaw using pure force rather than aerodynamics.
