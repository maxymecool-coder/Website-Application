# Trichromatic Theory
**Details**
This theory, coined by Thomas Young, states that our receptors responds to three primary colors, or in current day terms, three types of cones. The three types of cones includes one that is most sensitive to short wavelengths (blue), medium wavelengths (green), and another to long wavelengths (red). These wavelengths prompts varying levels of activity. And of course it is clear that we can mix these wavelengths to create certain colors.

Additional to this, colorblind people have genetics disfunctions that make them unable to see one of these cones, effectively losing their ability to view certain colors.
