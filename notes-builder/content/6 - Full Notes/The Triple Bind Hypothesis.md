# The Triple Bind Hypothesis
**Details**
The "Triple Bind" is a framework that explains the increase in suicidality and internalizing disorders among girls recently. It posits that girls in the modern society have to withstand pressure from three converging yet impossible factors. Not only do they have to possess the warmth and empathy of a woman normally but they also have to adhere to the competitiveness and academics of men as well. In addition to all of that, they somehow also has to look perfect and effortlessly beautiful all in all. This is inherently impossible and forces leads them to internalizing their problems and expressing it to themselves.
