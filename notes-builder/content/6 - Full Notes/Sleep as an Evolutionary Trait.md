# Sleep as an Evolutionary Trait
**Details**
EPs argued that back in the earlier stages of human evolution, or the earlier times, we had to find a way to avoid predators, since in the night some of them would be superior to us and we wouldn't have a way to find food and so on and so forth. So to combat this, some of the human species developed sleep, a way to conserve our energy and deal with the problem of predators as well. Others that did not adopt to this died off, leaving only the ones who could sleep to go on.

Of course, this theory is also not fully proven, but the thing is, none of the proof or hypotheses against this theory actually disproves it completely. What this theory helps us to do is to evaluate where people might have started sleeping.
