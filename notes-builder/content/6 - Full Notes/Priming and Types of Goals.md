# Priming and Types of Goals
**Details**
When discussing about priming, we already know its effects and how it practically allows us to point a person in the direction of doing something. But it has to be emphasized its merely pointing them, not teaching them to do something they don't already know, we are only convincing their minds to do so unconsciously. Nonetheless, there are some pretty miraculous effects of priming, alike increasing the scores of participants in an experiment consisting of a test by making them watch/think like a scientist beforehand (whilst they scored noticeably lower when thinking like a supermodel). This seemed quite unlikely at the time but then other experiments yielded the same results, basically forcing social psychologists to have to accept it. This though, still leaves us with a few important questions, one of them being... where does automaticity come from ?
