# Being Wrong in test comparisons
**Details**
Reality is one of two things and you make one of two calls, which gives a two-by-two. Either the null or the research hypothesis is true, and either you reject the null or you do not. Rejecting a null that is true is a Type I error, and its probability is alpha (the threshold that you set for yourself). Failing to reject the research hypothesis when it is true is a Type II error, with probability beta, and you do not get to set this one. You would actually want to reject a false null, meaning the probability is power = 1 - beta.
