# Official and Real story
**Details**
There is a profound tension in Nietzsche’s account between the "Official Story" (the surface-level myth) and the "Real Analysis" of what occurred.

The Official Story depicts Masters as "active/creative" and Slaves as "reactive/sterile." However the analysis reveals that it is the Slaves that are actually creative as they gave meaning to their internal struggles and selves through inventing new conceptual categories that are different from previous interpretations like Evil, Free Will, The Soul, Responsibility, and Inferiority. And so Nietzsche argues that Slave Morality may be something that is genuinely a contributing factor into society as it prevents it from falling back to the Greek aristocracy times.

Moreover, if we look at the last 2000 years of European history, we would see that the "Weak" have utterly defeated the "Strong." Europe was dominated by Christian morality at that time, which according to [[Master Morality]], would be Slave morality. The values of the weak have become the hegemonic standard. And so, in a dialectical twist, the Slaves proved to be stronger, not physically but conceptually and institutionally. They successfully made man interesting by introducing depth, guilt, and inferiority where previously there was only surface and action. And so in changing this power dynamic, they also changed how power could be interpreted as well, not just someone beating another person but being better morally.
