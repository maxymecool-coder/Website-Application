# The Functions of QuAIC
**Details**
It is crucial to understand the QuAIC's designated role within the broader threat management process. The authors are explicit that the tool "is not a validated formal risk assessment tool and should not be used to replace existing threat assessment protocols". Its purpose is to function as a "decision-making aid" or "screening tool". It is designed to achieve two primary goals at the earliest stage of a report: to help victims and witnesses decide when their concerns warrant a formal report, and to help institutional responders "prioritize cases and determine appropriate interventions and allocation of resources".  

The tool's output is not a definitive risk judgment but a categorized level of concern: Low, Moderate, High, or Highest/Critical. Each level is linked to a set of corresponding recommendations for both the target of the behavior and the first responder, outlining suggested actions and response timeframes. This action-oriented output transforms the tool from a passive measurement device into an active guide for immediate decision-making.

This structure is fundamental to its utility as a triage instrument.
