# Neglected Children
**Details**
These children don't understand the needs and emotions of other children, often resulting in them misbehaving or not fitting in with others, eventually being pushed out of groups and activities that others would be allowed to indulge in. Thing is this is not inherently their fault, this is the fault of not being taken care of correctly in their childhood (referenced in Attachment Theory) when their caretaker does not acknowledge their needs and emotions, prompting them to respond to it the same way when they grow up. 

These children instead of reacting to faces, tones and such as cues to their actions, they automatically assign those things as danger, and respond to it like so, defensively, scared, different from how others normally would. They can't stand being alone, as any human can't. And in so they would find connections in the worst places, gangs, getting themselves in trouble and into jail for those police officers,...

**Supporting Content**
Furthermore, these children also suffers from other factors such as not having enough food, care, which may cause severe drops in IQ permanently as well as stunted growth. The study of Romanian orphans demonstrated that neglect has a "dose-response" relationship with development: children removed from institutions and placed in foster care before age 2 showed significant recovery, while those who remained longer suffered irreversible developmental harm. This highlights that neglect is not just the absence of care, but an active assault on the developing brain's need for stimulation.
