# Goldbach’s Conjecture and the Trap of Conceivability
**Details**
Goldbach's Conjecture (the idea that every even integer greater than 2 is the sum of two primes). As of now, it cannot be proven, but it can still be conceived as true or false. True by induction as the past numbers have not shown anything different, false by imagining a counter-example. But mathematical truths are necessary; if the conjecture is true, it is _necessarily_ true, and its falsity is impossible. If the Conceivability Principle held, the fact that we can conceive of it being false would imply it is "possibly false," which is absurd for a necessary mathematical truth. This suggests that what we can "conceive" is often just a measure of our ignorance ("Epistemic Possibility") rather than a guide to the structure of reality ("Metaphysical Possibility").
