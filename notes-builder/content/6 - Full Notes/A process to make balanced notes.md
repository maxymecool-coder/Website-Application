# A process to make balanced notes
**Details**
To break out of this, we need a good system, and the mere necessities of the workflow will help us work virtuously without being more virtuous. First, we must turn the whole writing process on its head, and secondly, we find information indiscriminately regardless of what argument it supports.

This is the bottom-up approach of opening ourselves up to insights. We will be able to focus on the most insightful ideas we see and we can welcome the surprises that it may bring without it jeopardizing our progress, and even better, bringing it forward. And to do so we must separate the writing process properly, verify if the contents that we reproduced are true or not and lastly find relevance and connect them all together.

The slip-box allows us to be selective but only in what is relevant to a discussion in it, as it opens up connections within the slip-box. Everything can contribute to its development, contradictions, connections, even the questioning of ideas as well as the differentiation of an argument.

After that we can take a step back to look at what we have developed and only then can we make out what conclusion could be drawn from it. Plus, it is in fact a lot more interesting to write from a lively discussion with many pros and cons than from a collection of one-sided and seemingly fitting notes.
