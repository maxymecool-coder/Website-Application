# The problems of testing
**Details**
As we have said before, the most accurate testing method for neuropsychology is to test people with brain damage to see which corresponding parts of the brain affects which cognitive processes that they are unable to do. This way of testing can help us understand the brain more intimately, keep track of the progress of healing and treatment and so on.
