# The bane of multitasking
**Details**
Multitasking is not the action of doing two or more things at the same time and being able to concentrate on them both, but rather simultaneously switching one's attention between two tasks at the same time, which can be extremely mentally draining. Although the outcome is that most felt more productive, their productivity actually decreased - a lot. It even impairs our ability to deal with one more thing at a time, the very thing that we are actually trying to accomplish by multitasking.

And writing is like that, it is reading, understanding, reflecting, getting ideas,... And all of these are different tasks, which requires different kinds of attention. So it its impossible to multitask it all as if it was just one singular task.
