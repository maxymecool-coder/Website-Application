# Skill Generalization
**Details**
The last step in skill teaching, which is to help people use their skills in situations outside of therapy. There are a few ways that a trainer can go by this, firstly is to have between-session consultation, being with them and talking to them about performing such actions. Another way is to review session video recordings, which provide benefits such as allowing an easier way to review their actions out of sessions, as well as helping them to calm down if they are unable to cope and so on in between sessions. Moreover, people can also go online and app stores to find DBT apps/sites that allow them access to resources for them to learn and practice their skills.

Another big one is diary cards, which is how trainers check on their clients' behavioral progress between sessions, providing necessary feedback and coaching. This though does not replace any of the skills reviewing in the session, as it is only seeing if the person has remembered to do their skills, not seeing if they are doing it effectively. Any forgetting or such of the diary cards may be marked as self-management problems to be analyzed and discussed so that it can be solved by the skills being taught.

In managing an environment so that clients can reinforce their behavior, it is necessary for them to create structure, public commitments, finding communities to support their new behaviors, and how to elicit reinforcement from others. More than that, the problem with clients is that they sometimes hold such high expectations that it is impossible for them to reach it, so trainers then need to deal with this. Another help in the environment building can be family members, who can help them train their skills. Last but not least, trainers also have to learn how and when to start fading out from the skills training procedures of the clients. They have to follow an intermittent schedule and provide less help to the clients which helps them to become independent of the influence.
