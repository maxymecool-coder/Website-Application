# Definition
**Details**
Emotional needs are basically cravings that we feel that our significant others or friends have to fulfill for us. And when those cravings aren't fulfilled, we feel frustrated, angry or so. This can range from anywhere from affection to conversations. When we put too much faith or responsibility on someone to fulfill them, we are setting ourselves up for failure, we are asking too much from them, because a human cannot satisfy another human needs, they can only help to do so.

Partners whose emotional needs are helped met feel much more loved and valued, which may potentially help them to talk to each other more fairly, better, and so on. Needs are inherently basis of the relationship, they are basically what we want in relationships, to have our needs fulfilled by someone who truly loves and cares about us in every single way. When these needs go unnoticed or unfulfilled, partners may build up resentment and hatred towards their significant other which can screw up their relationship.
