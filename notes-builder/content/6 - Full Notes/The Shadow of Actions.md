# The Shadow of Actions
**Details**
No matter what action we take or anything we do, there will always be a shadow to it, and the larger the deed is, the longer its shadow. When we care about someone and divert our attention and love to them, the more we dedicate our time to that person, the more potential time to dedicate to others is lost, and it is unavoidable. Same goes for when we donate to charity, if we donate to preserve trees we also will not have money to donate to children in poverty.

There is no pure morality in this, as we cannot avoid the potential bad that goes with the good. And even if we do nothing, it is still bad as we are missing out on the potential good that we could have done. Even if this situation causes despair and paralysis, we can still come to the conclusion and accept that we have to deal with combinations of harm and goodness.
