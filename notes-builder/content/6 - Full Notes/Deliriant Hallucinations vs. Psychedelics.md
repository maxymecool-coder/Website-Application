# Deliriant Hallucinations vs. Psychedelics
**Details**
While classical psychedelics (LSD, Psilocybin) produce sensory distortions that the user can often distinguish from reality, tropane alkaloids produce **true hallucinations** (delirium).

The root of "hallucination" is _hallucinari_ (to wander in the mind). In high doses, anticholinergic drugs cause a total break from reality. The user may converse with people who are not there, believe they are in a different location, or perform complex actions without memory of them later (amnesia). These states are typically disorienting, dangerous, and rarely reported as "pleasant."
