# The argument for animal testing
**Details**
The concerns for animal testing mostly depends on the ethical validations of it. Understanding the anatomy and neuroscience of animals -> better able to teach/understand animals. Testing on animals also allows us to test the pharmaceutical properties and effectiveness on animals before human usage/testing.

Humas inherently shoulder a responsibility to protect lives, even if it is their own, in fact, including their own. And so they have to try to understand and test on animals so that they can produce life saving medicine and vaccines.

Animals have conscience but they do not have free will and cannot decide for themselves ? (And something more to show about Thalidomide which is a fairly outdated example). They do argue that animal testing needs to be careful and transparent, which they argue that these laws have been sufficiently introduced by acts and laws.

The 3Rs are the frameworks that it has to work upon and should be followed. Examples of animal testing working are outdated.
