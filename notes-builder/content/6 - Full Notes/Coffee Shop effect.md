# Coffee Shop effect
**Details**
Nowadays, people (mostly the youth) usually come to coffee shops to work or study, there are many reasons why they would want to do this ranging anywhere from signaling their identity or actually finding the location to help with their productivity.

Scientists (psychologists and sociologists) have conducted a study in 2012 about this particular phenomenon in the Canadian Journal of Experimental Psychology. By testing the performance of participants under different decibels of sounds from quiet to loud, they determined that those who were in the 70 decibels range performed better than others creatively. This level of noise is particularly found in coffee shops as well.

Furthermore, they also found out that an institution like coffee shops also provide social facilitation as people are working together and therefore also assists with the effectiveness of people's work. Adding to that, perhaps people also feel less stressed in these environments rather than somewhere like a workplace but also are more inclined to do work in comparison to sitting at home.
