# Intrinsic and extrinsic notes
**Details**
When we talk about these kind of notes, we mean reading a book in itself apart from other materials is intrinsic. Extrinsic is involving all the others. All the things we have talked about is intrinsic, but now we will do so with extrinsic aids as it can help immensely if you have put your own effort into the book, as long as you don’t slavishly depend on the other materials. 

Extrinsic aids fall into four categories. We will discuss them in this order: first, relevant experiences; second, other books; third, commentaries and abstracts; fourth, reference books. 

Knowing when and how to use these aids cannot be detailed for every situation, bit there are some general suggestions that can be made. The main is to use aids whenever a book is unintelligible to you, either in whole or part, after you’ve done your best to read it intrinsically.
