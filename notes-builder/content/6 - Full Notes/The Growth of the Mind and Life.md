# The Growth of the Mind
**Details**
Our mind is sharply different from our body as it is not limited to the certain ways the body is. An example is when the body does not continue indefinitely to grow in strength and develop in skill and grace. There is no limit to the amount of growth and development that the mind can sustain. And so the mind only loses its abilities and powers when we stop putting it to work and such.

This may relate to how people after retiring die, as their mind are stripped from working, and were propped up artificially by television, radios, and all sources of amusement and information that surrounds us. They can sure give us an impression of feeling so, but we do not grow, and so we begin to die anyways.
