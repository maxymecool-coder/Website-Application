# Obedience to Institutions
**Details**
In society, it is quite common for people to obey to a certain authority figure and to perform whichever tasks or requests they might ask them. This may create a large hole/vulnerability as a lot of the times these people do it without questioning the authenticity of the person of authority, rather just follow their sentiments if they "sound like" a person of authority.

An example that shows this is the **Stanley Milgram's Obedience to Authority Experiment**, conducted in the early 1960s. Positions were: Teacher, Learner, Experimenter. These people were of course applied the rules of an RCT so that the test would be truly random. The teacher and learner were placed in separate rooms but they could still listen to each other. The experimenter then would command the teacher to shock the learner, of course it was a fake but the learner would try to act the corresponding effects they would get from the shock. The rate of obedience of the Teacher was surprisingly high, about 60% in the United States and even more in other countries.

The reason the experiment is so famous is because of its horrifying results. Before the study, psychologists predicted that less than 1% of participants would go all the way to 450 volts. The reality was that **approximately 65% (nearly two-thirds) of the participants obeyed the experimenter and delivered the full 450-volt shock**, even when the Learner was screaming in agony and then fell silent.

This experiment showed us many things, it showed us that morally normal people who normally would not shock another person to death, a lot of them would do so under the presence of an authority. But moreover, it also showed us how we, as a society, could not function without the obedience to authority.

**Supporting Content**
A part of why people did this is because of the consistency, even if 15-volt in normal context wouldn't kill anyone, the line of it killing and not is fairly thin. And because people followed through since 0 til the killing amount, they feel compelled/misjudge how easy it is for the voltage to kill people, eventually resulting in them crossing the line without much realization.
