# To make habits
**Details**
We normally can’t guess what we will be doing in the long run, but there is one exception, though: we most certainly act according to our intention if we happen to intend to do what we used to do before. 

This leads to the fact that we have the best chance to change our behavior over the long term by starting with a realistic idea about the difficulties of behavioral change. Even though the more we do something, we feel more in control about it, its more likely that we are losing control over it. 

So the trick is to break old habits by strategically building up new ones that have a chance to replace the old ones.
