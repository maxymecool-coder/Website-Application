# Obsessions and Compulsions
**Details**
The content of obsessions is concerned with some remote risk or danger expressed in the form of a doubt or a warning. The person may continue to doubt whether he has perform an act to ensure his safety adequately. 

Compulsions consists of attempts to allay excessive doubts or obsessions through action. A hand-washing compulsion, for example, is when the patient believed that he has not removed all the dirt or contaminants from his body. As he regards the dirt as a source of danger, a cause of illness or general dirtiness. We often see the triad of phobia-obsession-compulsion. For instance, a patient afraid of being harmed by radiation, his phobia manifested from him avoiding things that might emit radiation, after a necessity to interact or come into contact with something of the sorts, he becomes obsessed about contamination, and so he acts on it by bathing long period to remove the contaminants (compulsion).

There are some neurobiological activities that have been studied in people with OCD (Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder), specifically different neurological activity in the occipital lobe. This does not show us that this maybe the cause or a symptom of it though. Moreover, the cingulate cortex is also active in situations where there is a conflict, something like the conflict between the reading comprehension and visual comprehension of a task, forcing the inhibition of one of the two parts.

Further cognitive testing has shown that thought suppression in anyone is impossible. Studies have shown that when a person is shown or is told something and then told they should not think about that thing, they will not be able to stop themselves from thinking of that thing. This applies closely to OCD.
