# The inability to transfer wealth
**Details**
In the major of the history of the human kind, it has been the goal for the privileged class to pass their wealth down to the line for their children. And even though we believe that material wealth is the easiest and main way to do it, it has proven quite ineffective due to numerous reasons.

Firstly, wealth cannot be easily passed down from parents to child unless there are exactly two children or one. If there are any more than that, the distributed wealth will instead be unequal. Next, the conflict between the children and parents can exist in the process of transferring their wealth. Due to their parents having to fully pass away or whatever so that they can inherit, the children must wait so that they can have the entire wealth, which creates tension. Last but not least is the nature of how wealth can be, it can be burned in a second from a bad investment, from an economical slump.
