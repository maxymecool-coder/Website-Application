# Cessation of Tobacco
**Details**
Quitting usually requires an extensive motivation and commitment to the process, which includes a multi-modal approach combining behavioral changes with pharmacological support. Commitment can come in the sense of leveraging social pressure, ritualizing, setting a quitting date, or even therapy. They also have to deal with cravings, even though short lived, they can mimic the breathing they do when they smoke to alleviate it.

Pharmacologically, Nicotine Replacement Therapies (NRT) mitigate withdrawal symptoms without the toxicity of combustion. These include:

1. **Over-the-counter:** Nicotine gum (buccal absorption) and patches (transdermal maintenance).
    
2. **Prescription:** Nasal sprays and inhalers, which mimic the rapid onset of smoking more closely.

Additionally, antidepressants can also help, Bupropion (marketed as Zyban for smoking and Wellbutrin for depression) has proven effective in reducing cravings and relapse rates.
