# Habits of A Good Student
**Details**
These are some of the habits of the most capable students, there are many other traits that can make some people different from others but of course these are the general habits found in this population.

Firstly, they are able to set aside distractions and they study alone most of the time. They tell others that they are not going to be able to be reached by the others so no one breaks their deep focus, they will also study for about 3-4 hours per day, broken up into manageable time frames for about 5 days a week.

The next is that they make an effort to teach their peers, almost like the Feynman technique so that they can refine their abilities as well probably. This can test their understanding and mastery of the material.

They also understand the times that they study best and they are able to schedule those times into their lives to make it a constant value so that everything in their body's biology is able to follow and cater to it. Remember that it is a skill to be studied, rather than something to just do.

Moreover, the motivation that are found in these students were that their work and their success would affect their family or themselves somehow, and it fueled them to study and all further.

Finding something to love in the courses that they learn is also a way they learn better, as it is of course more effective and all. So one should try to find something they love in a course that they at first found boring.
