# Consequentialism and Death
**Details**
I will not go through consequentialism from the base but basically. Death is bad, but death targets the deprivation of a good remainder, not intrinsic to the life living. So death is good when remainder bad. Utilitarianism would somewhat have the same shape as well.

Here might be when we look into the side conditions, which may include treatment existing and obligations to others continuing: single parent of small children whose death would devastate them strongly (as proven by statistics).
