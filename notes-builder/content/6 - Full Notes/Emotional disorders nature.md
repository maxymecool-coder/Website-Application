# Emotional disorders nature
**Details**
This is where common sense falls short. It fails to provide plausible and useful explanations for the puzzling emotional disorders. In order to understand someone that is mentally ill, one has to get inside the conceptual system and see the world through their eyes. We cannot depend on the preconceptions of most people, of society or folk to determine and understand them. Once we familiarize ourselves with their perspective, we will understand their behavior, and so give her explanations for her actions.

The point is, the crucial information in these people does not exist. However, once it is supplied, we can apply common sense to solve the puzzle. This proves that these problems are result of things such as faulty learning, incorrect inferences on the basis of inadequate or incorrect information, and an inability to distinguish between imagination and reality.
