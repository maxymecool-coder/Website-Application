# Pretreatment DBT
**Details**
Before the treatment of DBT, there are 5 tasks that the therapist or the individual handling must go through: conducting a pretreatment assessment and deciding the appropriateness of skills training for the client; deciding on the intensity of treatment and type of skills training necessary; orienting the client to the specifics of skills training; developing a collaborative commitment to do skills training; and beginning to develop an alliance. These tasks should be done through the first interviews determining the disorder of the patient, and step 3-5 should be repeated during each skills orientation.
