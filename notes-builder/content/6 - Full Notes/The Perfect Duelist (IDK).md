# The Perfect Duelist (IDK)
**Details**
In Valorant, a "perfect" duelist is a concept that is quite... ambiguous ? In the process of a game, there are so many aspects that are just not calculated and not statistically recordable like how much utility a duelist makes the other team have to discharge to get them off.

Though of course, I have particularly have some bias towards duelists like Canezerra, Aspas, and Zmjjkk (kang kang). These guys all have different playstyles in their matches and it is quite difficult to really categorize and compare them since they work towards the same goal just... different ways ? But, breaking it down to the ground, we have to talk about the "traditional" duelists, Aspas and Zmjjkk, who has both proven themselves in Valorant. They are extraordinary duelists overall, they don't overheat much, get the picks they need, play the advantage, so on.

Now even further than that, introduce Canezerra, who is playing with pure instinct every single match. And it works, depends on his form of course. But at the end of the day, Canezerra would push, a lot, in situations that would get literally anyone else into overheat, he actually translates a majority of these dumbass pushes. His stats in Stage 2 Challengers were completely bonkers and different from Aspas you cannot even say for a moment this dude baits. He takes insane timings, risks so much, but it pays off, more often than not.

Now of course regarding accolades and performance wise, we cannot compare a tier 2 player to 2 Champs winners, but just talking about them playing as duelists, Aspas is of course a more "matured" version of Zmjjkk, with just overall similar style just executed better whilst Canezerra is wildly different.
