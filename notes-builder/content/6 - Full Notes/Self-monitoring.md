# Self-monitoring
**Details**
There is this phenomenon that Plato called “internal dialogue”. It is in this that the self compares alternatives to decisions. 

This can be involved in maladaptive reactions, which usually involves the fact that they are overdoing these activities, leading to self-consciousness and over-regulation to excessive inhibition. They can interfere with a person’s self-expression as they constantly question and warn themselves before doing or saying something. In obsessional neurosis, it may even paralyze action. 

But as well, they can develop a deficit of self-monitoring. They cannot manage their impulses, they smoke and eat excessively.
