# The Syntopicon
**Details**
This is what answers the paradox of knowing what to read.

Without this, we would usually have to turn to our own experiences and knowledges, or the knowledge of those who  are a-depth in these subjects. In the first situation, there is no flaws, you just have to know though. But in the second, asking somebody what to read, what chapters, books may make their advices turn into hinderances as their own special experience and such may make them tell you how to read it rather than what to read. (The case of reference books mentioned in fictional literature)

This is where the Syntopicon comes in, it is an index of a set of books on a subject, though it may only give a very rough indication of where such passages are. Nevertheless, it would always provide you with a place to start on any project that requires Syntopical reading.
