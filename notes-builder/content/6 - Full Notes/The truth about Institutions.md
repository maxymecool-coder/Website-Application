# The truth about Institutions
**Details**
Perhaps from a lot of people's views, institutions are blood-sucking monsters who just wanna drain their clients' of all their fortune and wealth, and they should be judged due to how efficient, truthful they are, but of course there is more nuance than that. We are not detached from the institution, we are not opposing them, because we are a part of them.

Institutions are more like ideas or frameworks of how a particular thing function, companies in general are an institution, hospitals in general are also an institution. Organizations on the other hand are the singular parts of these institutions. So when we focus on how corrupt one company is, we are ignoring how the framework behind that company is in fact being used for may other organizations, even though each one is tweaked a bit differently. And so if we want a change to the corruption, we must focus on the institution, not the organization.
