# Biology in Retardation
**Details**
A staggering 25% of cases that are not attributable to the socioeconomic differences can be termed "organic" mental retardation due to specific biological defects such as chromosomal abnormalities or brain malformations. The most common of which is Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21), which relates to the chromosome 21. This condition is associated with distinctive physical features, moderate to severe intellectual disability, and a nearly 100% risk of developing Alzheimer's disease pathology by middle age due to genetic links on that specific chromosome. And because of this, these people would also experience these symptoms across culture and situations as it does not matter about socioeconomic factors.
