# The Plateau of Latent Potential
**Details**
A major pitfall in habit formation is a fundamental misunderstanding of how progress actually works. We tend to think of work as compounding linearly over time, expecting steady, highly visible improvement day after day. When this doesn't happen, we get discouraged. Instead, we must conceptualize habit building as the accumulation of latent potential. Think of it as building up energy to eventually cross a specific threshold, which then lets out a massive, sudden change. Like heating an ice cube from 25 degrees to 31 degrees, the work is happening, but the visible transformation (melting) doesn't occur until you hit 32 degrees. Understanding this plateau prevents us from quitting during the early stages of habit formation when our efforts seem unrewarded.
