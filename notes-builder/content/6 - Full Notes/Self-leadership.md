# Healing trauma
**Details**
There is nobody who can go back in time and prevent a traumatic event of someone from happening; but what can be done to deal with the trauma itself is with the imprints of it on the person. The sensations of the body that prevents it from staying on task and functioning fully; opening one’s heart to others. 

It robs you of the feeling of being in control of yourself, which Van der kolk has coined “self-leadership”. The challenge of recovery is regaining the body and the mind of one’s self. This means that they can feel free knowing what they are feeling and knowing it without becoming overwhelmed, enraged, ashamed, or collapsed. For most this includes finding a way to be calm, learning to maintain that calmness in response to images, thoughts, sounds and sensations that remind them of the past, finding a way to be fully alive in the present, and not keeping secrets from yourself (remembering fully what happened tp one). All of these goals might overlap with each other, depending on circumstances.
