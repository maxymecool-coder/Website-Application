# Its Not their Fault
**Details**
People with mental disorders can have their physiological system severely affected by said disorders, until the point of suffering from actual conditions. There’s something called the depressive five - conditions that the mentally ill usually suffer from: temporomandibular joint disorder (TMJ), migraines, fibromyalgia, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), and interstitial cystitis—that is very common among those with BPD, along with myasthenia gravis and rheumatoid arthritis. Physical pain and discomfort make all of us more vulnerable to emotion, and as you can imagine, for someone who is highly emotionally vulnerable these physiological conditions just make things worse, making additional crises even more likely. Physical conditions like these also may cause your loved one financial stress, job stress, and relationship stress, leading to crisis from those secondary problems.

As they don’t usually express with their body language, it might also be hard to believe they are suffering from said conditions, but they are totally real and more often than not they are actually experiencing said conditions. Moreover, they would also dig themselves into a deeper grave due to their cognitive deficiencies.
