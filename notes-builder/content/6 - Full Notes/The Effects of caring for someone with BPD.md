# The Effects of caring for someone with BPD
**Details**
When someone is taking care of a person with BPD or simply involved with them, the actions and emotions that the BPD person exude can heavily affect them. They can do nothing but be in a reactive state as the person goes from one extreme to the other, making sure nothing upsets the person and then trying to get away or hurt the person at all cost. This makes not just you but also any other person involved feels random and directionless. The actions of these people also usually causes them to lose their relationships more, fulfilling their own predictions that people will leave them. Moreover, they might even get paranoia as they are hyperaware and all of what you are doing (but then the trigger goes away and so does the paranoia).

One must understand that even though they have to of course protect their own mental health and such in the relationship, it is also necessary for them to know that its not their intention to ruin the relationship and that they really just need help.

**Supporting Content**
All these behaviors group together and create a vicious cycle. When someone is emotional dysregulated they make stupid decisions that makes people prove them right as they then believe it more and so on and so forth.
