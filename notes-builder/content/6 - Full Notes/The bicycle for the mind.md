# The bicycle for the mind
**Details**
As it goes for other inventions in the history of humanity, like how all kinds of vehicles have been invented to shorten the amount of time people spend getting from one place to another, the computer is something to accelerate the process of thinking and performing intelligent tasks overall.

Yet, most of the time, it fails at this particular task, it slows us down. Many can argue that the reason stems from ourselves, as it doesn’t increase our productivity because we do not dedicate our time and usage of it to something actually productive. I would argue otherwise.

To start doing work is the most difficult aspect of the process, and the computer particularly makes that difficult with the process of setting up and all.  Having a process with dedicated steps and applications for you to use for your productivity is particularly difficult when there are so many tools and so many ways to use said tools.

And so helping with this task, I use Zettelkasten, with Obsidian and the minimalistic style of it which helps me massively to retrieve, store my ideas. Same goes for a dashboard webpage which I coded, which functions basically the same as the Zettelkasten, but I self-coded it rather than using something like Notion as it can be clunky.
