# Medial prefrontal cortex
**Details**
This is located above our eyes, like a watchtower to identify danger, then sending it to the amygdala which in turn will send the signals to the brain stem and so on. It enables people to observe what is going on, predict what will happen if certain sequences happen to make a conscious choice. This enables us to hover over our thoughts, feelings, and emotions and respond appropriately to the executive brain to manage the automatic responses that it has. As long as this is working properly, we won't act irrationally or to our base instincts whenever something like a waiter messes up our order or an insurance agent puts us on hold.

In PTSD, the balance between the amygdala and the MPFC shifts radically, making it much harder to control emotions and impulses. Studies show that highly emotional states all increase the activation of the regions that include the amygdala and decreases in the frontal lobe.

There are two ways to effectively deal with this imbalance, there are two ways, the top down and the bottom up. Top-down regulation is strengthening the MPFC for it to monitor the body's sensations more accurately. While bottom-up is recalibrating the autonomic nervous system.
