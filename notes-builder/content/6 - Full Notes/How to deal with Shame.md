# How to deal with Shame
**Details**
Shame is evoked by past experiences involving the person being ashamed, talked down to or something because of their behaviors and actions. It is of course, a negative emotion that will only bring the mood and state of the person experiencing it down.

To combat shame (particularly in trauma/BPD) it is best to have a professional, trauma-specialized therapist to help with, but nonetheless, when there’s no available help or for the current time being, there are also ways for a person to fight the shame. If your loved one is being ashamed, you need to make them talk it over. Sounds quite forceful, and of course there are ways that you can soothe this, like validating them, explaining to them, but again, make them talk about the shame constantly and repeatedly until they no longer feel said shame.
