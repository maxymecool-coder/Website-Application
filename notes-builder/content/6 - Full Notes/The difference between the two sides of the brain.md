# The difference between the two sides of the brain
**Details**
Back in the early nineties, some people divided the world between left-brainers, who were more logical, rational people and right-brainers, those artistic, creative one, this relates to the fact that when past trauma is present in the patients mind, the left deactivates and the right activates.

From this, we understand that the right brain is emotional, visual, spatial, intuitive, and tactual whilst the left is linguistic, sequential and analytical. As an example, when developing or learning a language, the left brain is more likely to be in use rather than the right, which is utilized for non-verbal communication mostly. Moreover, the two also perceives memories differently, the left remembers the vocabulary, words of description, perhaps dates, statistics whilst the right remembers the emotions evoked, the smell, perhaps.

Under normal circumstances both of these work together more or less smoothly. However, when one side shuts down, even temporarily can have a significant impact. The left hemisphere when deactivated will directly impact the capacity to organize experience into logical sequences and translate feelings and perceptions into words. Which means we cannot identify the cause and effect, or create plans for the future. A common case of this is when one is saying they are "losing their mind", when their brain is overwhelmed and shuts down temporarily.

The other is when traumatized people remember of the past, their right brain reacts as if it is happening in the current moment. But because their left brain isn't working well, they may not be aware that it is just a reenactment and feel furious, terrified, enraged, scared,... After this passes, they may look for someone to blame for it. Of course this can also occur in people who aren't mature or something along those lines, but most of the time when they realize they will apologize and fix their mistakes, trauma directly affects the awareness of this matter.
