# Social Phobias
**Details**
These phobias represent a caricature of the desirability our society places on being liked and admired and on the undesirability of being unpopular and despised. Which means that it emphases an individual who fits into group norms. These kinds of performances are dramatized by two kinds of situations which practically everyone has gone through. The fear of examinations, termed "test anxiety," which sometimes produces sufficient distress, disability, and inhibition to be labeled a phobia. And also the fear of failure, which makes the person unable to exercise voluntary control over certain intellectual functions.
