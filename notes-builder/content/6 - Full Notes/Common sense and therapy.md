# Common sense and therapy
**Details**
As talked about before of humanity’s adaptability, we can use the tools of common sense like experimenting, making discriminations, and reasoning. Seeing the sound principles from one’s cultural and societal as well as ignoring the notions, myths, and superstitions that might reside in it.

Common sense exists in all medium of formal science, as J. Robert Oppenheimer: “All sciences… arise as refinements, corrections, and adaptations of common sense”. Similarly, observations of one’s inner consciousness, and events can provide the materials necessary to study the human behavior.

It also allows one to reflect, observe, introspect to determine why someone is upset, what is bothering them and what can they do to relieve it. Through this, one can determine the events or circumstances that have triggered such preoccupation, and stress. Moreover, he can then develop methods to tolerate, avoid it.

Most of the time, this kind of self-help is frequently applied to understand and help others. It is obvious that this doesn’t always work, but it helps a lot of people to maintain their equilibrium most of the time.
