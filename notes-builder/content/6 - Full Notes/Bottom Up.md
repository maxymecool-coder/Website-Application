# Bottom Up
**Details**
This production method is bottom-up because rather than starting with an outline in mind, according to the traditional school-taught method of writing, the Zettelkasten method emphasizes a more organic growth that starts with Zettels, or ideas, already learned from different sources. However, this approach only works when learning is almost faultlessly recorded in a Zettelkasten. Instead of having and spending time on putting notes in specific folders, you instead just let the notes roam freely and those with similar ideas connect with each other.

**Supporting Content**
Notes in a Zettelkasten are like stuff in a shipping container, all thrown into one and have different areas and characteristics but at the end the goal is still the same - insights.
