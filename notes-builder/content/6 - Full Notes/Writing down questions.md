# Writing down questions
**Details**
When writing a zettel/atomic note, it might be good to constantly question yourself what information you are missing that your source has not given you yet. This helps you to basically just have your brain always thinking, outsourcing info. This also helps with reading, if you constantly question what you do not know from the info given, its going to be more effective.

Moreover, you can even question the interconnection of a note between others whilst doing so, eventually answering these questions will in turn help you reduce the amount of time spent on doing Zettelkasten as well as increasing your own cognitive abilities and understanding of the subject at hand. It will even help you to expand on your notes and ideas, as more questions will derived from a simple "Why am I writing about this ?" or "Why does this work ?", and so on. By doing this, the limitations of ideas that we write down into our slip-box becomes clearer, and so we see the particular angle that they have on the problem, as well as helping us distinguish and clarify our own ideas from each other. 

The reason for this is that the first choice to answer these questions is looking into the slip-box, if the answer is not there then maybe there is an indication of where to look.
