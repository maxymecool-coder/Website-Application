# Internal vs External Attributions
**Details**
The attribution theory was founded by Fritz Heider, who emphasized the differences between these two types of causes. Internal attributions are based on someone's individual characteristics, like their attitudes, personality traits, or abilities. External attributions are explanations based on the situations, including events that presumably would influence almost anyone.

A more interesting side of this is the fact that we have self-serving biases toward ourselves. Meaning if we do something bad, we attribute it to the situation or something out of our control, but if someone else does it, we attribute it to them being the kind of person who would do something like that, or them just being a piece of crap.

Even further than that, we might create disadvantageous situations for ourselves so that we can have an excuse on performing badly although we probably have already thought or guessed about that fact.
