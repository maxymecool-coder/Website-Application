# Self-Stigmatization
**Details**
Due to societal rejection, people turn to stigmatizing themselves, turning the prejudice against themselves, inward. While some minority groups have successfully countered this through solidarity movements and pride campaigns, people with mental illness have historically lacked such cohesion. Without a strong group identity to buffer against societal rejection, individuals with mental disorders are highly prone to internalizing the belief that they are dangerous, incompetent, or unworthy. And this forms a vicious circle as people who are ashamed because of their own state avoid finding and searching help, as shown by statistics.

This may also negatively affect the people around the person who is stigmatized, their family members may be shunned and isolated from the rest of society due to how different they are, or that they are related to someone "like that". Most of the responses from these families was that though the financial burden and effort of taking care of them is much, it is not as bad as having to hide the diagnosis from others.
