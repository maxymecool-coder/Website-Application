# Durkheim's Types of Solidarity
**Details**
Durkheim identified two different types of solidarity that were differentiated by the social bonding of the individuals involved in those solidity.

Firstly, the mechanical solidarity, which are solidarity formed from the similarity, the likeness of individuals together. If they have similar hobbies, beliefs, statuses, experiences that can allow them to have an interest and liking towards each other. The individual conscience is almost identical to the collective conscience. The powerful, shared feeling you get when cheering with a crowd at a football game or singing together in a church is a modern echo of mechanical solidarity. It's a bond born from a powerful sense of sameness.

The other, is organic solidarity, which is highly defined by interdependence. In the current modern society, in the capitalistic market, we have different skills, abilities to do different jobs, and a lot of the time, those different jobs separate us from each other like a class of labor/skill. Though at the end of the day, we are still dependent on each other to live, the engineer still need the vegetables and crops of the farmer just as the farmer needs the engineer's skills to make technology to help them farm.
