# Treatment for Eating Disorders
**Details**
The treatment may vary depending on the eating disorder. For Anorexia, hospitalization is often required to restore weight and metabolic stability, necessitating a gradual increase in calories to avoid refeeding syndrome. Family therapy is also crucial to address boundary issues without reinforcing enmeshment, individual therapy is to reinforce autonomy. CBT has been proven to be more effective for Bulimia, specific triggers can be identified and modified. For Anorexia, it is more difficult due to the nature of body image distortions. SSRIs may be used for comorbid disorders rather than the eating disorder itself. Long-term recovery is possible but not popular as 80-90% retain lingering issues with food and body image.
