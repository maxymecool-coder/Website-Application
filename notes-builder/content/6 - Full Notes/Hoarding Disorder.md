# Hoarding Disorder
**Details**
This is a disorder where people need to just acquire stuff and that becomes a problem. They abhor parting with their objects even if they cannot see any potential value in them. Researches illustrated by the Clinical Case of Dena has shown that the person will acquire large collections of anything, even useless sandwich wrappers and bottle caps. About two-thirds of these people are unaware of the severity of their behavior which means they will be hard to accept the fact that they need help and may not get this problem resolved for themselves. This disorder can lead to active inhibition of convenience and livability of somebody's place. These people may not even be able to work, their place can't hold it, they get respiratory and physical problems because of the sheer dirtiness of the place. It can even get to them getting evicted or ending up homeless because they have no money.
