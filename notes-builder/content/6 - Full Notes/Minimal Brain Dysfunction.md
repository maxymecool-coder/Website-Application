# Minimal Brain Dysfunction
**Details**
ADHD actually started out from a plague, the influenza plague, which killed 80 million people worldwide. Though in survivors, particularly children, physicians found out that they were displaying a specific cluster of behavioral symptoms: severe impulsivity, an inability to pay attention, and unregulated hyperactivity. Because these symptoms appeared in the wake of a known viral infection that attacked the central nervous system, doctors termed the condition "Minimal Brain Dysfunction" (MBD). This of course is extremely wrong or "affirming the consequent." They thought that since brain damage causes hyperactivity then if hyperactivity appears it must be brain damage, not considering or lacking the knowledge of other possible causes.

The condition evolved through various names, shifting from the neurological "MBD" to the behavioral "Hyperkinetic Reaction of Childhood," then to "Attention Deficit Disorder" (ADD), and finally to the current "Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder" (ADHD).
