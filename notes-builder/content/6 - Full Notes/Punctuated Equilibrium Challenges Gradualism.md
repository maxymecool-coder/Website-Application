# Punctuated Equilibrium Challenges Gradualism
**Details**
Different from Gradualism, Stephen Jay Gould and Niles Eldridge argued that there are long periods of stasis, of nothing happening, the genes do not actually build up 1% at the time, they actually burst in a big step in a short period of time, which is called punctuated equilibrium. This came from the research of fossils by Gould, seeing the gaps in the record of the evolutionary record of fossils.

Though this is just a difference in views and occupations making people misunderstand the concepts and subjects of the other occupations. The field that Gould was working on was not exactly close to what biologists and geneticists were research about, which showed gaps in his argument when compared to their theory.
