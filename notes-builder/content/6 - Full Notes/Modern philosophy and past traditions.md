# Modern philosophy and past traditions
**Details**
We will call the questions that happens in the world, or about what men should do or seek “first-order questions.” Then there will also then be “second-order questions”, which are about the content of our thinking when we try to answer the first-order questions, the ways we express such thoughts in language (as our sense and mind are different). Most philosophers today only focus on the latter, as the former is believed to be impossible to be answered by philosophers.

And alike other subjects, people have also abandoned first-order questions and only write for other professionals, not for the layperson.

This calls for us to read the books answering first-order questions, which are books usually in the past if major philosophers.
