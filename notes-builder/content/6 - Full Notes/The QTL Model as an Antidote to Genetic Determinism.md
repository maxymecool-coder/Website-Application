# The QTL Model as an Antidote to Genetic Determinism
**Details**
The paper argues that the Quantitative Trait Loci (QTL) model is the best defense against fears of genetic determinism and misuse of genetic information. By showing that complex traits are influenced by many genes of small, probabilistic effect, the QTL model refutes the simplistic idea of "a gene for" intelligence or criminality. It blurs the line between normal and abnormal, suggesting we all carry some risk alleles. This complexity makes genetic prediction difficult and argues against simple, deterministic applications in areas like employment or education. It also implies that interventions will almost always be environmental, as seen in the PKU example.
