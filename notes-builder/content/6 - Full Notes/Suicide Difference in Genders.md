# Suicide Difference in Genders
**Details**
Suicide is a major public health risk, it is one of the leading causes in death, especially among younger adults or teenagers, currently the **second leading cause of death** for college students and the third for all people under 25. Even though women are the people who attempts it more, men are the people who actually succeed in doing it, as their methods are much more lethal and sure (guns, knives,...).

And even though mental disorders can be quite strong predictors, it seems like hopelessness is the strongest of all, as people who attempt tend to tell others that their thought was that their "will to die" was impulsive and temporary, and so intervening the action can actually save lives.
