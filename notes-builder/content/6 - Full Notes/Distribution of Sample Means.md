# Distribution of Sample Means
**Details**
Inferential statistics may also hinge on this concept. Simply, it is the concept of obtaining means throughout the years of a single experiment and plotting it out, hence the name: distribution of sample means. 

It has two defining properties, both reassuringly simple. Its center equals the population mean: **μ_x̄ = μ**. Its variance is the population variance shrunk by the sample size: **σ²_x̄ = σ²/n**.
