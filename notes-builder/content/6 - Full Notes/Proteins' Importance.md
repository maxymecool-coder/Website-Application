# Proteins' Importance
**Details**
Basically, in the DNA of the human body copies itself into an RNA and turns into a Protein. Protein is particularly important as it is responsible for a lot of the systems functioning in our body, for example, neurotransmitters, they act like those messengers to communicate in between cells. Furthermore, they also hold the structure of the cells together physically, and they become enzymes, which breaks down foodstuffs so that they turn into energy.

The way that these proteins can fulfill each of these different functions is thanks to their shapes, they can change their shapes by a change in the sequence of the DNA and "fit like a glove."
