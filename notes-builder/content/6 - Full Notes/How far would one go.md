# How far would one go
**Details**
Revenge is a course of action or a goal that quickly takes over the human psyche for the very first periods after experiencing an event that causes it. Within the discourse of discussions, there has been many arguments on if it is truly fulfilling or worth it or not, with the general consensus leaning over to the negative side.

It makes sense how a person would want revenge, after all, they are simply inflicting back the pain that was inflicted onto them, a pain that has taken a lot from them (or little depending on certain people and situations). The question is how far would one go for revenge, a small revenge, a pity one perhaps, is when a person sees the opportunity for it and of course, acts upon it. This can range from an insufficient amount of negativity or pain inflicted back or even more than that has been inflicted on them in the first place. The person usually feels bad and apologizes for it afterwards or something of the sorts.

Going to the opposite end of the spectrum on the other hand, is trauma so much that a person does not just simply give up on their life because of it but rather dedicates their whole life into avenging it. The psychological impact the trauma has on these people are already palpable, and the path that they take will certainly take any happiness out of their life, setting them on fulfilling the one thing they believe will relieve them of their trauma.
