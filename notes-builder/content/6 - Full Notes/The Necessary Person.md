# The Necessary Person
**Details**
When building a well-ordered society, the concept of the people in it is quite important, in which they must be “free and equal persons.” Rawls emphasized that this is political, of what people think about each other and such and in no way philosophical or metaphysical.

Within this framework, equality means that persons are equally capable of participating in social cooperation and equally capable of honoring the fair terms of cooperation specified by the principles of justice. This capacity is articulated through two moral powers that parallel the two elements previously distinguished within the concept of cooperation itself. In short, the powers are to understand and accept the principles of justice and the capacity to form, revise, and pursue a conception of the good.

And in this, even if it is somewhat circular, it is fine as long as we achieve the goal of fully realizing and making meaning to our well-ordered society.
