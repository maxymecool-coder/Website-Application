# The psychophysiological cycle
**Details**
Similar to stress or anxiety, there is also a vicious cycle to this. Even for a patient who has not exhibited any ill symptoms for a prolonged period of time, it may appear suddenly when she suffers pain in some area that she eventually perceives to be cancer. This can then be traced back to her mother dying of cancer, and then at the time she experiences the symptoms, her sister had just also suffered from it and died, making her feel anxiety, leading to the pain, and then back to anxiety, in which then the cycle repeats itself.

The threat was always there from her mother's death, she always perceived that eventually, she will die too, of the same symptoms, and so when her sister died of cancer, she experienced a revival of anxiety that caused her body to experience psychophysiological symptoms.
