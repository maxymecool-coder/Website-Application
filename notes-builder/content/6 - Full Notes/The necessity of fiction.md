# The necessity of fiction
**Details**
Fiction is usually read at one or another point of any person’s life, why so ? And why do people usually read this over any other genre of books ?

One reason I believe is that it satisfies our subconscious world. On the simplest level, we, as humans, like and dislike certain types of people, and in life, a lot of them usually go unpunished, but those in fiction usually can be and will be. Also it can be stories that we usually don’t and can’t encounter in real life, but in fiction, they can.

But a different point from all this that I want to mention is of tragedy, where bad things happen to good men, but even if those men didn’t deserve it, they come to understand, grow, and move past it. Which explains why it is still rewarding despite going against the “bad guy gets justice served” trope in our minds.

This is all to say that we must be careful distinguishing books that satisfy our own needs and satisfy the needs of everybody. The latter are undoubtedly great stories, and we would like to live and experience such worlds of such stories.
