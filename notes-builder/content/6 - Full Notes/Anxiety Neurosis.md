# Anxiety Neurosis
**Details**
The thinking is constantly dominated with themes of danger to his domain; that is he anticipates detrimental occurrences to himself, his family, his property, or to his status and other intangible he values. Which is opposite to the phobia patient by which he feels anxious in situations he cannot avoid whilst the phobia feels anxious in situations he can.

This can lead to the patient basically just experiencing and labeling simple, usual, even safe things as danger, like a shortness of breath arousing the idea that he is having a heart attack, unexpected sounds are disasters and so on.

Furthermore, many anxious patients are afraid predominantly of psychological harm. They are afraid that other people, friends or foes, will reject, humiliate, or depreciate them.
