# Trivers-Willard Hypothesis
**Details**
This hypothesis states that male and female ratio of being reproduced is not the same, it may depend on the situation the female finds itself in, social status and physical condition can influence the sex of her offspring to maximize her reproductive success. This mostly applies to tournament-bonding species as they have a clear discrepancy between birthing females and males.

For example, birthing males would be a gamble, as 95% of the males do not reproduce, but if they do, the amount they reproduce is 5x-6x of that of a female in the species. And so high-ranking females would have the resources to do this gamble. Oppositely, the lower-ranking females would have to birth females to make sure their genes get passed on at the very least.
