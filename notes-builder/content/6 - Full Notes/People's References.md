# People's References
**Details**
Usually you can write the reference for yourself with only the signature of the referee, this gives you a significant amount of control over it. 

At other times, when having someone else writing the reference for you, it is advisable for you to make sure that you have sent them what universities you are applying to, and the overall details of the university, what it values and all. Moreover, you should try to give them a direction of what you want them to write about you, most of the time they will appreciate this greatly as they do not have to think of the points that they have to do so. Lastly, you should provide them a deadline when you would need them to submit the reference. You may also allow them a month for them to be able to finish the letter.

When finished, you should also try to find a few people to get opinions from, a few laypeople as well, this is all to fix and perfect your essay as much as possible.
