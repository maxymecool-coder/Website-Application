# The ODD Controversy
**Details**
ODD, also known as Oppositional Defiant Disorder, is one of the most controversial diagnosis in the DSM 5. Why is it so ? This is because many clinicians argue that it pathologizes normal childhood behavior and stigmatizes children with a permanent record for being merely "ornery."

But on the other hand, many argue that it is necessary due to its predictive nature, as a warning system, so to say. About one-third to 40% of children with ODD progress to Conduct Disorder (CD), a much more severe condition involving violence and theft. And when it has developed to CD, the window for effective treatment has closed. ODD is a "heterotypic" risk factor, it changes its appearance overtime, what seems like merely defiance at age 6 can turn into delinquency at age 16.
