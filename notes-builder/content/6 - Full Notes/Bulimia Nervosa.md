# Bulimia Nervosa
**Details**
Another eating disorder. This disorder involves episodes of consuming large amount of food in a short amount of time and then following by a compensatory behavior, such as vomiting, fasting, or excessive exercise, to prevent weight gain. This is called "binge" eating, so like eating insanely more than what a normal person would in a short amount of time, not being able to stop and then try to get it all out. This does not mean losing a large amount of weight though, and that is the key difference between this and [[Anorexia Nervosa]].

These periods of eating usually come in secret and are triggered by stress and negative emotions, as eating is a comforting experience, until they are uncomfortably full. This is because they are usually ashamed of these periods, which makes sense due to the sheer nature of eating and vomiting it all out.
