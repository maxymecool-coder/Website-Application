# The Pharmacology of Nicotine
**Details**
The primary psychoactive substance in tobacco is nicotine, a pyridine alkaloid, an agonist at nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (nAChR). Unlike how [[The Solanaceae Family (Nightshades)]] are found only in muscles, these receptors are also found in the CNS, the ANS, and neuromuscular junctions.

As how shamans described the feeling as "grounding", nicotine does this: it is a stimulant to the CNS and also an anxiolytic. They may feel increased focus and alertness whilst also feeling grounded, reduction of stress and anxiety. This precipitates a high [[The Drug Abuse Potential]] clearly. It also affects sleep, increasing REM sleep and dream intensity.

Nicotine also increase the basal metabolic rate, which explains why people who give it up gain more weight. It affects the ANS, which raises heart rate and blood pressure through brain-mediated pathways. The body builds up tolerance of nicotine extremely fast, even though negative side effects include nausea and vomiting.
