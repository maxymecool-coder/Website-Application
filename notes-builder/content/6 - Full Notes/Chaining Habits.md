# Chaining Habits
**Details**
To extend one's list of good habits, we can use a method called Habit Linking (or Stacking). Because our environments are filled with cues, we can use an existing habit as the reliable cue for a new habit, chaining them together for ease of performance. The starting point is keeping track of a current habit and simply adding the new one immediately after. Furthermore, we can turbocharge this through "Pairing" (Temptation Bundling). Dopamine functions not just upon receiving a reward, but during the expectation and anticipation of pleasure. We can abuse this biological fact by pairing a habit we need to do alongside a habit we inherently want to do. The anticipation of the desired activity makes the difficult activity significantly more attractive.
