# A Tailored Approach in Empathy
**Details**
Not always is using empathy the right thing to do in therapy, you have to consider and assess how you should respond to the client. Some may view empathy and warmth in a negative light because they are suspicious of it, too little may make others feel unvalued, so you should try and watch their reactions to understand how you should react to it.

Moreover, clients who have different cultural backgrounds and characteristics can also influence the therapeutic relationship. They will see you in a different light and in the way you act towards them as well. The same also applies to you, however, and being aware of this fact allows you to be sensitive towards how you act to clients of differing culture. But there are always exceptions, you may find that most of the time, they may respond well to your style nonetheless.
