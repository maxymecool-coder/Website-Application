# Dispositional and Situational Explanations
**Details**
Dispositions are the enduring factors of a person, which are their characteristics, situational factors are external pressures, contexts, provocations. Something can be in a state due to both dispositional and situational. Joe kicks his dog Fido because he has a bad temper OR just because Fido bit him. These explanations are not mutually exclusive, they usually both contribute, but people tend to just lock in on one category and assume that is the answer. This dichotomy structures all of the attribution theory. The critical asymmetry is that behaviors are visible while dispositions and situations often are not. When we walk around a corer and see Joe kicking Fido, we lack the situation context. The question driving attribution research is that how do people actually assign causal weight between these two categories, and how should they ?
