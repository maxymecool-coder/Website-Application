# Reductionism
**Details**
Basically, Reductionism is the concept that any and everything can be easily boiled down to another thing that is simpler to understand. For example, in the 19th century, chemists had laws about "valence" (how elements combine) without knowing about atoms. Later, physics revealed that "valence" could be defined in terms of electron shells. This resulted in a deeper understanding and allowed physicists and chemists to explain how something like glass would crack when it gets heated to certain temperatures. From that, we can see how in hard sciences, Reductionism actually helps deepens our understanding of something, it allows us to "guess" or understand a macro concept from micro physics. One would think that the same could be applied to neuroscience but actually it is the opposite.
