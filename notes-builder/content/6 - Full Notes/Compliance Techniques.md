# Compliance Techniques
**Details**
Beyond group conformity, there are techniques to exploit psychological principles to change behavior without changing beliefs. The foot-in-the-door technique leverages our desire for self-consistency: residents who agreed to display a tiny "Drive Carefully" sign were later 76% likely to accept a large, ugly version of the same sign, versus near-zero compliance from those approached only with the large request. Agreeing to the small request caused people to encode themselves and identify themselves as "the kind or person who cares about the neighborhood safety," and subsequently requests that fit this identity were accepted to maintain consistency. The lowball technique is another: a car salesman offers $25,000, you mentally purchase the BMW during the 10-minute wait, and when he returns saying $35,000, the psychological calculation isn't "do I want to pay $35K?" but "do I want to pay $10K to avoid losing the car I already own in my mind?"
