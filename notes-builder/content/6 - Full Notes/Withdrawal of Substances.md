# Withdrawal of Substances
**Details**
This is a concept that entails the physiological symptoms of stopping the use of drugs, which are usually the opposite of the effects of the drugs. For caffeine, the effects are stimulating, but when retracted, the effects are lethargy and headaches (due to vasodilation). Whilst the withdrawal of caffeine is merely uncomfortable, the withdrawal of alcohol on the other hand could be fatal due to seizures that the patient experiences.
