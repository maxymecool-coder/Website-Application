# Environmental Invalidation
**Details**
We cannot state enough how important environments are to the development of a human being, and how it has an effect on our mental disorders and such. Talked about before in [[Neglected Children]] and [[Disorganized Attachment]] of how parents can cause risk factors in their children. This is the same case in other mental disorders like BPD. Though most children get invalidated throughout their childhood, they still can be fine. This shows that the invalidation can somehow negate the private experience of a child, their subjectivity, so to say. For example, when a child is hungry, and instead she is told by older people that she is not, she then believes that she is wrong, this can lead to disordered eating, or at least contribute to it. It teaches they cannot trust their own emotions and not only that but refrain them from learning how to.

But now moving onto the more common cause of risk factor - invalidation. This cause can easily range anywhere from when a person is a baby to when they are children or teenagers. The invalidation usually comes in form of people disregarding the problems and oversimplifying those of the children, teaching them so much to bottle it up, ignore it,… like in [[Defining symptoms of BPD]]. Now, it has been generally argued how invalidation is more prevalent in women because of the sexism that is inherent in society about how women should be, should act, which definitely puts them into much invalidation amongst the population. Moreover, they are severely prone to the worst kind of invalidation, sexual abuse, sexual abuse is common in female BPD patient and people usually make them shut up about it, refraining from talking or expressing it as they also follow that to a tee. Another rate is the values that they have to follow, people tend to amount females to empathy, caring, and males to academic and physical success, so women who possess male values can also be invalidated and even forced to cater.

**Supporting Content**
Even though parents may not mean it, or really want only the best for their child, they can still inadvertently hurt them. As said before, some children can just be born extremely sensitive and emotional, which may allow them to accidentally hurt the child.
