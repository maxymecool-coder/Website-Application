# Schizophrenia
**Details**
A story about the term "schizophrenia" which comes from Greek roots meaning "split mind," but the "split" in the term schizophrenia refers to a split between the intellectual and the emotion aspects of a person. Someone suffering from this may express strong inappropriate emotion or just fail to do so. It is no longer the defining symptom of it, but nonetheless it still occurs.

The main symptoms with schizophrenia are hallucinations and delusions, if these symptoms are severe, no other secondary symptoms may be necessary for it to be diagnosed. Some other symptoms also include, grossly disorganized behavior, or a loss of normal emotional responses and social behaviors. Before diagnosing a patient that could possible have this, the psychologist must rule out all other illnesses that can cause the symptoms.

These symptoms can be distinguished between positive and negative. Positive meant that the symptoms are present symptoms that normal human beings do not have. Negative symptoms means symptoms that humans are supposed to have.

Now we will go into detail about the symptoms. Hallucinations are perceptions that do not correspond to anything in the objective world. The most common examples are people seeing things, hearing voices, and such like that, these hallucinations do actually show brain activity in the auditory cortex and related brain areas (spooky). Now, consistency here does matter, if you do not hear these voices often, rarely only, then that does not mean you are schizoid.

Moving onto delusions, these are the unfounded belief that are strongly held despite the lack of evidences for them. There are three common types of this, which are persecution, grandeur, and reference. Persecution meaning there are fucking people chasing me ! Grandeur meaning I'm god basically. Reference meaning, oh the news are talking about Michael Jordan... it must be a sign for me.

The last big symptom to talk about is disordered thinking, which is basically people just functioning worse cognitively than others, people with schizophrenia I mean. Sometimes people with schizophrenia may suddenly stop talking and wait for you to talk although it is clear that their speech hasn't end, sometimes they may even be able to sit in a singular position for extended periods of time which is completely abnormal to the human being.
