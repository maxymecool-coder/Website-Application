# Slave Morality
**Details**
This is the other side of Nietzsche's arguments, which he describes as a profoundly creative act that is poisonous. He originally argues in Ressentiment: a specific physiological and psychological state where a subject is oppressed but lacks the power to react physically. If a Master is insulted, he strikes back immediately, but if a Slave is insulted, he must swallow it. And so slaves would then act inwardly and conceptually.

They do this by taking the very qualities that define the Master—strength, pride, violence, wealth, sexual confidence—and conceptually reframes them as "Evil." This is the birth of the concept of "Evil" as a moral category. They weaponize this redefinition of the word to be reactive and aim at the evilness of the Masters.
