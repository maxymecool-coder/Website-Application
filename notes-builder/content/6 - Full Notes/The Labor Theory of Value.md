# The Labor Theory of Value
**Details**
As Capitalism monopolize the means of production, preventing workers from ever being able to have access to produce for themselves, they are essentially empowered to charge the working class a structural "fee" for access to the tools and factories necessary to make their labor productive. This exploitation is fundamentally invisible because it is obscured by the wage system and the complex mechanisms of market pricing. Furthermore, capitalists justify their profits using frameworks like the "marginal productivity theory," which argues that every factor of production is paid exactly what it contributes. Marx aggressively attacked this as a "tax illusion"—an ideological construct designed by the owning class to legitimize the extraction of surplus value.
