# The misconception of Writing
**Details**
Contrary to what most self-help or writing guides tell you, writing doesn’t involve merely narrowing the scope of topics down and brainstorming information about it. 

Most of the time, in academic or formal writing, the topics and points that are written about must be logical or the whole truth, in which brainstorming isn’t all about, since the info comes from one’s brain, so it will include their opinions, biases and so on. 

But to decide on a good question to tackle or find the best angle for an assignment, one must realize that they have to have already put some thought into a topic, read on it and of course of other related topics and such. And so it connects with the whole process of writing, when we read, we write it down, externalize it. There is no plan that can do this effectively, because writing is not linear, and so we need to follow our own interests, curiosity and intuition instead of our unreliable brainstorming. 

And so later, when we finally have to decide on something to write, we don’t get stuck on brainstorming about it, we immediately know what to write because we have been making that decision and refining it every step of the way, through choosing relevant information between pieces of media
