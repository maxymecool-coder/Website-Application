# Discouragement
**Details**
Perhaps affecting us more than we think it does, the behaviors of BPD partners who reject or responds negatively to your attempt to help may deter you from doing kind things so that you stop wasting your efforts and protect your own mental health. But of course if the opposite is true, one would most likely be reinforced and continue to do what they have been doing.

They are not necessarily trying to punish or make us sad in these situations, but rather their reactions and behaviors can make us feel despondent, angry, hopeless, helpless, and so on. These reactions most of the time stems from their own emotional dysregulation, causing them to lash out and leave. To help them with this problem, it might be useful for one to note down the details about them lashing out at an instance or such like that.
