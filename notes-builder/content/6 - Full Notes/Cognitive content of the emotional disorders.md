# Cognitive content of the emotional disorders
**Details**
The capacity of human beings to adapt and integrate themselves into society (which consists of myriad environmental events) proves the ability and depth of our own psychological system. Despite all of the abilities that we have from this singular system, there are of course some challenges that we do not respond consistently to.

These are "fault lines" which stresses accumulate and may set up for total eruptions that can be labeled as "over-reacting." Under such conditions unrealistic appraisals will override the realistic ones, and we may realize that our reactions are largely irrational.

Some people can be so engulfed in these fantasies that their behaviors become controlled by it all. And so when these reactions burgeon beyond a certain point or level, then we are likely to label them into the categories of "mental illnesses" which can include "emotional disorder," "neurosis," "psychological disturbance" or "psychiatric disease". These things frequently assume a characteristic that allows them to be fitted into recognized category such as depression, anxiety state, or paranoid state. Although related to the aforementioned, these differ from normal reactions due to the intrusion of unrealistic thinking of the key aspects of the patient's life.
