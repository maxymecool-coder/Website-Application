# Nervous system
**Details**
All the things that we instinctively register during a conversation is linked up by a single regulatory system. Which is a synchrony between two branches of the autonomic nervous system (ANS): which acts as the body’s accelerator, and the parasympathetic, which serves as its break. These are the “reciprocals” that Darwin spoke of, so they play an important role for the body’s energy, releasing it and conserving it. 

The sympathetic nervous system (SNS) is responsible for arousal, including the fight-or-flight response. It partly pumps adrenaline into the muscle strands. The second branch is the parasympathetic nervous system (PNS) which is for self-preservative functions. It triggers the release of acetylcholine which puts a brake on the adrenaline, slowing down heart-rate and such. These are a part of the autonomic nervous system. 

A measurement called heart rate variability (HRV) can be used to measure this system, a good one will have many fluctuations which means both of the systems are working well and are in balance.
