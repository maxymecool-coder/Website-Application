# Moderators, Mediators, and Confounders in Psychopathology
**Details**
Basically, these are the types of risk factors in how they lead to an outcome. Studying how one led to the other inherently birth the existence of these three:

Firstly, moderators, a variable that alters the direction or strength of the relationship between a risk factor and outcome. The effect varies at different levels. Secondly is a mediator, a variable that explains the mechanism _through which_ the risk factor causes the outcome. (e.g., Poverty $\rightarrow$ Harsh Parenting $\rightarrow$ Delinquency. Here, harsh parenting mediates the link between poverty and delinquency). And lastly, a confounder, a third variable that causes both independent and dependent variables, creating a false association. For example, the statistical link between **Race** and **Delinquency** often disappears when controlling for **Social Class (Poverty)**. Poverty is the confounder that drives the association.

The effects that risk factors have also depends on the socioeconomic and cultural factors, for when tough parenting is presented in white households and black households. The former resulting in delinquency or just more aggressiveness overall in childhood and the latter found no correlation at all, even slight reductions in aggressiveness even.
