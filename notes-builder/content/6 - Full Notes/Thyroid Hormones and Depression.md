# Thyroid Hormones and Depression
**Details**
A significant link exists between the thyroid gland and major depression, highlighting how a dysfunction in a peripheral hormonal system can manifest as a primary psychiatric illness. Thyroid hormones regulate the body's overall metabolism and energy levels.

A deficiency of these hormones, a condition known as **hypothyroidism**, often presents with symptoms that are clinically indistinguishable from atypical depression. The primary overlapping symptom is severe **psychomotor retardation**—a profound state of exhaustion, physical slowing, and mental fatigue.

According to Sapolsky, a notable percentage of major depression diagnoses may actually be undiagnosed hypothyroidism. This connection underscores the importance of a full biological workup in diagnosing depression, as treating the underlying thyroid issue can resolve the depressive symptoms. It serves as a clear example of depression being a whole-body biological disease, not just a disorder of the mind.
