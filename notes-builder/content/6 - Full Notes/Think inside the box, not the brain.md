# Think inside the box, not the brain
**Details**
Brainstorming most of the time is what people recommend to generate new ideas to write on. But they don’t realize that it is rather an expression of an outdated fixation on the brain, reflected by the fixation of our educational system - learning things by heart, not externalizing it. 

The brain goes against providing interesting and easily available information, instead it provides information that is easily accessible for it. It chooses to dish out information that it has encountered recently, which has vivid emotions connecting to it. Everything that might be complex, abstract and actually worth writing about may be very far down the list of ideas of the brain. 

But the slip-box negates this, it promotes a more pragmatic solution as the notes we connect will eventually cluster up into ideas that we can write about. Ideas that are actually good and that we know well enough to write on. In the process of making the Zettelkasten, we are already deciding what to write on and what not, the aspects that we need to write on and such. That self-reinforcement helps us dictate where to continue reading on and thinking about, it keeps getting more and more useful, or practical.
