# Sensitivity of receptors
**Details**
The other part of neuroscience research has gone to this, which researches the mechanisms of the brain that adjusts the sensitivity of the neurotransmitter receptors of the postsynaptic neurons. There are many ways to do this, for example, if a receptor has been activated extensively over time, the cell may retune its sensitivity so that it is more difficult to create a nerve impulse. When a cell has been firing more frequently, the receptor may send out second messengers, they will adjust the sensitivity. Research has shown that antidepressant medications may be effective due to their effects on these receptors.

Another method of research is to use a drug that stimulates a particular neurotransmitter's receptors, referred to as an agonist. In contrast, a drug called the antagonist will inhibit the responsiveness on the receptors to dampen the activity of the neurotransmitter. (like how nicotine mimics a neurotransmitter in our brain).
