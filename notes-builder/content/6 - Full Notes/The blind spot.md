# The blind spot
**Details**
Now, to have something as the optic nerve that allows the eye to communicate with the brain, we also lose something. That something is vision, or **the blind spot**, which is where the optic nerve exits. It has no room for receptors because the exiting axons takes up all the space, this is also the place where blood vessels enter the eye. This is because the brain ingeniously fills out that blind spot with surrounding information, making us completely unaware of it.
