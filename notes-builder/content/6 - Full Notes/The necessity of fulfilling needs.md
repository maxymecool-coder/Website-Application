# Fulfilling needs
**Details**
Sometimes in a relationship, one person completely ignores/forgets their own or their partner's needs. Too much of any of this can crumble and degrade the energy of the party giving it away, eventually depleting it enough until they run out of energy completely and cannot continue to give it away anymore. This will then in the end cause problems, arguments between the two lovers, straying them away from each other.

Same way can go when one of the two becomes too ignorant of the other needs, they can build up hatred and resentment and self-sabotage the relationship to get what they want or be noticed (like a child but that is how we revert to our basest functions). Needs are the same, they are also one of our basest functions and most vulnerable, letting others into these secrets is the highest level of the trust. And even when already talked about, people may not realize about the reality of getting their needs fulfilled, about how they have to talk about it and maybe even plan it out. As sensitive as a topic it is, needs are difficult to talk about because of the inherent weirdness of it, how people may perceive it as the part of themselves that others will hate if they ever knew about.
