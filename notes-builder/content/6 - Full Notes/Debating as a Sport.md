# Debating as a Sport
**Details**
Perhaps even realized by the fundamental philosophers in history, debating was to be a sport dependent on formal logic, logical fallacies, and the showcase of incredible critical thinking and scientific knowledge. Yet, when Socrates took up debating, he realized that humans were emotional creatures, we were illogical, and perhaps influenced by our emotions and views rather than logical reasoning. This is the reason that he attributed not just logic to be important but also rhetoric, as people are much more amused of the spectacle and what they may perceive as a victory over the other rather than following the actual argument instead.

On the topic of Rhetoric, we may also discuss about what it is. It is not necessarily a subject alike philosophy or logic even though it is birth out of the former and by definition would qualify as a subject. It is more like a skill, rather, for someone to use in a debate or discussion.
