# Encouraging slow change
**Details**
Some random day, people may just get an urge or motivation to do something as they put their effort into some work. Now, as these people are perfectionists, they might be unrealistic and try to work towards impossible goals, the key to not discourage them from not doing anything then is to help them and encourage them nonetheless, encourage the parts that you do believe rather than the part that you don't.

Even if you know your loved one cannot possible meet these goals, try to tell yourself that its not your place to say that, and rather focus on the small things that you can validate about them. For example, if your loved ones say that he wants to stay off drugs permanently and it seems unlikely, tell him that you know he can stay of drugs TODAY.

Now for the other part, try to steer your loved one into breaking down the goal into many achievable steps and gradually building to the final goal that they are trying to get to. This gives them breathing room so that they do not feel like a total failure when they fail some small goal and such, maybe even help them find new goals that are more realistic.
