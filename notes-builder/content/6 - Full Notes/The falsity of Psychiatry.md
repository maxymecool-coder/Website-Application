# The falsity of Psychiatry
**Details**
Psychiatry is a subspecialty of medicine, aspiring to define mental illness as precisely as other physical illnesses. But given the complexity of the mind, brain and human attachment systems, we cannot even come close to that precision. It is usually diagnosed by the mindset of the practitioner rather than cold hard facts.

The first systematic manual of diagnoses, which was recognized by the American Psychiatric Association (APA). DSM-III warned explicitly that its categories were insufficient to be used in forensic settings or for insurance purposes. But it was indeed only used for the purpose of gaining money for capitalism instead of helping people. Moreover, it would likely diagnose patients with things that of course fits the bill but wouldn’t necessary be correct.

This can in turn lead to many diagnosis that won't actually help the doctors do anything about the patients' problems. Also researchers and clinicians would be unable to develop appropriate treatments for something that isn't even in the guide/knowledge.
