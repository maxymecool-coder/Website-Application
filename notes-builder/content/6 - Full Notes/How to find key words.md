# How to find key words
**Details**
Not every word that the author writes down is important. And so we must identify which are and which aren’t. Only words that are used with a special meaning are important, are used out of their ordinary meaning. 

But not all words that are used in a daily sense with a range of meanings say that they for sure mean that. Different times and places means that these words will have different meanings. For example if you read Meditations by Marcus Aurelius, the meaning of words like life may be drastically different than one of a philosopher today. 

And to locate such words, you need to understand the passage that contains them. If you do then you will know which words are the most important, if you don’t then it’s because you don’t understand the way the author is using certain words. Mark those words, in both situations, so that you will know those important words. 

Of course there exists those out there who don’t find difficulty in understanding these words, and those who struggle as much as the author.
