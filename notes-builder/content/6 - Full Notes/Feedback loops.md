# Feedback loops
**Details**
This is the key element to any learning process, and it also helps motivating one self to do more. And seeking feedback, knowing that we are improving with every note we make, with everything we do will nurture this.

Moreover, the most reliable predictor for long-term success is having a "growth mindset". To actively seek and welcome feedback, be it of any kind, is one of the most important factors. And oppositely, having a "fixed mindset", being scared of criticisms of one self because it might damage their self-image will quickly fall behind in actually performance, even though it protects themselves in that moment. This is inherently present in the gifted and talented individuals, who are constantly praised for what they are rather than what they do, and so they tend to try to keep this impression intact, rather than exposing themselves to new challenges and possibly learning from failure.
