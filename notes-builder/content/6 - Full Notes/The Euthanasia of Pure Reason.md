# The Euthanasia of Pure Reason
**Details**
Kant’s "discipline of reason" requires human reason to become its own pupil, identifying exactly where it unlawfully oversteps the boundaries of possible experience. Kant then divides transcendental logic into the Analytic and the Dialectic. Human reason naturally craves the absolute, leading to inevitable, structurally embedded illusions. The primary mechanism of this self-deception in rational psychology is the _Paralogism_. A paralogism is a formally fallacious syllogism that is transcendentally grounded in our cognitive apparatus.

Historically, rationalists like Descartes and Leibniz attempted to use pure reason to prove that the noumenal "Soul" is a simple, enduring, and indestructible substance. Kant doesn't believe this as true, rather he believes that they are confusing what is required for one to have rational thoughts with the empirical discovery of something. Because pure concepts are completely empty without sensory intuition, trying to know the noumenal self is akin to operating a clinical diagnostic framework without any actual patient data
