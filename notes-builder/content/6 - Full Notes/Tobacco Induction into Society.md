# Tobacco Induction into Society
**Details**
Even when considered as one of the most toxic and addicting substances on the planet, somehow tobacco is not condemned like other drugs ? Why is this ? Well it can be explained by its early onset in history, how it spread as a medicine amongst pharmacology, how it has been in use in cultures for so long and its effects as well. Movies, for example, are a huge factor as actors in certain movies smoke a lot of cigarettes even if they are fake, potentially influencing the next generation to participate in such acts. Moreover, the substance does not have the usual characteristic of inhibiting cognitive abilities, rather enhancing, though still at the cost of the health of the person. One last factor is the fact that tobacco is a profitable industry, and so people are actively promoting or don't condemn it as much (including the government due to taxes).

But when withdrawing from tobacco, the effects are quite opposite. Alike any other abusive substances, the user experiences irritability, anxiety, difficulty concentrating, insomnia, and increased appetite (often due to a drop in metabolic rate).
