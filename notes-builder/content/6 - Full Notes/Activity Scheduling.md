# Activity Scheduling
**Details**
In CBT, people also may need to or can write and show the activities that they perform in a day in a table to allow the therapist to understand how they are and what they are doing depending on the time of day. Perhaps patients may not want to do so, that is why you can ask generally about their day and what they are doing at certain periods.

But first, we will talk about scheduling, depressed individuals may most of the time avoid to do activities that may help them because it takes effort and well, they’re depressed and have cognitive distortions. So a part of therapy is to help them to integrate these parts of their days back, as trying this may help improve their mood, achieve their goals and fight against the negative beliefs they have about themselves instead of lazing around and unchanging. For those who are not able to do this, we would then assess the obstacles that got in their way and problem solve to resolve said problems.
