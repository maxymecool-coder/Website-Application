# Newton and the Death of Intelligibility
**Details**
With Newton's findings, we could finally understand mathematically that vortices could not create elliptical orbits, and such, but rather, gravity. While Newton’s equations were miraculously predictive—unifying the fall of an apple with the orbit of the Moon—they were philosophically baffling. How can two objects affect each other across a vacuum without any mechanical connection? Newton famously refused to speculate on the _mechanism_ ("Hypotheses non fingo"), settling instead for the mathematical success. This marked a profound shift in science: the goal moved from "rendering the world intelligible" (making sense of _how_ things happen, like gears or fluids) to "predicting phenomena" via equations, even if the underlying forces (like gravity) seemed like spooky, occult magic. This gave the explanation for Kepler's improved astronomy and such.
