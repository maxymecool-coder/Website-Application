# The rules of Criticism
**Details**
These rules mostly involve the rhetoric, as they include the communication among human beings. In conversations, we usually want to be agreed and understood, about theoretical matters of course, that ultimately affect action or feeling. The rhetoric is in the persuasion and convincing that the writer does to the reader and how the reader takes it. 

And so you need to be both responsive and responsible to the conversation that you are engaged in. You must follow what has been said and understand it, but you also have to take a position in it. This position is fully yours, no one else’s. 

The rules of criticism rests on the principles of rhetoric, it is a code of etiquette to allow the reader to talk back logically and meaningfully to the author.
