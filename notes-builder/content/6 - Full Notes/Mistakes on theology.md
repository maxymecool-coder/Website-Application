# Mistakes on theology
**Details**
Understanding the distinctions and how theologies operate usually are difficult, and so people make mistakes. Most commonly, they refuse to accept, the articles of faith that are the first principles of the author. The second is to assume that, because these principles are dogmatic, any points that are based on the, are also dogmatic. It is true enough, if certain principles are accepted, and the reasoning that is based on them is cogent, that the conclusions must be accepted too. As well as if the reasoning is defective, it will turn to invalid conclusions.
