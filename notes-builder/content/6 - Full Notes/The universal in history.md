# The universal in history
**Details**
Although we are not always able to read more than one history of an event, we must admit we probably won’t know the truth in question. But them we can turn to being the layperson between the historian who researches extensively and the amateur who reads for fun.

We can use the information from such that we read to come to conclusions of lessons as well. Now we come to the point of universal, which history isn’t quite as it is tied to events in a certain way, although good history is still universal.

History is the story of what led up to now. It is the present that interests us—that and the future. The future will be partly determined by the present. Thus, you can learn something about the future, too, from a historian, even from one who like Thucydides lived more than two thousand years ago.
