# The need of Therapy
**Details**
Many people question the authenticity of psychology as they think that people can change and apply therapy to each other without the help of therapists or psychologists. To answer this, that is correct, many people do work out their problems by themselves or with the help of someone who is "wise." Many of people do successfully apply the methods that are the stock-in-trade of behavior therapists by gradually exposing themselves to frightening situations or imagining themselves in these situations ("systematic desensitization"), or through pattern their behavior after others. Or they can access "folk wisdom," or some cultural shit like that.

The problem here is that the people who come to therapy are the ones who cannot do these. Which can derive from them reacting to their problems too acutely or severely to try to respond to usual life experiences or help themselves. It can also be that they have been too fragile to do so, or that their problems are so severe that they are ingrained. There are many other ways that they can be encouraged to seek professional help, maybe its due to the taboos, the myths, misconceptions that folk wisdom bring, maybe its "bad advice" from other people.

At the end of the day, the figure of the therapist is still a powerful one, and definitely more affective than the normal person as they know what to say, the most effective methods, together with the authority that the patient has placed onto them.
