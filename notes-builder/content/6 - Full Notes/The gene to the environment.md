# The gene to the environment
**Details**
The gene that is most relevant to the discussion of genetics related to mental health is the serotonin transporter gene (5-HTT). This gene has a polymorphism such that some people have two short alleles, or two long alleles, and some have one short and the other long. Researchers found that those with short alleles, meaning short-short or short-long allele combinations of the 5-HTT gene and were maltreated as children and were more likely to have MDD. as adults.
