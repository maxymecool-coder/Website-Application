# Automatic thoughts
**Details**
These are certain kinds of ideation where it is usually not omit in free association. This isn't due to the fact that there was any resistance or defense to not express these thoughts but rather of the patient not knowing the thought persisted in the first place. The thoughts are appeared to be of transference nature, which means they are related to the patient's evaluation of what he was saying or planning to say and how they expect the other to react. This meant that, unconsciously, the patients were communicating to themselves in every conversation everyday, and so by training them to focus on these thoughts, we can get to the key of the problems.

In order to access this information, one must recall the thoughts that one had prior to feeling some intense or unpleasant feeling or sensation. This will help sharpen patient's awareness of their thoughts, and eventually they will identify these thoughts.

But why are these thoughts so obscured in their minds ? M.D Aaron Beck says that it is because the patients are presenting material that had to do with current problems, dreams, and memories; or presenting narratives of their own experiences; or jumping from one idea to another in a chain of associations. But they were not concentrating on their thoughts, but rather what they "must be thinking".

He also theorized that the reason free association failed to recognize these thoughts was because people are accustomed to speaking to others and themselves differently. And even though these internal signals can have a powerful influence over them, they don't have a history of paying attention to them.

Continuing in depression, or in situations where the patient is disturbed - these thoughts are more prominent. Aaron also said that he become more cognizant of their presence when he tried to elicit the thought content from severely depressed patients. He also recognized the compelling nature of them in the ideation of obsessional patients.
