# Mirroiring
**Details**
- Basically repeating what your partners say, listening attentively, not bending, changing or even trying to answer or solve or apologize, just listening and repeating what they say until correct, this makes them feel acknowledged and in turn show your care and attention to them. Try to repeat and present your point and understanding of their idea as softly and understandingly as possible (not come as threatening or trying to defend yourself).
