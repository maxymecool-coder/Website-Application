# Misunderstandings of Well-Aware Society
**Details**
When observing the ideal society, we often run into three misunderstandings: Firstly, our point of view - as we think about it as studying philosophy and our ideas about justice, not how society actually works. Secondly, observing it as a game with fictional people and fictional understandings and fictional mindsets. Lastly, its the conception that all citizens in a well-ordered society realize the conception of justice.
