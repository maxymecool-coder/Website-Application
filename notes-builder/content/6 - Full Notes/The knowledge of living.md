# The knowledge of living
**Details**
The idling brain, or the “default state network” (DSN), is when we are not particularly focusing on imagining or thinking about anything really and rather just emptying our minds. It turns out that in this state, humans start working on ourselves, creating our sense of “self”. The midline structures are the most active during this process, and the posterior cingulate seems to work the most, it gives us a sense of where we are, like a GPS. It is also strongly connected to the MPFC, it is also connected with areas of the brain that registers sensations from the body. 

In PTSD patients, the difference was that they had none of this, their midline structures barely worked, the only thing that did was the GPS, which is responsible for basic orientation of space. The explanation for this is that they have effectively shut down these areas, not allowing the brain to feel terror or fear, but in such they have also prevented it from feeling other emotions, deadening their capacity to feel alive. What this also explains is why traumatized people are so lost. How could they know what to do, what decisions to make when even them couldn’t define what they want, when they have already lost their sense of self ? Or when their body can’t tell their brain what it needs, or wants.
