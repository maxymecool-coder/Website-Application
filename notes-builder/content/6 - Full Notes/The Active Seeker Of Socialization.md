# The Active Seeker Of Socialization
**Details**
With the models of [[The Passive Learning Of Socialization]] proving quite ineffective and nonetheless just not being able to explain or make sense of the socialization development of children, sociologists have come to this group, which will be discussed below.

Firstly, Cognitive-Development, which is coined by Piaget. This argues that at some point, when the child is entering their middle school years, their cognition development at this state categorizes people into gender groups, who they should be, who they are, and they start finding where they would fit, where they SHOULD fit, due to society's norms, not exactly their thoughts. Usually, what happens is that society exaggerate these genders groups at this point in time, making them follow also exaggeratedly, like I wanna be a princess, like I wanna be seen, known, popular, and so on (for girls of course). Combining with the state of mind that they have at the current stage of development, they do not think that they can change themselves, who they are, that they are gonna be in that group forever.

The next model is the [[Generalized Other]] model. Which are people learning their roles and others' roles in a community to guide them through their actions of socializations and for them to fit into the community.

Lastly is the [[Anticipatory Socialization]] model, which derives from the above.
