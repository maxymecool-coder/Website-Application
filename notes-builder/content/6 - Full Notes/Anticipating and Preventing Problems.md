# Anticipating and Preventing Problems
**Details**
Its quite important to predict these obstacles to increase the probability of the client completing the tasks and actually learning something from the process. It's then quite important to predict potential obstacles when setting it up, you should already think about these things when you are making the plan. Then you can ask them how likely they are to do the work actually. If they say it is less than 90%, try checking for what the remaining percentage is and where does it come from to help them respond to said unsureness.

For example, you can identify a client's automatic thoughts, then get to helping them identify it, solving it like telling them its an experiment then seeing the result and working from there, then reminding them of the [[Action Plans]]. You should also try to see their reactions, like negative thoughts and figure out right then and there and address it.

**Supporting Content**
Some more strategy is to weigh the pros and cons of their activities against each other for them to see and understand if it is helping them work towards their goals or farther away from it.

When a situation actually goes wrong and well, result in a negative outcome, it may be helpful to the client to be reminded from their action plan that its not their fault, and that perhaps the person is just who they are to everyone, not them, attribute it to the situation.
