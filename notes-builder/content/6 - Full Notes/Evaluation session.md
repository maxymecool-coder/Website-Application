# Evaluation session
**Details**
This is a totally different session from therapy. It is merely to collect the data necessary for [[Case Conceptualization]]. A diagnosis can be missed if there is incomplete information, clients deliberately withhold information, or the clinician accidentally misinterpret symptoms. There are also many factors that may determine if the session would be long or not. Like the questionnaires and such that they fill in and all.

It may also be of help for you to get people like their friend, partner, family member to help you get additional information or a different perspective. When involving someone like this, we have to come to an agreement with the patient how they are going to help them and so on.
