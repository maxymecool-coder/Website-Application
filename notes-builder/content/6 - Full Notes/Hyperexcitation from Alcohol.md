# Hyperexcitation from Alcohol
**Details**
The prefrontal cortex is monitored by two parts, the inhibitory system and the excitatory system. The excitatory system is one that basically tells the brain to go batshit crazy (like how drunk people start doing dumb shit and all) and the inhibitory system is what keeps that at bay. Now, the thing is, inhibitory system is weak, and so when alcohol comes in, the depressive symptoms that it produces affect that system, disabling it. But the excitatory system is strong as hell, and it does not care, so now it has free reign to do anything, including hyperexcitation.
