# The stomach and intestines in eating
**Details**
The fuel deficiency of the [[Hypothalamus in eating]] and [[The liver in eating]] are both problems that are resolved much time after the food has been eaten, then what causes us to stop before even that ? This is where the stomach and intestines come in. The stomach walls contain receptors that are sensitive to the nutrients dissolved in the stomach acid, and then the receptors signal that nutrients are on their way and so the host can stop eating.

Even further than that, satiety signals also come from the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine. When food passes through here, it releases a hormone from his mucous lining. This hormone is called cholecystokinin, or CCK and it sends messages to the brain to stop eating.
