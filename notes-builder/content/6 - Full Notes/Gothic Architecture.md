# Gothic Architecture
**Details**
So, what is Gothic Architecture ? This type of architecture is basically a style that originated in the mid-12th century in the Ile-de-France region. Originally called "Opus Francigenum" (French work), it evolved from Romanesque architecture and was succeeded by the Renaissance. The defining characteristics of these works are their pointed arches that channel weight more directly downward, allowing taller structures. Ribbed vaults, also, which reduce roof weights and allow for thinner walls. Flying buttresses to allow walls to open up for huge windows and support weight of the massive walls. An artistic and perhaps defining characteristic of this type of style are massive stained glass windows which are installed to flood interiors with "divine" light. And lastly, verticality, as belief in God was still very strong during these times, people believed that God was up there, and so verticality was also a feature, drawing the eyes upward to heaven.
