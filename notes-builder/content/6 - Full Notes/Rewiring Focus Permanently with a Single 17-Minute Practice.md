# Rewiring Focus Permanently with a Single 17-Minute Practice
**Details**
One of the most remarkable and efficient tools for enhancing focus requires no special equipment and, according to research, can create a lasting improvement after just one session. This practice, lasting about 17 minutes, works by rewiring the brain's attentional circuits to significantly reduce the occurrence of "attentional blinks." The technique is a form of simple meditation focused on **interoception**—the perception of your body's internal state.

The practice involves sitting quietly with your eyes closed and turning your attention inward. Instead of trying to clear your mind, you simply pay attention to the physical sensations of your body: the rhythm of your breathing, the feeling of your heartbeat, the contact of your skin against the surface you're sitting on. When your mind inevitably drifts, you gently guide it back to these internal signals. This brief, one-time investment in consciously attending to your inner world has been shown to produce a durable change in how the brain allocates attention to the external world. It appears to train the brain to maintain a state of readiness, offsetting the natural tendency to "blink" and miss information, and may even help counteract age-related cognitive decline.
