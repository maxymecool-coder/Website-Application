# Egoistic Suicide
**Details**
Emile Durkheim coined this term in his work "Suicide," it does not fully meaning egotism. This suicide happens when a person is too unintegrated into a social system, leaving them directionless and without somewhere to belong to. When a person is only living for themselves, when they do not have anything to live for anymore (their own happiness), they would off themselves. He argued that this "egoistic" characteristic of a person, their loneliness leads to their death. Durkheim observed higher suicide rates among unmarried individuals and Protestants, whom he argued had lower levels of social integration compared to married individuals and Catholics, respectively.

This is because Protestants had their own choices, they could choose to devote their lives to God and find their own meaning in how they interpreted the Bible, whilst Catholics were tied to a system that they had to follow.
