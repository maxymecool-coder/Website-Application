# Reducing Decisions
**Details**
Willpower, or otherwise motivation is a limited resources of the human mind. Though one can train and exercise to increase the limits and capacity of this resource, they can only go so far.

So what is the idea behind getting the most use out of it ? The answer is to reduce the amount of decisions. When we are writing about something, we should try to write in one notebook, one pen, one style, increasing any amounts of variables will just increase the amount of willpower used up in doing them. This also has the benefit of making writing very enjoyable, as we can spend only a certain amount of time in doing something, finishing an aspect of it that we consider work and then focus on relaxing, taking breaks (which are very important for learning as they allow the brain to process information and turn it into long-term memory,...).
