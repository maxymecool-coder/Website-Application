# Conviction and Mental Health
**Details**
In the court of the law, mental health is rarely considered a factor that would be beneficial to the prosecution of the particular subject. There are a few types of prosecutions that one who is mentally ill can have, such as the guilty with mental illness, or the defense with insanity. Although these all sound like they may be letting the person who commit the crime go for free, but most of the time, these people are assigned to mental hospitals that are like asylums to help them, and stay there for quite a long time.

**Supporting Content**
It is quite rare for people to enter an insanity plea, in fact, it is only 1% of the time, and only 25% it is successful. Even when they are released from the mental hospital, they have to report back their mental state or else they are going straight back
