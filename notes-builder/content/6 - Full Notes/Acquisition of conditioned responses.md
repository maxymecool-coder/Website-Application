# Acquisition of conditioned responses
**Details**
Initially, the CS does not mean the CR will happen. But after several pairings with the US, the CS is able to elicit it. And then the CS will be presented with the US after. These pairings are said to reinforce the connection; trials in which the CS and US are both presented and called reinforced trials. Trials in which CS is and US is not are called unreinforced trials.
