# Neuroplasticity
**Details**
Neuroplasticity is the ability of the brain to change and morph accordingly to what the host needs. A form of neuroplasticity that is popular is synaptic, which is the process of two neurons giving information to each other, connecting by using neurotransmitters, or inhibiting information. Another form of neuroplasticity that is talked about a lot in the media is neurogenesis, which is the creation of new neurons, this process actually takes part in only a trivial amount of neuroplasticity.

Now the major method of neuroplasticity that drives our daily actions and making information more solid is the strengthening and the weakening of neurons, or the formation of new connections in between existing neurons.
