# Outside factors in eating
**Details**
Sometimes, its not about maintaining a state of homeostasis, sometimes its just about eating for fun, eating because people are around, at certain times of day. Of course, these factors will combine with the internal factors to determine if we should eat or not as well.

And so the attractiveness of food depends on the degree of hunger that we are at, an once again by us potentiating certain responses.
