# The most common misconception in relationships
**Details**
Relationships are not always easy-sailing, people think that the perfect relationship is one that exists without conflicts, without arguments. The truth is much harsher than that, one person may be enmeshing themselves so that there is no conflict, suppressing their own identity or that they are both emotionally detached and the relationship is just by name.

Conflict is necessary because it builds resilience and skills between the two people engaged in it. It allows them to understand and learn that it is safe to present their true selves as they can deal with the fallout and continue being together. Skills are their ability to resolve said problems as effectively and without raising or escalating the conflict.
