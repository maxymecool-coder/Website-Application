# Karl Marx's Dialectical Theory
**Details**
Setting out to answer the question of **"What are the forces driving historical change ?"*** Marx came up with the Dialectical Theory of History. In Marx's theory, every force, concept, "thesis" has an "antithesis" so to say. There are situations where these two things come into conflict which each other, and create something bigger, higher, a "synthesis." For example, a society that is built on total hierarchy (monarchy), this society is of course challenged by those who believe in its opposite (radical equality). Throughout this conflict, they come to a conclusion of a new form of society when one defeats the other or they just birth a new idea.
