# Modern types of economies
**Details**
I would consider these “models” of how different economies function. The most extreme of them can be simply divided into two: the command and the capitalist models. These basically never exist in practice due to how…impractical these extremes maybe. A pure laissez-faire capitalist economy would let individuals and firms decide what to produce and consume without government interference. In reality, even the United States is not pure capitalism. It is market-driven but constrained by government rules, taxes, regulations, and social norms. Firms may decide how many cars to produce, but they face safety standards, emissions standards, labor laws, taxation, and legal constraints. The market decides much, but it operates inside institutional boundaries. The bowling analogy captures this: in constrained capitalism, individuals throw the ball, but government and social rules act as bumpers that keep the system within acceptable limits.

The command economy is the opposite. In this model the government makes the production and allocation decisions. This was pretty much the idea of Karl Marx and how he wanted to run the government with his philosophy of communism. In practice, however, as many economists argue, it is simply too overwhelming a burden for the people governing to make, and they will eventually use it to further their own prospects. It is also overwhelming in the sense that there are simply too many decisions to be made and the government simply cannot make these decisions all by themselves and correctly.
