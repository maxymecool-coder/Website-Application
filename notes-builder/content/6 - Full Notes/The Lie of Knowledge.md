# The Lie of Knowledge
**Details**
Pristine colleges promise the proliferation of knowledge, they promise the best education that the world can offer, but in fact they are merely building a wall, a guided fort around knowledge itself. The guards of the fort take the form of money, of status, and bizarrely, of intelligence. 

It is nothing new that colleges are inherently expensive. For this alone, these institutions should be ashamed of themselves. The deans and presidents of these places should know only embarrassment. In the mirror, they should see only greed. The systems of application and admission also ensure that colleges largely accept individuals of status rather than produce them. Yes, these institutions do offer scholarships for those who have the sufficient "academic ability", but these must be recognized as cruelty, not benevolence. These programs are clunky and even Kafkaesque. Why should manipulating, pushing youth become the default state for education ? Are we really applauding the father who beats five out of six of his children ? If education’s a parent, it’s one that picks a favorite kid—the one with the right pedigree or test score—then thrashes the rest with rejection letters or debt.
