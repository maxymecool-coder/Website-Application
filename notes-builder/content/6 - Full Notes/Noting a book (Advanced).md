# Noting a book (Advanced)
**Details**
- During an analytical read, the reader needs to give answers to the questions about the truth and significance of the book. The notes at this level are not structural but conceptual. They are about the author's concepts and the reader's as they've been deepened by their reading.
- Above it is when a reader is reading several books syntopically. Make notes shaping the discussion of all the authors of the similar books. This type of notes must be done externally as it involves many books ideas together. A structure of concepts is implied, order of statements and questions about one singular subject matter.
