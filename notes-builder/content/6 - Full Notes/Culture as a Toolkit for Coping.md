# Culture as a Toolkit for Coping
**Details**
People do not just cater to a set of values and rules to navigate life and social situations but rather they get it from their cultural understandings and lessons, these may include symbols, stories, rituals, and ways of thinking that are collectively generated, passed down through families, subcultures, and the broader society. These can contradict each other lots but they help the person develop schemas so that they can navigate each life situations. This toolkit can be split neatly into three different areas, the cognitive, the social, and the emotional. Each providing and cooperating with each other different aspects to help the individual.

Depending on the environments and such that a person grow in, they can develop different toolkits that may prove effective or counter effective. An example to say this may be when having a family or a relationship, both of the person involved in these situations act differently depending on their background which can cause disputes or mesh together fine.
