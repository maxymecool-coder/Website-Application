# Projection of the Shadow
**Details**
In our daily lives, we naturally encounter people and situations that would refer to the shadow part of ourselves, and in said situations we inherently reject and even condemn them. Like how we condemn others at the lightest, littlest of interactions, as it includes the "shadow" that we repress, which is unfair and illogical. Even as we do this, at the end of the day, we have to understand that the same concepts exist within us, even if not much or as representative like the people we hate. Another situation is when we lash out at others because of our repressed shadows, parents project shadow onto children through emotional or physical abuse, planting seeds of that same behavior for the next generation.

With this, we can come to the conclusion of why even the best people can participate in much atrocities, it is because they are attacking their own repressed shadows, unrecognizing of the meaning of their actions.
