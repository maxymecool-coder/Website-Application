# The Original Position
**Details**
The sequence of fundamental ideas develops through a natural progression. So now we have to understand how the fair terms of cooperation are determined.

Rawls offers three possibilities. First, the fair terms could be from an external authority, as was done with God in the former times. Second, they could be determined through common moral knowledge and intuition that derive from ourselves. Third is the alternative that Rawls pursues - the parties themselves determine the fair terms by agreeing in an appropriately characterized situation. This is where the social contract idea enters justice as fairness: the original position is offered as a way of specifying how this agreement might work.

Crucially, the parties can also choose from a somewhat menu of conceptions of justice drawn from the history of moral philosophy - two principles of justice, two forms of utilitarianism, intuitionism, and so on. The parties do not generate principles through their own reasoning; they select from a pre-existing list. The original position is thus a selection device, not a generative one. This means that we can only choose to use what is conceivably the best currently, not something superior in the future.
