# Types of reading
**Details**
- There are 3 purposes of reading, for info, for understanding and for entertainment. When reading for information, you aren't actually improving anything that you haven't known before, you're just increasing your knowledge in the parts that you already have. Understanding is when tackled something new, something never seen before, and the author of the piece of reading currently being read actively helps and guides the reader through the process of the piece of information. Lastly, entertainment reading is very light reading, the reader isn't actively trying to consume or digest the content but rather most of the time just reading it for shits and giggles then forgetting it later.
**Supporting Content**
- The identification of knowing the purposes of reading a book is important as it guides the reader through reading one, with them always knowing what they're trying to get from the book and therefore will read it more efficiently/effectively.
