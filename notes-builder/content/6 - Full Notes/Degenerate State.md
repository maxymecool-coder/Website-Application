# Degenerate State
**Details**
To live in a degenerate state is to live in a state of thinking without action. In this state, humanity becomes a mere thinker, or even worse, a parrot of other men's thinking.

Emerson said that once we collectively exist under this state, society puts a lid upon itself, it stops us all from building any higher, of reaching higher heights. And all of the flashy exertions of skills are mere useless knowledge-academia for the sake of academia, not for the sake of intelligence or knowledge itself.

A society in the degenerate state severs its pieces from its whole. The worker, the tradesman, are boxed into their roles. These people are not understood or even considered by anyone of true societal power. Their efforts are not really applauded, and their perspectives are not really considered. The individual accordingly resigns himself to this role. He becomes subject to his own toil, sectioned off from the rest of a society. The farmer becomes the hands, while the academic becomes the brains. But in reality, Emerson notes that a society is one body. We are all connected. The hands are worthless without the brain, and the brain too is worthless without the hands.
