# Why are treatments of Depression different
**Details**
Talking about medications and therapies again, the question here is why are they different ? While medication works from the bottom-up, as they chemically modify the overactive limbic system (amygdala/brainstem), therapies do so from the top-down, strengthening the prefrontal cortex's ability to inhibit emotional reactions.

Steve Maier’s research on learned helplessness in rats provides a biological model for this. Basically when rats cannot control the shock, they get learned helplessness, this is a known concept. But the biological underpinning said concept is how they develop a specific neural pathway where the Ventromedial Prefrontal Cortex (vmPFC) that inhibits the Dorsal Raphe Nucleus (the brainstem's serotonin center). This helps protect them from future stressors, but it may only develop this neural pathway if the rat actually is under stress.

And the same applies for therapies, in therapy you still have to think under stress, problem solve under stress, remind yourself under stress, which helps your brain learn how to regulate it all.
