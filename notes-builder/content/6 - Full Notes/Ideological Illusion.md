# Ideological Illusion
**Details**
Marx views the entire project of political liberalism — including constitutional limits on power, the extension of the franchise, religious toleration, and equality of opportunity — as merely an ideological framework designed to regulate a system of "personal independence" that hides the exploitation behind it. These concepts generate a powerful form of "ideological consciousness," which serves as an illusion that people are free and that the system is fair while the structure is extracting unpaid labor time.

Because of how these liberal rights are boundaries protecting egotistic individuals and their property within this illusory market system, Marx argues that it is not adequate merely to demand a "more just" economic distribution or "fairer rights" but rather to radically move beyond justice entirely, move beyond the needs for these rights entirely.
