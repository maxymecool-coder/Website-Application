# How Easy it is to Go far
**Details**
This decision seemingly exists in our everyday lives. It’s usually called doing the right thing or the easy thing. When you are pissed off and all by somebody, dealing with these emotions and their actions, you can either rage out adequately or calmly talk to them and move on. In these situations, most of the time, people feel threatened, or that their rage is logical, infallible even. And that is why this is so difficult, you are indirectly fighting your own emotions so that you can do “the right way”.
