# The Metaphysics of the Subject and Grammatical Illusion
**Details**
Nietzsche also believed that the slaves revolution depended on a linguistic trick: the exploitation of the Subject-Predicate structure of Indo-European languages. Grammar suggests a distinction between the "doer" or subject, and the "deed" or the predicate. However, the Slaves exploit this grammar to posit and "neutral subject" which is the Soul/Self of the Master behind the action, which is free to choose not to act or to act differently/oppositely. This invention basically allows the Slaves to hold their Masters accountable, especially morally, for being strong, which inherently then turns strength into a sin and weakness into a voluntary virtue.
