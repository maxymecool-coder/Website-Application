# The use of AI in applications
**Details**
AI has grown quite a lot in recent years, and it is undoubtedly going to be utilized in many things, which include scholarship applications. And of course no one actually taboos or completely banishes AI from the process of making an application. It can be used to create ideas, rewrite paragraphs and so on.

Although it has the capability to do so, it is not recommended as AI usually have a very neutral tone, there would be no personality that the examiner would be able to see inside the essay that is written to see the author's personality and such. Moreover, though it is not clear if universities and colleges refuses or forbidden the use of AI in general, they sure do not appreciate the dependence of it by the applicant.
