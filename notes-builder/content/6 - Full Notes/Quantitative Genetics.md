# Quantitative Genetics
**Details**
Quantitative genetics is a field that addresses the first-order questions of genetic influence: _whether_ and _how much_ genetic factors contribute to individual differences in psychological traits. It partitions the variance of a trait within a population into genetic and environmental components. Its primary methods are twin studies (comparing identical and fraternal twins) and adoption studies. The main statistical output is **heritability**, which represents the proportion of phenotypic differences attributable to genetic differences. This approach does not identify specific genes but establishes the overall importance of genetic influence, which has been found to be ubiquitous across nearly all psychological traits.
