# Functionism
**Details**
Putnam then proposed that the brain should instead be defined by its function or the functions of its parts, basically what a part does or how does an action relate to a part of the brain. This is similar to a circuit, we only care about the switch, the power source, and such like that, we do not care about the material of it (it can be copper, carbon, gold, anything for that matter).

He proposed that its not exactly necessary to know about the details of how neurochemicals and all respond to pain but rather that it is a functional state of being that allows us to protect ourselves from further danger, that's its purpose, and that it interacts with other internal states (like memory or fear).

This model has been the dominant model of the brain until now, featured in [[Fodor’s mind model]] and [[Massive Modularity Hypothesis]].
