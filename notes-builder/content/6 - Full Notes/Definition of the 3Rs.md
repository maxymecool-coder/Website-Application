# Definition of the 3Rs
**Details**
Coined by Russell and Burch, these are the aspects and methods in which they think up that would be applicable to reduce the inhumanity in animal testing. We will proceed by defining what 3Rs as a whole is, any definition or characterization of it, that must be “the ways in which inhumanity can be and is being diminished or removed.” Also, these concepts must be in a way so that they do not compromise any of the scientific or medical progress. According to Russell and Burch, the main problem in this community is that they struggle to define what is and what is not humane, and how humanity can be promoted without prejudice to the goals of experimentation.

Firstly, Replacement, "Replacement means the substitution for conscious living higher animals of insentient material. Reduction means reduction in the numbers of animals used to obtain information of a given amount and precision. Replacement means any decrease in the incidence or severity of inhumane procedures applied to those animals which still have to be used (p 64).
