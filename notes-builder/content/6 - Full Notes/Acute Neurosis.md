# Acute Neurosis
**Details**
This is the epitome of the misery caused by psychiatric disorders. In its most flagrant form, this reaction is manifested by the intense, unpleasant experiences. Anything familiar may seem distant, the inner self may be distorted, one may lose the sensation in his limbs or the interior of the body. He may feel heavy or weightless, things before would take on new meanings and significances.

Moreover, he can even lose himself, his perceptions of self, get disorientated, and knowledge of other people, as well as having difficulty in maintaining or framing his thoughts. The extreme form of this disturbance has been labeled a "catastrophic reaction." The person can express his uncanny experience by sentences of familiar things looking weird, feeling the loss of control, etc....
