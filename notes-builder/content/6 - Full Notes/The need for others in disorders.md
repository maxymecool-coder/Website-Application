# The need for others in disorders
**Details**
A point that derived from [[A New World's Theory]] by Carl Rogers, any and every single person who has a mental disorder or a neurobiological illness needs someone to be there for them, which can help them from spiraling even deeper into their illnesses. People with dementia, schizophrenia, a lot of the times they are hard to deal with, a lot of the times they are not the people you knew them to be, you thought they were. But at the end of the day, they are still people, they need relationships, they need company, and so no matter how hard they are to deal with, it is incredibly advised for their close ones to be there for them as loneliness is the basis for even further mental disorders.

Not just in these cases where people are old, or stop being themselves, but also in cases like conduct disorder, where people act knowing it makes them hated, despised. When someone is there for them, believes in them, they can get the encouragement to change themselves, believe in themselves to be better.
