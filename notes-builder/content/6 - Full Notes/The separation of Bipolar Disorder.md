# The separation of BPD
**Details**
The phenomenon of bipolar disorder can be so overwhelming to the patient and the care providers that it can dominate the focus of the therapy. Basically it is simple to forget the person and treat them around the symptoms rather than what those symptoms and all have on the person, forgetting what is hurting them most and the fact that you have to manage and talk it out to them. They are so depersonalized from themselves they do not even know where their mental disorder ends and their own personality begins.

These people need solidarity in their lives when this illness has made it so rocky. And so it is perhaps necessary that therapists start a session by caring about the patient, their daily lives, and so on, rather than just getting into the therapy. Be someone they can trust, be someone that understands their needs and all. Try the best one can to view the person behind the illness, their intelligence, sense of humor, social comfort, interests, sources of pleasure, and so on and so forth.
