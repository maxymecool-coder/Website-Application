# The error argument of EP
**Details**
Evolutionary psychology argues that the generality of Fodor’s modularity means that the brain cannot understand how to identify errors in specific parts and will develop a prevention mechanism for multiple functions, which is inefficient and not present in humanity. Take for example, mating, if the domain-general module perceives mating too much, leading to incest, and so on as an error, it would most likely also expand this concept to other modules, like courtship and such, which would be of course inefficient. This leads to their point that modules are specific and errors are specific to those modules for them to change independently.
