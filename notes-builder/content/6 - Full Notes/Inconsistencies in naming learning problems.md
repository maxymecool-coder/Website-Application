# Inconsistencies in naming learning problems
**Details**
These issues' naming are often inconsistent due to how stigmatized they are, and so some may use general terms like "Learning Disability" all the way to specific Greek-derived symbols. "Dyslexia" may refer to reading difficulties while "alexia" is the loss of it due to brain injury, there are also multiple other instances of this happening. DSM is trying to use LD defined by a failure to achieve academically at a level that one should do at a age and cognitive potential. Distinct from these are communication disorders, such as Expressive Language Disorder (difficulty producing speech) and Receptive Language Disorder (difficulty understanding speech), which involve the processing of language rather than the specific academic skill of reading or math.

The theory of where these disabilities originated from is rooted in evolution, as humans evolved for biological mechanisms for speech, reading is a recent invention, only about 5000 years ago. And we don't have a center in our brain for this, but rather just a jumble of other systems to replicate the function. The problem is also exacerbated with countries which have difficult languages, exposing genetic vulnerabilities in phonological processing.
