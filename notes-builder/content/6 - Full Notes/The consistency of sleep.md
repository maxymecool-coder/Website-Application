# The consistency of sleep
**Details**
It is nothing new that consistency will assist to the effectiveness and restfulness of sleep, it is generally advise for people to not waking too much later than their usual schedule by about an hour. 

When you do have an off-day when you stay up late and tend to wake up later, you would want to only stay in of course, that one hour range. If you're still feeling tired after, you should refer to NSDR, meditation and so on.
