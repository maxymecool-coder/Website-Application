# Stereotypes and Prejudices
**Details**
Stereotype is a belief or expectation about a group of people. Prejudice is an unfavorable attitude towards a group of people. It is usually associated with discrimination, which is unequal treatment of different groups. It is very easy for us to develop false stereotypes because of our tendency to remember bad or unusual things that people do. For example, if a girl who's a person of authority in your class badmouths you, you are gonna have a tendency to hate those who are of authority, or someone of the same thing.

Contrarily to many beliefs, whenever we say that culture influences behavior, we imply that people of this stereotype behave differently on the average and that it is partly correct that.
