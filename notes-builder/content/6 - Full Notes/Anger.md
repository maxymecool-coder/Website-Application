# Anger
**Details**
The general meaning of anger is probably generally known, but the depressive factors are the things we need to consider as it can also lead to anger, together with fight-or-flight, and etc... Another familiar situation is the frustration of a wish or drive, which proposes that someone would become angry when the frustrating agent is unjustifiable and is attributed to a person's incompetence or something of the sorts. For example, a husband who generally becomes angry is his dinner is not ready when he comes home will not be annoyed if he discovers his wife is too sick to preapre the meal.
