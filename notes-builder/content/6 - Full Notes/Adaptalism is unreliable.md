# Adaptalism is unreliable
**Details**
In the earlier days, biologists, evolutionary, argued that any trait can be explained by them being an optimized evolutionary trait, no need for experimental evidence or anything, just who had the better story of how a trait came to be. This was clearly a vulnerability in the concept, as proven by spandrels, which are traits that are not necessarily optimized for the specie but rather just a trait that is there. For example, the human chin, we are the only primate to have it, and that is because our evolutionary process consisted of a foreshortened muzzle and a particular jaw angle.
