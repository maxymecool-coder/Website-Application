# Definition
Typically you would note things done then build connections, after that you would have ideas from those connections and start continue building from that, then you review it, notice some holes or missing info in your arguments. Then you add to that... and repeat. Every time you make more notes, your knowledge increase, if you keep filling holes it will solve the problem of having too many notes lying around as you know what topics has been the most fleshed out and makes the most sense to choose.
