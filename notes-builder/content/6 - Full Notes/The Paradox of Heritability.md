# The Paradox of Heritability
**Details**
There is a central paradox in the findings of quantitative genetics. High heritability estimates are used to prove the importance of genes. However, the same data provides the strongest evidence for the importance of the environment. For example, if identical twins have a 45% concordance for schizophrenia, this means that in 55% of cases, these genetically identical individuals have different outcomes. This discordance can _only_ be explained by non-genetic (i.e., environmental) factors. Therefore, heritability studies simultaneously establish the influence of genetics while also highlighting the profound impact of the nonshared environment.
