# To not be scared of drafting
**Details**
When writing, it is important to keep in mind that your first version is only your first draft. It is only writing down some ideas for one self, and then turning it into something publishable later, and so that encourages us to actually write on it. By the time we stopped writing, we would be surprised to find that the only thing left to do was revise the draft already had (or do more revisioning and drafting, depends.). 

Also another advice is not to be afraid to delete what has no function within an argument, which is pretty hard once we get sentimental with all our piece of writing. To bypass this, just move it to another document and tell ourselves we’ll use it later, for something else. More often than not, we’ll forget and just never touch these things ever again.
