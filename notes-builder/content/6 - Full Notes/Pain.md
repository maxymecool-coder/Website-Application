# Pain
**Details**
Pain is extremely important, as it act as the sign for us as humans to basically act and defend ourselves, of course it also has a deep correlation to depression and anxiety as well. It is both a mixture of sensation (tissue damage) and emotion (unpleasant reaction). Both of these qualities depend on different area, the anterior cingulate cortex responds to the emotional pain. Even though the physical pain may be intense at the moment it happens, it is the emotional pain that sticks with us. To exemplify this, try to think of a time you got beaten by your dad or was humiliated in front of a group of people.
