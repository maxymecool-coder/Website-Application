# Getting Stuck in Thinking
**Details**
There are two ways one can get stuck whilst doing this. When someone is learning something new, they can get stuck by not understanding the information that hey are being taught. Unfortunately, one cannot get through this easily since its a problem about their own knowledge and familiarity on the subject and they will just need to go restudy it.

The second way is when diffused mode may actually come in handy. This is when someone has already understood everything but they still cannot solve the problem. This is because the diffused mode has not had a chance to overview the problem about all the connections yet. To ensure that you finish your homework, one can diffuse into another subject rather than full rest (still though, take a break !)

A good judge of time when you need diffuse mode is when you have been sitting on a problem for at least 10 to 15 minutes. Then take a short break or something of the sorts.
