# Depression as an Adaptation
**Details**
Evolutionary biologist Paul Andrews proposes that depression is not a biological malfunction, but an evolved adaptation designed to solve complex social problems. As how a fever is actually designed to fight against viruses and pain is to protect tissues, the "psychic pain" we feel from depression is actually the body pulling energy in order to refill "rumination".

Now, rumination allows us to imagine and problem-solve as well right ? (According to the "Analytical Rumination Hypothesis"). If we medicate, we are stalling or undermining the effects of depression (not that it shouldn't be get rid of) but this may result in the problem persisting through one's life due to how it was unresolved. Social problems are worst at the peak of adolescence/young adulthood (mating and social hierarchy formation).
