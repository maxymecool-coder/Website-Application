# Diffused Mode
**Details**
This is the mode that allows people to gain new perspectives and outlooks on a problem, it gives a "big picture" of what the problem is. This happens when you relax and let your mind wander the possibilities, all the different aspects of the problem ? Who gives a shit ? Just let it wander through all the possible things that might be the solution to it. It derives from focused mode, which means the thinking that has already been established in that mode helps to kickstart things in here. In a game of pinball, this is basically the bumpers being so far from each other that thee ball travel significantly larger distances, it doesn't allow you to think of the whole solution to the problem, but it allows you to get closer to it, which is recommended for new problems. The goal of the ball here is not to reach the solve, since it doesn't know where it is, but instead, graze it, have a feel of where it is.

There are certain principles about how we should determine a diffused mode activity. Some are more effective than others, with activities like sleeping, exercising, or going for a ride helps the most.
