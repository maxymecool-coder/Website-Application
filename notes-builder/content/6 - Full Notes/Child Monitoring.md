# Child Monitoring
**Details**
One of the difficulties in parenting is child monitoring, or more specifically, the degree of it. As the child grow, one cannot treat or monitor them as if they are still a child when they are a teenager and so on, but nonetheless it is difficult to assess how much monitoring one actually needs. Over monitoring stifles the child's autonomy and results in rebellion or secrecy. Under monitoring results in them maybe getting into groups of delinquents, drug use, or risky behavior.

The key to understand this is disclosure. Disclosure here basically means the child sharing the information about their lives to their parents openly, without them needing to ask or request. This helps the parent to adjust accordingly to what the children might need, if they openly share, you monitor less, and the same applies for the other. But before all this, there needs to be a stage of scaffolding that occurs, meaning the parent has to still already have a degree of monitor already there, just that it needs to be removed or added correspondingly.
