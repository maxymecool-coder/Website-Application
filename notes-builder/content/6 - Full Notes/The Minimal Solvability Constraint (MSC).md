# The Minimal Solvability Constraint (MSC)
**Details**
The MSC states that for any proposed cognitive architecture to be considered a valid hypothesis, it must, in principle, be capable of producing the "minimally adaptive behavior in ancestral environments". This means the proposed design must be able to solve all problems that were essential for survival and reproduction during the Pleistocene era. The justification for this constraint is straightforward: humans have survived, which would have been impossible unless our minds could produce the behaviors necessary for survival and reproduction in that ancestral environment.
