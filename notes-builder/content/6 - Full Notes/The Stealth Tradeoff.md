# The Stealth Tradeoff
**Details**
The design of the F-22 Raptor and its materials and paint allows it to be practically invisible on radars, nonetheless this is achieved by a compromise between electrical engineers prioritizing low observability and aeronautical engineers prioritizing flight characteristics. Now, to achieve this level of stealth, you would need to have a planform composed of specific angles and a lack of right angles to scatter the radar signals from the source. But this would lead to an airframe that is aerodynamically unstable. The aircraft would then need a digital flight control system to impose stability and make adjustments where humans cannot.
