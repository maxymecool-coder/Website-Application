# The Necessary Embedding
**Details**
Transitioning away from the [[Old Philosophical Culture of the Analytic Mainstream]], philosophers, notably Herder, critique that the concept of treating these concepts and propositions as pure mental entities, ignoring the influences of languages and such on the human cognition. Because language is a social mechanism built through shared history, any philosophy that takes it seriously must abandon strict individualism. And so to understand human cognition, one must look at these structure that handle it, taking those structures away would isolate the mind from the community and what built it. Marx was a part of this paradigm.
