# The cause of Mental Disorders
**Details**
To treat a person with a mental disorder, it is usually necessary for one to identify the cause or something that is maintaining the disorder, or just the a strong group of symptoms of it. When going about identifying these things, we must consider if the factor that is being examined is antecedent or concurrent with the mental illness for us to categorize it. For example, in a panic disorder, of course the amygdala is always in high alert and sensitive, so what will we consider this ? Was the amygdala this overly sensitive before the panic disorder ? Or is it only doing this now WITH the disorder in play ?
