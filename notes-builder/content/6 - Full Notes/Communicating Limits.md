# Communicating Limits
**Details**
Limits is a very sensitive topic in between people, anyone as a fact, not just certain people with mental illnesses. And so for that, one must have ideas on how to handle these situations more effectively.

For example, you have to be strong, don’t bend, don’t change to their limits, including validation along the way (validating how hard it is to withdraw from something they’ve been doing dor some time). Remember, limits are for your own wellbeing, not your love one. Your life would be better if you limited someone from screwing or doing something to it after all.

Another way is to offer them another choice or just something that resembles what they are asking for but in a better time and place. Like offering to talk to them the next morning rather than middle of the night (speaking from personal experience you don’t wanna be waiting scared that they need you at midnight).

If it is still too hard, you might want to remind yourself that giving in will reinforce their escalated emotions (meaning the next time they want something they would do the same thing). Also keep in mind that it might be painful to hear their pain which might make you reconsider which is a bad decision.
