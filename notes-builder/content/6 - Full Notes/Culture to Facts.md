# Culture to Facts
**Details**
By viewing social constructionism, we can understand how these values come to become such a staple in the culture of these countries. Essentially, they argue that knowledge and reality are constructed across historical and cultural contexts through social interactions, and are passed down from generation to generation by word of mouth (same case as how folk lore gets passed down). Over time, they gain factual existence and people start taking those ideas as facts, being internalized into the culture and the later generations without realizing how they are just ideas.
