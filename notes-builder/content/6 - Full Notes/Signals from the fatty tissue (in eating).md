# Signals from the fatty tissue (in eating)
**Details**
Remember those fatty acids that were mentioned ? Neither do I, but these are crucial to this part, as the fatty tissue or adipose cells of the body absorb these and swell. These fatty tissues are then used as longer-term reserves, even longer than glycogen.

More than that, they also generate a chemical called leptin, which when secrete into the bloodstream, is sensed by receptors in the hypothalamus and areas near the brain's ventricles. Some has theorized that this chemical is what announces to the brain to stop eating.
