# Ease of Invasion in DBT
**Details**
A skills trainer or therapist using DBT must realize the effects that the external environment can have on the participants. This is why communicating with other participants outside the therapy hours are so forbidden, they can make the closed-loop environment yield unpredictable results, or even mistakes. For example, two people deepen their relationship outside of therapy can one day get into an argument which may result in one or the other refusing to talk or have tension in the session, something the therapy did not account for and so would yield different results. The psychologist managing must then remember to account for these factors as well to effectively continue the session.
