# The Cultural Context of Eating Disorders
**Details**
Depending on how a culture views the ideal body weight and such,  we can understand what kind of eating may be classified as an eating disorder in that society. For example, in the U..S., the ideal is "thin" whilst the main problem they have is an obesity epidemic, and so they may drive for thinness, which may be even heightened in professions that rely on that factor. Clinically, "Anorexia Nervosa" is the syndrome characterized by a refusal to maintain body weight and distortion of body image despite normal appetite. "Bulimia Nervosa" involves a cycle of bingeing followed by compensatory behaviors, which can include purging (vomiting, laxatives) or non-purging methods like extreme exercise.

A controversial addition is Binge Eating Disorder, which is binging without compensating. A big problem in diagnosing is of over labeling or under labeling those with these disorders, as they can present all the symptoms but not meet the requirement of losing body weight.
