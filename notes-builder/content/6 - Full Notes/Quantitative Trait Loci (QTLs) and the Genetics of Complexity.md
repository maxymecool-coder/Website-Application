# Quantitative Trait Loci (QTLs) and the Genetics of Complexity
**Details**
The QTL perspective is fundamental to modern molecular genetics. It posits that complex traits and common disorders are not the result of a single gene, but of the cumulative, additive effects of many genes, each with a small impact. This means there is no single "gene for" a complex trait like intelligence or personality. Instead, we all inherit a unique combination of alleles that contribute probabilistically to our traits. This perspective suggests that many disorders are simply the quantitative extremes of dimensions that vary across the entire population, blurring the line between normal and abnormal.
