# Deliberate Practice
**Details**
- Deliberate practice is learning something with a clear view, goal, method towards it. You have to be laser-focused on by figuring out the deliberate aspects that you want to improve in them and constantly ways to improve them. This is different from normal learning as you are actively trying to improve everything rather than just doing one thing until you are good at it. This type of practice is stressful and users will need much rest after a few hours of it.

**Supporting Content**
- Deliberate practice is never fun as you are constantly failing to find out the right and the most effective method for yourself. You have to push yourself out of the comfort zone, whenever your brain feels stress means that its experiencing something new and wants to go back to its comfy space.
