# The most important food in dieting
**Details**
People usually talk about foods in diets that are necessary for those diets, like how nutritious and how it fits into the diet, which ignores the psychology and motivation required to even follow said diets. When one is following a diet, it is difficult as they are working off their willpower to get themselves to cater to the diet. This inherently tires their mental capacity and to maintain it they would need to include something to reward themselves of.

This is the most important part of a diet, reward. People need snacks to refuel their mental capacity and continue dieting, and so as a part of the diet, the same can also be said for snacking and all.

The same can be said for writing, or for learning, if you do not reward yourself sufficiently with something like break time for video games and snacks and so on, you will run out of energy and motivation to do anything, let alone do work.
