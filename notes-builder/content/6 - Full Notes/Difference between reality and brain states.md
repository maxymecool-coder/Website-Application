# Difference between reality and brain states
**Details**
Brain states are normally categorized by its complexity, by how many neurons our brain has, the reactions that happens, the complex chemical gradients that happen in it to then produce a brain state. Yet, said complexity is only used to then attribute to something so simple in reality like the redness of a rose. And so it is a significant hurdle for physicalists theorists about the "commensurability problem".

The discrepancy raises a question about how all that complexity can produce the sensation of redness. It seems impossible to logically derive the simple sensation from the complex mechanism; they appear to be different categories of reality. Same as the fact that no amount of complexity of an equation can generate a shade of blue.
