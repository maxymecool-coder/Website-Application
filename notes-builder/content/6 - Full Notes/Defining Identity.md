# Defining Identity
**Details**
If we think about defining identity via the brain or psychology, we encounter many problems, one revealed through the "Star Trek" Transporter thought experiment, or like Scorn (the horror game) where if a transporter scans you, destroys your body, and rebuilds you on Mars, is it still you. Let's say it malfunctions and duplicates you, now there can't be two of you, so you would have to say that it was killing you and just making a copy of you.

Derek Parfit resolved this by arguing that we are asking the wrong question. This is survival, not identity, and so we have to abandon the strict concept of "Personal Identity" just as how we abandoned the "Soul." The only thing that matters is that our mental life continue, not the metaphysical sense.
