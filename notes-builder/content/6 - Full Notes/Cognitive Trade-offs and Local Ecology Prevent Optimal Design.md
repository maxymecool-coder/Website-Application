# Fodor’s argument against EP (Engineering)
**Details**
In his book, he presents an argument against EP model is that since there are trade-offs, it prevents the modules to become maximized along all possible aspects, and that the fitness of a cognitive system depends on its local ecology, therefore a single optimal system does not exist.

In the case of psychological traits, in order to use optimality considerations with any confidence one needs to know (a) what features were being optimized by the evolutionary process and (b) what range of phenotypes were available to natural selection. As a matter of fact, however, we have little knowledge about either of these matter
