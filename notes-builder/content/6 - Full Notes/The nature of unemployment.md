# The nature of unemployment
**Details**
In different societies, different values and sets of rules exist, as well as cultures, and so people react differently in the same situations. Considering unemployment for example, the American society is inherently built on the independency of its citizen, and so when someone is unemployed, they personally feel that it is their responsibility and their own failure, talking down and spiraling down, only making it harder to get out of the hole.

On the other hand, considering Israeli, they blame their government, they blame the system, which only prompts them to get into protests and overthrow.
