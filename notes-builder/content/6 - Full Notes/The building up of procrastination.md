# The building up of procrastination
**Details**
When we have to do something hard and we dislike, our brain physically experiences pain, not the kind of pain of neuroplasticity though. This makes us unmotivated to work and put it back a few minutes, or a day, or a night. But continue with this cycle and eventually the procrastination builds up and stops us from doing something, or anything at all.

There are though, efficient methods so that we can avoid procrastination, such as Pomodoro, or just doing something one likes (that requires some effort) and then transitioning into actual work.
