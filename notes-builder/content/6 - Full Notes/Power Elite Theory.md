# Power Elite Theory
**Details**
This theory states that basically the political systems of countries such as the US are ruled over by a small, cohesive, and often hidden clique of individuals. They would control everything behind the scenes, all the decisions and so on.

The landmark research to proved this was done by Floyd Hunter in his study of Atlanta (Community Power Structure), asking informants and then analyzing those who are in their answers. There are many characteristics for this group, founded by different sociologists, who found out that they are socially connected with each other. Going to the same elite schools, attending events, playing with each other perhaps and so on. This creates a shared worldview as well as tying their interests together.

A more complex version of the theory was advanced by C. Wright Mills, who argued the power elite was composed of leaders from three interlocking institutional domains: the corporate elite, the political elite, and the military-industrial complex.

Though these studies have a glaring problem, they do not demonstrate power, they just show that there exist connections between the powerful.
