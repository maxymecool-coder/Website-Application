# The Relation and Individuated Self
**Details**
In the human being, learners of the psychodynamic of Freud came up with the theory of the relation self and individuated self, explaining the parts of the human being.

Firstly, the relation self, which is created from the connections between others, like the love, like, and so on. The other is the individuated self, which is the self that contains all the values and abilities and all of the human being.

Linehan, using this theory, applied it to those who had BPD, stating that their two selves are going against and destroying each other, not working together and intertwining in the suitable situations. For example, the overly clingy and loving part is fueled by the relation self, desperate to have someone whilst the other self batters down on the same person to protect their own values.
