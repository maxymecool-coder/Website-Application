# Types of Samples
**Details**
As we all know statistics are used to gather data and use said data to comment on what happens in the population, or just used to describe the characteristics of the sample.

This same relationship divides statistics into two branches. **Descriptive statistics** stay inside the sample: you summarise what you actually measured — the range, the mean, the standard deviation, a histogram. It is always the first step: "I have a pile of data; let me look at it." **Inferential statistics** reach beyond the sample: you analyse the sample in order to draw conclusions about the population and test hypotheses. Put simply, descriptive _describes what you have_; inferential _infers what you can't see_.
