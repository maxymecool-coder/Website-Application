# Self-Discrepancy Theory and Emotional Nuance
**Details**
While Wicklund’s theory successfully explains the basic aversion to self-focus, Tory Higgin’s Self-Discrepancy theory provides the nuance for exactly how we fall short of our standards. Higgins argue that there are two selves we are constantly comparing our real self to: the ideal and the ought. The former represents our ambitions while the latter represents our obligations. Failing to live up to the former may make you sad but the latter makes you outright anxious due to the possibility of group expulsion, corresponding to our evolutionary triggers.

Though it is effective in these situations, for when we are performing functions such as athletic and sports, it’s not particularly helpful for us to consider everything and reflect alike this. The brain understands that deeply and functions accordingly so.
