# Tobacco Roles
**Details**
In the past, or specifically proposed by Dale Pendell in _Pharmako/Poeia_, Tobacco is considered not only Thanatopathia (the "Death Plant.") but also a healer and killer, ally and seducer, stimulant and tranquilizer. While it is the most dangerous plant regularly consumed by humans, being able to kill us in a mere single pack (if administered all at once) it is also revered as the primary shamanic plant of the world.

The plant was discovered in history in Europe even before food plants, while in the Americas, the indigenous people use it as a spiritual tool, as if it was connecting them to the earth whenever they have a "flighty" nature of anxiety. It can be administered all kinds of ways. These people use it by drinking tobacco infusions or administering enemas to induce near-death states for spirit communication.
