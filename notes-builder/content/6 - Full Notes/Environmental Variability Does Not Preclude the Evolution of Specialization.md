# The rebuttal of EP in engineering
**Details**
Specialization does not mean maximization, and trade-offs are a central point to the evolutionary analyses of cognition, as they are if biological systems more generally. Instead, they evolve to sacrifice generality to increase all the other aspects: speed, effectiveness, reliability.

For his argument about the environment specificity, EP argues that these systems evolve for processing patterns associated with conditions that are invariant across generations and lifetimes. And plastic systems are the one modifiable within broader constraints and for variable information patterns.
