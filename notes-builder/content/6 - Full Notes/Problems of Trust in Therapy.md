# Trust
**Details**
We must establish that if the trust between the patient and therapist isn't done carefully, it may result in either the complete acceptance and ingestion of the patient (which ruins the whole process of therapy) as it is supposed to be a critical evaluation by the patient.

On the complete opposite side of the spectrum, the type of patient who automatically react to the therapist's statements with suspicion or skepticism. Such a reaction is the clearest in paranoid and severely depressed patient. In an attempt to expose and recalibrate the thinking system of the patient, the therapist may become mired in their deeply entrenched belief system. They are often concerned that their problems will persist or get worse despite of the treatment. And so for example if the therapist responds with optimism, they may think that he is faking it, or doesn't understand the gravity of the situation, or is just incompetent.
