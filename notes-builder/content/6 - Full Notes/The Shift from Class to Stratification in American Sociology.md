# The Shift from Class to Stratification in American Sociology
**Details**
The inequality theory was approached by the American through the usual lens of class, as distinct groups in conflict or competition. But furthermore, this approach evolved into stratification. It instead reconceives society not as discrete classes but a ladder of occupational prestige. This instead focuses on an individual’s life chances and the mechanisms of social mobility rather than the collective group action. Which became the dominant framework in the USA.
