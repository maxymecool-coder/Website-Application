# Constituent Power and the Fiduciary Trust
**Details**
So where does Locke come to ? The radical separation of the Constituent Power and Ordinary Power. Ordinary power is simply the day-to-day administrative, executive, and legislative authority exercised by the established offices of the government, strictly within the boundaries of the existing constitution. Constituent power, however, is the supreme, unalienable, and perpetual authority of the people themselves to determine the very form that the constitution will take. This closely resembles the [[Justification as Public, Practical, and Distinct from Moral Truth]] of John Rawls's philosophical framework. Basically, the community now exercises its constituent power by placing a fiduciary trust into a specific legislative structure. People can still revoke this power though, depending on how their ruler/regime acts.
