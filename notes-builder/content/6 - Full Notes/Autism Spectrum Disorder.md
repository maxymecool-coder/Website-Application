# Autism Spectrum Disorder
**Details**
ASD affects about 1 in 88 children around the world, and although some people with autism have an unnatural ability for certain subjects, others still suffer from its symptoms quite a lot. Starting with the social and emotional disturbances, autism can cause children to have profound problems with the social world, meaning they are gonna act really weirdly whilst socializing, if you can call that socializing at all. They also suffer from joint attention, which is they have a problem with communicating or paying attention to one person in a mutual activity. Weird symptom but they usually focus on the mouth (lower face) rather than something like the eyes (upper face).

These children also suffer from communication deficits. Babbling, for example, which is usually used to describe the nonsensical sounds that infants make before they begin to use words, is less frequent than those with ASD and conveys less information as well (Ricks, 1972). ASD children also lag behind on the language development process. They also suffer from echolalia, which means they are gonna repeat whatever the person is saying to them, indicating that they do not understand what the other is saying. Together with echolalia, they also suffer from pronoun reversals.

A "symptom" that sometimes can turn into a blessing is repetitive and ritualistic acts that these children perform, they can keep their habits of these things up to even when they are adults, and they would become extremely talented in that one specific thing.

**Supporting Content**
Children with ASD may even have an undeveloped "theory of mind", they will not be able to recognize that other people have a different understanding/view of the world than them.
