First having a storage/bank of ideas. When you have written a lot of material and so many notes of different information then when you take a look at those notes maybe then you will have a new idea at what to write to connect 2 vastly different notes of one topic.
Looking bottom up from your system to see what questions, ideas, projects pops up that are currently unfulfilled or hasn't been answered yet. Read more on those topics to continue strengthening, adding to your arguments, take notes on those resources that you found. Just continue developing and see where things takes you, prioritize your interests and hobbies. When a topic gets slow and one with more momentum presents itself ? Follow it ! Maybe your interests have changed over time.
