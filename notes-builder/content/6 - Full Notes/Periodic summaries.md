# Periodic summaries
**Details**
It has been discussed that we need a final summary at the end of every therapy session, but there also exists periodic summaries to use in session, like when you want to summarize an issue or goal that you have discussed about for the client to interpret in their own way what they understood and regarded as most important from it. But you will also do this, to make sure that you understand what they are talking about and everything. Remember, validation, and you should try using their words or words extremely similar to it to do so.
