# The methodology of using AI for notes
**Details**
Clearly, the Zettelkasten system is something extremely personal as it synthesizes and organize the user's own interpretation of the information they absorb in their daily life. This means that using AI mindlessly is completely against its principle (as any other abusive practices of AI does). But we also recognize that Zettelkasten is a grueling system to maintain, capturing immense amounts of information and then synthesizing them is a tiring process at times.

So to combat this, AI can still be used, take for example when the user is not in the mindset to take extensive notes on something, the AI can then take the information through a transcript of sorts, and then synthesize the main ideas, or arguments and then the user can come back to those ideas and then expand on them himself.

Of course this is the same as the user capturing such ideas himself, but the point is, it can be better as a lot of the times, the user can be tired and miss the point of the lecture completely, only hearing bits and pieces of it, but a main idea would help wring all those bits and pieces back into a coherent note.
