# Ventral vagal system
**Details**
The VVC in us evolves to support our complex social life. The more it efficiently synchronizes with the activity of the nervous systems, the better the physiology of individuals will be attuned to that group. The VVC can reward us immensely, it can foster a deep sense of pleasure and connections through us mimicking each other, being “in tune” so to say. But when a trauma event happens, most of the time it is because the VVC isn’t working, you’re screaming and begging but the assailant ignore your pleas, the father’s beating the screaming mother despite knowing and hearing her pain. This is when DVC kicks in, which slows your entire body down, dissociates you from reality and makes you faint, perhaps collapse.
