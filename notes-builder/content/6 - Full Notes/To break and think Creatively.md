# To break and think Creatively
**Details**
The advices that are included in this are generally not included in the slip-box, and so I will do so here. 

First is to make sure that you see what you think you see and describe it as plainly and factually as possible. Of course we need to check if we’re unsure. Our perception doesn’t follow the order of seeing first and interpreting second, it follows both at the same time. And so we don't even notice stuffs that are missing in our perception, in our vision. So we need to trick ourselves into seeing what we don’t. Try to make it so that our reinterpretation or the detection of missing information comes after we have seen the image. 

This can be applied to our reading, we don’t see the words in our books as merely words, but parts of a sentence to build up the meaning. And so to understand it we need to constantly revise our first interpretation of it, hold back the urge to jump to conclusions as well. 

As we encounter things that have already proven to not work, most of the time we don’t actually see them. But when we do it is actually pretty effective to try and come up with new ideas that do work. 

(A way to do this is to ask counterfactual questions, like “what if?”. Like to learn the value of money in a world without it rather than focusing on the obvious we already have in our money-driven world.)
