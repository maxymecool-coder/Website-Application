# Capitalism's Final Contradiction and the Proletariat Revolution
**Details**
Due to how the capitalist society strips individuals of everything they stood for, their family, nationality, skills, abilities, individualism, it inherently places them into a singular class.

The conflict of the thesis and antithesis exists due to the “social relations” of the capitalist world holding the productive power back. What fuels the capitalist class and then them making jobs for others to do work is profit. And so when a business does not make profit anymore, these companies stop, pause. This has been seen by Marx in his time. Factories shutting down, people desperate for jobs, raw materials piling up, consumers needing products to survive, this was the state of the economic nightmare that was the Great Depression, and what Marx saw were the “fetters” that would incite a “burst asunder.”

He believed that when the class altogether saw that these companies, factories that are basically their masters, that they have no choice to work in are actually institutions that are theirs, not the capitalist class, as it is their productive power, they would break down the gates and bring a new age. The synthesis would be a new mode of production, unalienated, where people would work, not because they are forced to and exploited to, but because they flourish from it.
