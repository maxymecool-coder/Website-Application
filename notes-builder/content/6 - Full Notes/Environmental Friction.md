# Environmental Friction
**Details**
We all want to get better and achieve our goals, but the realization of those goals is heavily restricted by the amount of friction we have to go through to perform the necessary actions. To rectify this, we must act as architects of our environment. We can reduce the friction for positive habits by preparing tools in advance (e.g., laying out clothes the night before) and cleaning up after we are done so the space is ready for next time. Conversely, the opposite is true for bad habits: we must intentionally introduce friction to make bad habits difficult to perform. Note that a noisy, cluttered environment inherently adds friction, making focused work significantly more difficult and discouraging.
