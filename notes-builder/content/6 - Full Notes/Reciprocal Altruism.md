# Reciprocal Altruism
**Details**
This is the final building block to fully understanding the cooperation in between species and between groups in a specie. Animals, all species, tend to cooperate with each other to help each other survive, get a better chance at reproduction, although it usually comes with a caveat, that caveat is that the work that they do must be less than the result that they can get.

The nature of the cooperation may lead to situations where one animal decides to cheat as it is the optimal strategy, not doing the work that it is supposed to do and rather just trying to get the fruitful success. Because of this, animals have developed systems for them to identify and understand when one is cheating, so perhaps they can avoid or even punish that act. Much of complex social behavior revolves around this dynamic of managing cooperation, cheating, and vigilance.
