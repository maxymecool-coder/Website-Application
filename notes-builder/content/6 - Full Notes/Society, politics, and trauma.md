# Society, politics, and trauma
**Details**
People think that these things can be and should be separated from each other. But that doesn’t makes sense, politics influence heavily the society that we live in, and that very society is what is causing trauma to happen all throughout the world. 

In a capitalistic world similar to the one that we are living in, our needs of being cared and treated mentally are usually dismissed, instead we are labeled as outsiders of society, that we are different, when it is the very thing that made us like this. And capitalism also doesn’t care enough to solve this problem, psychiatrists these days would rather give patients pills and instructions rather than help them to understand how, why and such to stop the sensations, the feelings that kills them from the inside. Though the patients may live, they may be a shell, an emotionless corpse that is walking day by day. I’m not saying there are not cases where medications didn’t solve the problem, thing is in most of such cases is it the will to live, relationships and changes in the patient that fosters the result, not the medications themselves (although patients have told me that medications helps “bonk” the invading feelings, I think that there’s enough scientific evidence to prove its more of their own efforts and the people around them). 

This case is also present in other places of society, at school, where children are bullied and scolded for their actions instead of understood and guided to grow and learn from it all, to feel and to be safe. Clinics if anything only narrows the PTSD diagnosis down to soldiers who come back from the military, not those who experience it in their daily lives. 

I believe that trauma is a major problem that needs to be addressed like so many before it, but our society doesn’t recognize nor accept it due to its very nature… though I can hope that one day we will become more empathetic one day, that is still merely a dream.
