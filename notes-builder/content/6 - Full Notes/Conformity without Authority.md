# Conformity without Authority
**Details**
What drives conformity ? Two distinct mechanisms: informational influence operates when situations are ambiguous, Sherif's autokinetic effect had groups estimate movement of a stationary light dot in a dark room, and within trials, individuals' wildly divergent estimates converged to a shared group norm. Due to the ambiguity, people believed others might possess better information, and so they internalized that the group answers were correct, which reduced anxiety. Another follow-up study demonstrated the extent of this effect even further, replacing group members one by one across 20 rounds; by round 20, no original members remained, yet the arbitrary norm established by the first three persisted. Normative influence on the other hand, operates when the correct answer is obvious: Asch showed subjects a line-matching task with an unambiguous correct answer. And you know what happens next. The majority giving the false answer making the minority do the same thing out of fear of social rejection.
