# Classical conditioning
**Details**
This term consists of actions and things that happens correspondingly to each other. For example, in the famous Pavlov experiment, where he repeatedly rang the bell and had food leading to salivation of the dog (even when unaccompanied by food). This led him to a finding, a distinction between unconditioned and conditioned reflexes. Unconditioned reflexes are in born, which means they existed in the organism without being taught. And in contrast, conditioned reflexes, which were acquired through learning, upon experience.

And so we now lead back to the stimulus, a hard-wired connection to the responses. And of course, same as two types of responses, there are two type of stimulus. Now knowing these concepts, we will try to describe the process of the above mentioned example. Firstly the CS (bell) is a neutral stimulus, then it comes to do so and elicit a CR (salivation), some of the CS are followed by the US (food in mouth).
