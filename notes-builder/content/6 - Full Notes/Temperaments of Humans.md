# Temperaments of Humans
**Details**
Temperament is basically a person's reactivity and self-regulation abilities that is genetically-based, shows early and is relatively stable. While Thomas and Chess originally identified nine dimensions (activity, mood, adaptability, etc.), Mary Rothbart refined these into six, highlighting **Effortful Control** as a critical dimension.

Effortful Control basically means the ability to suppress a dominant need to perform a subdominant one (eg managing impulses to then do work, focus on homework,...). Other factors that matter include emergence, when it happens, predictive factors, what can be understood from it and so on.
