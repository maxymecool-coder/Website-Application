# Institutions shaping Identities
**Details**
In our society, something we take for granted is just how much our environment shape us to be. We underthink about how much it really does so much, we do not realize the sheer amount of influence it has on our lives, from our relationships to our education and so on. These ideas influence us in different ways, from our friends to our marriage choices. It bleeds in from education to your own families.

For example, a large scale example is the difference in the education of America and France. In America, you get second chances, you fail, you fail a lot, and you will learn from those mistakes and build upon it to get better. Unlike systems with rigid national exams, a person can change their trajectory late in life (e.g., decide to go to medical school at 24 after doing nothing since high school). This institutional design reinforces a core American belief in individual reinvention and the idea that one's fate is not permanently fixed at a young age. 

But in France ? In France people use high-stakes national examinations at young ages (11, 14, 18) to sort students into fixed educational and career tracks. Failure on these exams can permanently close off opportunities like university or medical school. This structure creates a different sense of self and fate, where one's potential is officially evaluated and decided early on. It also fosters a culture where elite status is clearly defined and credentialed by the state.

Another would be friendship groups, in America, they would usually be a big group who would be incredible close with each other for a really long time, a lifetime perhaps. They would have normal disagreements sure but it would not define their relationship a lot. These people also invite each other to more personal activities.

What about Europeans ? These people have less friends in a group, rather just a few. They do not have obligations with each other, they don't just invite each other to go to personal activities and such as well.
