# The feeling of losing control
**Details**
As mentioned before, medications can help dampen the emotional responses of traumatized patients. But sometimes they also make them feel like they have no control.

These medications primary effect is that they affect your emotions. And emotions, are part of people, parts of people that can connect with them, can even replace them, take over them. And so when medications change these aspects, its natural that they feel threatened that a part of them is being suppressed, and so they fight back.

Of course there are other reasons like shame, dependency, and so on. All of these can be attributed to the automatic thoughts that are preventing them from taking medication. They are all irrational, but the patient is quick to accept them as true anyways, perhaps due to their past of being judge by people, people being disappointed by them and such.
