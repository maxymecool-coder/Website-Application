# Experimental Method
**Details**
When helping a patient identify and correct his own distortions, we will need to apply certain principles of epistemology (justified belief from opinion): the nature, limits, and criteria of knowledge. The therapist will have to convey these principles to the patient. Firstly, a perception of reality is not the same as reality itself; only something close to it. The patient's sampling of reality is restricted by inherent limitations of their sensory functions - meaning seeing, hearing, smelling, etc. Second, his interpretations of his sensory input are inherently dependent on his ability to distinguish and interpret stimuli, which means that these states can significantly affect his perception. Moreover, distortions can also happen when someone is under the influence of drugs, tired, or a state of high arousal.
