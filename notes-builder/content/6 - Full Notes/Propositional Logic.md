# Propositional Logic
**Details**
In whole, language alone has a lot of holes and shortcomings in its approach to logic, which is why people may turn to symbols and math in logic to combat this. We can visibly see the limit of its capabilities through examining the phrase "Bad food is better than nothing. Nothing is better than good food (under certain circumstances) and so bad food is better than good food." Now that makes sense linguistically, if a is better than b and b is better than c then a is better than c, but logically ? It doesn't necessarily makes sense, and so we turn to propositions and its syntaxes to express logic, as it is a more fool-proof way for us to do so.

But why did the phrase fail here in logic, well if we turned to propositional logic, we would be forced to recognize that nothing in the first phrase is inherently a different variable from the second. And so we would have to use different symbols to represent them, X and B.
