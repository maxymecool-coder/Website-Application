# No Safe Smoking
**Details**
A fallacy in reasoning that most people have is that consuming tobacco moderate negate health risks. Research denies this. A major Norwegian study of 43,000 subjects demonstrated that "light" smoking (1–4 cigarettes daily) is not benign; it is associated with a threefold increase in cardiovascular death risk and a three-to-fivefold increase in lung cancer mortality. The risk to one's health is non-linear and can be highly toxic even at a low dose.

Furthermore, even as the industry markets "Light" or "Low Tar" cigarettes, user compensates by smoking a crap ton more. And also additives or mere flavorings and preservatives added to "change" the nature of these products does nothing as the problems stem from the tobacco itself.
