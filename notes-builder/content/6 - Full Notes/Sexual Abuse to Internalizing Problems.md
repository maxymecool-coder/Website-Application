# Sexual Abuse to Internalizing Problems
**Details**
As this has been heavily discussed before, we will only skim through this topic. Sexual abuse usually lead to internalization-based mental disorders like depression, anxiety, or PTSD. It may also accompany the child with a sense of profound distrust as the person who did this to them is a trusted caregiver. People who are sexually abused from a young age also tend to develop hyper sexualization, which can be similar to bipolar/mania. Trying to gauge out and know if the child has been abused or not in this sense is quite difficult as they don't wanna talk about it usually as it is traumatizing and such. Previous attempts has been made in this matter but it hasn't been quite good.
