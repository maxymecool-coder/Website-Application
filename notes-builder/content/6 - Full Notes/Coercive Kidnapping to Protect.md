# Coercive Kidnapping to Protect
**Details**
In some primate species like baboons, a subordinate male, when threatened by a dominant male, will quickly grab a nearby infant and hold it to his chest. This is not because the child is "cute" and will reduce aggression, this is because they are kidnapping the child of their attacker as if to say "If you attack me, your kid is gonna get it" and the attacker usually backs off. These beings notice who is the kid of their attacker by thinking about it, and so sometimes they might be wrong, pick the wrong kids.
