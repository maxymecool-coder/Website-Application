# Gestalt Psychology
**Details**
This school of psychology is dedicated to perception, as "Gestalt" is German for it. The founders of Gestalt argued that "the whole is different from the sum of its parts." and that something cannot be understood completely just by breaking its parts down.

This approach does not reject the existence of feature detectors but insists they are insufficient to explain perception. It highlights the importance of top-down processing, where the brain actively organizes sensory input into a meaningful whole. The ability to distinguish an object from its background (**figure and ground**) or to see an ambiguous image in multiple ways (reversible figures) are core examples that support the Gestalt view that perception is an act of imposing order on sensory information.

There are many groups that Gestalt argued to have existed, principles that organize perception into wholes which the brain uses to structure sensory information.
