# Manic Episodes
**Details**
Bipolar is considered a mood disorder, as it consists of the person having "mood swings." In all honesty, these mood swings are actually a swing from a hypomanic state and a depressive state. This means that the person can suddenly be extremely happy, in ecstasy perhaps, and then they will suddenly fall into a deep depression after that. These people are constantly active and inhibited, they will do stupid things and such and they would not mind about it. This could cause them to allow themselves to be exposed to dangers and the people close to them as well.

Psychologists have a distinguish between two types of bipolar disorders, bipolar I where the patient has had at least one episode of mania, bipolar II when the patient has had episodes of major depression as well as hypomania, which is a milder degree of mania. Prolonged time in the mania episodes can cause a person to lose touch with reality completely, leading them to having psychoses. The reason this disorder is considered arguably the most dangerous disorder is because its high death probability due to how easy the manic episodes come and so on.

**Supporting Content**
The contradiction is that Bipolar is associated with higher socioeconomic status and creativity, suggesting that "mild" bipolar genes might confer advantages such as energy and productivity that are only pathological when fully expressed.
