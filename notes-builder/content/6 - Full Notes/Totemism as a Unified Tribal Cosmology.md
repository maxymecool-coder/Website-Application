# Totemism as a Unified Tribal Cosmology
**Details**
One must correct the misconception that totemism is merely a collection of isolated clan cults. The reality is far more complex and systematic. The various totemic cults within a single tribe are not ignorant of one another; they are constituent parts of a single, overarching religious system. This is evidenced by the mutual respect and belief clans hold for each other's totems and by their participation in one another's rituals. The most compelling proof is the methodical division of the entire known universe among the totems, with no overlap. Such a systematic classification could not arise by chance; it implies a tacit, tribe-wide agreement. Therefore, totemism must be understood not at the level of the clan, but at the level of the tribe. It functions as a complete cosmology, a shared religious framework that gives order to the world, much like the later pantheons of polytheistic religions.
