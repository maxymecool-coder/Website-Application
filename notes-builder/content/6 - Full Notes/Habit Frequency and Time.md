# Habit Frequency and Time
**Details**
When thinking about how long it takes to build a habit, people often ask, "How many days does it take?" (e.g., the myth of 21 days). This evaluates habits by the wrong metric. Instead of time, it makes much more sense to determine habit formation by the frequency of performing the habit. This is justified by the neuroscientific principle of Long-Term Potentiation (LTP). LTP states that the connections between our neurons are reinforced and strengthened based on the sheer amount of times they fire together. Therefore, a habit becomes automatic not because a certain number of days have passed, but because you have performed the repetitions enough times to physically rewire the brain's circuitry.
