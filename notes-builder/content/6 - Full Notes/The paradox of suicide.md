# The reduced risk recognition in Depression
**Details**
Usually, the brain would perceive threats through the medial prefrontal cortex. But yet they would want to suicide, and the brain does not recognize that is a threat to the host's life, which presents us a paradox.

To answer this paradox, we must look further into the perceptions of the patient themselves. As the patient is constantly feeling depressed and experiencing negative emotions, the amount of cortisol released by the HPA axis affects the integrity of the mPFC, weakening it until it can barely do its job anymore.

Moreover, suicide is not inherently a threat, it is not something physical, but rather a concept, a self-realized concept that may be an escape from the constant pain the patient is experiencing rather than the ending of their life. And the amygdala realizes that, and is overwhelmed by the pain that they are experiencing in their life, not the potential danger that suicide poses, and thinks that it is the way to escape.
