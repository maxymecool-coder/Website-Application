# Agency of your own life
**Details**
Agency is the technical term for being in charge of one’s life, knowing where we stand, knowing that we have a say in what happens to us, and we can attribute to our circumstances. In traumatized patients, they are constantly trying to take back control of their lives, something that seems so impossible because it involves us being aware of ourselves, our body, our feelings. Knowing what we feel is the first step to finding out why we feel. But we can’t do this unless the MPFC learns to observe what’s going on inside us. 

Traumatized patients naturally feel unsafe in their own bodies, the past is always alive and gnawing at them from the inside, they’re bombarded by visceral warning signs and they numb themselves, they hide from their own selves to stop this discomfort, not knowing that it also prevents them from feeling pleasure, happiness, and all. The more they push these all away, the more they are likely to feel ashamed, they don’t know what is going on side and shut down at the slightest sensory shift, which develops a fear of fear itself. Scared of being scared, of feeling the way they do when someone makes contact with them. 

It makes so that even when the patient is aware that their fears are irrational, the sensations keeps them escalating into a full-body emergency.
