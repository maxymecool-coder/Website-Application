# Four-Step Behavioral Loop
**Details**
Working off the foundational theories of B.F. Skinner and behavioral psychology, we can gain actionable insight into how we condition ourselves. The entirety of the habit formation process can be categorized into four distinct, sequential steps: Cue, Craving, Action, and Reward. Each stage naturally constitutes and triggers the next. A behavior will only initiate depending on how promising or visible the initial cue is, and whether the anticipated reward justifies the amount of effort the action requires. If a cue is hidden, the habit won't start. If the action requires too much effort, the craving won't be strong enough to overcome it. Mastering habits requires manipulating these four levers.
