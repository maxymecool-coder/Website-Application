# Keywording
**Details**
The particular process of figuring out what subject the note is dedicated to is quite daunting as the tag matters a lot to the idea connection and sorting of the system. When figuring out keywords for notes, using something generic like Psychology may oversimplify and force notes unrelated to each other to overlap, which is inefficient in a Zettelkasten.

And so to solve this problem, people have tried linking notes by using tags that relate a concept with each other, with a balance between specificity and general knowledge so that it gets the best of both worlds. Remember the more time you spend on this the less time you have for your notes.
