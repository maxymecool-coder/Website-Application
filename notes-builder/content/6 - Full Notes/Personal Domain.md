# Personal Attachments
**Details**
A person can attach special meanings to certain objects that he considers to be close or relevant to him, this in turn makes him emotionally vulnerable to be moved by such things. This shows how a person attaches special meanings to certain things and are moved by them. And so they all have an involvement in his personal domain, tangible and intangible. 

This explains how a person can be strongly affected when something that is geographically remote from him is involved. This will become more relevant when we consider the nature of a person's response or disturbance, it all depends on whether he perceives events as adding to, subtracting from, endangering, or impinging upon his domain.

Although this has been discussed about, it has not been delineated of the particular kinds of appraisals that lead to specific emotions. In fact, most of the psychological literature hardly touches on the question of what content engages what response.
