# The Difference Principle
**Details**
Elaborating further on the Difference Principle made in [[The Architecture and Priority of Basic Liberties]], we have to establish a certain concept for it: the optimality curve (OP curve). When economists or philosophers map social productivity like this, they are illustrating how variations in economic returns to the more advantaged groups can actively incentivize a system that ultimately produces a greater total yield for everyone. These greater returns are necessary to cover the costs of advanced training, to mark out positions of high responsibility, and to encourage necessary economic innovation.

Criticisms against this are the extreme mathematical possibilities of it and such. They worry that a society might justify massive, thousand-to-one wealth disparities if the optimality curve rises too slowly or tapers off infinitely without dipping. However, this is only because they are failing to consider that the Principle is subordinate to prior principles of justice, specifically the guarantee of fair equality of opportunity. This fairness in the background framework ensures that no matter what happens, in a just world, it will be certain that the resulting distribution of wealth will organically be just.
