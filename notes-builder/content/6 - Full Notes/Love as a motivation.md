# Love as a motivation
**Details**
A more fitting classification for love is as a _motivation_. Similar to biological drives like thirst or hunger, love has a strong affective component and motivates an individual to perform specific actions—in this case, to get closer to, bond with, or possess the object of desire. This framework prompts the functional question: what is love _for_? From an evolutionary psychology perspective, the primary purpose of this motivation is to solve the problem of gene perpetuation. While the sex drive ensures reproduction, the motivational state of love encourages long-term pair-bonding. This bond creates a stable environment for the intensive and lengthy process of raising human children, thereby increasing the likelihood that one's genes will be successfully passed to the next generation. This evolutionary goal is so fundamental that it can even be served indirectly; by being a protective aunt or uncle, one helps ensure the survival of a niece or nephew who carries a significant portion of one's own genes.
