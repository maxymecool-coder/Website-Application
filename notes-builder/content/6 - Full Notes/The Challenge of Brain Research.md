# The Challenge of Brain Research
**Details**
The correlation between the spatial and temporal resolution is inverse. Meaning if you want to take a detailed picture of the brain, it will be very slow, and the opposite is true too for temporal. As specified in each methods, they explain why they have bad or good temporal/spatial resolution. Question is, do we have other methods ? And if yes, why not use it ?

As said before, methods like [[Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) in Brain Research]] or [[Electrical Stimulation in Brain Research]] yield great results in all factors, the only problem is it is extremely invasive, you literally are gonna have stuff in your brain to gain all this information, and it is just not a good method for the healthy brain to be tested on by. That is why we try to squeeze out every bit of information from the non-invasive methods.
