# Mechanisms of Intergenerational Depression
**Details**
As depression is 30-40% heritable, there is another 60-70% you have to account for, this is due to the environment. Now comes the tricky part, when determining the environment, we look at the [[Gene-Environment Interaction in Depression]]. It clearly states that the serotonin interceptor gene, the short variant of it, only makes a difference if abuse happens. As depression is transferable from the parent to child, there is a very likely chance that the child wasn't cared about sufficiently as a child and suffered from [[Unhealthy Attachment]] due to their parents dealing with depression.

Firstly, the parent can observe people who withdraw or is irritable when they're under stressed rather than learning healthy coping mechanisms. Secondly, they maybe punished or rewarded due to their parents' moods rather than them doing well or not, making them confused of the cause-effect relationship. Thirdly, they can internalize blame because their parents are not taking care of them, as that is much simpler to attribute to rather than how abstract and unfair this world is. Finally, similar to conduct disorder, depressed parents may use coercive discipline due to irritability and fatigue, oscillating between laxity and harshness.
