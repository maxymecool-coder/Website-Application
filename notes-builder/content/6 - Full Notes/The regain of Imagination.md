# The regain of Imagination
**Details**
The lost of imagination in trauma is what makes people unable to move on from the past. They cannot think themselves out of those situations, like how they are strong enough to beat the thing that made them feel that way and so on.

This leads me to my point, traumatized patients feel this way because they can't control their emotions. If they cannot control the effects of how the trauma is making them feel, how can they have ever controlled the trauma itself. And so in order to help them deal with the trauma, we must first help them to deal with its effects, like how they experience Alexithymia. And so when they can deal with these thoughts, emotions and such, they will have enough strength and confidence in themselves to combat the trauma itself.
