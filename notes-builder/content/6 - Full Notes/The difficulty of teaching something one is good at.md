# The difficulty of teaching something one is good at
**Details**
The phenomenon of teaching someone something one is good at is quite difficult to explain due to the sheer abstractness of it. Nonetheless it is still explainable how this happens. When someone has learnt so much, they usually have a world of information accessible to them, and their own intuition and opinions on the subject usually makes it quite difficult for them to find a starting point to teach you about it.

This is why teacher's textbooks exist, so that they can guide the teacher through the process of it. And often, they have developed mental shortcuts and schemas so that they can quickly synthesize all the knowledge. So when you are having a difficulty understanding a concept, ask a friend, not a teacher.
