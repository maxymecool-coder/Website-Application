# The intuitive appeal of teology
**Details**
Theology is the studies of religion, and is something that quite heavily ties into philosophy (consider the earliest iteration of thinkers, Plato, Aristotle,…), and also because how strongly they are against each other (Nietzsche,…). A partial reason for why Aristotelian worldview, which dominated Europe for nearly two millennia, lasted so long because it aligns perfectly with human psychology. It is one of the only philosophies to ever be integrated or allowed into Christianity, and “science” was just finding meanings to things rather than explaining them.

Teology is the science of meaning, stating that we mean in some capacity, little or much, and which we mainly view the world through. Studies of children and Alzheimer’s patients have entail this, showing that when someone who has forgotten science or hasn’t developed it, they will default to teology.

Dwarwinism supports this, as we evolve to better interact with nature, and so we have a natural teology towards it while we don’t for abstraction subjects like physics and such. Our brain that are specialized for one thing is being used for a much different and complicated focus, which is why it feels so difficult.
