# The Reason for Case Studies
**Details**
In pursuing findings and understandings of psychology, there is a need for objective, generalizable data that RCTs give us. But nonetheless, these studies rather eliminate the subjective and human richness that the participants have which can play  a large part. We can use it to identify the relevant variables and questions worth asking of the matter, allowing us to see the complex interplay of factors such as resilience, trauma, family dynamics that may be completely loss in RCTs.

Even if they don't confirm anything, case studies can allow clinicians and psychologists to have a view about who and what is happening to a particular patient and then get ideas on models explaining why or other concepts around the case study, which is the whole point of the field.
