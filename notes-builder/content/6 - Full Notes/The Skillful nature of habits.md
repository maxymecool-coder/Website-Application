# Making of Habits
**Details**
A person is skilled at something when they have and know the habit of doing it, not because they know the rules to doing it. Sure the rules help but the mastery is from turning the rules into one fluid habit. Following the rules as separate acts and then putting them all together. The more rules there are, the more complex the habit is, not multiple habits.
