# The instances of trauma
**Details**
In most situations, the case is usually that the more traumatic situations that occurs in people's lives, the harder it is to treat the symptoms, the mental illness, the more difficult it is to live, and just an overall worse life. This is quite the opposite case, as a large number (57) of recent RCTs specifically about single-trauma vs multiple-trauma treatment efficacy in children and adolescents, they found that generally the effectiveness (Hedge's g) is virtually indistinguishable from each other, and that EMDR or TF-CBT were also virtually as effective as each other.

Overall though, any therapy methods (effective) were more effective than passive or active control anywhere from medium (0.6) to high (>1). The difference between the methods were too small (<=0.1) to notice any difference in practical situations. The quality of the RCTs evaluated were also quite high due to the criteria set out before starting the research.
