# Marxist Class Theory
**Details**
This theory derives from the general norm gained from the [[Pluralist Theory]] and the [[Power Elite Theory]]. It instead goes to understand the outcome of power, who wins and what does that say about the politics and society ?

Basically, in a society, who rules would depend on if their values correspond with the long-term interests of those who governs such society. It does not matter if a president comes from a poor background (like Bill Clinton) or if they are a member of the social elite. It also doesn't matter if many different groups are involved in a decision. If the final policies enacted consistently benefit corporations, banks, and the wealthy, then the system is functioning to reproduce the power of the capitalist class.

This is because the society and economy itself depends on these people taxes so that they can use to fund the government. It is quite literally required by the economy to allow rules that create chances for these people to make their money.

Criticisms of this theory include its non-falsifiability, like Freud theories, it is quite difficult for one to prove or disprove this theory. Trying to define what is good for capitalism and what's not and what's the result that capitalism is aiming for is too difficult. Moreover, this theory inherently makes politics irrelevant, as no matter what happens, the ideals and all do not matter when the end goal is maintaining capitalism.
