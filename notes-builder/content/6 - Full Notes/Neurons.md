# Neurons
**Details**
These are the building blocks of the systems of the nerves, or the nervous systems, together with nerve impulses of course. There are many different types of neurons.

The neuron is a singular cell with three subdivisions: the dendrites, the cell body (soma), and the axon. The first of whom are usually branched, so they resemble a Medusa like bush. The axon, which extends like a wispy thread (looks weird as heck like it has nodes and stuff), may fork out into several axonal branches at its end. The dendrites receive nerve impulses from other neurons and the axon transmits that impulse.

The diameter of a neuron is very small; cell bodies can go from 5 to about 100 microns in diameter (1 micron = 1/1000 millimeter). The average hair has a diameter of 100 microns. Dendrites are typically short, only a few hundred microns, but axons can be longer.
