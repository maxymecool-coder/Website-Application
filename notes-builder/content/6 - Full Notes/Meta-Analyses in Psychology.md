# Meta-Analyses in Psychology
**Details**
These are the kinds of papers that evaluate the efficacy of methodologies or comparing two methods against each other, as well as other differences. Basically people compile RCTs from databases, PsycINFO, PubMed,... throughout a set period of time, coming up with parameters to sort it out into the suitable RCTs that they can use for the meta-analyses and so on.

They then compile the data and calculate the important statistics to come up with a table containing Hedge's g, CI, SMD, and so on.
