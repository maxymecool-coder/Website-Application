# Defectiveness lifetrap
**Details**
This is the kind of lifetrap that exists due to the fact that one’s self-esteem has been destroyed ever since they were small. People who are have this lifetrap usually has been either abused physically, mentally, or even sexually in their childhood by their family members - the people that are supposed to be their caregivers.

In the later stages of their lives, they feel immense shame inside themselves though they are unable to distinguish and characterize it as it feels like it is a part of them or inside them. This prevents them from making actual relationships and such as they are afraid of the intimacy and goodness offered to them by the person they love. These can come in many forms including surrender, which is when they accept and let it happen, escape, which is when they express nothing, and counterattack, which is when a person boasts and becomes everything that they can be so no one can ever hurt them again.

Talking about examples, we can easily get one from looking at a woman who is constantly thinking to herself about the fact that she does not deserve anything in life, not love, not nothing. And so she becomes so vulnerable she actively avoids situations and such because she thinks she will just ruin those opportunities, let her partner sees who she really is and subsequently making them leave her.

On the opposite side of the spectrum is a man, a narcissistic, but in the end successful man. Throughout his life he goes around conquering women or whatever, but he never has a stable relationship or sustained happiness throughout his life because he never finds his ideal women. This man is so delusional in his own view about his self-worth tying to someone else that he will chase anyone and when they start to be interested and care for him, that is when he loses interests, because that is when their “worth” goes down.
