**CORE BELIEFS** 
"I am a burden to others." "My needs and emotions are unreasonable and excessive." "I am fundamentally unworthy of care."

**INTERMEDIATE BELIEFS / RULES / ASSUMPTIONS** 
"If I show people my problems, they will be overwhelmed or annoyed." "I should handle suffering alone to protect others." "My problems aren't serious enough to mention." "Talking about myself is selfish and embarrassing." "If I feel physically uncomfortable, something must be done immediately."

**COPING STRATEGIES** 
Internalizing and concealing problems. Purging to regulate both physical discomfort and emotional dysregulation. Minimizing her own suffering. Self-erasure in relationships.
