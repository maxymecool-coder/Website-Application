# Automatic Thoughts Illusions
**Details**
At times, when being asked or elicited about automatic thoughts, clients would be wholly unaware of what it might actually mean and instead reply with questions or uncertainty instead of what you’re probably looking for: a statement. They could interpret the situation from a third point of view rather than well, something more normal. There is a form that substitutes the words and sentences for mere expressions as well, like “Uh-oh.”, which you will have to dig into more to truly understand what the client is getting at.
