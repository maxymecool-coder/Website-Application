# Sexual Totemism as an Intermediator
**Details**
Between the collective totem of the clan and the personal totem of the individual lies a curious intermediate form: sexual totemism. Here, the entire male population of a tribe is mystically linked to one animal species, and the entire female population to another. All men are "brothers" to their totem (e.g., the bat), and all women are "sisters" to theirs (e.g., the nightjar). This system partakes of both other forms. Like clan totemism, it is collective and implies a relationship of descent from a mythical ancestor. Yet, like individual totemism, the bond is so intimate that the death of a single totemic animal is thought to endanger the life of a corresponding human, and the cause should be punished and hunted for accordingly. This structure reflects the profound social reality of the separation of the sexes, creating two distinct corporations within the tribe, yet also uniting them together somewhat, each with its own sacred protector, demonstrating again how religious classifications mirror social organization.
