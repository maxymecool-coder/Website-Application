# Understanding Read
**Details**
Reading to understand as said is to read on something that the reader had to prior knowledge about and is only reading now which means there are two conditions to active this.

The author is going to have more knowledge than the reader coming into the book, and he conveys the info to the reader in such a way that the reader understands and communicate with the author. Without this inequality, the writer author would have not have anything of value to teach to the reader and that would miss the point of reading.

So don't feel dumb or criticize yourself for not knowing a lot of the things the writer is talking about, this is something that commonly happens due to the nature of reading books.
