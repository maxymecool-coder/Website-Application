# Reasonable Expectations
**Details**
Giving clients reasonable expectations for treatment can help them to continue with the treatment rather than terminating the therapy and may even lead to better treatment outcomes. Try to assess their conditions, how severe their depression is, or if it comorbid with other mental disorder, some patients may be quick, a mere dozen sessions, others not so much, requiring years of therapy, though these sessions will become fewer and fewer as the condition improves.
