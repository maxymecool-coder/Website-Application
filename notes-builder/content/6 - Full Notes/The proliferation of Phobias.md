# The proliferation of Phobias
**Details**
Funnily, when it was fashionable to classify phobias on the basis of a feared object or situation, learned physicians coined at least 107 different names. There were many that were exotic like: ailurophobia (cats), anthophobia (flowers), astraphobia (lightning), brontophobia (thunder). And depending on the context of the century that these words were created, they will be centered around what was the main fear of that time period, like in the 16th century, phobias were around demons and Satan. When syphilis was a common concern - in the first half of the 20th century - many people were hypervigilant and afraid of contracting it by touching dirty objects, or people.

What does this show ? This show that there are no limits in which kinds of situations, objects, creatures that can arouse excessive or inappropriate anxiety and phobias. Nowadays, we encounter fear of flying on airplanes, and so on.
