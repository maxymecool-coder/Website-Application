# Pretrains
**Details**
Its basically the base model that have been trained with many different types of voices, genders, ages, languages, manor of speech and are much longer than normal models (the one being made). The main purpose is to decrease training time and increase the quality of the model. Making a model without pretrains would require hours upon hours of datasets for good results. There are three types of pretrains: Scratch: trained without pretrain Finetune: trained with a pretrain
