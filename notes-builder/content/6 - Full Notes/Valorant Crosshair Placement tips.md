# Valorant Crosshair Placement tips
**Details**
When passively aiming, the player should focus on the audio cues of people to know where they relatively are and where they should be aiming. Due to the materials, the reloads, everything else, audio cues can be extremely telling of where the enemy is and allow for easier crosshair placements.

Furthermore, we can consider the radar, looking at it for information to be aware as teammates does not always tell us where the enemy is and we have to find it ourselves by looking at the radar and anticipating.

It is also advised that we must be aware of the location of the utilities of the enemy, as it can give us information on where certain players are. Say we have an Omen on Split on a B mid execute, the Omen would most likely stay at bottom mid to use his blind into heaven for maximum value, after that, he would try to speed up into main to get into site, and we can anticipate that. After the timing window for him getting into site has run out, we should be trying to find him in mid or something along those lines.
