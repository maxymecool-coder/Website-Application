# The Flynn Effect
**Details**
This effect states the phenomenon that IQ scores have been rising by approximately 3 points every decade across the globe. So how is this happening ? Perhaps more explained by the environmental factors about how more kids are being educated and improved nutrition rather than evolutionary factors (as it is too soon/short of a time). Modern society also trains people to reason abstractly as well, in the direction that IQ tests work towards so the scores may be inflated due to that, with constant revisions to keep the average at 100.

The heritability of IQ also functions quite different to other traits as it is heavily affected by socioeconomic factors. Research by Eric Turkheimer demonstrates how in wealthy families, the heritability of IQ is very high (about 75%) while for the poor it drops to under 20%. This is because the environment that these people are in, as the rich are able to complement their genetics with environment, they can fulfill that potential, the opposite is true for the poor as they are deprived of these factors.
