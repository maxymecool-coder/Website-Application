# Undesirable Difficulties
**Details**
For the bulk of the information given, most of it have been about how difficulties are desirable and should be prompted in learning. But of course there also exists undesirable difficulties that really impede and prevents our learning, although its not definitive.

To start, challenges that one cannot overcome are clearly undesirable, as they prevent all learning. Other difficulties may include reading and understanding things in a language that you have no clue in. Or just completely irrelevant skills that one occupation does not require.
