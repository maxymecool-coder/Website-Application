# Academia on society
**Details**
The society that runs on a low tank of intelligence will make those such as artists lose the inspiration to paint, as they have seen every painting ever to find inspiration, but the man who lives will lost nothing at all. As we take a look on humanity itself, we have all become these herds, these masses. Their value has congealed into some monolith in which each is nearly indistinguishable from one another. Perhaps this has some utility in broad public measures or policymaking, but this tendency creates subjugation.

Emerson has an example, the low who exalt the glory of the chef. These are the individuals who are willing to cast aside their individuality, to accept their status as a part of the herd, they sun themselves in the light of someone who they believe to be a great man, thinking that to be their own element. People believes that he lives for us, and that we live in him, but this is a lie. This can be solve by letting the masses run into the wild, to dissociate themselves from the herd, only then can each lamb's beauty be understood and valued to their utmost potential, only then can the voices of individuals be heard, instead of the voice of institution; and only then, can true nourishment, be achieved.
