# Structural Family Theory
**Details**
Unlike parenting styles (which focus on warmth/control), the **Family Systems** perspective analyzes the structure of family relationships and the boundaries between generations. This structure basically consists of rigid boundaries, which even though would be tough on the child, restrict them from engaging in externalizing behaviors. On the other hand, enmeshed boundaries where parents may not have any boundaries at all may make the children develop internalization problems.

A classic example of enmeshment provided in the lecture is a mother telling her 7-year-old son that wishes don't come true unless he tells her what they are—denying him  even private mental boundaries. However, causality is difficult to determine: does enmeshment cause the disorder, or does a family become enmeshed to protect a vulnerable, symptomatic child? The answer is usually both, they are reciprocal and they reinforce each other. This is a hallmark risk factor of eating disorders !
