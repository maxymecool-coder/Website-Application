# The Cognitive Model
**Details**
In psychological phenomena, the most important data are subjective and intrapsychic rather than objective and external. Only the person who directly experiences these ideas and emotions can truly understand, therefore make and report his observations. Generalizations are developed from the psychological experiences of one person and confirmed to another. The sequence also matters, how one goes from the event to their thoughts and eventually their reactions may be traced to determine the consistencies and differences among individuals.

This can have direct application to understanding emotional problems. A person who attaches an unrealistic to an event is gonna experience excessive emotional responses to it. And repeatedly this can turn into emotional disorders.

This is the core of a cognitive model, the meaning of it is encased in a cognition - a thought or an image. This also poses the difference between the other schools of thoughts as well. These generalizations may seem too obvious for a fine-grained analysis at times, but they are crucial to understanding emotional disorders.
