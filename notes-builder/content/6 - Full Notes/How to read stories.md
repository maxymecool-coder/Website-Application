# How to read stories
**Details**
The most important advice here is to read it quickly with maximum immersion. Ideally it should be read in one sitting, though this is seldomly possible, we can approximate it still. This is because if not read quickly, you will forget what happened, lose the unity of the plot (characters, environment,…).

You should next, try to let the characters enter your mind and heart, don’t judge or scold them for doing what they did before understanding the reason that they did so. Try as hard as possible to live in their world, not ours.

Now we will examine the terms of the story (characters and events). Try to become acquainted with these things and try to sort them out. Some of the times in large novels, you can be overwhelmed when confronted with copious amounts of characters, this is normal, and necessary for good books.

This is completely normal, just read through that, wt the end of the book, when its all said and done, those memorable will be those of importance to the story, and those who are not will usually fade away in the back of your mind.

Another is to learn what is important, it will be extremely easy as the author will try to point you in that direction. And so even at the start of the story, when it isn’t clear, don’t be scared, its supposed to be that way. Books ends, and there is nothing further than that, so there should be no further questions than that.
