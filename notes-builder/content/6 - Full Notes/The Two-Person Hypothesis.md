# The Two-Person Hypothesis
**Details**
Philosopher Roland Puccetti argued that split-brain cases prove we are actually **two people** inhabiting one body (even in normal brains), and the corpus callosum just keeps the two people in sync.

- **Campbell’s Rejection:** While Puccetti's view is clear, it doesn't survive scrutiny. In everyday life, split-brain patients are functionally integrated (walking, playing tennis). They do not act like two people fighting for the steering wheel.

The reality is more unsettling: There are not two souls, but zero "souls" (in the unified sense). The brain is a collection of mechanisms. Science can easily explain the wiring (The "How"), but it cannot explain how a unified subjective experience arises from these fragmented parts, nor can it help us imagine the fragmented experience of the patient.
