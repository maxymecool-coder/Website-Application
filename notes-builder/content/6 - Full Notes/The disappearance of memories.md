# The disappearance of memories
**Details**
There are theories on why our memories are lost the longer that we don't use them or consider irrelevant. Some researchers believe that our neurons of that part dies, or move on to another part of the brain. Some researchers think that its like a computer, we put the information in the recycle bin (very low-priority), so that we can use the neuron connections being occupied to remember new things.
