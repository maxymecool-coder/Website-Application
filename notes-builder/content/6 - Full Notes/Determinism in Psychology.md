# Determinism in Psychology
**Details**
Science necessitates determinism and the assumption of order in the universe so that it can truly function and predicts what will happen in the future. Classical determinism stated that absolute predictions could be made about physical bodies, but modern science has shifted towards probabilistic or statistical determinism. A clear biological example of this is the quantum release of neurotransmitters at the synaptic cleft: an identical presynaptic input does not guarantee an identical postsynaptic output, but rather a mathematically predictable probability distribution of vesicle releases.

We cannot predict human behaviors with 100% accuracy, but this is more about the confounding variables that have to be controlled. We cannot do this, and so statistics and determinism helps us to move through those variables.
