# Traits and Stages
**Details**
Before this, Freud usually proposed that the way one acts in any situation at all is a sign of their traits, of who they are, not the situation that they are in. A psychologist named Gordon Allport actually developed the distinctions between these two concepts. In his definition, trait is a consistent, long-lasting tendency in behavior, and a state is a temporary activation of a particular behavior. For example, being scared in a horror movie is a state; being scared all the time is a trait.
