# Depression
**Details**
The thoughts of depressed patients usually centers on a significant loss. They perceive that they have lost something they consider essential to their happiness or tranquility; and so they anticipate negative outcomes from any important undertaking; as well as regarding themselves as void of the attributes required to accomplish their goals. This theme can be formulated in terms of the cognitive triad: a negative conception of the self, of life experiences and a nihilistic view of the future.

And so after that these senses can lead to typical emotions associated with depression: sadness, disappointment, and apathy. Furthermore the feeling of being trapped in unpleasant situations or insoluble problems increases, spontaneous constructive motivation dissipates. The patient, moreover, even feels compelled to escape via suicide.
