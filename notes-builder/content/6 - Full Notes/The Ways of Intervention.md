# The Ways of Intervention
**Details**
Now, this isn't talking about how to intervene or therapy methodologies like CBT, IFS, DBT, and such like that but rather the two different way of swimming in a river. Imagine you have the client/patient who is stuck in the river and are flowing down to their death. So what do you do with this ? You can either jump into the river and swim downstream to catch them and drag them out or you can walk upstream and shut off the source that is the reason they are drowning.

Clinical psychology has been mostly focused on the former of course, whilst prevention science is what is focused upstream. But of course there are huge biases in this, with children being referred usually due to their parents or caretakers and those being referred mostly having externalizing symptoms due to said symptoms affecting everything more than internalizing does as those with the aforementioned symptoms suffer in silence.
