# Paradoxical Sadness
**Details**
Although sadness is ubiquitous, it is also at times puzzling as we cannot fully understand why one is sad. Similarly in situations where people actually gained something in their domain by attaining some long-awaited goal, their dominant interpretation was that they actually lost something.

It is actually simple to observe how these situations can be interpreted at a lost and can lead to a devaluation of the domain. Some of the kinds of events that may do so can be categorized as follows:
	Loss of a tangible object that is considered a source of gratification or is valued.
	An intangible loss such as the diminished self-esteem produced by an insult or disparagement.
	A reversal in the value of a component of the domain; for example, what was regarded as an asset is now judged negatively.
	A discrepancy between what is expected and what is actually received.
	A fantasy or expectation of a future loss: They ten to live through this loss as if it was actually happening in the moment, so they experience the sadness before it occurs.
	Hypothetical loss: "Loss that could happen"
	Pseudo loss: The person incorrectly perceives an event as subtracting from his domain.

Moving on, actual loss may refer to the deprivation of something tangible and definable. An intangible loss, like the loss of another person's affection, may produce the same feeling. Similarly, a reversal in a person's evaluation of their own attribute may also result the same way.

The intensity of the sense of loss is proportional to the devaluation of the attribute rather than its "max value". The ideation in unfulfilled expectations and disappointment also incorporates the theme of loss and triggers sadness. When a person expects something to be given to him, he takes a title to it, as it were his. If he doesn't actually receive it though, the he may experience as much sadness as if he did receive it and lost it. Intense disappointment happens despite the unrealistic expectation.
