# Dealing with BPD
**Details**
Emotional dysregulation in BPD may not just only look like panicking or such like that. In fact, it may result in complete and utter nonsense/illogical sayings or thoughts or just a refusal to comply to questions. In these situations, these actions may come across as ignorance or "ragebaiting", even if the other person may not recognize. And so it is important for people to try to disregard the purpose/motive of the person saying it and rather just on their actions, and try to communicate how much hurt they are doing to the other person. Or another method is to try to get the other person to emotionally regulate themselves using DBT.
