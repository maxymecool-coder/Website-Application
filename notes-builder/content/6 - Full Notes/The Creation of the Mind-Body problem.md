# The Creation of the Mind-Body problem
**Details**
Back at the peak of Newtonian physics, his followers proposed their explanations on how any and everything in the world can be perceived from a physics standpoint. As the world contains, consists, and is created of atoms, any and anything is really just a specific arrangement of atoms that particularly tickles your mind and makes you perceive something the way you do.

To further exemplify this, before this proposition existed, the medieval peasant could and would view a flower just as it is, a plant, colors, beauty or nature, the properties all related to the flower. But in Newtonian physics, these things are just atoms, void, and mathematical laws.

And so the philosophical problem here is that this interpretation allows for no subjectivity in the person's perception of said object, it is completely and utterly objective and devoid of the personal beauty of one's understanding. Our own understanding is only our own biases projected onto an objective meaning of something.
