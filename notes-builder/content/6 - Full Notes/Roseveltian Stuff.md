# Roseveltian Stuff
**Details**
Practice streaks of super deep concentration.
Taking a high-priority task from your list and giving yourself a hard deadline on it just to force you to complete it fully without distractions. This makes it so you force your brain to actively have to focus fully on doing it so it won't mess up.
