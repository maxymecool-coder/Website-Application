# Computer Tomography (CT) in Brain Research
**Details**
- **Methodology**: Uses X-ray beams at multiple angles to create body section images; denser tissues absorb more X-rays, allowing localization of damage or abnormalities (e.g., tumors). Produces static pictures of brain structure, not activity.
- **Advantages**: Localizes damage/abnormalities effectively. Non-invasive with no patient discomfort. 
- **Disadvantages**: Exposes subjects to high radiation. Poor spatial resolution. Shows only static images, no brain activity.
