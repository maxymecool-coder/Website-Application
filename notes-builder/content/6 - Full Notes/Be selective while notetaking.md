# Be selective while notetaking
**Details**
This is the key to smart note-taking, as it is important to choose information that is disconfirming arguments and facts that challenge our thinking. But although that is the case, we are naturally drawn to everything that proves us right, confirms what we already know, which makes us feel good.

The moment that we decide on an idea, we automatically go into search mode, and we scan for only supporting data, which is detrimental to our learning and research. It is present in every aspect of our lives, as our friends are those who think alike us, the things we search are also confirming our hypothesis, and so on.

Although even the best of the best are aware of the problem, they actually do something about it. For example, Charles Darwin forced himself to write down the arguments that were critical of his theories, this was because that these ideas can easily escape one's memories than the favorable ones. And so it assisted him in noticing and answering these objections, resulting in very few ones that he wouldn't know about.
