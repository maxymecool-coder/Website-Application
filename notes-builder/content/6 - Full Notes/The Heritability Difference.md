# The Heritability Difference
**Details**
While examining the heritability of mood disorders, there seems to be quite a difference between Major Depression and Bipolar Disorder. Though the heritability of depression is 30% of males (40% of females), it is substantially higher for Bipolar disorder. For bipolar disorder, the rates is double that, ranging up to 80%, being one of the most heritable mental disorders in all of psychiatry. This suggests that depression is mainly caused by environmental factors, what happens in the environment, whilst bipolar is determined by biological or genetics rather. Consequently, though medication is optional in depression, it is highly recommended or even necessary for bipolar disorder due to its nature.
