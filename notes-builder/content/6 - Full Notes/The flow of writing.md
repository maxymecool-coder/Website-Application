# The flow of Zettelkasten
**Details**
When writing about an idea that one has understood from reading into the Zettelkasten, one cannot help but transform the information they read into something new and is their own. It is simultaneous process, as the series of notes in the slip-box develops into arguments, ideas that are shaped from the theories, the writings that one has in their head. All of those things that are in our head are shaped by the thing we read. All of this information grow alongside the slip-box, they are constantly changing, and being challenged.

To get to the final writing from the slip-box is pretty straightforward. The content itself is already meaningful, they are well-detailed and well-connected, they only need to be put into a logical order. While the notes were developed to be understand by their own selves, they are also a part of something more that will enrich their meaning altogether. You are already thinking critically to get to this point, and drawing from the slip-box is like that also, rather than a mechanically placed act.
