# Stages of Psychosexual Development in Freud’s Theory of Personality
**Details**
This theory has been heavily refuted and disproven in recent times by researchers. Nonetheless it is still an important landmark in the field of psychology and should be talked about somewhat. Freud's theory was that psychosexual interest and pleasure begun in infancy as we are sexual beings at the end of the day. The term he used for it - **psychosexual pleasure** - broadly include all the bodily stimulations that we feel and excite our emotions and thoughts. He also maintained the view that our actions and how we deal with things are inherently realted to this.

The psychosexual energy is what he called libido, which is a term that is still used today. Now he also argued that there were stages through how this libido affected and presented itself through our childhood. Moreover, he also thought that it heavily affected us as a human being long after the stages. The effects correlated to certain stages, if we did not get the sufficient sexual development at that stage, the effects will carry on into adulthood.
