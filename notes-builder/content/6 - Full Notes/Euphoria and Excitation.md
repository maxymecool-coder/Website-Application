# Euphoria and Excitation
**Details**
Just as said before about paradoxical sadness linking to loss and such, the condition for euphoria or excitation is the perception or expectation of gain. This effectively means the person will increase his evaluation in his own domain.

But not only does this produce euphoria but the anticipation of achieving euphoria may also lead to pleasure. In fact, it can even have an escalating effect. For example, someone posting his work on a recognized journal and it doing well will lead to him thinking to himself that he is also important as people appreciate his work, then he thought of the people that would see it, his euphoria increased. And so on...

Whether one experiences pleasure or not heavily depends on the meaning that he attributes to the object or activity that is being linked to. As said above, the man attributed his work being mentioned as it being recognized, and so he was ecstatic.

This pleasure does not need to have a source before it, meaning that it isn't necessary for there to be a need for it before, the only thing necessary is for it to be pleasurable. But, once someone has felt this pleasure, they might develop an active desire for more of it.

This leads us to feedback loops, which will help depressed patients to become motivated and eventually be treated.
