# Manipulative Actions in BPD
**Details**
When in a split period, BPD patients may act quite manipulative, even if it’s not their intention. These actions can be seen as quite manipulative though they aren’t by nature as these people are not trying to manipulate, deceive, or use others that they are affecting. They genuinely believe that what they’re doing is asking, begging, showing that they need help, not anything else.

In these emotional frenzies, people around usually get swept up into the same storm that BPD patients are experiencing, making them feel so pressured and all into doing all sorts of stuff. This is a stark change in between states, from someone who needs comfort and care and love so much into an aggressive and violent victim. Still, they should not be categorized as manipulative, which can falsely accuse BPD patients and make people misinterpret them, leading to stigmatization.
