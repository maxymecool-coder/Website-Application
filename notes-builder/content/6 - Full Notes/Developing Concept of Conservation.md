# Distinguishing Appearance from Reality
**Details**
This problem is related to how a child might try to understand how a ball is still white even after it is put through a blue filter, as children in the preoperational stage usually have this problem. They fail to understand that objects conserve such properties after changes in the shape or arrangement of the objects. Their brain cannot yet comprehend and perform the mental operations necessary to understand these transformations.

For example, when you get a thin and tall class of water, filled to the brim and pour it into a wide but short glass, the child most likely will not understand that the amount of water in both glasses are the same. Instead, they will say the wide and short glass probably has more water in it.
