# Validation
**Details**
To start validating, one must first concentrate on what the other person is saying, so that they won’t judge them. Another step is to focus on the facts and not allow one self to make opinions of the situation. We all rush to judgement, and that doesn’t help the person in this situation, don’t use extreme words and such.

The second level is to validate accurately, wording it around what the person tells you. The next level of this is mind reading, which is thinking what the person want to say before they do. And when you’re wrong, go back to focusing on the facts. Those are the general steps to validating and being empathetic.

The last step, perhaps most deserving of how hard it is radical validating. The idea is basically not to treat your loved one differently from any context, don’t fragilize, condescend them.

**Supporting Content**
To maintain one’s own mental health and to cope, one can also validate themselves and their emotions.
