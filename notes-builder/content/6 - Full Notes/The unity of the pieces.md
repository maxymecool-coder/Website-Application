# The unity of the pieces
**Details**
When applying for a scholarship, it is clear that there are many parts that one has to create to do so. And though each of it may present a certain part of the applicant, in the end, there must be a unity point in between all of it to present what kind of person the applicant may be, and that unison must correspond to the ideal candidate that the university is looking for, or close to it.
