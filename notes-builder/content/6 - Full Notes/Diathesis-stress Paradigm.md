# Diathesis-stress Paradigm
**Details**
The more popular or actually the main paradigm nowadays is the diathesis-stress paradigm which is diverse and combines the factors of all the other paradigms and how they interact with each other to help come to a conclusion and treatment for the patient. It links genetic, neurological, psychological, and environmental factors. It is not limited to one school of thought. It relates to the predispositions of factors that may contribute to a person eventual stress and mental illness. More importantly though, it is the predisposition of these factors, meaning these factors all interact with stress in a way that leads to mental illness.
