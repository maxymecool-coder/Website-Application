# Criticism Opinions or Knowledge
**Details**
A trouble that many people encounter in criticizing is the difference between knowledge and opinion. They think that everything the writer is conveying are merely opinions, not knowledge, and so they think that they can differentiate from the author and they do not need to change their views. 

This approach will give the reader nothing of value when he finishes the book, as his opinions will just stay the same, nothing will have changed. 

If we are sure that knowledge is what is at stake, genuine knowledge, not opinions, then we must be patient, open-minded to that fact and resolve our disagreements to rationally come to the conclusion of knowledge. 

And so comes Rule 11: Respect the difference between knowledge and mere personal opinion, by giving reasons for any critical judgement you make.
