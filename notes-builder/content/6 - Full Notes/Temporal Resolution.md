# Temporal Resolution
**Details**
Temporal resolution refers to how accurately a technique can capture the **timing** of brain activity. It's about the detail in time—how quickly changes can be detected and how short the time intervals are that we can measure. High temporal resolution means you can track events happening in milliseconds (thousandths of a second), while low temporal resolution might only capture changes over seconds or minutes. Since we also need to research dynamic activities like hearing sounds and such in the brain, we will also need good temporal resolution to quickly capture all of this.
