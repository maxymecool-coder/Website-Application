# Sensory neurons
**Details**
These are neurons that convey impulses from receptors toward the rest of the nervous system, it is something that gives information inward. They receive information basically.

There are also neurons called "interneurons" which are interposed between to or more, and they perform both functions of the sensory and motoneurons.
