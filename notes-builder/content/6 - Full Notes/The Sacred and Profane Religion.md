# The Sacred and Profane Religion
**Details**
In his book "Elementary Forms of Religious Life," Emile Durkheim went in-depth about the nature of religion, and tried to define and understand it. He eventually came up with the two sides of religion, the sacred and the profane. The sacred meant the meaning and the principles that people attributed to something, they all believe, they all devoted, gave meaning of themselves to that thing, like how they respect readings or scriptures to the highest of degree. This all accumulated to something that is the symbol of society, the religion, the sacred, it is all just a symbol of a group of people in society that believed in something so hard that others started believing too.

To further reinforce people's belief in this, they do rituals, which results in "collective effervescence" (experiencing the collectivity of the group in the ritual) which increases everybody as a group together, as a microcosm of society perhaps. The collective of symbols such as religion, in the name of the country, the ideal of the country, is what creates society.
