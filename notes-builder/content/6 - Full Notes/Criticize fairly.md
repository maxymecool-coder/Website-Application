# Criticize fairly
**Details**
This is the second maxim of critical reading. It is Rule 10: When you disagree, do so reasonably, and not disputatiously or contentiously. There is no point in winning an argument if you know or suspect that you yourself are wrong… all that matters, is the truth. 

This is easy in reading a book, since the author isn’t there to defend himself of your criticism, so you must hold yourself accountable to not unreasonably disagree nor agree. So he should not ultimately try to just go to one side, but be prepared for both.
