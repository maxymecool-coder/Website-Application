# Childhood in Phobias
**Details**
As we examine the more common phobias amongst people, we recognize that most of these stem or incorporate some degree of fears that stems from their childhood. Of course, most children learn how to cope with this and thus "outgrow" the fear, but some are not as lucky as the fear sticks to them throughout their adulthood as well.

This is called fixation phobia, and it is caused by many reasons, one of the main are because of the parents' own phobias. If the parent has a phobia, that would likely also get passed down to their children, not genetics, but behavior wise. As they avoid situations, as they respond irrationally to such situations, their children observe and learn from such to be scared.
