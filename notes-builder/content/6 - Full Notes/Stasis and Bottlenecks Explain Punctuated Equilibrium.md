# Stasis and Bottlenecks Explain Punctuated Equilibrium
**Details**
Due to the mechanisms of Macroevolution, it is quite simple to see why there are long periods of stasis, as changes are quite random, they depend on chances. Most of the time these traits do not actually help but rather are drawbacks, leading to **stabilization selection**. However, during an **evolutionary bottleneck**—a period of intense environmental pressure where survival depends on a single trait (like the Ice Age or the meteors)—a major macro-mutation might be preserved. Even if it has negative side effects, if it provides the one trait needed to survive the bottleneck, it will be carried through the surviving population, resulting in a rapid, "punctuated" jump. They would then breed (having a high rate of genes similarity or even inbred) and fill the population with similar species.
