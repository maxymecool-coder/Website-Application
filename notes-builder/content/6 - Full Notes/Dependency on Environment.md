# Dependency on Environment
**Details**
Scientists discovered that there are multiple variations of a gene, but more than that, they discovered that certain variations performed worse in a controlled environment, yet this is not the case in the outside world. Genes in different environments can thrive in that environment more than they do in an environment that they are not used to most of the time. For example, if you study male and female (XX vs XY chromosomes) and compared their mathematical abilities in a country, since male had higher scores, could you come to the conclusion that that is the result for the whole world ? No, because do that somewhere in a country where male and female equality are virtually the same (Like the Scandinavian countries) and you would find out that they compete with almost the same scores there. Same with depression, having a "bad" serotonin receptor gene does not make you depressed, that only happens if it is coupled with childhood maltreatment.
