# Transference and Resistance of Unconscious Thoughts
**Details**
Furthermore, Freud theorized a “defense” of sorts of the patient themselves, a resistance to access those thoughts that the mind is so against. It can appear in many forms, societal, ideological, philosophical,… and basically he argues that knowing these secrets will give the patient awareness of it and help them relieve. 

But he also speculated that these defenses, these thoughts that pushed those memories out of consciousness in the first place needs to be exposed, identified, and dissolved to also relieve the patient fully. This is achieved through his analysis of patients’ free associations and their resistance to it.
