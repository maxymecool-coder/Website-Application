# Instead of planning, become an expert
**Details**
As discussed before, in writing there are multiple tasks that need attending to. And so we need to practice to be able to become good at moving in between each of these tasks depending on which is the most important and promising ones, judged by nothing else but our intuition of the situation.

Experts are the same, they rely on embodied experience, which enables them to reach a state of virtuosity. They have a feel for the process, an intuition to which task will help them to write a good piece and which will waste their time. This intuition is necessary as there are no universal sets of rules that will help with this. Every new writing is different, every stage is different, at different stages, it may be best to read up on something, to review a passage, to discuss an idea or change the outline.

This is what the slip-box allows you to practice, it gives you a structure of clearly separable tasks, which can all be completed within reasonable time and provides you with instant feedback through writing interconnected notes. It allows you the chance to deliberately practice and so your intuition will get better and better.
