# The rise of Individual Totemism
**Details**
Beyond the public, collective totemism of the clan, there exists a private, individual form. This "individual totem" is a personal patron—an animal, plant, or object—with which a single person shares a mystical and intimate bond. This relationship is distinct from the clan totem in several ways. It is not hereditary but is _acquired_ through a deliberate ritual act, often a vision quest involving fasting and self-mortification around puberty. The bond is one of alliance, not ancestry; the totem is a "friend" or "protector," an _alter ego_ whose fate is intertwined with the individual's, not a forefather. This personal totem gives the individual their sacred name, is represented by a personal emblem, and is believed to grant them special powers and protection. It is the religious principle applied not to the group, but to the personality.
