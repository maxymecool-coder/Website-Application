# Dynamic Testing
**Details**
Robert Sternberg and Elena Grigorenko using the [[Sternberg's Triarchic Theory of Intelligence]] proposed the idea of testing dynamically. This stems from the fact that Sternberg believes that we are always moving to a higher state of competence from a lower one as well as the fact that the standardized tests cannot accurately show our potential. Together with this test though, incorporating deliberate practice into it, tackling the weaker parts of one's competence to improve upon the overall skillset.
