# Competency to Stand Trial
**Details**
When someone has committed the crime, and they clearly have mental health issues, like not understanding why they are being charged, why they are having to stand trial. And so a person must understand certain things to prove that they are able to talk through with the attorney about their crimes, such as they understand what they have done, or that they are not in a state to defend themselves. If they do not fulfill these requirements, they will have to be admitted to a psychiatric hospital and helped until they are competent so that they can stand trial (Maximum of 3 months each time).
