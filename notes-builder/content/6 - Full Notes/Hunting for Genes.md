# Hunting for Genes
**Details**
Gene hunting is in fact, quite simple. Identifying the trait that one wants to research about, we can then try to find the proteins that incite such traits (it has to be special and have a baseline to be compared to), then finding the stretch of DNA that may contain that gene (as it is incredibly difficult to pinpoint). Then one would have to go even further, even deeper to find the gene itself. They can find the specific genes by looking for genes that share similarities to such genes in other animals, for example, vasopressin in voles and look for variations of it in humans. Again, due to [[Molecular Genetics]], we understand that many genes can have a hand in a trait, not just one.
