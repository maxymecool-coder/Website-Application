# Tailored approach to mental illness
**Details**
Although the contemporary literature has generally advised for the therapist to be empathetic, to let out their feelings, this had lead to both good and bad effects. The bad effects being that, since they accepted their debased self-image and pessimism, it increased their sadness, passivity, and self-blame. 

And so to effectively help the patient, the therapist must tailor a technique to the selected characteristics and personality of the patient, which has been proven to be far more effective. Moreover, a highly structured, problem-orientated approach appeared to have a more immediate result than the other methods. And in these approaches, the cognitive and behavioral techniques were most effective.
