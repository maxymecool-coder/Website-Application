# Self Constructs
**Details**
In a person, the personal part, the self-construct, is how we see ourselves, and so in BPD patients, they view themselves as being out of control, having a lot of emotional pain, not being able to do things,… even if it is negative, we should still validate it so that people gravitate and accept us more, giving us chances to change their minds.

Even if you don’t necessarily agree, you can still validate, you understand why a person might feel that way.
