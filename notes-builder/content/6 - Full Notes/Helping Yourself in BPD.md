# Helping Yourself in BPD
**Details**
Throughout reading and researching on BPD, caring about one’s self has constantly been a part of the procedure, perhaps the most important principle to the helper. The reasons are simple to understand and it has been mentioned how mindfulness and self-care would work.

Moreover, though, something that can the loved one can do is to learn DBT themselves through classes that organizations like TARA and NEABPD offers to help people understand and learn DBT to help their loved ones.
