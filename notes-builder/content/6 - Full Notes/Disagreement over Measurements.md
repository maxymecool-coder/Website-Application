# Disagreement over Measurements
**Details**
Now comes the other caategory of problems: substantive questions. Comparing lives and how good and bad they are would now depend on the theory of well-being, and the theories are of course, divided.

Hedonism is the cleanest one: sum everything together, weight by intensity and duration, then subtract. A positive result means a life worth living, negative means other wise. But of course this would structure down the problem for us to have to find a way to evaluate good things and bad things.

Another revision is revising life itself, or in other words: container theories. A neutral container view would say being alive contributes nothing. Modest would say intrinsic value, and fantastic would be being alive is so great nothing can ever bring the total to zero. Most people would of course say that the neutral case is probably where they reside.
