# Dominance of Finance Capital
**Details**
Other than just knowledge workers and the traditional ones, there also exists another system that functions as a separate system from the labor workforce. In simpler examples, the system consists of the coin market, the stock market, and such like that.

This system makes money by getting tax basically from every little transactions and fluctuations that happens in the market. It does not produce any MORE money and such, rather acting as a casino where the house eventually wins thanks to those taxes.

The most unique and different characteristic of this system is that it is completely detached from any country or government. Unlike industrial capitalists who rely on a national workforce and infrastructure, the financial elite operates in a truly global, placeless space. This creates a new and powerful class whose interests are not tied to the well-being of any particular nation-state, its workers, or its productive capacity.
