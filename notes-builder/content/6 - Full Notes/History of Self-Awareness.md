# History of Self-Awareness
**Details**
Baumeister argues that the modern self-concept is roughly a thousand years old due to specific historical conditions. Originally, we never really had decisions that forced us to see ourselves and such, just who we are: A thousand years ago in Western civilization, none of the major life decisions existed. Education: none unless royalty. Career: inherited. Marriage: arranged or limited. Location: lifelong. Religion: universal, unchosen. Without these pressures, the reflective, anxious, self-monitoring identity we consider natural had no reason to develop. But then finally as diaries, autobiographies, novels, and written depictions of mental states all track with urbanization, expanding social complexity and so on. Nonetheless, this might not be the most positive of developments, as anxiety practically correlates with the growth of the "self."
