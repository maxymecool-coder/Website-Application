# Solace in Helplessness
**Details**
Sometimes it is too late to help, sometimes people with BPD are too far gone and they instead make decisions that are illogical, that will only make them suffer more and so on. Why will they do this despite getting, having all the help they need ? Because that help is tough, it requires them to have to fight, to act, to do something, when they’d rather it all just go away and not have to take accountability, responsibility, they keep on spiraling, driving faster in a car that will inevitably crash. And when it eventually does, that’s when they can actually get help. Because they crash, they need something to start over, to start fixing, building their life back, this is when you suggest and help them to find therapy.

The best thing anyone can do in these situations is to work on themselves, regulate yourself so you don’t pour more resources and efforts and get into your own crisis.
