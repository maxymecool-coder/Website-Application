# Mental Retardation
**Details**
Mental retardation is one of, if not the most stigmatized and neglected topics in Psychology. Individuals with intelligence out of the norms in history have been prejudiced and judged, excluded out of society. In the modern era, this exclusion manifested in the creation of large state institutions to segregate these individuals from the mainstream. Originally, the definition was simple, just anybody that was falling at the bottom tail of the IQ bell curve (two standard deviations below the mean). But of course this was flawed as it would mean the geniuses at the top would also be pathological (statistically).

Even further, to address the clear cultural and socioeconomic gap in IQ tests, as middle-upper class children may have environments and chances that increase their IQ more than others, critics have argued that we need to not only value verbal reasoning, abstract logic, or vocabulary but also adaptive skills and street smarts areas. And to address this, a diagnosis now would also require deficits in adaptive functioning like self-care, social skills, ability to utilize community resources.
