# Neuromodulators
**Details**
Neural communication extends beyond simple, direct excitation or inhibition into a more subtle and powerful domain known as neuromodulation. In this form of signaling, a substance does not directly cause or prevent an action potential, but instead alters a neuron's response to other incoming signals. This creates a sophisticated system of conditional, "if/then" logic that dramatically increases the brain's computational power by making communication context-dependent. The effect of a neuromodulator is contingent on the presence of a primary neurotransmitter, changing how the pre- or post-synaptic cell responds to it.
