# The preference of socialization models
**Details**
As already mentioned in [[The Active Seeker Of Socialization]] and [[The Passive Learning Of Socialization]], there are two groups in which we can choose to have a view over the development of socialization. In modern times though, the former group has largely prevailed due to the more logical stance that it gives. The key point in which why the active seeker is more fitting than the passive learner is because children usually have an active part in choosing who they wanna be when they grow up, or else they would just be perfect imitations of the people and the environment they are in. A lot of the times, children may completely miscorrelate from their upbringing and caretakers, showing that the active seeker is more plausible.
