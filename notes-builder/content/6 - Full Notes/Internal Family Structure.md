# Internal Family Structure
**Details**
Many adaptations or coping mechanisms can affect us (which are classified as psychiatric problems) including obsessions, compulsions and so on, all started out as strategies to protect ourselves. But after the trauma has passed, this can become troublesome as it affects our lives and our capacity to function properly and normally. 

IFS is basically a collaboration in between all parts of the human being, even the suicidal and self-destructive to help recalibrate one self. When the self is no longer in charge, its called “blending” (in IFS at least) as it blends with a part of it that wants to die, although it’s not really the Self.

The assertions that Richard Schwartz made is that the Self is not what is inherently necessarily changed, rather it just needs to be revealed under the various protectors that have emerged to shield it under the destruction that they all endured. Moreover, the Self can also help reorganize the inner system and help the parts to allow it to gain back control of things again.
