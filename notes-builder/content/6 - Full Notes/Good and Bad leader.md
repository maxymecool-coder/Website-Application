# Good and Bad leader
**Details**
As there are many factors in predicting if a group would be cooperative or not, at times they may be uncooperative and refuse to work with their leaders and skills trainer. When this situation happens, the leader will try to coax members into working with each other, which may be viewed as being “pushy”. Next, the co-leader will be the “good guy” and validate the members and their effort. Eventually, this will allow them to do some work of DBT at the end of the day.

Of course, it is obvious which of the roles are comfortable and uncomfortable, and the co-leaders can switch place with each other rather than being the same every time, it depends though.
