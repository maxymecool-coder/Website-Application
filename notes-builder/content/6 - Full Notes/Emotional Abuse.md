# Emotional Abuse
**Details**
Perhaps the most difficult to identify as it is subjective to the child who suffers from it. Though that’s not to say that it doesn’t affect much, as it precipitates both internal and external symptoms. The reason for this difficulty comes from the gray area of poor parenting like using sarcasm and psychological cruelty, like ridiculing them. But more than that, emotional abuse can also come from witnessing domestic violence or further so. This type of abuse though often cause a cascade of negative symptoms leading to such outcomes.
