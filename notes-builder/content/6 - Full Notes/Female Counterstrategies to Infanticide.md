# Female Counterstrategies to Infanticide
**Details**
To deal the infanticide that males do, female develop defensive strategies, either physical (which is not inherently effective in a tournament-bonding species) or psychophysiological responses.

One of the most remarkable is pseudo-ovulation. When a new, potentially infanticidal male arrives, a pregnant female can develop the external physiological signs of being in heat (estrus), even though she is not ovulating. The new male, seeing these signs, will mate with her. When she gives birth a short time later, the male is tricked into "believing" the infant is his own premature offspring and will not harm it. These males usually do not have the ability to cognitively think about how his children are being birthed way faster than the usual time of his species.
