# Recognizing maladaptive ideation
**Details**
A "Maladaptive ideation" is applied to an ideation which interferes with the patient's ability to cope with life experiences, disrupts his internal harmony and produces inappropriate emotional reactions that can hurt him. When applying this term, the therapist must make sure that both him and the patient are on the same page about it.

Some possible exceptions are maladaptive ideations that are consonant with reality, but even in such circumstances, it can still be regarded as maladaptive as it interferes with functioning. For example, as talked about before, the people who work dangerous jobs. Of course in most cases these ideations can be dealt with by someone being taught the techniques to deal with them, but in other cases, medication might be required to deal with it instead.

There is an obvious question to be made here about these thoughts. Even though they are always in our lives and in our apprehension, why are we not aware of such thoughts ? Yet, here we are, with lives that are shaped a lot by these automatic thoughts. For instance, a man struggles to sleep yet he does not know why, until further examined in his own mind, he realizes that the sound of the clock ticking is the thing that is disturbing and keeping him up at night. It exists there, but he does not recognize it until trained to.

**Supporting Content**
The nature of the characteristics of these thoughts can variate from conditions to conditions, depression, paranoid states, obsessions, phobias are all different.
