# Incompatibilist and the Cartesian Error
**Details**
The intuitive pull of incompatibilism often rests on an implicit, perhaps subconscious, commitment to Cartesian dualism. It presumes that a "Self" exists separate from the biological machinery, viewing the brain's causal process to be whips and chains rather than the vehicle of the self. Neuroscience and evolutionary biology of course is completely rejects this as it perceives that there is no immaterial controller floating above the neural architecture. To wish to be released from this is alike, as Kant famously noted, like a bird wishing to fly in a vacuum to escape air resistance, failing to realize that the air is the very medium that allows it to fly. And so neural determinism is not a prison for our will; it is the very mechanism that allows our will to exist in the first place.
