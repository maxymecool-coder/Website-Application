# The Sauce Bearnaise Syndrome
**Details**
Different from how the Behavioral Psychologist views the world, that there is only reinforcement to any two things to make them more or less prevalent, ethologists disproved this by the following example. A man eats a curry that really makes him sick, but not before he goes to an opera after it still, after that, he gets diarrhea, terrible stuff happen to him, etc. behavioral psychologists in this situation maybe would say that the man would be scared to go to the opera because he associated the symptoms he experienced to the food, not the opera. This proves that there are some processes of learning that is more "prepared" than others. Even animals have this, a monkey scared of flowers, but other monkeys see it and they are not scared of it at all.
