# The pyramid of books
**Details**
The great majority of books that have been written-more than 99% of them- will not make sufficient demands on the reader for them to improve their reading skills. These books are merely to amuse the readers, and so they should only be skimmed.

The second class is from which you can learn both how to read and how to live. It is said by Mortier J.Adler that less than one out of every hundred books belongs in this class, or a thousand, or even ten thousand. These are the good books, which were carefully written and crafted by their authors and of course are worth reading analytically. If the reader is skillful, they will be able to extract all they need in a singular good reading.

But how do you realize such books are no longer useful to you anymore ? This can only be answered by your own reactions and feelings towards it. You will only stop reading, stop learning from it once you feel that each time you pick it up, there is nothing meaningful left for you to change for and to it.

There is a last class of book, which very few exists, that you would discover upon returning that is has grown with you. You will have seem to see new things in it-whole sets of new things. Your previous understanding is not invalidated, but now the book is true in other ways than that too.

One may question how this occurs ? And to answer, we must look at ourselves, with time, our knowledge and our mind will become wiser, will become more, and so such books perceptions change to cater to the level at which our minds have grown, presumably presenting a new way of looking at said books.

**Supporting Content**
Not every books can or will produce the same effects as one another might have on you, even the greatest books. The point is for us to find books that have this value for us, not be sheep.
