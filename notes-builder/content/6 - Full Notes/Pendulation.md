# Pendulation
**Details**
The adrenaline that the body pumps out during stressful situations usually help the person to do something to deal with a disaster. But those who are traumatized, the adrenaline usually just fuels their reactions to trauma, tiring them out completely, leaving them unable to do anything.

In order to return to proper functioning, this has to stop, the body needs to be restored to its normal state of safety and relaxation and then it can mobilize to take action in response to danger.

To deal with this issue, treatments usually include approaching what has happened and taking a backseat to explore the physical sensations and the imprints of trauma on the body. Patients are helped to build up their resilience to safely access these sensations and emotions that overwhelmed them at the time, which helps them expand their window of tolerance. Moreover, allowing the actions that patients did at the time to protect themselves to finish is a good way for them to be able to transform the energy from adrenaline that fuel their traumatic responses into energy for them to utilize and fight back.
