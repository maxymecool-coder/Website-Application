# The Architecture of DBT
**Details**
The fundamental architecture of Dialectical Behavior Therapy rests entirely on the synthesis of opposites, specifically the tension between acceptance and change. This dialectical emphasis permeates every aspect of treatment, requiring the therapist to navigate five major strategy categories that operate in pairs. The overarching dialectical strategies frame the treatment, while the core strategies balance problem solving against validation. Communication strategies toggle between an irreverent style and a reciprocal style, just as case management strategies balance consultation-to-the-patient with environmental intervention. These pairings ensure that the therapy does not veer too far into demanding change without validation, or offering acceptance without facilitating necessary growth.
