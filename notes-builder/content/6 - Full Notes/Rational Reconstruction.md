# Rational Reconstruction
**Details**
To be able to extract the maximum amount of philosophical value from a thinker like Marx, scholars often use a method known as rational reconstruction. They would approach the most profound ideas or intuitions—such as Marx's early focus on alienation or his later structural analysis of social formations, forces of production, and surplus value. He would then impose order on these ideas to elaborate them into a consistent and coherent framework.  Even though this method can attribute certain ideas that the author never had, it allows their theories to be viewed at their strongest. Crucially, this also allows the scholar to bring their own external criteria into the text.
