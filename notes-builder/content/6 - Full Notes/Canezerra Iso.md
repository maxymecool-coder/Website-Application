# Canezerra Iso
**Details**
In his ult he will hold the left side of the opponents wall if they swing he kills. But more than that, he moves back to take pull advantage of his peeker’s advantage. 

When moving into site, he usually lets the movement or teleporting duelist get in first whilst he speeds himself up. Example is when Eggsterr tped into site, he sped up his garage push to fully push into site (probably cleared stuff) and then get the two kills in backsite (pinching), this happened because he got info. This is not to say he doesn’t entry, he does, but with utility like a blind or something. He also always pops his shield A main or close to entering site.

In another situation, Eggy clears site alone with his ult as everyone moves in, they mostly get off site. Cane pushes CT thinking this and also thinking that they would be unaware even though he doesnt get kills. He immediately vulns to delay them from getting him, activating his shield and holding backsite to fight.

A tip when he is fighting even with shield is that he recalibrates very wide angles, he tries to get peeker’s advantage every fight. He also does so without shields as well.

Cane also likes to play mid or B on defensive sides, he does somewhat go peek aggressive, doesnt hold much. He responses very sensitively to the other team’s actions, movements and strats, pushing, going back,… and he mostly rotates quickly. 

He also has a habit of using his ult to get first blood than anything, info does get involved at times. Mostly just uses the vandal or phantom in defense or attack.

When in ecos, he would try to play with his team to get flashes, push, pop out to peek and get kills from that.
