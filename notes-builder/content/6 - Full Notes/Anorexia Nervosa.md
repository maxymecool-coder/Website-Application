# Eating disorders
**Details**
This is an eating disorder that refers to a person trying not too eat because they fear themselves that they are becoming too fat. These people may prepare good meals for their family and such but does not care about their own starvation. The features that are required for a diagnosis is:

1. Restriction of behaviors that promote healthy body weight.
2. Intense fear of gaining weight and being fat.
3. Distorted body image or sense of body shape.

From a cognitive view, these people have a very distorted view on their own body and about the nature of gaining weight and eating in general.

The consequences of Anorexia Nervosa can even range to biological effects due to the nature of people starving themselves and using laxatives, such as falling blood pressure, heart rate slows, kidney and gastrointestinal problems develop, bone mass declines, skin dries out, literally so much stuff. 

**Supporting Content**
It is a very threatening illness due to the high deathrate of the group that has it, and mostly comes from health complications because of the illness.
