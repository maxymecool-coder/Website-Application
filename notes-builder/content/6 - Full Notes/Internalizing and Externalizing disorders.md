# Internalizing and Externalizing disorders
**Details**
When examining mental disorders, one can sort the disorders into two types, internalizing and externalizing. This sorting is characterized by the concept that disorders' symptoms are able to be sorted into these two categories. 

Firstly is the internalizing disorders, which includes depression, or anxiety, or withdrawal. These are emotional problems, or emotional disturbance in the minds of the child, caused by overcontrolling their own behaviors which causes resentment and stress on their minds. Continuing with that, we come to externalizing disorders, which includes conduct disorder, or just behavior problems, manifesting into aggression, defiance, impulsivity. These are the undercontrolled behaviors directed outwardly. Stability wise, internalizing problems are lesser than externalizing problems, but severe cases can be stable.

While these two are separate concepts and types of disorders, they are highly correlated and often co-occur, or in other terms, having high chances of comorbidity.
