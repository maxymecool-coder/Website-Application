# Role Diversification in Species
**Details**
Experiments have shown that the tit-or-tat method is a behavior that many species possessed. But what incited that it may not be applied for every single specie is the fact that some species do not punish certain members of their group who does not reciprocally work with the others. For an example, naked mole rats, there exists big naked mole rats who sit around and do nothing for the whole duration of times when the others have to work to get food and check on the children, and yet they are not punished ? Came out that actually they did do something, when the rainy seasons came, they were the one who plucked the entrance hole to the colony to prevent flooding.

This helped researchers came to the conclusion that interactions in nature is not just a singular tit-or-tat game but rather many games where each members have their own roles in it, and the roles and strategies can bleed in and affect each other.
