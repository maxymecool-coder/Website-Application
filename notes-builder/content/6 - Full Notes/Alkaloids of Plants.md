# Alkaloids of Plants
**Details**
An alkaloid is a basic organic compound that plants produce that contains a "basic" nitrogen atom (an atom that can pick up a positive charge in acidic environments). This class of molecules includes many of the world's most famous psychoactive substances: caffeine, cocaine, morphine, nicotine, and ephedrine.

Historically, plants do not produce these substances for human consumption, rather, they are produced to compete in nature. Insects might land and consume/come in contact with t these alkaloids and instead back away or refrain from interacting with said plants. Though as mentioned in [[The name Pharmakos]], humans have learnt how to consume these in small doses, allowing them the usage of the pharmacological benefits of said substances.
