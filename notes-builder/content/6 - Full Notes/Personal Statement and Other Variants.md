# Personal Statement and Other Variants
**Details**
This is where the applicant will show the examiners why they should be accepted and why they should be given a chance. Though that is the case, it is not a place for one to boast themselves, the writer should not put others down or write in an egotistic way and such as it will show their personality in such way. They should try to show it through a story that was not particularly focused on highlighting them above the rest but rather just showing what they are.
