# Differentiation of anxiety neurosis from phobias
**Details**
The patient with multiple phobias may have continual anxiety because he cannot avoid the phobic situations. A person with a phobia that is exposed to a particular situation that he cannot escape will experience an acute anxiety attack instead, which is not essentially different to any other type of acute anxiety attack.

The difference between phobia and anxiety neurosis then is that phobia is highly specific, and they may remain relatively free from anxiety through avoidance. For the person with anxiety neurosis, however, they are not ready to avoid the noxious stimulus. In any situation, he can suddenly relate something to danger, not just a specific object or situation like phobias, but anything.
