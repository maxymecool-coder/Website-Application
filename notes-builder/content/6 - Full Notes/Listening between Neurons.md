# Listening between Neurons
**Details**
When two neurons communicate with each other, it is like a conversation. The presynaptic neuron has its own ways to make itself heard more, by dedicating more glutamate (neurotransmitter) to it, or get more receptors listening to it. But on the other side, how does it know that it is loud enough or not ? The answer depends on the postsynaptic neuron, as it can send a gas through to the presynaptic neuron to give it feedback. This process is called retrograde signaling.
