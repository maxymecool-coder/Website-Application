# Dissonance Cognition
**Details**
So, is this a deliberate process that we think about to do ? Well, not really. Amnesic patients - people who cannot form new explicit memories, were examined, and they had the same spreading of alternatives as the health controls. Yet, when they were showed it again, they didn't remember a single thing about the choices they made before, let alone which one. If explicit memory is required for the dissonance, then amnesiacs should show no effect, they showed it fully.

In other experiments on monkeys and children, the findings were the same. In fact, the research results would push the point further due to children and monkeys not having the sophisticated metacognitive reasoning Festinger's model requires.

The results would more correspond to it being an automatic process that we do not even understand completely. Its something that occurs without awareness, memory, and reflective capacity. The friend who insists he was "never that into her" may not be lying. He may genuinely not know his attitude has changed. The mechanism that rewrites the belief also erases the evidence that rewriting occurred. If you could feel yourself rationalizing, it wouldn't work — and these studies suggest that much of the time, you can't.
