# Marx's Cooperation and Oppression
**Details**
In the process of a new liberation, a new idealism, Marx thought of the process of it, the "lifetime" of the idealism so to say. When starting out, the people who led these idealisms would align their interests with everyone else, with the working class and such, to push society forward. And this does push forward society for some time, until the people become oppressive and start trying to do push it too much, when they start doing the interests by monopolizing, by abusing the workforce, that is when it gets oppressive and a revolution starts to brew.
