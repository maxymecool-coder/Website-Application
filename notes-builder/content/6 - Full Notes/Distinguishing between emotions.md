# Distinguishing between emotions
**Details**
Everyday observation indicates that the same external conditions may produce different reactions in different people, as we have said before, it depends on their depictions of the event itself. And in knowing it only then can we predict what would happen to one person when it does happen. Paramount meanings are determined by the person's habitual patterns of conceptualizations as well as his psychological state at the time it occurs.

We will now talk about the situations that has not been present before. Firstly, sadness and anxiety, when a person has already experience a loss, or that the domain has already been devalued in expectation of a loss, he will be sad. But anxiety comes as long as he is still intact and the loss is only imminent. Though he can also feel sad if he projected that it happened in the present rather than the future, which means his domain already subtracted it.

A typical ideation leading to any of the aforementioned emotions has a counterpart in the ideation found in anxiety reactions, depression, mania, and paranoid states. A significant difference between psychological disorders and normal emotional responses is that these emotions come from a distorted perception of reality, and so it will have a greater degree by internal factors.
