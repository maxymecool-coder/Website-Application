# Getting the Gist of information
**Details**
When we are reading about something, we must practice looking for the gist and distinguish it from merely supporting details. This is a process that involves deliberate practice, as the more often we do it effectively, the better we become at it.

Patterns are things that help us navigate texts and discourses, but they are not only concepts or the general terminology , but also typical mistakes we scan an argument for, general categories we apply, writing styles or mental models that we can learn from and develop from different insights and collect as if it were our own thinking models. And so with that we can read texts faster, understand information better, and so on.
