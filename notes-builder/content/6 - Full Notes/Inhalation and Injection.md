# Inhalation and Injection
**Details**
Inhalation is the fastest way to get something to the brain (about 10 seconds) as it has a massive surface area and blood flows from it to the heart and brain. This is why anesthesia is used in gas form and can be controlled during surgery.

Utilizing a hypodermic syringe allows for precise dosing but carries high risks (this is injection). There are two types of injection, to a muscle and to the vein, named intravenous and intramuscular. Because this way bypasses all of the body's defensive mechanism, it can be quite dangerous and carry infections or diseases like HIV due to needle-sharing or insufficient sterility.

Of course, the reason people do injection is that it is fast, and so for substances that can't be transferred in air form this will be the other alternative.
