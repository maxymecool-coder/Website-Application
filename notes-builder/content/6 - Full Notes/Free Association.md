# Free Association
**Details**
Basically the patient will have a safe space for them to be induced a state of mind midway between waking and trancing (Neurofeedback ?). The analyst (therapist) would be behind them or out of view. the patient then says whatever comes into their mind, no select thoughts or screen, and is encouraged to become a passive observer of her own stream of consciousness. The process to get here is another can of worms to dig into. 

This process hopes to discern the secrets, those unconscious ideas, while the defenses are still active (and in turn addressed if they are the very heart of discomfort). It is encouraged for the patient to converse of all their thoughts, even fleeting ones that they think are unimportant as this helps bypass the normal selection process that screens out conflictual content. 

This has limits though as it cannot be done for very long. The defenses block the thoughts that are too close to the repressed secrets. Furthermore, the feelings derived from the approach of these ideas or relief of them are soon transferred to the analyst, who becomes an object of intense emotions that are associated with the ideas. This in turn makes it so that sometimes they won’t express certain thoughts out of embarrassment as it pertains to the analysts themselves.
