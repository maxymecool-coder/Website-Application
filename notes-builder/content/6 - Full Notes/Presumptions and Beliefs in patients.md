# Presumptions and Beliefs in patients
**Details**
To proceed with the treatment, participants must abide by a few beliefs about themselves:

- ***People are doing the best they can***: The idea that all people are doing the best they can, given the causes of their behavior that have occurred to this moment.

- ***People want to improve***: They want to do it, uhhhh well at least its common.
  
- ***People need to do better, try harder, and be more motivated to change***: Even if people are trying their best and they're not getting better doesn't mean they can stop there, they still need to try even harder.

- ***People may not have caused all of their own problems, but they have to solve them anyway***: It may not have been their fault, but for it to be better, they must handle it by themselves

- ***New behavior has to be learned in all relevant contexts***: New skills have to be practiced in the situations where they are needed.

- ***All behaviors are caused***: There's always a cause for any actions/reactions, even if people don't know at first where they come from.

- ***Figuring out and changing the causes of behavior is a more effective way to change than judging or blaming***
