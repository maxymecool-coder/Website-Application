# The Oedipus Complex and Further Implications
**Details**
Due to his theories being heavily debated and illogical, Freud proposed another concept to explain the childhood experiences of being molested and abused. He replaced them with theories focusing on children's sexual fantasies. The gender he developed the most about this were boys: During early childhood, they all go through something called the Oedipus complex, when they develop a sexual interest in their mother and competitive aggression towards their father, same as the opposite.

The thing is, most boys are scared of presenting this outwardly, because they feel like they would get punished or abandoned after doing so. They keep all that emotion and need pent up, which leads to long-term personality difficulties.

He also coined other terms like penis envy and such, further implicating the sexual relations that our unconscious mind has on our actions and personality.
