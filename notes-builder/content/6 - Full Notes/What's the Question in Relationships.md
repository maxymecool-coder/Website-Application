# What's the Question in Relationships
**Details**
Long have we moved on (partially) away from satisfaction and longevity and instead focused on the one thing that truly still troubles us to this day: change.

The hard problem is that two people stand in front of everyone they know and sincerely promise forever; and two years later one of them has changed their mind while their friend in the same position hasn't. Same two people, in both groups, across time. Karney's own newlywed data show it: satisfaction measured repeatedly on a 5–45 scale, husband's and wife's trajectories crossing and diverging, with a visible inflection at the birth of a child. The task is not to explain why bad relationships are bad, but how good ones become bad.
