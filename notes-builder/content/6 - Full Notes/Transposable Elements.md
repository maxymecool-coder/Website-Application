# Transposable Elements
**Details**
Discovered by Barbara McClinton whilst researching corn, she discovered that there are genes in animals and organisms in generally that "plunk" themselves to new locations in the genome. By "plunking down" a promoter in front of a new gene, a transposon can create a new "if/then" cause. For example, there's an "if" clause about dehydration, and the "then" is to make the kidneys do something, the transposase can transport a promotor to make it so that "if" you are dehydrated, "then" mate so that you can give birth to your children in a wetter, more suitable season. Moreover, these elements can also transport exons between functional genes, combining them with each other to create new proteins.
