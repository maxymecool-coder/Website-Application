# Imago Workout
**Details**
Have you ever thought about how much your childhood, parents affected onto you ? Well to understand it all, you can do it in an exercise called "Imago Workout".
  
Sit down in a quiet and peaceful environment and think back to your childhood, inspect how you and your parents acted toward each other, finding out things that you had problem with them about. Then, tell your partner about it as if you were the child and they were the caregiver, after they mirror your sayings, come back to the receiver - giver dialogue and give closure, words of empathy between each other. Tell them what need you might want them to fulfill, though make those big goals into small actions.
