# Arising Methods of Therapy
**Details**
In recent times, many methods have come up, though criticized and often disputed between psychologists, they can work for certain groups of people as well. Some notable methods are: ACT, Somatic experiencing, Theater therapy, group therapy, family system structure,... They all have different basis and theories that have somewhat been proven as well.

Another arising method that is tailored to the client is that they have a wide range of methods so that they can use them effectively so that they can cater on the disorder and the relationship they have with the client. At different stages of therapy, they may apply different methods accordingly.

With even later information, neuromodulation techniques and other psychosocial methods have been taken into account and have proved to be a powerful addition/method. Not only does it open up novel therapeutic avenues but also contributes to the identification of neural circuits in emotional regulation, social rejection and impulsivity.
