# Being stuck
**Details**
The reason fight and flight happens is adrenaline, which is a chemical that helps us to defend or flee from danger, it can increase our heart rate and blood pressure. This is also present in traumatized patients, its just that these hormones take immensely more time to return to normal and spike quickly and disproportionately while reacting to light stress. The result of the spike in hormones can attribute to memory and attention problems, irritation, and sleeping disorders. It can also effect the body in the long-term, depending on which body part is the most vulnerable. In trauma patients, they cannot get themselves out of this state as their brain/body constantly misinterpret normal things as danger, pumping adrenaline and getting them into this state.

Moreover, the body pumping this much adrenaline can also affect the patient's physicality: sleep disturbances, headaches, pain, oversensitivity. Being so agitated or shut down keeps them from concentrating and focusing.  And to counter this, they would engage in activities to relieve their tension (Hurting themselves included). If they spend all their energy on staying in control and staying emotionally capable, they won't have sufficient energy to focus on studying and such.
