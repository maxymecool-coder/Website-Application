# Focused Mode
**Details**
- Thinking of solving problems with rational, logical trains of thoughts that involves significant concentrating. Imagine it as a game of pinball, when the thought is launched against bumpers and bounces constantly in between so many, travelling little distances many times, this is because the brain is basically trying to trace back something that has been done before, with steps and so on, the goal of the ball is to reach that.
