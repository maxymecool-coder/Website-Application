# Practical Works
**Details**
- Most, if not all practical works describe *how* something should be done, explaining the steps to get to it and so on. This practically means that guidebooks are practical works, any books that tells the reader how to live, how to invest and such are also practical works even if they are wrong, because that is for the reader to decide to themselves.
- Theoretical works are explanations of what the *something* is and also reporting about that *something*. 
- 

**Supporting Content**
- The usual misconception is that theoretical works are methods that has not been proven to be effective in real life and most likely will not. Practical works are methods that are guarantee to. This is wrong because of the above.
