# The Big Five Model of Personality
**Details**
Psychologists have devised questionnaires to measure belief in a just world, and hundreds of the other traits. They have examined the English dictionary and narrowed down, cut down the words that are similar to each other down to 35 words, then they mathematically narrowed it down even further, words that may be synonyms or antonyms of one another, but overlap. They looked for these clusters and then narrowed it down to the **big five personality traits**: neuroticism, extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness, and openness to new experience (McCrae & Costa, 1987). The case for these five traits is that they correlate with many dimensions of our personality and none of them correlate with the other four as well. The traits are described accordingly:

- **Neuroticism**: A tendency to experience unpleasant emotions frequently. Psychologists have proposed another term *emotional stability*. This can determine to the person's anxiety, hostility, depression, self-consciousness, and frequent squabbles with other people.

- **Extraversion**: A tendency to seek stimulation and enjoy the company of other people. This is what people usually call extraversion and introversion. Unlike Neuroticism, there are actually two sides to being high or low on this trait, the positives are warmth, gregariousness, assertiveness, impulsiveness, and a need for excitement. The unpleasant side is an increased chance in alcohol abuse and risky behaviors. In researches, this usually corresponded with happiness, so take that how you want to take it.

- **Agreeableness**: The tendency to be compassionate towards others. It is very closely related to empathy or Adler's concept of social interest. These people usually place a lot of trust into people and also expect the same amount of trust back. The also seem to have more stable marriages and stable employment (Roberts et al., 2007).

- **Conscientiousness**: A tendency to show self-discipline, to strive for achievement and competence. People high in this scale works really hard and complete their tasks on time. They just tend to avoid risky and dangerous activities and tend to have successful jobs, marriages, and greater than average life expectancy (Roberts et al., 2007).

- **Openness to experience**: A tendency to enjoy new experiences and ideas. People with this trait enjoy modern art, unusual music and though-provoking things, like films and books. They are just really open to everything, be it meeting people or doing something new.

There have been further research that suggested that this is still too many traits and it could be reduced down to three, which are neuroticism, extraversion, and agreeableness as the others correlate with each other in one way or another. The use of this depends on the purpose that it is being taken on for.
