# The limits of Priori
**Details**
Newton's discoveries were some of the first to ever prove the limits of priori and intuition. This trend reached the peak in modern physics with the Double Slit experiment. When light is fired through two slits, it behaves as a wave (creating interference patterns), but when detectors are added to see which slit a photon goes through, it behaves as a particle, and the interference pattern vanishes.

We can describe this behavior with mathematics (quantum probability distributions), just as Newton described gravity, but we cannot visualize it. Our natural, common-sense concepts of "waves" and "particles" fail to capture reality. This suggests that the universe is not obligated to be understandable to the human mind. As science digs deeper, it reveals a reality that is fundamentally alien to our daily experience of medium-sized dry goods.
