# Differences between books
**Details**
All kinds of books are inherently different, from the way they are read to their formatting, words… and so rules that applied for theoretical books (the ones we have talked about until this point), no longer applies fully to practical or fiction. Does this mean we have to fully learn or revise these rules ? No ! Most of the rules are generalized, and so there are fewer rules as well as more intelligible… the only downside is that it isn’t as intricate to the actual situation when followed (meaning not every mistake, roadblock is addressed).
