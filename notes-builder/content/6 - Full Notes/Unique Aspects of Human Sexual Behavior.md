# Unique Aspects of Human Sexual Behavior
**Details**
There are many aspects that we think are unique to us as humans in mating procedures, but in fact, they are not, like how we have non-reproductive sex, or foreplay. In fact, many animals have much more patience with their foreplay. But there is still one thing that is quite unique, serial monogamy.

While social pair-bonding is the norm across cultures, these bonds are not always lifelong, and sexual exclusivity within them varies greatly. Paternity studies indicating a 10-40% discrepancy in some populations underscore the difference between social and sexual monogamy. Finally, the concept of **romance** as the primary basis for a lifelong partnership is a very recent cultural invention, demonstrating that the social frameworks surrounding our biological drives are uniquely flexible and complex.
