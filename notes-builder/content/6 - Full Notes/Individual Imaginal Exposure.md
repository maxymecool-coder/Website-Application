# Individual Imaginal Exposure
**Details**
This is a core component or the core component of PE, it lasts 90 minutes and are conducted daily on weekdays during the 2 weeks of IOP. Accompanying the patients will be a tape recording to instruct them through the imaginal exposure. Some patients might be treatment resistant as former attempts have failed, that is when you have to convince them how it is different and such.

Then comes the time for review PTSD symptoms as we help the patient understand the rationale and their symptoms (so that they are more aware of said symptoms) as well as the things that make them avoidant since that’s also a part of their trauma. Cover and psychoeducate them on how avoidance exists, why they do it and what it prevents them from doing.

After that, we explain how exposure works to reduce this avoidance, by habituating the situation so that they become less scared and disconfirm the belief that anxiety and other negative emotions continue forever,…
