# Genetic Vulnerabilities
**Details**
Serotonin gene (known as the short and long serotonin transporter alleles). In humans the short allele has been associated with impulsivity, aggression, sensation seeking, suicide attempts, and severe depression. A doctor-Suomi showed that, at least in monkeys, the environment shapes how these genes affect behavior. Although in humans it has also been proven that children with short allele usually had higher rates of depression, they usually only had that when coupled with parents who didn't provide them with a sufficient childhood.

This means that safety provided by the parents is also (neurologically proven) to be extremely important, especially if the genetic vulnerabilities are passed down from the parents to the kids; but fear not as this can be resolved thanks to good parenting.
