# Counterargument to Dualism for Death
**Details**
Suppose a person is the _pair_ — soul plus body. Destroy either member and the pair no longer exists. If the person is the pair, the person no longer exists. On the composite view, immortal souls still get you no survivors: what persists is a former component, not the thing that was the person.

And so for the argument to be plausible, one must change the argument to a person being just the soul instead (which is similar to dualism and what not). But this argument has its problem. The one that I prefer the most is how the argument actually changes the subject. If the person is the soul, then it would have fundamentally failed the meaning of dualism as now any physical effects that was supposed to be happening to the body is actually happening to the soul. In this situation, it may not be immediately plausible to assume the person is still who they were before their survival
