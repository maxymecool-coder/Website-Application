# The Central Dogma of Genetics and the Disprovement
**Details**
The classical view of molecular biology, known as the **Central Dogma**, posits a one-way flow of information: **DNA → RNA → Protein**. In this model, the DNA sequence dictates the protein's shape, and shape dictates function. This implies that evolutionary change happens through small, gradual mutations in the DNA sequence that slightly alter a protein's effectiveness.

This was then disproven by a Nobel Laureate who figured out something about viruses, that they were cellular hijackers. They derived actions that would allow them to permanently affect the host and keep the multiplication of itself there in retroviruses, which were basically RNAs turning back into DNAs, the copy of information turning into the viral information itself.
