# Self-Dissolution of Christianity
**Details**
Christianity's dominance relied on its believers' not being able to exactly figure out retorts to its ideals. Yet in this aspect, it emphasized introspection, which trained people to become intellectually conscious and gaze back upon Christianity beliefs. The answer, revealed by this rigorous honesty, is that the Christian worldview is a lie. It is based on a fabrication (that suffering is caused by sin, not weakness) and a "holy lie" invented by priests to gain power.

This led to Christianity dying at the hands of its own morality, as its own weapon disproved its dogmas. This leaves European culture in a precarious position: they have refined the Will of Truth to a razor's edge, but they destroyed what made it valuable in the first place, the metaphysical foundation of God. This eventually leads to Nihilism, as Nietzsche argued, as the Will of Truth is flowing in a void.
