# Evidence Based Practices
**Details**
When studying at school, teachers are usually given materials that they will use their own methods and own teaching styles to teach the classes that they are assigned to. This can be ineffective for some due to the fact that these methods can be suitable to some learners whilst others none at all. When confronted with these facts, the teachers may often dismiss and scold the students as if it was their fault, affecting them not only academically but also mentally as well.

This is where EBP comes in. Evidence-Based Practices (EBPs) are instructional strategies and methods that have been proven effective through rigorous scientific research. Instead of relying solely on tradition or individual observation, EBPs provide teachers with a toolkit of validated approaches. By grounding instruction in research, EBPs reduce the guesswork involved in teaching. This shifts the focus from a teacher's abstract sense of what _should_ work to a concrete set of strategies that are _proven_ to work for diverse learners.
