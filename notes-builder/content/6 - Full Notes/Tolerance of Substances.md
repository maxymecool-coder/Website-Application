# Tolerance of Substances
**Details**
Basically as our body consumes anything harmful, like alcohol or nicotine, it does actually reacts, though it would take time until it eventually shows. Every subsequent time we consume the same substances, the liver will release “counter” substances, specifically enzymes, to quickly flush out everything. In the neurochemical sense, the brain may adjust to the abundance of drugs by reducing the receptors for said substances, which overall reduces their effects.
