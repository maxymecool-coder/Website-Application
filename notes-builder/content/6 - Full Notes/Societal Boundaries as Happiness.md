# Societal Boundaries as Happiness
**Details**
Since individuals cannot limit their own insatiable desires, the limit must be imposed by an external force. Durkheim argues this force cannot be physical, as "hearts cannot be touched by physico-chemical forces." It must be a _moral_ force that individuals recognize as just and yield to spontaneously. He argues further that humanity needs a higher force, a force that will make them follow codes, rules of their own restrictions so that they understand their limits and can be happy. He also argued that people will follow these rules out of respect, not fear. And that force is society. Therefore, the bond that regulates humanity is not physical, but moral and social. We escape the "yoke" of the body only to be subject to the "yoke" of society.
