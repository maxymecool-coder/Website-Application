# The Growth of the Mind
**Details**
Even after having understood how to read a book effectively, the reader's job is not done yet. There is still much to do after reading such a practical book, the real goal is for the reader to finally apply the knowledge they have gained to apply to books. Perhaps one may not accept the primary aim that has been endorsed as to read as well as possible. But if you do accept that aim and agree that the means provided have been appropriate, then you must make effort to read.
