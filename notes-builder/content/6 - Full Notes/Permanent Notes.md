# Definition
Combine ideas from your rough notes and literature notes to create ideas, develop arguments and discussions. 

Try and reserve one idea for each note and write as if you were explaining to others. Use complete sentences, give sources, references and write as clear yet brief as possible. Write in your own words to explain your understanding of the subject. This allows the notes to be understood even when taken out of the original context that it was written for.
