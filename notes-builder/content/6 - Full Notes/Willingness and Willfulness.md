# Willingness and Willfulness
**Details**
In skills training, there of course exists a tension between willingness (catering to the needs of a situation) and willfulness (catering to the needs of one’s self). And willfulness is usually the force that causes tension in the group. For example, about the willfulness of the leader of a group to bend the group to their ways, they may then cause a member to be affected, then the others to withdraw due to how undeveloped their skills are. Then comes the skills trainer themselves, who would rather be passive or instigate it to be further so they can refrain from dealing with it.

One cannot fight willfulness with willfulness, this would escalate the situation only. And so what can ? Willingness. Of course, we don’t combat it in a way that we accept everything requested of us, as that would constitute laziness and reduce accountability. We just accept that they are willfulness and move on to evince others.
