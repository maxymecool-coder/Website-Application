# Carl Roger’s Life
**Details**
In his early life, Carl was raised in a strictly Christian household, with strict rules and more. His parents were not necessarily supportive or intelligent in the way they loved and cared for their children, but Carl prevailed nonetheless. In this time, he was also reading many books, none of it was related to psychology or anything yet, but it would still enrich his world in a way so that it influenced him in his later life.

When in college, he did not started out studying psychology, but after going to several seminars and such based on religion, his views changed as he wanted to help troubled individuals, learn more about the human mind and all. Then he would try to enroll in psychology with his views, which were rejected. But the psychiatrists did accept him.

From then on, when he eventually interacted with patients, he found out that most of the time they themselves could understand what is wrong with themselves once educated and perhaps guided by the therapist. This eventually amounted to him believing in this principle of acceptance and guidance for the patient rather than what others were doing at that time.
