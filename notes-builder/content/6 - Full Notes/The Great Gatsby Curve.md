# The Great Gatsby Curve
**Details**
The ideal of social mobility, the notion that one’s birth does not determine one’s destiny, is a cornerstone of the American ethos. However, compelling economic evidence suggests this ideal is under significant threat from rising inequality. The Great Gatsby Curve, which shows us the correlation between intergenerational income and the degree of intergenerational mobility, shows us that we can barely move our classes up in society, showing its inequality.

In countries who boast about it like the USA, this curve show that it is in fact the most unequal amongst all its peers. And the Scandinavian countries are the most equal societies. This brings us to an important point, in these countries where inequality is high, it is due to how society values people's status and wealth and how they can reinvest that wealth into their children so that they can come to gain a stature like their parents.
