# Writing… To One’s Self
**Details**
It isn’t necessary to talk to access one’s inner world of feelings. One of the most effective way is through writing. When writing to one’s self, one doesn’t have to worry about other people’s judgment- you just listen to your own thoughts and let their flow take over. In some cases, when rereading what has been written, people often discover surprising truths. 

In society, we are usually pushed to not be ourselves, we have to tend to standards and keep our feelings from interfering with the task at hand. When we talk with someone with whom we don’t feel safe, we immediately keep our guard up and be careful with saying things out without even knowing that we have our guard up. 

But when writing to ourselves, it’s different, there is nothing that we need to hide from, so we can go into a sort of state with whatever we have and channel our thoughts from the inside. We can connect the two self-awareness part of our brain without worrying about the reception of others. In a practice called free writing, we can use any object as our personal Rorschach test for entering that stream of associations… anything that comes to our mind when looking at an object and keep writing without pause. 

This doesn’t only help the patient but also the therapist for them to decide what treatment to use. It helps patients by reducing their blood pressure and such, also improving their immune function and other immune markers in the blood.
