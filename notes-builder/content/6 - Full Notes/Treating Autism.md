# Treating Autism
**Details**
People in the earlier times, due to not knowing what Autism, tried to do therapy through chelation (leeching heavy metals from the blood based on unfounded toxicity theories of ASD) to the administration of secretin (pig hormone) or even LSD trials... But at the end of the day, even now, there's not a pharmacological cure for autism. The medications really are only used for comorbid symptoms; stimulants in high-functioning cases; antipsychotics for when they NSSI.

The only evidence-based intervention for it is early, intensive behavioral modification, specifically ABA or Applied Behavior Analysis. Requiring 20 hours a week of one-on-one coaching to reward speech, eye contact, social interaction. RCTs show that with early identification and intensive treatment, approximately 50% of children can achieve functional gain to enter a standard kindergarten classroom. The biggest thing right now in this field is the critical need for early diagnosis, how early can you know when a child has ASD ? "Under a year ?" seems to be the biggest goal right now.
