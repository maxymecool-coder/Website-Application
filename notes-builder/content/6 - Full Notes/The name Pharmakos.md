# The name Pharmakos
**Details**
The world pharmacology originated from the Greek word Pharmakos, which paradoxically meant both medicine and toxins. This linguistic root anticipates the foundational principle of toxicology established by Paracelsus (1493–1541): "All substances are poisons; there is none which is not a poison. The difference between a poison and a remedy depends upon the dose."

This principle though, does not only apply to drugs, the same can be said for food or water, or anything to be very honest. And so Pharmakos does not just restrict to drugs but exogenous chemicals that, in relatively small amounts, affect physiology. Moreover, also in Pharmakos, the relationship of these drugs to us were stated to be as an ally more than anything, with its own power and agenda, giving it conscience. And so as it has all of that on its own, it can also hurt us and give us knowledge to questions that we never asked for.
