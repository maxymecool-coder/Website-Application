# CBT Principles
**Details**
Conceptualization of clients using their data and cognitive formulation (cognitions, behavioral strategies, and maintaining factors). We can also include data on their strengths and such as well, then throughout the therapy process we will try to continue adding information to the conceptualization to better understand the patient.

For example, Judith Beck's popular case - Abe - was conceptualized into three time frames. From the beginning, she identified his cognitions that prevented his aspirations, then the behavioral obstacles that server to maintain his depression and then precipitation factors that influenced his original depression. These factors were because he struggled at work -> his wife became increasingly critical and divorced him, leading him to think he was incompetent. Third, she then hypothesized what were the key events in his past that may have created the suitable conditions for him to experience depression.

Additionally, various studies have shown that monitoring the therapy sessions and even in-between sessions improves outcomes. Getting feedback also helps this as they can be sure about the progress they are making and where they need to improve.

Even if you do not think it is in place, emphasize the positives. Recent research demonstrates the importance of this as it can cultivate their moods and subsequently allow them to hope. If we continue letting them ruminate, they would be able to go on to the end of the session, so its not advisable we do that. Redirect them.

An important factor is to LET THEM WORK WITH YOU, please, this will foster the relationship further and also improve and help them improve their skills and abilities. But before this, you need to remember that CBT is educative, teach them about the process, walk them through what you're going to do and also the skills of course.

CBT is flexible, very very flexible. From its base form back 50+ years ago, so many therapies and such have emerged from it, schema therapy, dialectical behavioral therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy,... and you can use the modules and skills from these therapies to use alongside CBT as well.
