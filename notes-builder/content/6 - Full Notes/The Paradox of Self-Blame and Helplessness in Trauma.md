# The Paradox of Self-Blame and Helplessness in Trauma
**Details**
Inherently, self-blame is a defense mechanism of PTSD patients, it makes them feel as if they were in control of the situation and could have done something about it. But their inner world knows the opposite, it knows that they couldn't have protected themselves in any capacity, and so is why it still hurts so much to self-blame.

And so you can't exactly rewire it like an automatic thought in CBT, as it isn't an illogical thought, it is a coping mechanism that has been developed to protect the self. If the self feels safe enough through emotional regulation, to gain back the control that it has loss, then this self-blame will go away by itself.

Of course, this does not mean that CBT or CPT won't work on trauma patients, but rather that it works when the trauma patient experience self-blame not as a defense mechanism, but an automatic thought. Further pushing to recalibrate something that is not a thought but protecting the self will only further put pressure on and even damage the patient further.
