# Apparent Competence
**Details**
As talked about before, BPD individuals have a significant deficit in cognitive abilities, and this would also affect their generalization abilities, meaning they cannot solve one problem then generalize the solution to apply to another. This is basic and found amongst children as they’re still developing, but remember, BPD causes a deficit in cognitive abilities.

Secondly, the effects can also vary depending on their emotional states or traumatic experiences. Of course, the same applies for people in general but BPD again causes this to become much more severe and present in its effects.

Thirdly, you, yes you, you can also be the problem as you expect way too far and wide about your loved one’s own competence, leading to you treating them as if they should have that ability, which further results in invalidation as they genuinely don’t have that ability.

The last part of this is masking emotions, the person suffering from BPD may not show it on their face because being so negative has caused them no good and has been a negative reinforcement. What bad can this cause ? As they don’t show their emotions, they can say that they’re having a really shitty time whilst not showing so. We depend on human facial and body cues to assess if people are lying or not, people with BPD don’t show this correlation, but we HAVE to assume it is true and help them/validate them nonetheless.
