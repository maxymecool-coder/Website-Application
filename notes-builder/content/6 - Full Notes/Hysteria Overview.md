# Hysteria
**Details**
In hysteria the patient believes he has a physical disorder. Since the imagined disorder is not fatal, he tends to accept it without severe anxiety. Patients with hysteria are essentially “sensory imagers”; that is, they imagine the particular illness and then take the sensory experience as corroborative evidence of having the illness. The patient typically experiences sensory or motor abnormalities that fit the pattern of his erroneous concept of organic pathology.
