# Zuboff Counter Argument
**Details**
Against Block's skepticism, defenders of Functionalism (like Arnold Zuboff) offer the **Prosthetic Brain** thought experiment. Imagine your brain cells are slowly failing, and doctors replace them one by one with perfect silicon chips that perform the exact same functional role (input/output) as the original neurons. You would likely agree to the surgery to save your mind.

If you continue this process until your entire brain is silicon, Functionalism says you would still be "you" with the same feelings, because the functional structure is preserved. If you deny this, you must argue that at some arbitrary point (e.g., 51% silicon), your consciousness suddenly vanished, which seems implausible. This suggests that "Blockhead" (the China Brain) might actually be conscious, or at least that our prejudice against it is merely a bias against its "clunky" constituents (people instead of neurons), similar to the prejudice against metal or silicon intelligences.
