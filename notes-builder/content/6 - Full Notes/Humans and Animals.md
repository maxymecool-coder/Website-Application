# Humans and Animals
**Details**
More than you would believe, psychology is not just observing or understanding human behaviors but also the behaviors of animals and such like that. In between species, we act radically different to each other, in our actions, like how we eat, how we find our mate, and so on. This could be explained by the difference in genetics (the variance) causing the psychological behavior that we have and such species have. Peacocks, for example, find their mate by wiggling their tail or something around, whilst us as humans cultivate a long-term relationship (usually) based on our liking and so on of the individual.
