# The adaptability of man
**Details**
In the modern society, we confront many complexities and pressures of the daily life, yet we can still function effectively in it. Not only can he adapt to changes in his environment and confrontations with others, but he also manages to deal with his own hopes, wishes and expectations and as well as others. 

We are forced to make these decisions under extremely short amounts of time as well as in difficult situations. (Determining if it is dangerous or only seems to be). It would be very chaotic if we weren’t able to deal with the stimuli we encounter everyday and monitor our own minds. Even further, we are able to distinguish the subtle clues that allow us to separate our adversaries and our friends. See through other people’s social masks, differentiate truths and lies, friendly jabs and insults. Impressively all of these happen pretty much automatically without evidence or much cognition, deliberation, or reflection. 

This proves that people have acquired highly refined, sophisticated techniques to deal with these problems and environments. Moreover, we learn and absorb information which help us to deal with our psychological problems. Even in situations where there isn’t already a solution, we can still make corrections, judge, interpret, predict them and how’d they turn out when applying certain solutions.
