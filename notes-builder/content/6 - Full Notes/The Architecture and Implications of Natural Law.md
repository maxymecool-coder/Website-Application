# The Architecture and Implications of Natural Law
**Details**
And so in the process of conjuring such an argument, Locke came to establishing a framework of rules that exist above any earthly king, which brings us to the profound theological and philosophical mechanics of Natural Law. Locke draws a strict distinction between Divine Law, which requires religious revelation, and Natural Law, which is simply the will of God decoded through human reason by observing the natural order of the world. He effectively conjures that since God created us, he has legitimate authority over us and such. The fundamental law of nature dictates that all mankind is to be preserved, and when absolute preservation is impossible, the innocent must be given strict priority. This law governs human behavior entirely independent of any government; for example, we are forbidden from casually destroying nature, animals, or resources without a genuine need, laying the groundwork for how we interact with the world before the state even exists.

Furthermore, as God did not intend an earthly lieutenant, and as the capacity to reason exists in everyone,  the original state of nature is inherently a state of equal, reciprocal political jurisdiction. Every individual is essentially their own sovereign, possessing absolute jurisdiction over their own body and possessions.
