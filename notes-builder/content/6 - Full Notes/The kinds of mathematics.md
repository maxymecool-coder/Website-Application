# The kinds of mathematics
**Details**
There are many kinds of mathematics out there, but the two that can be taught how to read are great scientific and mathematical classics and the modern scientific popularizations. These advices can be applied on the reading of specialized monographs on abstruse and limited subjects, but we cannot help you read those.

The reason is that we are not qualified to do it, and most works nowadays are written for professionals to converse with each other, not for the average layperson to understand. They do this because its just more convenient as they can communicate with each other more efficiently for breakthroughs and so on.
