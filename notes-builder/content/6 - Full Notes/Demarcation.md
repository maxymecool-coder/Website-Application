# Demarcation
**Details**
When doing science, we must be able to establish what is and what is not science. Following the epistemology of Karl Popper, scientific inquiries must be refutable. Domains like religion, art, and politics exist outside this boundary, while subjective experiences (like consciousness) remain on the very edge of our scientific capabilities. Furthermore, Popper noted that science never definitively "proves" a hypothesis; it only provides supporting evidence.

However, we just cannot rely on empirical experimentation, we must also have a theoretical framework to work off of. A hypothesis tested via experimentation without an underlying mechanism is often indistinguishable from pseudoscience. For instance, testing whether Mozart's music improves cognitive abilities is mediocre science if the explanation relies on "magical" qualities of the music. We must explain the phenomenon using whatever has already been framed and confirmed within neurobiology. Alike any spiritual framework, those could also turn into bad science as well.
