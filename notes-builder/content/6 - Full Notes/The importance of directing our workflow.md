# The importance of controlling learning courses
**Details**
When even highly intelligent students fail in their studies, its most often because they haven’t seen the meaning in what they were supposed to learn and can't make a connection to their own goals, or lack the ability to control their own studies and on their own terms. 

There is an inherent risk we take in doing something long-term without much clue what to expect, as we can get bored and perhaps drop it completely. The thing is we can mitigate this by applying a flexible organization scheme that allows us to change whenever necessary. 

Alongside our work, we can ask about the relevancy and interest of something instead of just going with it. By elaborating on this, we also discover aspects we didn’t know anything about before and develop our interests along the way. 

Since dividing writing into small parts allows us to work on exactly what is needed at a certain tike then taking the next step from there, which gives us control over our work and where it is going, subsequently giving us more motivation to work on it and make it sustainable.
