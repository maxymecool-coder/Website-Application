# Freud's Structure of Personality
**Details**
Freud claimed that a person's personality consisted of three aspects: id, ego, and superego. The id is what contains the sexual and biological drives. The superego contains the memory of rules and prohibitions that keep us in check and acting properly in society. One can easily see that these two concepts clash with each other, and in fact, Freud admits that they do constantly, and the result are strong negative emotions. So to manage and take care of these things, there is the ego, which is the rational, decision-making aspect of the personality that helps to monitor and balance between these two things.
