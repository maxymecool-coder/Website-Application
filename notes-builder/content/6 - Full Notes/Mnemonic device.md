# Mnemonic device
**Details**
As said before, a story can make the memorization of certain things easier or more compelling. This is what a mnemonic device is, it can be weaving a story together or just a fun sequence that rhymes, nonetheless, it helps us memorize more than just simply rote memorization. For example, if you had to remember a set of words like: person, tree, bench, bus; you might make a story of a person going to a tree and sitting on the bench under it waiting for the bus, which is much easier to remember rather than just disconnected words.
