# The Versatility of the DSM
**Details**
DSM, despite how corrupt and bad it it at times, still allowed us a whole new perspective and widen our ability in how we approached therapy. When a therapist did an interview on a patient, it was easier for them to communicate and work with the patient. When the same therapist could not put their finger on the problem and helped the patient, they could easily require assistance from another therapist and that person would be able to understand the conditions underlying of the patient and get to helping. 

Even more than that, DSM even allows patient to not have to go through the interviewing process where they have to open up about themselves and the things that affect them which can gauge and encourage some patients to be more cooperative in therapy. For example, a patient with PTSD may not want to repeat the event to another person once they have already done it once, DSM can solve this problem.
