# Identity-Aligned Rewards
**Details**
While adding immediate rewards to our habits is crucial, poorly chosen rewards can backfire. Standard rewards might be ineffective if they drain valuable resources or contradict the very habit you are trying to build (e.g., rewarding a successful week of dieting with a massive junk-food cheat meal). What is the solution? You must choose a reward that actively reminds you of the identity you are trying to embody. For example, if you are trying to lose weight and you successfully resist buying fast food, do not just pocket the money and forget about it. Instead, put that saved money towards a specific prize for yourself that aligns with a healthy identity, like new workout gear or a massage. This proves your new identity to yourself without sabotaging your progress.
