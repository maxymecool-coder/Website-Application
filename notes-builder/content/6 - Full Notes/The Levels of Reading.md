# The Levels of Reading
**Details**
- Elementary reading: Its just normal, everybody goes through this phase, on a new language too.
- Inspectional reading: Skimming systematically, going through a book within a small limited time (Most of the time is to see if the book is worth reading or not). Should be reading the table of contents at this stage as it's really helpful for things to come.
- Analytical Reading: Reading the book to grasp it fully and turning the knowledge it offers into the reader's own.
- Syntopical Reading: Comparing and linking information about all types of reading altogether without them being of the same subject. Then after that constructing an analysis of some subject that isn't in the books.
