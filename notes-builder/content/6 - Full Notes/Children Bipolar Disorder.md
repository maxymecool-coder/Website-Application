# Children Bipolar Disorder
**Details**
For most of history, bipolar disorder has been considered a teenage or adult mental disorder. However, roughly 35 years ago, clinicians started diagnosing children who had ADHD or conduct problems with bipolar disorder. This change caused a massive upheaval in the psychiatric space as people argued whether they had bipolar or were just severely dysregulated. The main point was how different bipolar disorder presented itself in adults and children. Though adults may suffer from grandiose, euphoria in manic episodes, children have these things all the time. And so people would have to look for chronic irritability and explosive temper tantrums rather than distinct manic episodes. But because of this diagnosis, children started to be given heavy-duty mood stabilizers and antipsychotics, causing them to overdose.
