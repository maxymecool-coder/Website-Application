# The Cognitive System of Therapy
**Details**
This is a system of therapy that has been validated by numeral empirical studies, and of course is widely applied in the current state of the world today.

A central aspect of this theory is that a content of a person's thinking affects his mood. This has been proved by many studies that made subjects focus on ideas of a self-enhancing or self-deflating content to produce subsequent positive and negative feelings. (For example, Velten, 1967, Colemen, 1970,...)

These controlled studies show that cognitive therapy is largely based off of the theory. The treatment manuals clearly delineate how the therapeutic strategies are derived from the cognitive model.
