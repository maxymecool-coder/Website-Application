# Path Diagrams and the Role of Education
**Details**
The [[The Outflow Table and Its Findings]] showed that social status was inherited, this shows us how. It shows us the relationships between the parent's status and occupation and their children's status and occupation as well. The most important founding showed that it was not the occupation that affected the correlation of the occupation of the children, but rather the level of education.

Essentially, this showed us that the level of education is what transferred through from parent to son. A high-status father can provide his son with the environment, resources, assistances so that they can flourish and also get a high-ranking job.
