# Animal Testing FDA (This is a part of the FDA Modernization Act 2.0)
**Details**
In April 2025, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announced it will begin to phase out mandatory animal testing for investigational new drug (IND) applications. This reflects how far the road has gone for animal testing, how much we have improved upon the alternative methods (NAMs), proving scientifically the inefficacy of animal testing as well.

This is a huge step-up for us due to the ethical and scientific implications of it. In recent years, more and more research has come out about how ineffective animal testing really is. The drugs that comes from this method barely pass the stages of verifying its effectiveness and usually cost extreme amounts of money and time. And so to have methods that are recognized by the FDA and endorsed by it to be more efficient as well as ethical.

Of course the process of rolling this out must be understandably careful due to how much of a landmark animal testing has been for the majority of the recent history of the scientific community. It will require extensive training and piloting workshops.

Do note that currently NAMs are not fully replacing all of this yet, for specific decisions it has proven extremely effective.
