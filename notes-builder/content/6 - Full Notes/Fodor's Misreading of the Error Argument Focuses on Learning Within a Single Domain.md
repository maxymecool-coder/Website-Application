# Fodor’s knowledge of stopping
**Details**
Fodor argues that just like anything in life, the putative ancestor understands when to stop, when enough is enough. And he thinks that if a domain-general mechanism is able to understand that mating is necessary, it would also understand how much is likely to suffice and so when to stop.
