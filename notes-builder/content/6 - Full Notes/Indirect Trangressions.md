# Indirect Trangressions
**Details**
We will now continue with situations where the transgressions are indirect. For example, a host feels annoyed at a guest for showing off his knowledge at a dinner party, or a cleric is irritated at his friend's stories of success in business. This is all because they assault on the self-esteem of the protagonist. It indirectly exposes them to self-devaluation. Like how the host feels like the guest is stealing all his attention, or the cleric feeling less successful.

All of these represent a kind of loss, but instead of sadness, they experience angriness, why ? This is because the protagonist can minimize the devaluation of himself by focusing on the negative attributes of the offender. But if he accepts all of these things as logical, reasonable, only then will he feel sad. If he fluctuates, these emotions will do so as well.
