# The Solanaceae Family (Nightshades)
**Details**
The _Solanaceae_ (Nightshade) family contains over 75 genera and 2,000 species. Its name come from the Latin Solanum ("to quiet") alluding at its toxic nature. Morphologically, they are defined by a five-petaled flower, often tubular in shape.

Despite being poisonous, many critical food plants originating from South America actually come from this family, notably like potatoes, tomatoes, eggplants and chili peppers. Sweet  potato though is in a completely different family, the Morning Glory (_Convolvulaceae_), and the **Yam** (_Dioscorea_) is from the _Dioscoreaceae_ family. They are botanically unrelated to the potato despite culinary similarities.

The poison actually resides mostly in the leaves, that is why people are fine eating the fruit and so on from these plants.
