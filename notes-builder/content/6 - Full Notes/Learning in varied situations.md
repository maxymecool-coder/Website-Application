# Learning in varied situations
**Details**
Retrieval practice with different times, contexts and interleaves different learning material can help one find new connections to that material, as the brain has to form new connections and associations for different situations. This helps broaden the mastery and versatility of one's skillset, as they are now practiced to use it in different situations. Think of it as a dictionary of your own, as you experience more scenarios, you put more definitions into it, understanding what to apply in which more. For example, a chef would understand what good a wok is for and a cast iron pan is for. A basketball player would know what situations to go for a layup or a shot, and so on and so forth.
