# Reviewing Action Plans
**Details**
Before each session, prepare the review these notes to give the impression/idea that the Action Plan is something important. The amount of time on this depends heavily on the situation like the crisis the client is encountering and all. This is when you ask the client to reread the note, to repeat, check it and everything for them to understand and have a collaborative experience on it.

Now comes the problem solving part of the Action Plan when difficulty arises. First is the practical problems, like them forgetting why the Action Plan is important, disorganization (being unable to find it) and difficulty with performing a task. Here you can sure to do it yourself on how you would encourage the client, help the client figure out the task and everything else.

On the other side, there's the interfering cognitions, there are several situations you can encounter from this. The first being the cognition whenever they think about doing it, perhaps they think they're incapable, or that they are pathetic having to do this and all. Identify that automatic thought and suggest them otherwise, to prove that they're not incapable through behavioral experimenting or suggesting for help and so on and so forth. 

**Supporting Content**
Same for you as the therapist, review your own thoughts about the person to see if there's any negative cognition to be dealt with if you think you're being dragged into their negative thoughts.
