# Social Media's Impact on Human Hierarchies and Self-Perception
**Details**
Humans repurpose ancient biology for novel contexts, and social media exemplifies this, expanding hierarchies infinitely and often detrimentally. We use the same hormones and circuits as baboons but abstract them—feeling belittled by a stranger's luxury car or an uninvited party in Singapore. Feeds bombard with mishmash contexts (meals, bodies, intellects), eroding self-esteem via endless comparisons.

Evolved for limited interactions, our PFC now navigates vast landscapes, triggering stress from abstract inadequacies (e.g., envying Bezos's space flight). This fosters novel misery, using dopamine-testosterone loops for virtual status chases. Mitigate by curating feeds or limiting exposure, reclaiming constrained hierarchies for well-being.
