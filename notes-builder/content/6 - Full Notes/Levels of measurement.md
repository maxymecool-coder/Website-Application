# Levels of measurement
**Details**
These are the variables used to describe the characteristics of what is happening in the data, confusing, yes. Nominal variables are pure labels for categories with no inherent order: gender (male/female), smoker/non-smoker, securely vs. insecurely attached. Since these are not ordered by magnitude there is no need to classify them as so. Ordinal variables carry rank, but the rank does not indicate the same space (first-born, second-born, etc...). Scales are the finalized version which includes everything aforementioned to be missing from the other two measurements.
