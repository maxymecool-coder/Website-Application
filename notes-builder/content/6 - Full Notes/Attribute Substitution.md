# Attribute Substitution
**Details**
After defining heuristics for so long, Kahneman proposed a single mechanism underlying all of them: attribute substitution. This practically allows us to attribute an irrelevant answer to a question that we were asked in the first place. The important thing is we never really realize that we substituted: as seen in heuristics, we replace questions constantly, and its almost automatic.  The availability heuristic substitutes "how frequent is X?" with "how easily can I think of examples of X?" The representativeness heuristic substitutes "what is the probability of X?" with "how much does X resemble my prototype?" Anchoring substitutes "what is the true value?" with "how far from this number should I adjust?" In each case, the substitute attribute is computationally cheaper to evaluate than the target attribute. In conclusion, this framework explains why we aim for the inaccurate explanation, because its easier, and it feels right for us.
