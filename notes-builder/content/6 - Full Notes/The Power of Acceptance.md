# The Power of Acceptance
**Details**
The silence that trauma patients exhibit can lead to the death of their own souls. It reinforces the isolation that they feel from having such an event happen to them. 

If they have been hurt, then they need to acknowledge and name what has happened to them. Doing so is a way of finally resolving that point in their life, is a way to forgive themselves for doing the things that led them to that situation. As long as someone is keeping all this information and trauma to themselves, they are fundamentally at war with themselves. They are trying to hide things that consumes a large amount of energy, it stops them from being able to enjoy or pursue anything else. Meanwhile, the adrenaline floods their systems, making them overall just sicker. 

To exemplify this, one can look to the story of Helen Keller, of course I am not going to explain and list out her stories in full content here. But in the context of her books, being able to express her feelings into words because of how Ms Sullivan taught her allowed her to experience freedom and happiness for the first time in her life.
