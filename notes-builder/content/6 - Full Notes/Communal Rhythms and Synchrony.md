# Communal Rhythms and Synchrony
**Details**
The foundations of attachment are responsivity. When we are born, we are embodied in responsive faces, gestures, and touch. But trauma breaks this down.

This is something that is closely integrated with mirroring. As said before, when we are not mirrored, it causes even more severe symptoms of trauma and such. This is where sensorimotor therapy comes in.

Van der Kolk once treated a girl named Ying Mee. none succeeded in making contact with her, and her rhythmical engagement system did not work. So they tried sensorimotor therapy with her. The sensory integration clinic in Watertown is an indoor playground for kids. Ying Mee played with the staff there, and made contact with the toys there. Eventually after six weeks, she started to talk.

Although he has not thoroughly research the effects of this for traumatized adults, he regularly incorporate it in his seminars. It is also particularly a good way to become attuned which provides families with the visceral experience of reciprocity. (PCIT) and (SMART).
