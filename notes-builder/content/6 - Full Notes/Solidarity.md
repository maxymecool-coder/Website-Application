# Solidarity
**Details**
From Emile Durkheim's point of view, this was what that people needed to avoid suicide, as it is the core concept that society stood on. People needed to solidate their relationships for them to feel like they are in the same community. 

Many in his times feared that the modern society would lack this, and would start deteriorating in the populations due to the promotion of individualism that was happening. They thought that if people could think for themselves, they could not mind be similar to each other in any form of way. As the farmer who had his own routines, his own principles, if he was any different from the businessman, they would not have solidarity together.
