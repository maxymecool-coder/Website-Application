# Difficulty of studying power
**Details**
Power is a central concept in sociology and political science, yet it is notoriously difficult to define, observe, and measure. This challenge stems from several fundamental problems that complicate its study. How would one define power, see power in its nature and so on ? There are so many forms of influences in our daily lives that we cannot accurately understand and see which is power and which are others. How would we see power ? Not just in conflicts and battles which results in one side winning but where it prevents such conflicts from happening because of its overwhelming nature.
