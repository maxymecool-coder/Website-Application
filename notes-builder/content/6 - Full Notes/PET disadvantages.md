# PET disadvantages
**Details**
However, this method also has disadvantages, one of the most significant being the cost. PET machines are extremely expensive, meaning not all hospitals can afford one, and they are available in very few universities or research centers. A great disadvantage is also the exposure to radioactivity, which is a primary reason why PET scans are rarely used for research purposes. Furthermore, PET has a low temporal resolution because blood flow is a slower measure than the electrical activity picked up by methods like EEG. Another major drawback is the long interval required between scans, which makes the entire procedure rather time-consuming and resource-intensive.
