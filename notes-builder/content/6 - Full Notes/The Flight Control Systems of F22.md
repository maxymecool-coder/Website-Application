# The Flight Control Systems of F22
**Details**
For high-performance fighters utilize irreversible systems powered by high-pressure hydraulics and commanded by electrical impulses. At supersonic speeds, the aerodynamic forces on the control surfaces are so immense that we cannot turn them. Consequently, the pilot would instead input into the computer which causes the hydraulic actuators to move the surfaces.
