# Managing Homework
**Details**
When reviewing homework, the strategy is to validate the progress of practicing skills correctly and effectively and to then problem-solve when it is not done or not practiced correctly, and/or problems in using the skills are identified. Even for those who try their best to learn and collect these skills, they can still encounter the same problems. Thus it is important to not eliminate review time in favor of any other thing.

There are 2 stages in this process: understanding the problem at hand; and attempting to generate new, effective use of the skills. To understand these problems, it requires the person to highlight and define problems in using the skills, highlighting patterns and implications of current skills use, and developing hypotheses about factors interfering with effective use of the skills. The second stage, then, is to target change, providing feedback on correct implementation of skills when needed; and encouraging efforts to practice the skills by providing the rationale and such for the skills.
