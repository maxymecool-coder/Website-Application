# Modularity of Therapy
**Details**
Many psychotherapies actually have this feature as they allow the therapists to have separate parts of the procedure of psychotherapy that they can change accordingly to what they need in the process. For example, DBT as a therapy has many modules, including those from mindfulness, stress tolerance, interpersonal skills, emotion regulation,... to address the cluster of symptoms of BPD. And so depending on the patient, there is flexibility for the therapist to do what fits best for the situation (Not mentioning the further "type" of DBT for BPD). Perhaps this may also allow the therapist to integrate other therapies into their work which can increase the effectiveness.
