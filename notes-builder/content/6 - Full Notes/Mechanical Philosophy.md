# Mechanical Philosophy
**Details**
As Teology collapsed, people turned to an alternative view about the world, a more “scientific” and “explanatory” way perhaps. This view was led by Galileo and Descartes, who argued and tore down Teology by redefining how matter and physical objects functioned. If before, people believed in rocks falling down to the earth because it wanting to return to its origin, we would now consider that a non-explanatory statement which is useless.

A strong example of this would be inert movement, things did not just move on their own but something was making them, which presented in how the swirling of a bowl of soup would leave a trail of soup filling up the space left behind, leading to Descartes theorizing about vortexes in the universe and cosmos as well.****
