# The Psychometric Approach
**Details**
The **psychometric approach** is a method for studying intelligence that is based on the measurement of individual differences in performance. Pioneered by Charles Spearman, this approach uses statistical analysis of scores from various cognitive tests to identify the underlying structure of intelligence.

The core idea is that if performance on different types of tasks (e.g., arithmetic, spatial reasoning, vocabulary) correlates, it suggests these tasks share a common mental faculty. By analyzing these correlations, psychometricians aim to create a "map" of human intelligence, identifying its core components and how they relate to one another. This approach forms the foundation for most modern intelligence testing.
