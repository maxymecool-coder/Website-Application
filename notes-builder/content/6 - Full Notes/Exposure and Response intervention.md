# Exposure and Response intervention
**Details**
Treatment, therefore, focuses on breaking this link through exposure and response prevention (ERP), a therapy where patients are exposed to their obsessive trigger but are prevented from engaging in the compulsive ritual, thereby learning that the anxiety will subside on its own without the compulsion. Of course, depending on the patient, the psychologist would need to adjust their own methods accordingly. They might have to slowly ease the patient into it, by expressing small amounts of the trigger slowly, or they will have to teach the patient, give them methods of dealing with their triggers. Moreover, these patients might even have to bring these situations and skills to the outside world and deal with it there.

Although this has shown to have worked, it is quite hard to get people to accept and sign up for it due to its nature of involving a lot of pain and fear at first (as you have to literally face your fears). Medications can be used in these situations to help ease the patient into the treatment, although it is highly unnecessary due to the effectiveness of the therapy.
