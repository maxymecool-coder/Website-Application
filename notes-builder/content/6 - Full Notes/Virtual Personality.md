# Virtual Personality
**Details**
Critics of Searle argue that it is not the internal system of the computer but rather the whole computer system itself that understands. Even if the man in the Chinese Thought experiment doesn't understand, the room, with the book, and the man understands. But this is a shallow argument perhaps, as Searle refutes by mentioning even if the man master the symbols and is able to rearrange it however he likes, he still does not understand the language.

**Supporting Content**
Similarly, people have argued that the computer merely needed more data or different types of data like visually, alike attaching a camera to the computer. Searle argues that the computer merely processes this by way of interpreting the data into a different output, not understanding the input really.
