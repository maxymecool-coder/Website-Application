# Blockhead: Chinese Experiment
**Details**
Ned Block then proposed an experiment to refute Functionalism known as the China Blockhead. He asks us to imagine converting the entire population of China to Functionalism. Each citizen is given a two-way radio and a simple rule book: "If you receive signal Y, send signal L to person Z." In this scenario, the entire nation of China acts as a single functional system that perfectly mimics the functional organization of a human brain for one hour.

He argued that the nation now was a brain and should feel pain and be able to access mental states, but they don't do that, as that would be absurd, the whole country wouldn't feel pain if one person did, they clearly lacked any qualia -> Functionalism is false.
