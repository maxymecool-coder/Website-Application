# Electrical Stimulation in Brain Research
**Details**
- **Methodology**: Inserts electrodes into exposed brain (e.g., during surgery) to electrically stimulate areas. Observes behavioral or functional changes. Less common in humans; often used in animal studies or pre-surgery. 
- **Advantages**: Selectively activates/inactivates areas. Identifies locations/functions without reorganization (unlike brain damage). 
- **Disadvantages**: Highly invasive; limited to patients or animals. Imprecise/approximative results.
