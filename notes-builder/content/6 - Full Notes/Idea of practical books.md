# Idea of practical books
**Details**
The most important thing to remember here is that you must understand these books does not solve the problems it says but it can guide you to solve it when you do finally act on it. Take the book we are writing about for example, it is teaching us to read a book analytically and good sure, but it does not solve the problem of not reading a book effectively, that must be done through the act of reading. 

Every action is special, it happens here and now and under a set of circumstances, never the same as what the books says, and so you cannot act in general. But these books can state general rules that applies to a lot of such situations. And so using such book we must apply the rules, and exercise practical judgement in doing so. Which means the reader must apply his own knowledge into the rules for it to be applicable in such situations.
