# Method of Tracking
**Details**
First when pivoting we would want to tension in the wrist, enough so that it doesn't feel too much, then tense in the forearm to support a further track. Don't be afraid to reset our aim into the center of the mouse whenever the bot is strafing or when in a spot of low mobility. 

We will need to reset when the bot is approaching a pivot and we are at the edge of our mousepad. Otherwise, it is when the bot is about to change direction and we are losing space to move our mouse.
