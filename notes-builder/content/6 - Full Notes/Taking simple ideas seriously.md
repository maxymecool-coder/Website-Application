# Taking simple ideas seriously
**Details**
Taking the example of Warren Buffet, he thinks about the relationship between price and value. He doesn’t even look at the price from yesterday. And he understands that simple is not the same as easy, and that the worst thing you can do is to make a simple task unnecessarily complicated. 

Understanding if a stock is worth buying or not requires one to look into their underlying fundamentals. Or how it may be worth buying than others. 

True breakthroughs and understanding often come from identifying simple principles within complex systems, rather than getting lost in superficial complexity (like focusing on flapping wings instead of aerodynamics for flight, or creating overly complex financial instruments while ignoring basic value).
