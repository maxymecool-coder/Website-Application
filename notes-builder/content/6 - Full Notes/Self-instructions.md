# Self-instructions
**Details**
Evaluating situations, deciding what to do and such leads to self-instructions. In a number of conditions, this may be overwhelming as the person constantly drive themselves. These people are called “overachievers” in the early or mild states of depression, and they keep criticizing what they should do. Another type is of those who avoids such situations, when faced with adversity, when faced with a dangerous situation (misinterpretation) then they would find ways to escape, to not do it. 

These thoughts are more prominent in the ideation of anxious and depressed patients. They don’t have confidence in their abilities to cope with stress, or danger, and so they want to escape to safety or think about it. They detach themselves from the burdens or from life and stick to a usual routine.  On the other hand, they instead take action against the offense. They will retort, propel themselves into angry emotions and fight back.
