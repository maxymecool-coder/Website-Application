# GLP 1 as a weight loss substance
**Details**
The origins of GLP are quite interesting, coming from the Gila Monster connection (Exendin-4) was discovered by Dr. John Eng. He found a hormone in the venom of the Gila monster that mimicked GLP-1 but lasted longer in the body. This became exenatide (Byetta), the first GLP-1 drug approved in 2005.

The drug is an agonist of the receptor well, GLP. It allows the person to be able to eat though to a much less extent as they will feel as if they are full. It basically makes it physically and neurologically impossible to eat an absurd amount of food. It stimulates POMC neurons (which tell you "stop eating") and inhibits AgRP neurons (which tell you "eat now"). And a multitude of other mechanisms.

Another reason that GLP is used is to help diabetic patients as it prompts the pancreas to release insulin, but only when the blood sugar is high. It suppresses glucagon, a hormone that tells the liver to release stored sugar into the blood.
