# State-dependent cognition
**Details**
In phobias, when a phobic is far from a situation that might make his phobia act up, he can think incredibly rationally, like normal people. He can assess the situation logically and even point out how irrational his thinking was in such situations. Yet when approaching such situations, he gets more and more anxious, his cognitive distortions overrides him, his amygdala hijacks his brain into catastrophizing more and more until it completely takes over.

Whilst not present in such situations, the PFC is completely aware and can recognize the illogical fears, use logic. This is called a "cold" cognitive state.
