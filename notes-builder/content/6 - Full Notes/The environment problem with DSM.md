# The environment problem with DSM
**Details**
Viewing the 4th axis on DSM, [[DSM - A tool for classifying mental illnesses]], we see the environmental problems that may contribute or affect the diagnosis or the state that the person is in currently. Now, the problem is that we are still forced to abide by and follow the treatment plan of DSM regardless of the environmental factors' effects.

This is bad/not efficient due to the implications that these environmental factors can have on the patient, and the information that can be inferred from them, and maybe they are not depressed at all due to it just being temporarily states. For example, if someone lost his job, he would be very depressed, not necessarily have MDD, but just an episodic period of depression, in this situation, would we say that the patient has MDD ?
