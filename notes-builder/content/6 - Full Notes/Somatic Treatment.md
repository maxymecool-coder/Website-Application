# An approach to treatment
**Details**
In people who are traumatized, we need to rebuild their social-engagement system, which can involve activities like yoga, like sports, team games, anything but yelling because it will only lead to further dysregulation. Despite this, most programs involve trying to promote new ways of thinking. When patients are numbed, are defensive, oppositional, it’s important to realize these are actions that were established to survive the threats that the host encountered. All these activities rely on interpersonal rhythms, visceral awareness,… it all helps the body to calm down and go back to working properly.
