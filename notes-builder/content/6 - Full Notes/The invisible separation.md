# The invisible separation
**Details**
Each lovers in a relationship sometimes find things to do that invisibly takes away the intimate moments without the both of them realizing. Maybe something that goes along with the activities they are going to do together then just adding someone else into it, avoiding the experience. This can be detrimental in a relationship as it fools all parties involved into thinking that they're spending time and caring about each other but they are actually just doing their personal usual tasks, just with someone who is doing the same thing, near them.
