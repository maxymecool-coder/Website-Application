# Branches supporting mental health
**Details**
Currently there are 3 distinct branches of sciences that has immensely affected trauma.
The first is neuroscience, studying the brain mental processes.

The second is developmental psychopathology, studying of the impact of adverse experiences on the body and the brain.

The third is interpersonal neurobiology, which is studying how one's behavior influences the emotions, biology and mindsets of others.
