# The Retinex Theory
**Details**
This theory derived from the question that was asked about how one would go about perceiving the colors of things in different lighting (blue lighting, green lighting, etc...). It was proposed that there is a tendency of an object to appear nearly the same color under a variety of lighting conditions, called color constancy.

The reason for such thing is that the cerebral cortex compares various retinal patterns and synthesizes a color perception for each area. This theory helps complete the other theories to give us an explanation on our vision and how we perceive it.
