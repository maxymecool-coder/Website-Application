# Models of Substance Use and Abuse
**Details**
Substance use is probably a universal phenomenon that appears in every single society past or present and perhaps future. Morally, it is wrong and should be completely prohibited as it is a character flaw. The Disease Model regards it as an acceptable way to treat illnesses and even though it reduces agency. The Sociocultural or Ecological Model emphasizes context and "fit"; what is considered normal in one culture may be unacceptable in another.

Internalization processes of this include: if the person has OCD or ADHD it may cause them to find drugs (impulse problems), those who have depression and such may find drugs to cure themselves of these conditions. A third use it to rebel and/or peer modeling.

**Supporting Content**
Genetic conditioning can lead a person to find drugs, and same as the environment can influence someone to find it as well through peer modeling ([[Multi-Systemic Therapy (MST)]])
