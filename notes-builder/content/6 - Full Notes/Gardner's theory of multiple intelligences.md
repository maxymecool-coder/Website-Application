# Gardner's theory of multiple intelligences
**Details**
This theory does not follow the [[The Psychometric Approach]] at all, rather it takes a view of there existing multiple categories or types of intelligence, relating to language, music and so on rather than a general intelligence approach. This means that we can be bad in one and awesome in another type of intelligence. For example, Roger Federer may be incredible at playing tennis but that does not mean he can easily understand Einstein's theory of relativity.

He argued further that if we could test these intelligences in pure form, they would simply be different manifestations of g, meaning stuff like language, logic, spatial reasoning... But of course there is no proof to do so currently.
