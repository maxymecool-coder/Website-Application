# Teaming in DBT
**Details**
To ensure the mental capacity of the guiders in DBT, it is most necessary for a person to form a team that will be able to keep them not only on track but also stable as well. Furthermore, DBT also have 4 modules which can be a lot for one person to teach a group of people effectively so splitting it between multiple people may help. Though there are other factors as well, like how each members have to be chosen according to a criteria depending on the characteristics of the group, availability and so on. Their skill level must also be sufficient, in how they know the basics of therapy and the specific skills of DBT.
