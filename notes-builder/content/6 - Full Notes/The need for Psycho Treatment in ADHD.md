# The need for Psycho Treatment in ADHD
**Details**
Although ADHD is a developmental disorder that usually does not have a particularly effective line of psychotherapy that particularly works for it, it is still encouraged that we try to help them in some way, shape, or form. Of course due to the neurobiological nature of it, medications are and should be the first line of treatment for ADHD, stimulant medications, such as methylphenidate, or Ritalin, have been prescribed since the early 1960s. These medications can reduce the disruptive behaviors and also improve people's ability to concentrate.

Though these medications can treat, there are methods like changing the classroom and training parents/teachers to help children with ADHD. These programs have demonstrated at least short-term success in improving both social and academic behavior. In this line of treatment, children are behaviorally conditioned so that they perform more positive tasks than negative ones so they can work, study, and so on.
