# Writer and Critic
**Details**
Separating yourself from a proofreading and editing standpoint, biased and knowledgeful about your own writing and turning into a critic who is judging every single point of the piece of information can be extremely hard as it is switching in between two different ideas, attention. This is still very important as you will need to read in your critic self to find out holes in arguments, badly explained parts. But also for the Writer it can be difficult as you can accidentally interrupt your thought process and line of thinking while writing as you keep thinking about what you are missing.

This forces the writer to be more critical, to differentiate whether the info that is being given actually coincides with which is already in the slip-box. This allows the notes to be corrected, complemented and improved upon, different info are crucially important because of the idea they convey. And when a paradox is encountered (both piece of info contradicts one another yet both are equally convincing) then now the writer has a problem, and has to think to solve that problem (its not unsolvable it just means the piece of info wasn't thorough enough or all the possibilities of the paradigm has been exploited).
