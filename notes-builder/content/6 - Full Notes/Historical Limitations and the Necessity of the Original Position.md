# Historical Limitations and the Necessity of the Original Position
**Details**
Locke's framework was so all-encompassing that it even basically neutralized his own political party's movement as they were practically a "parliament" which would also be stripped of its own power when the government is dysfunctional. And so they had to compensate to be able to continue their movement by downgrading his argument.

As impressive as it was for the time and as effective as it was, Locke's framework still had some glaring problems considering the practical implications of the time. Locke genuinely still rationalized that those who possessed wealth and what not were the ones allowed to make these decisions concerning these issues and so it would be rigged against them. Or in other situations it would always be rigged to the majority, to the strongest, etc... Which compelled John Rawls to develop the [[The Original Position]] which contained the veil of ignorance thought experiment [[The Necessary Person]].
