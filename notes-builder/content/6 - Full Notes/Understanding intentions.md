# Understanding intentions
**Details**
The last rule of Analytical Reading is Rule 4: Find out what the author’s problems were. Authors write books with a set of questions in mind to answer, and the book are those answers. 

And if they do not state these questions or answers to us, it is then our job to formulate them as precisely as we can. Consisting of the main question and then the subordinate ones, they will need to be put into order as well. 

Something that we also should not fall into is intentional fallacy. It is basically trying to discover what was in an author’s mind when he was writing it. Nevertheless, it is often still helpful to identify what he was trying to do. This rule helps that, by answering why it has the unity that it has; and their apprehension of the book’s skeletal structure ill lack comprehension of the end that it serves.
