# Comorbidity of LD and External Disorders
**Details**
Through researching, it is easy to see how comorbidity exists between reading disorders and externalizing disorders like ADHD, with roughly 20% of children having ADHD also having a reading disorder, suggesting shared neural substrates involving attention and processing. The discrepancy between genders in LD is also quite a bit lower, with it being about 1.5:1 ratio rather than something like Autism (5:1 ratio), CD (6:1) or ADHD (3:1) or even equal. This discrepancy can be explained because boys usually tough it out and do not show their suffering, girls, however, suffer in silence.

Biologically, these conditions are also highly heritable. With ADHD being around 75% (as talked about before) and reading disorders being at least 60%, showing a lot in the Chromosome 6 area. Neuroimaging provides even more detail where the brain of a person with a reading disorder resembles "the Fourth of July," activation scattered all over as the brain has to compensate for a missing function. However, as the brain is plastic, interventions can help rewire these pathways, normalizing brain activation.
