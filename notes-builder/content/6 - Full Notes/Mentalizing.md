# Mentalizing
**Details**
Mentalizing is the main part of MBT (Mentalization-Based Therapy) where a person is able to dwell upon their own thinking and others' thinking without completely assuming or getting wrapped up in their emotions.

As people with personality disorders are affected by [[Unhealthy Attachment]] due to how their caretakers treated them, they usually lack certain aspects of their own mentalization. Same as how DBT views these people as having trauma and extending said trauma to their current state.

The definition of the lack of mentalization capabilities in patient with personality disorders or merely people who lack the capability is quite similar to how Aaron Beck conceptualized automatic thoughts in his CBT work.

Difference is that in MBT, people are rather trained to recognize how they are interpreting their inner state/emotional state at the time as reality itself, whilst in CBT, people are basically interpreting it too quickly, falling into the fallacy of their thoughts.
