# The multiple meanings of phobias
**Details**
An example of this may be a barbershop. A number of separate fears of people from entering the barbershop, though it all deters them from coming to it. One patient, for instance, would run out of the barbershop before his turn. His fear, which was also present in other situations, such as attending church, school auditoriums, etc... had to do with public scrutiny. He was afraid of any situations where he could be ridiculed for his behavior and actions.

Another could not bear the waiting period involved. He was equally impatient in traffic jams. Some could be made anxious by the confinement in the barber's chair. The essence of their apprehension of being unable to get away. They may feel like prisoners.

This proves that in the same situation, place, or scene, different people may experience the same reactions for different reasons, different phobias.
