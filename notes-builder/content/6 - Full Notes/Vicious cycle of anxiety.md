# Vicious cycle of anxiety
**Details**
When facing with a situation that makes the person anxious, he may interpret his unpleasant affective state of anxiety. That state is then scanned, interpreted, and given meaning just as with an external stimulus. Since he associates anxiety with danger, he reads his anxiety as another danger signal. And so the vicious cycle is set up: ideation with a threatening content produces anxiety, the feedback of cues of anxiety leads to more production of it, and repeats.
