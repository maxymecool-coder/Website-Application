# Sunlight in the Afternoon
**Details**
Light is a powerful stimulus, and so we got to be cautious about how much light we view in the late afternoon and evening hours as well. Getting some sunlight, maybe 5-10 or 30 minutes may inoculate the nervous system against the effects of artificial light at night. This is because the kind of light in the late afternoon or evening signals to a different part of the Circadian clock that sleep is coming, serving as a second reference point for the brain to understand when it currently is.

**Supporting Content**
An extra tip is to view the light when a sunset is happening, research has shown that although this does not displace all the negative affects of artificial lighting in the evening, it does solve SOME of the problems that are caused by that. Still, it cannot replace actually getting some decent time outside in the sun.
