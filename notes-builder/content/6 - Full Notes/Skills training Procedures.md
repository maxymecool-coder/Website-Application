# Skills training Procedures
**Details**
When taking care of skills, the trainers must insist the clients on engaging with the process as much as possible, challenging the passive problem-solving style or in fact just ignorance. The procedures are basically as followed: skill acquisition; skill strengthening; and skill generalization. In the first stage, the trainer is trying to teach something new, in both the latter, they are trying to refine said skills.
