# What’s wrong with BPD
**Details**
When talking about emotional dysregulation in BPD patients, I believe that it’s not exactly the emotions that are the symptoms. It doesn’t mean that they are unjustifiable or anything, but rather the intensity and sensitivity of it all is catastrophic towards the person and their loved ones. After all, we also get unfairly angry at times.

Most of the time, these people don’t have the ability to regulate or control this emotional engine, which is the reason they lash out. The awareness they have over these emotions don’t help them to manage it, but instead causes even more emotion - like shame or guilt. All these emotions make the person shift their own anger to you or someone for not helping or not understanding. Any attempt for one to act against or try to be upset back may only result in the person being even more dysregulated, including not responding at all.

**Supporting Content**
Another point of this is that once dysregulated, a person may have the emotion for the longest period of time. They may constantly feel this annoying feeling throughout the whole week or something along those lines.
