# The Origins of Automaticity and Moral Responsibility
**Details**
Excluding innate biological reflexes, the automaticity of human behavior originates from two distinct mechanisms of practice. The first is unintentional or incidental practice, in which constant, repeated exposure to an environment allows cultural norms and native languages to be effortlessly absorbed and automated without conscious consent or awareness. Or, turning it into a habit through practicing (interesting its not like I have a whole series of notes on it). Legal and social systems inherently hold individuals far more accountable for actions executed through deliberate, reflective control (such as premeditated crimes) than for impulsive, automated reactions (such as crimes of passion), acknowledging that automatic behaviors bypass the willful consent characteristic of reflective consciousness.
