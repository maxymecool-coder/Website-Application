# The Ambiguity of DSM
**Details**
Due to the constant changing development of psychopathology, with each addition to the DSM, the more sensitive it gets. If someone does an intake interview, and their stress or symptoms are only mild, they may get a diagnosis. In RCTs using the DSM, half of the participants were diagnosed to have some form of mental disorders at any given time. These symptoms that the DSM perceives as abnormal are not actual signs of mental illness when taken a look at by psychologists.

But there's another side to this, the ambiguity of DSM also allows us to use it against cooperation and law firms  that try to manipulate and use it for their own good.
