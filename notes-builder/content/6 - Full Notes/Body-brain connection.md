# Body-brain connection
**Details**
Intense emotions are felt throughout the whole body, via the pneumogastric nerve, which is involved in the expression and management of emotions in humans and animals. This makes it so that even when we can manage emotions that are happening in our brain most of the time, the gut wrenching sensation of being physically beaten is simply unbearable… That is why we will do anything for it to go away, be it numbing ourselves with drugs or alcohol or cutting ourselves to replace these overwhelming emotions with sensations.
