# The Processing of the SCU
**Details**
The core of the SCU's method is a structured yet flexible case management system designed to handle reports from various sources, including students, staff, and external parties. Upon receiving a report through multiple channels such as a dedicated phone line, email, or an anonymous online form, the SCU conducts an initial triage. This critical first step ensures that emergencies are immediately escalated to police or security, while other matters are appropriately directed. For cases falling within its purview, the SCU employs a triage risk assessment to guide its response, which can range from simply recording the information to implementing more formal resolutions. This systematic approach allows for a tailored response to the specific level of risk and need presented by each case.

A defining feature of the SCU's approach is its "victim/survivor-centered" philosophy. By prioritizing the needs and wishes of the person who has been harmed, the unit aims to mitigate the potential for re-traumatization throughout the reporting and resolution process. This ethos dictates the management actions taken, which are decided in consultation with the reporting party. The options are broad, encompassing informal advice for self-management, formal warnings, or escalation to other university bodies for disciplinary action. This empowerment of the victim/survivor marks a departure from purely punitive systems and aligns with contemporary best practices in trauma-informed care.
