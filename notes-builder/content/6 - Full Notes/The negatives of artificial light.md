# The negatives of artificial light
**Details**
Now, this is unfair, sunlight when you wake up is necessary in a large amount and intensely, as it would setup your whole day before you, and in this aspect, artificial light cannot help you. But when you are about go to sleep, artificial light can completely screw you up as it will mess with your circadian rhythms, opposite to what happens when you wake up.

As the brain is extremely sensitive throughout the evening, not just before you are sleeping, you will also need to try to keep everything dim at this time, dim enough that you could still work and all though.
