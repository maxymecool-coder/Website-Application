# In suggesting stories, plays and poems reading
**Details**
Most of the rules that applied to imaginative reading applies here too. The clearest difference lies in the last rule, that the question of “What of it ?” Is usually irrelevant.

Strictly speaking, no action is called upon your part when reading a novel, play, or poem (analytically of course). You don’t have those responsibilities that you did in reading expository books.

This is only “strictly speaking” since usually books like these evoke something in readers making them act on the emotions they gained from the book.

But at the end of the day, that is not the main point of the book, it is to please, it is a fine art, it starts and ends with itself, in itself, and does not move beyond that. (Emersonian)

If it does make you want to do something, try to understand what made you feel such way and did it truly exist in the literature. All you need to do well to read it is to experience it, any further is just your own doings.
