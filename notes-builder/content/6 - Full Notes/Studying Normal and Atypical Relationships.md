# Studying Normal and Atypical Relationships
**Details**
The studies between these two types of relationships are synonymous as they are so closely connected to each other. To see how atypical relationships are, one must study what normal relationships look like so that they have a baseline of what to expect. A disorder does not appear in a vacuum; it is a deviation from a typical trajectory. Therefore, to comprehend conditions like autism or ADHD, we must first understand the typical development of social cognition, empathy, attention, and impulse control.

But also atypical relationships helps us understand what is in the normal. Take H.M.M for example, one of the most famous patients in psychology. Understanding that his hippocampus prevented him from making long term memories since they had to remove it to treat severe seizures helped us understand that about the human brain as well.

This also extends to the larger field as well, studying people or kids with autism, or conduct disorder helps us understand what systems underpins those actions and therefore understand more about the psychology of the human brain.
