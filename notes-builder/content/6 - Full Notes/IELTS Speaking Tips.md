# IELTS Speaking Tips
**Details**
When speaking, or simply talking to each other, people tend to gravitate towards stories that actually happen to them rather than extravagant or made-up ones, but in an IELTS test, no such things actually exists. This is because the examiner is just a random person you meet, they are not going to meet you again probably and even if they do it wouldn't matter because one place is a testing place and the other is the outside world.

So use your language as a vehicle to show off your skills, and do not let your experience or mind stop you from lying out a good story to show it off. The foundational rule to this is to just make the story at least believable, that's all, and use your brain power in wording that story, not spending brainpower to think of the story.
