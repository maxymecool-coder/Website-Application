# Technical words
**Details**
A lot of the times in expository works, we will encounter words that the author puts explicit stress onto. Which means he can mark it using quotations or italics, he may even discuss its various senses and even tell you he’s gonna use it. 

Alongside that, we can also use our own prior knowledge of the subject matter to understand what the writer is conveying, or the other way around, you don’t get a couple words that are special in those fields. 

An oddball situation are philosophers, who are notorious for having private vocabularies. Although there are some words that are staple in the field, they usually mean different things by different writers, and are never really technical. Thankfully, these guys know that, and so they give warning whenever they’re gonna adopt a new word. 

In this connection, one clue to an important word is that the author quarrels with other writers about it. When you find an author telling you how a particular word has been used by others, and why he chooses to use it otherwise, you can be sure that word makes a great difference to him.
