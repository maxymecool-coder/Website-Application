# The Organizing Principle of the Limbic System
**Details**
Literally everything in the Limbic System is trying to influence the hypothalamus in some way, through all the wirings, the nuclei and so on. This is because the hypothalamus is like the output for the limbic system, as it influences the endocrine system, the autonomic nervous system, which is interconnected with the rest of the body. The parts of the limbic system will go as far as inhibiting each other to get its signal through to the hypothalamus rather than the others.

A good way to see which component's ability and signal will be stronger is counting the number of neurons between them. The farther the distance of neurons the part is from the hypothalamus, the more signals from other parts it would have to contend with, resulting in an altered signal.
