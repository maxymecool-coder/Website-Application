# The ways cells communicate
**Details**
There are a range of ways that cells can communicate with each other and coordinate. The most intimate is touching, cell to cell, the next is paracrine signaling, which is chemical-transmission, then neuronal communication, which depends on electricity. The last is the endocrine system, which the body sends hormones through the bloodstream and throughout the whole body, which means it can coordinate the body for all sorts of stuff.
