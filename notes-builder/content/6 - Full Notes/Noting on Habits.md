# Noting on Habits
**Details**
By the nature of science and psychology, established habits are inherently automatic. Most of the time, we do not even realize a habit is happening; our brains have offloaded the processing to the unconscious mind to save energy. Because of this, we are often incapable of changing how we behave because we lack an indication of what we are actually doing. To tackle this issue, the very first step in habit change must be building a "meta-habit" of pointing out and documenting our current behaviors. By creating an inventory of our automatic actions, we bring them back into conscious awareness. The more detailed our descriptions are, the more memorable they become, allowing us to finally intervene.
