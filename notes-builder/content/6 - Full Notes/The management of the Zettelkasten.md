# The management of the Zettelkasten
**Details**
A lot of the time, people get lost in the process of researching more knowledge and integrating it into their system that they forget to maintain their past knowledge and perhaps even forget about it.

This is why maintenance is such an important task in Zettelkasten, it allows the user to retain their knowledge as well as more effectively word and explain the concepts. Moreover, the topics and subjects can also be more efficiently sorted as they periodically improve their base even if it is perhaps the most boring task.
