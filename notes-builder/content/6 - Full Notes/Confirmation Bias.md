# Confirmation Bias
**Details**
- Confirmation bias is the tendency of which a human constantly tries to find things that it understands and makes itself seem to be the right one in every case. We have to understand that people have different perspectives, learning this and applying it to life situations can allow us to learn and gain way more information.

**Supporting Content**
- Why do we like being around people who think like us ? (Because they think like us)
- Why do we just stick to stuff that confirms what we already know ? (Because it makes us feel right and smart)
