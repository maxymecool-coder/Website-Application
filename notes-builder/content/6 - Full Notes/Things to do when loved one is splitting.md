# Things to do when loved one is splitting
**Details**
Firstly, before doing anything, trying to give any advice or whatever, try to rather focus on your emotion, what you are feeling and not responding with that same emotion, as it will only get you wrapped up with your loved one's situation and make it even worse. If that is simply too hard try to go and be mindful of your own feelings and wellbeing, meaning you can go take care of yourself to calm yourself down.

Secondly, try not to say anything that can make them emotional, try to be validating first, giving advice, trying to distract or telling them that "its not so bad" are all invalidating. You have to say and GET how badly they are feeling right now. Don't try to ask them to be different, don't withhold a solution to a problem if you have it but also don't say it if they don't want to, just try to encourage them rather and regulate yourself, the general rule is just to not do anything that you think could be invalidating.

Remember, you must also cheerlead them and remind them of their abilities to get through this as well as validating how hard it is, for example, asking interested questions is a way to do so. Then and only then can you start asking them what they need help with doing or if they can help. The person may say that they cannot think of a solution and all, this is because in that state they are still way too high on their emotions that they cannot get into a clear state of mind to do so, that is when you come back to that later and continue validating/regulating them. 

**Supporting Content**
Additionally, suggest them to do the opposite action of what is causing them to be negative and all might also help. Of course do it in a way that is validating.
