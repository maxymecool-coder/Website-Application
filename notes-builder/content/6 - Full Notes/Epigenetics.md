# Epigenetics
**Details**
This is the field of studying changes in the gene expression without changing the structure of the gene itself. There is a layer that covers DNAs called chromatin, and this layer can be changed by the environment (temporarily or permanent) to basically be impenetrable by the transcription factor and so it would no longer be changed or affected by it. For example, a mother rat's nurturing style can permanently alter the expression of stress-related genes in her offspring's brain due to the offspring feel like it does not need to be hyperaware to ensure its own survival anymore.
