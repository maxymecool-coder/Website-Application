# Politics and Identities
**Details**
Same as how institutions build identities [[Institutions shaping Identities]], the same can also be said for politics. This street goes both ways, perhaps just like how institutions are. Politics shape us as we shape politics.

A study by Larry Bartels on perceptions of the economy under President Reagan provides a stark example, the policies that that specific party advocates for would be seen as growing or becoming better, meaning there is bias in this, lots of it. In 1988, despite the fact that both unemployment and inflation had fallen significantly over the preceding eight years, **over 50% of strong Democrats incorrectly believed that inflation had gotten worse**. It was not just a lack of information but pure psychological bias.

This would also affect the economy because supporters of a political party will be optimistic and spend more if that political party is in term or something along those lines.
